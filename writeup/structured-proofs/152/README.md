   the infinite places Φ(P, s) as a group ≅ Gal(E/K) ≅ (ℤ/N₁)^× (`galOfPlace`, `placeMulEquiv`, `placeOfInt`,
   `placeClass`), the CM type, the different (`relDifferent_spec`, `ϖ`, `prod_varpi_eq_rootDiscr_pow`).
2. `Data` (AdmissibleData.lean): read every field in the member text; then the names k, t, a, b₁, b₂, w, the nominal
   weight, `primeSum`, `budget`, `jetSize`, `dirCost`.
3. `Data.HasLogBound` and `Data.le_of_hasLogBound` (Magnitudes.lean): the size calculus. The anonymous instances are
   its rules (products add exponents, sums take the maximum plus 1, …) and the bounds of the quantities of D.
4. `exists_data` (DataExist.lean), the existence of admissible data, with `exists_three_term_free` (Behrend) and
   `exists_mul_principal` (class-group pigeonhole).
5. WeilDataOfE.lean (Frobenius elements, the places above p ≡ 1 mod N₁d, the Weil data of E) and
   ChosenPlaceLevels.lean (chosen places, neat levels, Minkowski's bound) serve later units.
