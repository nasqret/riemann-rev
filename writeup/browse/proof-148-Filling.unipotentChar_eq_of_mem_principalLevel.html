lemma Filling.unipotentChar_eq_of_mem_principalLevel

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), u,v\in GFin(S) in \mathsf{U} (implicit; hu, hv) and g in the finite adelic unitary group of X_0: if u^{-1}\cdot(h^{-1}vh)\in K(g) for h= inclHFin(S)(g) (hk), then unipotentChar(S,v)= unipotentChar(S,u).
The argument in briefThe character is trivial on \sigma of K(g)-elements divided by g, and \sigma is additive on \mathsf{S} and vanishes on \mathsf{H}.

    Let k=u^{-1}(h^{-1}vh)\in K(g). Since K(g)= unitaryLevel(\hat{O},g),g^{-1}\sigma(k)\in\hat{O}, so selfDualChar(g^{-1}\sigma(k))=1. Hence unipotentChar(u)= unipotentChar(u)\cdotselfDualChar(g^{-1}\sigma(k))= selfDualChar(g^{-1}(\sigma(u)+\sigma(k)))= selfDualChar(g^{-1}\sigma(uk)) (\sigma is additive on \mathsf{S}, simpleRootSum_mul) and uk=h^{-1}vh with \sigma(h^{-1}vh)=\sigma(v) (\sigma vanishes on h,h^{-1}\in\mathsf{H}); so unipotentChar(u)= unipotentChar(v).
