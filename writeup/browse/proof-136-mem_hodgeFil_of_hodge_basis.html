lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.mem_hodgeFil_of_hodge_basis

What it saysLet L be a lattice datum, x \in fracSpan(L) (hx), i a letter, n \in \mathbb{Z} and \mu : W(C^\flat)\otimes L \to B_{\mathrm{dR}} A_{\mathrm{inf}}-linear. Suppose (H): for every \kappa \in \mathbb{Z}, every \nu \in formsOfType(L, i, \kappa) and every B_{\mathrm{dR}}-linear map D from the A_{\mathrm{inf}}-linear functionals on W(C^\flat)\otimes L to B_{\mathrm{dR}} such that D(\mu') \in hodgeFil(m + \kappa) whenever \mu'(1 \otimes t) \in hodgeFil(m) for all t \in L, and D(\mu') = g\cdot D(\mu'') whenever g \in \Gamma' and \mu'(1 \otimes gt) = g\cdot\mu''(1 \otimes t) for all t: D(\mu)\cdot\nu(x) \in hodgeFil(n). Then \mu(x) \in hodgeFil(n).
The argument in briefChoose a Hodge basis: L is potentially crystalline; with A = P\cdot\operatorname{diag}(t^{\kappa_m}) (a unit), functionals \nu_m of types \kappa_m have as basis values the rows of A^{-1}; \mu = \sum_m D_m(\mu)\nu_m on fracSpan with D_m(\mu) = (basisValues(\mu)\cdot A)_{m} (the reconstruction multiplies by A^{-1}: (basisValues(\mu)\cdot A)\cdot A^{-1} = basisValues(\mu)), D_m equivariant and shifting by \kappa_m, and H applies to each term.
