lemma AuxPair.heckePolyOnForms_testValue_eq_zero

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data (instance taken by the lemma), r, n finite, O : AuxPair E r n an auxiliary pair, w a finite place with \overline{w} \ne w (hw) and w off the places of O and their conjugates (hS). If an algebraic form y on V satisfies Lifts(O, y, z) for an eigensystem z (h: at such places y is spherical with Hecke polynomial \prod_{0\le l<n-r}(1 - q_w^{l} \nu X)\cdot p(X), \nu = frobValue(a(O), r, w)), then the Hecke polynomial operator heckePolyOnForms(O, \mathit{hw}, hS) at X = testValue(O, w) kills y.
The argument in briefThe Hecke polynomial acts on y by its value at X = testValue(w), whose first factor vanishes there (the rank difference is positive).
