lemma Filling.isIntMatrix_middleBaseChange_mul

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), a type m and a \kappa\times m matrix l over \mathbb{A}_f (both implicit): (a) if \operatorname{diag}(\overline{\pi}^{-1} at k_0)\cdot l is integral, then middleBaseChange(\mathfrak{s},\overline{\pi})\cdot l is integral; (b) middleBaseChange(\mathfrak{s},\overline{\pi})\cdot l is integral iff l is integral and every entry of the row k_0 of l lies in \overline{\pi}\cdot\hat{O}.
The argument in briefThe rows of middleBaseChange(\overline{\pi})\cdot l are l_i (i\ne k_0),\pi\cdot l_{k_0} and -\overline{\pi}^{-1}\cdot l_{k_0}; compare integrality row by row.
