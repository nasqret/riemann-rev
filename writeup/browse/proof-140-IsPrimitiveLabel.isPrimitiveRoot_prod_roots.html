lemma IsPrimitiveLabel.isPrimitiveRoot_prod_roots

What it saysLet k be a finite field, c in k[X] and r a natural number with \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c). Then the product of the roots of c in the algebraic closure of k, counted with multiplicity, is a primitive root of unity of order |k| - 1; and there is a natural number e such that \xi^e equals this product for every root \xi of c in the algebraic closure.
The argument in briefThe roots of the irreducible c are the Frobenius conjugates \xi^{q^i}, i < r, so their product is \xi^{1 + q + \cdots + q^{r-1}} for every root \xi; for the root \xi_0 of order q^r - 1 this power is a primitive (q - 1)-th root of unity.
