lemma IntegralPlace.phase_besselS_mul

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. Lemma. If x\in\mathsf{S} (the Bessel subgroup), u\in\mathsf{U} and u^{-1}x\in K(g), then \operatorname{phase}(g,x\cdot y)=\psi(g^{-1}\sigma(u))\cdot\operatorname{phase}(g,y) for every y.
The argument in briefCases on whether y\in\mathsf{U}K(g): if y=k\cdot(element of K(g)), then xy=(x k x^{-1})(x ...) gives the representative x k u ... with \sigma additive; otherwise neither xy is in \mathsf{U}K(g).
