lemma Data.exists_isTidy_diagonalPeriodForm_eq

What it saysLet P be parameters and D admissible data. There are C \in \mathbb{C} and a family \Lambda (\Lambda(\vartheta) \in GridDatum(\vartheta)) such that for every m-fold twist \vartheta: \Lambda(\vartheta) is tidy of order 4m on path(0), and for every real point r and forms \Xi, \varphi at the nodes (0, m) of the grids V and Z: triangleOrder(false, \vartheta, m)\cdotdiagonalPeriodForm(\vartheta, 0 \le m, r)(iotaPlus(\vartheta, false, 0)(\Xi), iotaPlus(1, true, 0)(\varphi)) = C\cdotgridPeriod(\Lambda, (\vartheta, \Xi, \varphi), 0 \le m, r).
The argument in briefAverage the level-tidy datum of Data.exists_isLevelTidy_iotaPlus_eq (b = 0) over coset representatives t(x) of the node levels, weighted by their edge characters: this makes it equivariant for all admissible families (tidy of order 4m); the identity follows from Data.diagonalPeriodForm_tupleAction and the index ratios of Data.triangleOrder_eq_mul_levelIndex.
