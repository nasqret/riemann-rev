lemma block_defect_le_at_place

What it saysLocal loss at one place above \ell. Let D be admissible data, \ell a prime with \iota \ell fixed, \Gamma a topological group with j = 𝒥.toGp : \Gamma \to G_\ell of open image (OverGp), e : E \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell with IsCompletionGroup e j (hj), and r, t \in \mathbb{N} and jumps J with IsRowType D r t J (hJ). Then for all sufficiently large coefficient rings O (eventually along CoeffRing \ell): for every family L of Galois lattices over O for \Gamma indexed by Option (\operatorname{Fin} t), every F, K and every family U(g) of finite sets of integers indexed by the embeddings g : O \to \mathbb{C}_\ell, if (\ell = g_n for some n and K(s) is of tame type IsTameType \ell D.r ((D.r + D.t)\cdotD.r) for every s with (j(s), 0) a Weil pair, i.e. s in inertia) or (F = \top and K = \perp), every a \in U(g) is a jump at g of some L_i, L_{\mathrm{none}} has the jumps J(complexEmbOf e g) at coeffEmb O g and L_{\text\{ some \} c} the single jump c, and \operatorname{rk}_O L_{\mathrm{none}} = r, then for every c \in Option (\operatorname{Fin} t), with x = 1 if c = none and x = D.t otherwise: if L_c lies in the class InClass F K U, the normalised O-length of Z_f(L_c^\vee (1)) modulo cupOrthogonal (InClass F K U) is at most [G_\ell : j(\Gamma )]\cdot \operatorname{rk}_O L_c\cdot(loss D \ell / x), L_c^\vee (1) the twisted dual (tateDual).
The argument in briefChoose a Kummer chart for \Gamma. In the tame case (\ell = g_n) the tame-types exponent theorem bounds the defect of the dual by normLength_defect_le. In the crystalline case apply crystalline_defect_le to the twisted dual with cutoff Q = D.k\cdotD.t and low-jump multiplicity bound \mu = \lfloor \operatorname{rk}(L_c)\cdotD.m/(D.k\cdot x)\rfloor. The thickness and signs are read from the image of a root of unity \zeta (cmSign_eq_of_thick) and the spacing of \operatorname{thick} places (not_thick_of_short_arc).
