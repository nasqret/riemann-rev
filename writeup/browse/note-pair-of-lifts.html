defIsAdmissibleComb

Definition 2.8 of the note · unit 141 · New/Seesaw/AdmissibleComb.lean, lines 12–13

Definition. For a type \alpha, a complex vector space Z, a family v : \alpha \to Z, a set A \subseteq \alpha, natural numbers n and d, a proposition p, a real number B and z \in Z: IsAdmissibleComb(v, A, n, d, p, B, z) holds when z = \sum_{x} r(x)\cdot v(x) for some finitely supported r : \alpha \to \mathbb{C} with IsAdmissibleCoeffs(A, n, d, p, B, r): the support of r lies in A, d\cdot r(x) \in integralAway(n) for all x, and, if p holds, \sum_{x} |\sigma(r(x))| \le B for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}. In words: z is a finite combination of the v(x), x \in A, with coefficients integral away from n up to the denominator d, of size at most B at every conjugate when p holds.
