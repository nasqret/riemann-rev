lemma TwistingData.exists_projector

What it saysLet N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, O a valuation domain (a commutative integral domain whose ideals are totally ordered), T : TwistingData N W O (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to O, w \in O with w^{2} = p), and write Fam for the families \mathbb{Z}/N \to Monomials (Fin k) n, \mathfrak{m} for the maximal ideal of O, \kappa_{O} = O/\mathfrak{m} and T mod \mathfrak{m} = map (residue O) T, O of characteristic 0, p not a unit in O (hp), k, n, c \in \mathbb{N}, a, r \in Fin k with a + 1 = r (ha), \ell \in \mathbb{N} with \ell + \ell = N (e) and N\cdot n\cdot k\cdot(\ell +2) + n + k < P^{\ell} (h), and \theta \in \mathbb{R}. There are b : Fam \to O and a square matrix Z over O indexed by Fam such that: (i) Z\cdot F \in excessCond T n a r (w^{c}\cdot O) \theta for every F; (ii) for every y \in excessCond T n a r (w^{c}\cdot O) (\theta -(4c + 2)) and every F, w^{c} divides y\cdot(\operatorname{diag}(b)\cdot Z\cdot F) - p^{(k+k^{2})\cdot o_{0}N\cdot N}\cdot(y\cdot F) (b \ast Z is the row scaling \operatorname{diag}(b)\cdot Z); and (iii) b(\beta)\cdot \rho(\gamma^{\mathsf{T}})_{\beta \alpha} = b(\alpha)\cdot \rho(\gamma)_{\alpha \beta} for every \gamma \in \mathrm{GL}_k(W) and all families \alpha, \beta (\rho = letterRep T n). (The text reading also lists the section variable \beta, which the statement binds in \forall \gamma \alpha \beta.)
The argument in briefAverage the Frobenius-twisted generic operator over Levi-invariant means: Z(m) applies the tensor product of an N-tuple m of linear forms (exists_tensor_dual) to frobOperator T r \ell n (frobGenericMatrix). With the mean \kappa_{P} (exists_mean, one per letter) and the invariant diagonal form b_{0} (exists_invariant_diagonal_form), Z' = Z(-\kappa_{\text{False}} + \kappa_{\text{True}})\cdot \operatorname{diag}(b'). Key (step 6): if the tuple m is the conjugate of a tuple l by a Levi element g (m_j = l_j \circ conjSubst(\iota_{j}, g)) and the coefficient condition H holds — for every row \beta at which the coefficient of the translate of y is not in w^{c}\cdot O (the contributing rows; the others are disposed of by dite at line 174) and every monomial \nu, either the product of the values of l at the letters vanishes or \nu satisfies the hypotheses of pow_dvd_coeff_conj_frobOperator (equal row and column indexWeight-weights at every letter j \ne i, and a gap strictly greater than 2c + 1 at i between the excess of \beta at i + \ell and the row weight of \nu) — then y\cdot Z(m)F \in w^{c}\cdot O (frobOperator_map, pow_dvd_coeff_conj_frobOperator). Levi invariance of the tuple alone does not give this: evaluation at the identity matrix in every letter is invariant, it sends the generic Frobenius operator to the identity matrix, and then the pairing y\cdot Z(m)F = y\cdot F can be 1. The means \kappa chosen by exists_mean supply H for the rows needed (their properties h_{1}, h_{2}). Expanding multilinearly over the letters, only the all-\kappa_{\text{True}} term survives modulo w^{c}, and it is p^{(k+k^{2})o_{0}N\cdot N}\cdot(y\cdot F) by \mathit{hz}; the congruence (ii) is tested on y in the excess condition at \theta -(4c + 2).
