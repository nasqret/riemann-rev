lemma Filling.one_sided_poisson

What it saysLemma (iterated one-sided Poisson summation). Let S be admissible, T a finite set of finite places, A a finite set of units of E with t,t^{-1} integral outside T for t\in A, and g^{-1} integral outside T. Let n\in\mathbb{N} and let K be a sieve system (SieveSystem) of depth n on \mathbb{C}^{f}\times\prod_{w\in T}E_w with forms J_i and unit map u. The map u replaces each zero complex coordinate by 1. It keeps the finite tuple when all its coordinates are nonzero and sends the whole tuple to 1 otherwise. The system uses the map p\mapsto(p_{\infty}^{-1},\mathtt{ratToPlaces}(T)(g^{-1})\cdot p_T^{-1}), character x\mapsto\prod_v\operatorname{wave}(x_v)\cdotcharOnPlaces(S,T)(x_T), bottom bound En, weights \Lambda and frequency bound \beta (so J_i integrates J_{i+1} over a common entry, J_{n+1} is continuous and positive on equal entries, |J_0|\le En, and unit frequencies and unequal entries are bounded by \beta\cdot\Lambda\cdot\Lambda). Let \Gamma,\varepsilon be reals with \varepsilon\ge0,\Lambda\ge0, and assume: for every a and every c=(c_v) with |c_v|^{-1}\le|\kappa_v|/g for all v and every finite set X of nonzero T-integers, \Sigma_{y\in X}\beta(\mathtt{ratPoint}(T,(c_v/\mathtt{frameScaleSq}(S,v,a))_v,y))\le\varepsilon. Put hd(i,p,t,a)= the unit image of ratPoint(T, (frameScaleSq(S,v,a)){}_v, padTuple(p,t,a)) for p : Fin i\to A and t\in E^{\times}, and c_a=\operatorname{vol}(\hat{O})/\prod_v frameScaleSq(S,v,a)^{2}. If (\Sigma_p\Lambda_i(\mathtt{hd}(i,p,1)))^{2}\le\Gamma\cdot\prod_{a<i}c_a for every i\le n, then for every i\le n and every V\subseteq A: \operatorname{Re}\Sigma_{t\in V}\Sigma_p\Sigma_q J_{i+1}(\mathtt{hd}(i,p,t),\mathtt{hd}(i,q,t))\le(\prod_{a\le i}c_a)\cdot(En+(2i+1)\cdot\Gamma\cdot\varepsilon),p,q over Fin i\to A.
The argument in briefStrong induction on i. The step function F of the sieve system at level i (the integrand of J_i in the last entry) is nonnegative and integrable. After rescaling and restriction to the adeles integral outside T, the resulting function is lower semicontinuous at the rational points t\in V, where the entries are units. One-sided Poisson summation there bounds the diagonal sum by c_i times (the zero Fourier coefficient J_i(\ldots) plus the sum of the other Fourier coefficients); the latter are bounded through \beta by \varepsilon(\Sigma\Lambda)^{2}, and J_i(\ldots) by the induction hypothesis (and the off-diagonal bound for the unequal entries).
