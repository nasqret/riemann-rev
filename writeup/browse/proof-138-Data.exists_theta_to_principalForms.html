lemma Data.exists_theta_to_principalForms

What it saysFor admissible data D, a tame character \vartheta of D and a natural number j: there is a \mathbb{C}-linear map L from the forms on V_j ((auxPair D \vartheta j).FormV) to principalForms(D, spaceW(D, j)) such that, if 1 \le j \le m (j \in D.LiftIndex), L(\Xi ) = (auxPair D \vartheta j).\theta \Xi as functions on U(W_j)(\mathbb{A}) for every \Xi.
The argument in briefIf 1 \le j \le m, L is \theta _j with its values restricted: they are forms of the right type with principal level at the chosen places. Otherwise the condition is void.

      Case 1 \le j \le m: take for L the map \Xi \mapsto \theta _j\Xi with its values restricted to principalForms(D, W_j).

      1. 1.1.1factlines 218–220

        \theta _j\Xi is a form of type typeKernel(paramW) on W_j: by theta_eq_smul_star_lift_swap it is a multiple of the conjugate of the descent of the conjugate of F_\Xi, and the pair (W_j, V_j) is an edge of the real parameters (fitsParams_auxPair \ldots isEdge.isArrow), so the descent of a form of parameter paramV is a form of the parameter of W_j (isForm_ofAlgForm).

        UsesAuxPair.theta_eq_smul_star_lift_swap unit 134, Data.fitsParams_auxPair unit 147

      2. 1.1.2factlines 220–222

        \theta _j\Xi has the principal level at the chosen places: for k in that level, the reduction s of k is 1, so (\theta _j\Xi )(g\cdot k) = \theta _j(1\cdot \Xi )(g) = (\theta _j\Xi )(g) (theta_mul_eq_theta_formAction with s = 1; mem_levelSpace_formRep_iff).

        UsesAuxPair.theta_mul_eq_theta_formAction unit 138, Space.mem_levelSpace_formRep_iff unit 102

      3. 1.1.3factline 222

        By construction L(\Xi ) = \theta _j\Xi for every \Xi.

    2. 1.2caseline 222
