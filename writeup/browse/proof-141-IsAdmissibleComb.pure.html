lemma IsAdmissibleComb.pure

What it saysLet n be a natural number and p a proposition; let \alpha_j (j \in \mathbb{N}) be additive groups, F_j complex vector spaces, v_i : \alpha_{2i} \times \alpha_{2i+1} \to F_i, A_j \subseteq \alpha_j additive subgroups, f_i \in F_i, d and m natural numbers and B real. Assume (premise) IsAdmissibleComb(v_i, A_{2i} \times A_{2i+1}, n, d, p, B, f_i) for every i < m. Then the pure tensor f_{m-1} \otimes \cdots \otimes f_0 (TensorChain.pure f m) is an admissible combination IsAdmissibleComb(y \mapsto v_{m-1}(y_{2m-2}, y_{2m-1}) \otimes \cdots \otimes v_0(y_0, y_1), {y : y_j \in A_j for all j}, n, d^{m}, p, |B|^{m}, f_{m-1} \otimes \cdots \otimes f_0) of pure tensors indexed by tuples y.
The argument in briefInduction on the number m of factors, one tensor factor at a time (map_bilinear), then re-indexing.
