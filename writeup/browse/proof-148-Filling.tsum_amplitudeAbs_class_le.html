lemma Filling.tsum_amplitudeAbs_class_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified and S admissible; let j,c,c',s and \gamma\in termsOver(S,c,s,c') with HasLevel(S,j,\gamma), and assume termDiscNorm(S,\gamma)\le N^{f}\cdot minorNorm(S,j,\gamma) (\prod_v|\Phi_v(\mathsf{g}(\gamma))| at most N^{f} times the norm of the leading minor). Then, for the cut Bessel functions, \Sigma_{\delta\in\mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g)\gamma\mathsf{U}_c'^{0}(g)} amplitudeAbs(S,c,c',\delta)\le\nu\cdot classSize(S,j,\mathtt{classGauge}(S,j,c,c',\gamma)), where classGauge(S,j,c,c',\gamma)=\max(\mathtt{termDiscNorm}(S,\gamma),N^{f}\cdot\mathtt{minorNorm}(S,j,\gamma))/(i(k)\cdot i(k^{-1})),k=h_c^{-1}\gamma h_c',i= xRowsIndex(S,j,\cdot), and \nu= unipCovolume(S).
The argument in briefCauchy–Schwarz over the class: (\Sigma\mathtt{amplitudeAbs})^{2}\le\Sigma_{\delta} schurIntegral(c,c',\delta)\cdot\Sigma_{\delta} schurIntegral(c',c,\delta^{-1}), bound both factors by tsum_schurIntegral_le (for \gamma and for \gamma^{-1}, which has the same minor norm and inverse term position), and compare with \nu^{2}\cdotclassSize{}^{2} using the hypothesis k and \tau^{128}\ge\ldots from the admissibility bounds.
