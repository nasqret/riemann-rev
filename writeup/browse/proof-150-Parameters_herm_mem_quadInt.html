lemma Parameters.herm_mem_quadInt

What it saysFor parameters P, an exponent s and x, y \in E such that \alpha^{-1}x and \alpha^{-1}y are integral finite adeles (finiteImage id x ∈ Ô, finiteImage id y ∈ Ô, \alpha = \alpha(P, s) the finite idele of the Weil data): \sum_\tau \Phi_\tau(x)\cdot \operatorname{conj}(\Phi_\tau(y)) lies in \operatorname{quadInt}(d), the image of \mathcal{O}_K in \mathbb{C}.
The argument in briefThe sum is \operatorname{Tr}_{E/K}(x\overline{y}). Since \alpha^{-1}x, \alpha^{-1}y are integral and \alpha \cdot \overline{\alpha}\cdot P'(\zeta) is a unit at every finite place, x\overline{y}\cdot P'(\zeta) is an integer of E; as P'(\zeta) generates the relative different of E/K, x\overline{y} lies in the inverse different and its trace lies in \mathcal{O}_K.

    \operatorname{Tr}_{E/K}(x\cdot \overline{y}) \in \mathcal{O}_K. The relative different of E/K is generated by P'(\zeta) \ne 0 (relDifferent_spec), and an element whose product with the different is integral has integral trace (the different criterion).
