lemma Filling.exists_step_function

What it saysLemma. Let A be a semilocal field with an involution and an integral place structure P (integers R= P.R), n a finite index type, G a subgroup of GL_n(A),H a subgroup of G whose elements are integral matrices (i:H\le\mathtt{GL\_n}(R)\cap G),\iota a type, p:\iota\to A^{n},f:A^{n}\to[0,\infty],s\subseteq\iota a finite set and c:\iota\to\mathbb{R}_{\ge0}. Suppose (hf) that for every z\in s, for every x in some neighbourhood of p(z), every k\in H and every y\in A^{n} with k\cdot y=x satisfy c(z)\le f(y). Then there is \Phi:A^{n}\to\mathbb{R}_{\ge0} with compact support such that \Phi(x+v)=\Phi(x) for all x and all v in some neighbourhood of 0,\Phi(k\cdot x)=\Phi(x) for all k\in H and x,\Phi(x)\le f(x) for all x, and c(z)\le\Phi(p(z)) for every z\in s.
The argument in briefChoose a unit u so small that, for every z\in s, all points p(z)+u\cdot r (r\in R^{n}) lie in the neighbourhood of hf and u\cdot(p(z)+u\cdot r)\in R^{n}. Put \Phi(x)=\max\{c(z):z\in s,\exists k\in H,u^{-1}(k\cdot x-p(z))\in R^{n}\}. \Phi is supported in the single compact lattice u^{-1}\cdot R^{n} (if u^{-1}(k\cdot x-p(z))\in R^{n} then u\cdot k\cdot x=u(p(z)+u\cdot r)\in R^{n} by the choice of u, and k preserves R^{n}), invariant under translation by u\cdot R^{n} and under H (H preserves R^{n}), \Phi\le f by hf, and \Phi(p(z))\ge c(z) (k=1).
