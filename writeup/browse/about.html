About this site

How far to trust it

A program compared every quotation and every line range with the Lean source. That shows the dossiers describe the lines they cite; it does not show that the English is right. A note says what its author read and what was taken for granted. Whether the artifact compiles, and on which axioms its theorems rest, is said by Lean and recorded in the notes, not by this site.

The formulas in the structured proofs and in the readings of declarations are typeset from an overlay that rewrites the plain text with TeX. An overlay entry is shown only when its formulas render and it keeps every word of the original; 15,330 texts are typeset, 0 have no overlay entry yet and 0 entries did not pass, so those are shown as written.

How to open it

The pages of the notes are drawn by PDF.js, which a browser runs only for a site served over http. Served from GitHub Pages it works as it is; on your own computer run ./serve.sh in the folder of the site and open the address it prints. Opened as a file, the reading pages fall back to the browser's own PDF viewer.
