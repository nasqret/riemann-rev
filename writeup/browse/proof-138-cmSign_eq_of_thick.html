lemma cmSign_eq_of_thick

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed dividing N_1d (c), e : E \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell and g, g' \in G_\ell such that the places of complexEmbOf e g and complexEmbOf e g' are \operatorname{thick} (t, t'): cmSign(complexEmbOf e g) = cmSign(complexEmbOf e g').
The argument in briefRule out opposite signs. If \ell \mid N_1: \ell divides the norm of c_0 = (1+\sqrt{-d})/2, and an isometry exchanging e(c_0) and 1 - e(c_0) contradicts the ultrametric inequality. If \ell \nmid N_1 (so \ell = d): the ratio of the two \operatorname{thick} places would have class -d^n \bmod N_0, but it has class 1, and d^n \not\equiv -1.
