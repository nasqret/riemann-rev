lemma IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel.floorForm_top_eq_integral

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and an integral place structure P,T a middle form with invertible hermitian split Gram matrix, F a standard level of T with m+1 pairs (h:\mathtt{IsStdLevel}F),Z\in IntermediateGroup(F) and sequences of units p,q. Lemma (unfolding at the top floor). For every \xi\in A (not required to be a unit), with \varphi(x)=\psi(\xi\cdot\mathtt{frameGram}(x)(top, top)) (=\psi(\xi\cdot h(z,xz))) and f(l)=\varphi(l^{-1})\cdot\langle\operatorname{correlation}(Z,p,l),\operatorname{correlation}(Z,q,l)\rangle: f is integrable for stackMeasure(F,\varrho,2), and floorForm(F,\varrho,p,q,\mathtt{besselVector}(Z),\varphi)=\int f d stackMeasure(F,\varrho,2).
The argument in briefChange variables to identify the floor form at the top floor with the integral over l of the product of correlations: an identity of distributions between the two parametrisations (Fubini on products of the floor measures), plus integrability from the gauge bounds.
