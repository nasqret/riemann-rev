lemma mulVec_mem_weightedTranslations

What it saysFor a CM field E, finite index types n, n_{0}, a natural number q, an integral n \times n_{0} matrix M over \mathbb{A}_f (h), weights d : n \to \mathbb{A}_f and d' : n_{0} \to \mathbb{A}_f, and y \in weightedTranslations(q, d) (hy); assume h': for all i, j and every finite place u, if d_i and d'_{j} have different valuations at u, then the component of M_{ij} at \overline{u} is 0. Then M^{H}\cdot y \in weightedTranslations(q, d').
The argument in briefM^{H}y is a sum of vectors \operatorname{conj}(M_{ij})y_i; each lies in the group by h'.
