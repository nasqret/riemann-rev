lemma IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel.eventually_correlation_eq

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma (local constancy, uniform in l). For a standard level, Z and p: for all (p',l') near (p,1) with l'\in U(V_{\varrho}) (GT \varrho) and every l\in U(V_{\varrho}): \operatorname{correlation}(Z,p',l\cdot l')=\operatorname{correlation}(Z,p,l).
The argument in briefNear (p,1), the conjugates y^{-1}w_p z_2w_{z_1}^{-1}y lie in K(g) uniformly for y of bounded size (compactness), and the head densities agree; hence the coordinates of the correlation agree, using that W_Z is K(g)-invariant on the right and vanishes off bounded sets.
