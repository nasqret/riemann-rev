lemma Data.isEigenform_of_hasHeckePolyAt

What it saysLet D be admissible data over P, n a finite type, M a type, f a function from G_f(E(D), n) to the functions M \to \mathbb{C}, f nonzero, and Q a family of complex polynomials indexed by the finite places of E(D). Assume that at every finite place w split off the level (IsSplitOffLevel(D, w): w split, neither w nor its conjugate a chosen place) f has Hecke polynomial Q(w) at w (HasHeckePolyAt: the maximal compact subgroup at w fixes f, each Hecke operator T_{w,j} acts on f by a scalar t_j, and Q(w) = \sum_j (-1)^j q_w^{j(j-1)/2}\, t_j\, X^{|n|-j}). Then IsEigenform(f, Q): f is nonzero, and for all but finitely many finite places w of prime norm, w is split and f has Hecke polynomial Q(w) at w.
The argument in brieff is nonzero; all but finitely many places w have w and its conjugate off the chosen places, and such a w of prime norm is split, hence split off the level, where the hypothesis gives the Hecke polynomial.
