lemma Filling.enorm_besselKernel

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), the side \sigma,\gamma\in G(S,E) (implicit) and an archimedean point y: \lVert\mathtt{besselKernel}(S,\gamma,y)\rVert=(\prod_v\lVert W_{\sigma,v}(y_v)\rVert)\cdot(\prod_v\lVert W_{\sigma,v}((\gamma^{-1}y)_v)\rVert) in [0,\infty],\gamma^{-1}y= ratToInf(\gamma)^{-1}\cdot y.
The argument in briefCompare squares: the squared norm of a tensor of product vectors is the product of the squared norms.
