lemma TwistingData.of_pairing

What it saysLet N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, O a valuation domain (a commutative integral domain whose ideals are totally ordered), T : TwistingData N W O (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to O, w \in O with w^{2} = p), and write Fam for the families \mathbb{Z}/N \to Monomials (Fin k) n, \mathfrak{m} for the maximal ideal of O, \kappa_{O} = O/\mathfrak{m} and T mod \mathfrak{m} = map (residue O) T, k, n \in \mathbb{N}, X and E finite, Q, M \in Mat_{(X\times Fam)\times E}(O), R, \beta \in Mat_{E\times(X\times Fam)}(O), a, z, w \in O, a labelling G of Fin k and a set B of families. Assume R\cdot Q = 1 (hR), w is not a unit (hw), a\cdot w divides every entry of \beta \cdot M - a\cdot 1 (h), and whenever Q\cdot u - M\cdot v =(a\cdot w)\cdot F for vectors u, v \in O^{E} and F \in O^{X\times Fam}, every block F(x, \cdot) lies in translatesIn T n G \mathfrak{m} B (hn). Then there is l \in \mathbb{N} with |E| + l \le \dim_{\kappa_{O}} (X \to translatesIn (T mod \mathfrak{m}) n G \perp B) and IndexDvd O^{E} (image of \beta \cdot Q + z\cdot O^{E}) (a^{|E|}\cdot z^{l}).
The argument in briefSmith normal form of (QRM - M)R = U\cdot \operatorname{diag}(t)\cdot G over the valuation ring. The columns of Q together with the columns of U at the indices i with t_i | a\cdot w, t_i \ne 0, reduce into the residue space (hn) and are recovered by R and V - VQR, which bounds |E| + l. The index statement follows from the determinant criterion (of_det_dvd) for the matrix AGQ + \betaQRM, whose determinant is a^{|E|} times a unit, combined with the diagonal criterion for z.
