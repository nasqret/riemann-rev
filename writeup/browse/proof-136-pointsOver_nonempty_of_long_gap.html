lemma Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.pointsOver_nonempty_of_long_gap

What it saysLong gap. Let the Kummer chart be unramified (hk), l > 0 (hl), S a side, i a letter, \kappa, s, g, \delta, N, A \in \mathbb{N} such that p^{\kappa} kills the cokernel at \sigma^{-l}(i) (hK: KillsCokernel(S, \sigma^{-l}(i), \kappa, 0)), E^{s} kills it at i (hz: KillsCokernel(S, i, 0, s)), N = s + g\cdot\sum_{t<l} p^{t} (hN), every jump of S at \sigma^{-t}(i), t < l, is \le g (hg), N < A (hA), 3e \le p^{l} (hq) and e\cdot A \le p^{l}\cdot\delta (hδ), e the index of the chart. Then for every c \in C, pointsOver(S, c, i, \kappa + 4\delta, shiftedStd(l, u \mapsto \min(A, -u - N)), shiftedStd(l, u \mapsto A)) is nonempty.
The argument in briefFrom the point y over c (E^{s} kills at i) build f(E)\cdot y with f monic of degree N and the standard exponents up to g below level l; the frame theorem writes p^{4\delta}f(E)y through vectors invariant modulo t^{A}; the coefficient of E^{N} of this expansion (coeff_mem_constantsModE), corrected by a lift from the cokernel killer at \sigma^{-l}(i), is the point.
