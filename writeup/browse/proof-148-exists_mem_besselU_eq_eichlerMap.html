lemma exists_mem_besselU_eq_eichlerMap

What it saysLet A be a commutative ring with involution, \mathfrak{s} a shape, T a Gram matrix on V_{\varrho} over A such that J= T.splitGram is hermitian and invertible, and \tau\in A with \tau+\overline{\tau}=1 (TraceOne A). Let a\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho\},j\in A^{\varrho+1} with j_b=0 for all b\ge a (hypothesis l), r\in A,v a vector of the whole space and m=\overline{v}^{T}J=h(v,\cdot) (hypothesis Z) with m(f_b)=0 for all frame vectors f_b,b<a (hypothesis H). Then there is u in the unipotent group \mathsf{U}= besselU(T) with \sigma(u)=r\cdot\sum_{b\ge1}m(f_b)\cdot j_{b-1} (\sigma= simpleRootSum, the sum of the entries of u just above the diagonal on the frame) and whose matrix is the Eichler map E(x,v,\varsigma): w\mapsto w+x\cdot h(v,w)-(v+\varsigma x)\cdot h(x,w), for x=r\cdot(f j)=r\cdot\sum_b j_b f_b and \varsigma=\tau\cdot h(v,v). (The census also lists the file's section variables C,U,\tau,u,A,[\mathtt{CommRing}C],[\mathtt{MeasureSpace}C],[\mathtt{TopologicalSpace}U], [Fintype \tau],[\mathtt{CommRing}A]: the statement contains the letters u and \tau only as a bound variable and inside TraceOne.τ.)
The argument in briefThe Eichler map with these data is unitary because x is isotropic and orthogonal to v; it lies in \mathsf{U} and has the stated simple-root sum, by an entrywise computation.

    The Eichler map E(x,v,\varsigma) with x=r\cdot f j is an element u of U(J) (exists_unitary_eq_eichlerMap) once J is hermitian, x is isotropic, h(v,x)=0 and \varsigma+\overline{\varsigma}=h(v,v); the last holds since \varsigma+\overline{\varsigma}=(\tau+\overline{\tau})h(v,v). Here x=r\sum_{b<a}j_b f_b lies in the span of e_1,\ldots,e_{\varrho} (j_{\varrho}=0 because a\le\varrho), which is isotropic, and h(v,x)=r\sum_{b<a}j_b m(f_b)=0 by H. Besides isotropy and orthogonality it remains to show u\in\mathsf{U} and the formula for \sigma(u); unfolding the Eichler map, the frame, the levels and \sigma entrywise (simp_all) reduces these goals to identities of finite sums, closing some of them.
