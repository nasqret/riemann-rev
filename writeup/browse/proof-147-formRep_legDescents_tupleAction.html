lemma Data.formRep_legDescents_tupleAction

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data, \vartheta an m-fold twist and g a family of invertible matrices at the nodes with g(z, 0, m) = 1 for both grids (h). Let z be a grid, b a natural number, S a tuple, F a form at node (0, m) of grid z, and k \in K_{0} with reduction modulo gridModulus(\vartheta) equal to g(z, 0, b) (e) and k = 1 if b = m (n). Then formRep(k)(legDescents(tupleAction(g, S), z, b)(F)) = edgeCharProd(g, legEdges(z, b))\cdotlegDescents(S, z, b)(F): k translates the descent for the moved tuple to that for S, times the edge character product over its edges.
The argument in briefInduction along the descents (functional induction on legDescents): at each step the descent with the moved matrix is the translate of the descent with the old one, by the transformation of the modular datum, and the character of the step is collected.
