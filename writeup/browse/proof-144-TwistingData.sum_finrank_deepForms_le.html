lemma TwistingData.sum_finrank_deepForms_le

What it saysLet N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, K a field and T : TwistingData N W K (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to K, parity isEven), K of characteristic p, k \in \mathbb{N}, natural numbers n, r, \ell and reals \kappa, h, z with HeckeSizes P o_{0} N k n r \ell \kappa h z (big), and M \in \mathbb{N}: \sum_{d<M} \dim_K deepForms T k n r d \le(1 + z)\cdot m^{N} (in \mathbb{R}), m = \#Monomials (Fin k) n: forms deep in every frame are rare.
The argument in briefA form in V_d is determined by its coefficients at the monomials whose weight (exists_le_weight_of_mem_deepForms) is at least d; summing over d < M counts each monomial at most \lfloorweight\rfloor + 1 times. Averaging over the rotations b \mapsto b - a of the rows, the mean weight of a monomial is bounded through the number of deep variables (N/2 per row), which the size conditions bound by z\cdot m^{N}.
