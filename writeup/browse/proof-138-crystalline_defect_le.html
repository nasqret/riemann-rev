lemma crystalline_defect_le

What it saysLocal loss, crystalline case. Let \ell be a prime, \Gamma a topological group over G_\ell (GroupOverGp: j = Γ.overGp.toGp : \Gamma \to G_\ell with open image), O a coefficient ring, L a Galois lattice over O for \Gamma , Q, m, h, \mu , G, b, \delta , \eta , W natural numbers, th and \varepsilon functions of the \mathbb{Z}_\ell-algebra embeddings g : O \to \mathbb{C}_\ell (a thickness indicator and a sign), and U(g), U'(g) finite sets of integers, such that: Q > 0; 99(Q^{2} + (W + 1)Q + Q + W + m) \le h; 99h^{3} \le G; 99(Gh^{3} + G) \le b; 2 \log (4b) < m; \delta < \eta; 99((\eta + W)Q + \eta + h) \le h^{3}; for every g, U(g) \subseteq [-\eta , \eta ] \cup (-\varepsilon (g))\cdot [\beta _g, \beta _g + W] and U'(g) \subseteq [-\eta , \eta ] \cup \varepsilon (g)\cdot [\beta _g, \beta _g + W] with \beta _g = b + \delta if th(g) and G + \delta otherwise (IsInJumpWindow); L lies in the class InClass \top \perp U' (crystalline lattices with jumps in U'); either (for every g, every negative u in U(g) \cup U'(g) has 2u \le -h, and the number of jumps of L at g in [-\eta , \eta ] is at most \mu) or (every U'(g) \subseteq [-\eta , 0]); y \ge 0 with 10^{5}\cdot (\operatorname{rk}_O L\cdot \lfloor 2\lambda /Q\rfloor \cdot m + \mu \cdot \lambda ) \le y, \lambda = lambdaLoss h b \ell; k a Kummer chart of \Gamma with a root \zeta \in O of the letters (\zeta ^{\ell ^f - 1} = 1), T and s functions on O, j an open embedding (hj), e\cdot f = [G_\ell : j(\Gamma )] for the chart (hf), e \le h (he); for every g with th(g) or Q < \ell, th(g) = T(g(\zeta )) and \varepsilon (g) = s(g(\zeta )) (hs); either (the chart is unramified and \ell \ge 7) or (\ell \le Q and s is constant) (hu); and, if \ell \le Q, for every z and 1 \le a, a' \le \log _\ell (h^{3}), T(z) and T(z^{\ell ^a}) imply \neg T(z^{\ell ^{a+a'}}) (hT). Then the normalised O-length of Z_f(L) modulo Z_f(L) \cap cupOrthogonal (InClass \top \perp U) L is at most [G_\ell : j(\Gamma )]\cdot y.
The argument in briefBound the defect by normLength_defect_le once \ell ^{10^{4}Kx}\cdot Z_f(L) is cup-orthogonal (K = \lfloor 2\lambda /Q\rfloor , x \le m), which follows from scaled cup bounds of the pairings. If \ell \le Q, sparse \operatorname{thick} letters give the bounds; if Q < \ell and h^{3} \le \lambda, the two-sizes bound; otherwise \ell \ge 9G divides no integer near 0, \pm G, \pm b, and the few-low-jumps theorem bounds the defect directly or shows it is zero.
