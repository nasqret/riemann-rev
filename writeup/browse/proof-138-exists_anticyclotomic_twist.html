lemma exists_anticyclotomic_twist

What it saysOne anticyclotomic twist makes all Selmer groups short. For a prime \ell, a stably unramified CM field E, finite sets S \subseteq S_0 of finite places of E (hS), an element z of \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) in the inertia above the places of S_0 not above \ell (hz: z \in inertiaAbove(S_0 \setminus placesDividing(E, \ell )), the union of the inertia groups of the places of \Omega over these places), representations \rho _i (i \in \iota, finite) over a coefficient ring O balanced up to \beta _i (hρ: IsBalanced \ell S \rho _i L_i \gamma _i \beta _i M), finite types \kappa , T, families (\mu _k, \operatorname{val}_k) with IsFamily S_0 (\mu _k) (\operatorname{val}_k) for each k \in \kappa (hf), bounds B_i < \infty (hB) with normLength(\beta _i + \sum _b v(\det (\rho _i(z_b) - 1))) \le B_i, z_b \in \{z, (z^c)^{-1}\} (hβ), c, \eta \ge 0 with v_\ell (\mu _{k,t}(z) - 1) \le c for all k, t (hc), \eta > 0 (hη), and m \in \mathbb{N}: there are a finite set I of places and a character \psi : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell such that for every 1 \le d \le m: (1) for every k, v_\ell (\operatorname{val}_k(I, \psi ^d)) \le v_\ell (\operatorname{val}_k(\varnothing , 1)) + 8c + \eta; (2) IsAuxTwist S_0 I \psi ^d \eta; and (3) for every i and all sufficiently large coefficient rings O' \supseteq O, some \psi ' with values in O' agreeing with \psi ^d gives normLength_{O'}(length of the Selmer module of \psi '\cdot \rho _i for the places S \cup I \cup \overline{I}, relations L_i \cup {vanishing at \gamma _i}, modulo \ell ^M) \le B_i + \eta.
The argument in briefTake a pencil (exists_isPencil) with Frobenii near z. A point y on the line through the roots of unity, y_P = 1 + t_P(u - 1), defines the character pointChar; Fitting polynomials (exists_sharp_fitting_poly) bound the Selmer lengths of the twists at y, sharply at the base point; first a single root of unity u keeps all polynomials (Selmer and family values) small along the line, then common \ell ^N-th roots of unity \zeta do so for all powers d \le m; \psi = pointChar \theta \zeta.
