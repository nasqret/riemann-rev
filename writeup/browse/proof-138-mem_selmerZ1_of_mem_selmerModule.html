lemma mem_selmerZ1_of_mem_selmerModule

What it saysFor a stably unramified CM field E, a finite type n, a commutative ring O, a discrete O-algebra B, \rho : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to M_n(O), sets S, P, Q of places, relations K \subseteq L (l), places A_v, c a continuity datum, S \subseteq P \cup Q (k; by default for P \cup Q = unionWithConj \ldots), and x in the Selmer module of \rho with values in B^n for (S, L) (h): x lies in selmerZ1 (the continuous cocycles of B^n with \rho, vanishing on the inertia above the places off P \cup Q and on the complex conjugations of \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) — a vacuous condition here: E is totally complex, so \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) contains no complex conjugation (IsComplexConj.false_of_isTotallyComplex); the chosen conjugation c that induces conjGal lies in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /\mathbb{Q}) and exchanges a place of E with its conjugate —, whose restrictions to the decomposition groups at A_v lie in the local cocycles localCocycles \rho K _ B for v \in P).
The argument in briefUnfold both Selmer conditions: cocycle, continuity, local conditions and inertia.
