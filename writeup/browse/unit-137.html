Chapter VIII · Unit 137

A Taylor–Wiles prime at a time: the deformed trace at one auxiliary place clears the denominator

note and structured proofs · 10 declarations · 6 structured proofs · 1 note

At one auxiliary (Taylor–Wiles) place w_j with q_j ≡ 1 mod ℓ^ν, the stabiliser of a line in GL_n(k_{w_j}) carries a character to ℤ/ℓ^ν whose transfer factors through the determinant; an abstract first-order lemma about weighted sums over a finite group then produces an operator F at w_j that ties the trace of tame inertia to that of Frobenius on forms, modulo ℓ^ν (exists_tie). Taking one such tie at each auxiliary place, the top coefficient of Ribet's element (a polynomial in one variable per place) clears the denominator of the projection to the eigensystems 𝓧 up to the factor d·c (exists_diagOp_mem_ellIntegralForms).
