lemma Lift.KummerChart.few_low_jumps

What it saysLength bound for crystalline classes with few low weights. Let j : \Gamma \to G_p be an open embedding (ho), O a p-adic integer ring, L a lattice over O, U, U' jump sets indexed by the embeddings O \to \mathbb{C}_p, k an unramified Kummer chart (hk) with residue degree f, \zeta \in O with \zeta^{p^{f} - 1} = 1, p \ge 5 (hp), a, \eta, W \in \mathbb{N}, b : letters \to \mathbb{N}, \varepsilon : letters \to \mathbb{Z}^\times, with b(i) + W + \eta \le (p - 3)a (ha), \eta \ge 1 (hη), 6(a + \eta + W) < b(i) (hb) and NoMultipleNear(p, 6(a + \eta + W), b(i)) (hs) for every letter i, L in InClass(\top, \bot, U') (hL), and letterJumps(k, \zeta, U, i) in [-\eta, \eta] \cup (-\varepsilon(i))[b(i), b(i) + W], letterJumps(k, \zeta, U', i) in [-\eta, \eta] \cup \varepsilon(i)[b(i), b(i) + W] for every i (hV). Then: (1) if no u \in U(g) \cup U'(g) lies in [-a, 0) for any g, or U'(g) \subseteq [-\eta, 0] for every g, then Z_f(L) \subseteq cupOrthogonal(InClass(\top, \bot, U), L); (2) for every m with \sum_{u\in[-\eta, \eta]} \operatorname{jump}(L, g, u) \le m for every g: relLength(Z_f(L), cupOrthogonal(InClass(\top, \bot, U), L)) \le 11\cdot f\cdot(m(a + 1) + \mathrm{rank}_O(L)\cdot(W + 1))\cdot\mathrm{length}_O(O/pO), relLength(S, T) being the O-length of S/(S \cap T). Conclusion (2) does not assume the alternative of (1).
