What it saysExchange with implicit constant. Let E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, m, r, p, b finite index types with p non-empty; signs s, s', t, u; W : Space(E, n, s), X : Space(E, m, s'), U : Space(E, b, u); \mu weights; A a base-adapted splitting of hypSum(Unit, X) (X plus one hyperbolic plane) over W along p; V : Space(E, r, t); \sigma : b \sqcup r \simeq n; z, y rapid real data (r_{1}, \mathit{hy}); 1 + negCount(s') = |p| + negCount(t) + negCount(u) (\mathit{hs}, by default proved by simp) and |n| even (\mathit{hn}, by default proved by simp). There are a real C and a rapid real datum v such that every split tame character \mathfrak{c} of weight \mu, with \gamma = rankTwist(\nu_{\mathfrak{c}}^{-1}, n), has c \in \mathbb{C} with C\cdot |c|^{2} = lValueProdInt(\mathfrak{c}, |r| - |n|/2)\cdotequalRankThetaRatio(hypSum(Unit, X), \mathfrak{c}, z) and, for all partners \Phi, \Psi for \chi_{\mathfrak{c}} over O with respect to \sigma with \Psi locally constant and every point g: thetaIntegral(W, V, \gamma, 1, localDatum(W, V, v, \Phi, readsAt(\mathfrak{c})))(g) = c\cdot \int_{[U(U)]} pairTheta(U, W, \gamma, \gamma, localDatum(U, W, y, \Psi, readsAt(\mathfrak{c})), (h, g)) \mathit{dh}.
