lemma Filling.amplitudeSum_eq_zero_of_not_agrees

What it saysUnder the standing hypotheses, for filling data S over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit), the side \sigma, a set T, classes c,c',s\in\mathsf{G} and T stable (hT): if s does not agree (hs: \negAgrees(S,s)), then A_T(c,s,c')=0.
The argument in briefA nonzero amplitude sum would force the characters to agree.

    Suppose A_T(c,s,c')\ne0. For u,u'\in K\cap\mathsf{U} with \operatorname{reduction}(u)\cdot s=s\cdot\operatorname{reduction}(u'), amplitudeSum_besselU_mul gives A_T(c,s,c')=A_T(c,\operatorname{reduction}(u)\cdot s\cdot\operatorname{reduction}(u')^{-1},c')= (unipotentChar(u)^{-1}unipotentChar(u'))\cdot A_T(c,s,c'), so unipotentChar(u)^{-1}unipotentChar(u')=1 (scalar multiplication of a nonzero vector is injective): s would agree, contradicting hs.
