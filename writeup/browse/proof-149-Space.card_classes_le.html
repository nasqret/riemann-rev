lemma Space.card_classes_le

What it saysLet E be a CM field with a CM type (CMType E: a CM number field with a chosen complex embedding \Phi_w inducing each infinite place w), N a nonzero natural number (NeZero N) whose image in the ring of integers lies in the different ideal of E (h), and n a finite index type. Then the number of classes of the standard definite hermitian space of rank |n| at the level K_f, i.e. Nat.card of StdClasses (Kf E n) = \Gamma_f\backslash G_f/K_f (G_f the finite adelic unitary group of the identity form on E^{n}, \Gamma_f its rational points, K_f = G_f \cap \mathrm{GL}_n(\hat{O}) the stabiliser of the standard lattice \hat{O}^{n}), is at most (1 + 2\cdot C^{3})^{4\cdot f\cdot|n|^{2}}, where C = shortBasisConst E n N = 6\cdot N\cdot(f\cdot|n|)^{2} and f = numInfPlaces E is the number of infinite places of E.
The argument in briefFor each class g pick a short basis Y_g with g.out^{-1}Y_g integral, the Gram matrix G_g = Y_g\ast Y_g, and an approximation X_g \in \operatorname{Mat}_n(E) of (g.out^{-1}Y_g)^{-1} modulo \hat{O} with small archimedean entries. The entries of G_g and G_gX_g are algebraic integers bounded by C^{3} at every infinite place, and they determine the class: equal entries give a rational unitary \gamma = Y_gY_{g'}^{-1} and an integral \kappa relating the representatives. The count of such integral vectors (Filling.encard_integral_le) gives the bound.
