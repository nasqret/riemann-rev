lemmaData.exists_common_hecke_rallis

Lemma 7.1 of the note · unit 140 · New/Trace/GoodFormTransfer.lean, lines 27–47

Let D be admissible data over P; s, s' signs on \operatorname{RowIndex}(D); V, V' hermitian spaces over E(D) with index set \operatorname{RowIndex}(D) and signs s, s'. Assume that for every infinite place v of E(D) the doubled parameter \operatorname{paramW}(D, v, .) has gap h(D) for the signs s (HasGap: injective, entries of the right parity, entries at indices of different sign at least 2h(D) apart), and likewise for the signs s'. Let \theta be a tame character of D, j a natural number, a and a' complex numbers, B a complex function on pairs of forms in \operatorname{principalForms}(D, V) and B' one on pairs of forms in \operatorname{principalForms}(D, V'). Suppose that for all z, y in \operatorname{principalForms}(D, V) the series over the cosets g of a \operatorname{rallisTerm}(D, \theta, j, V, z, y, g) has sum B(z, y), and that for all z, y in \operatorname{principalForms}(D, V') the series of a' \operatorname{rallisTerm}(D, \theta, j, V', z, y, g) has sum B'(z, y). Then there is p in \operatorname{heckeAlgebra}(D) such that B(z, y) = a \langle z, p_V(y)\rangle for all z, y in \operatorname{principalForms}(D, V) and B'(z, y) = a' \langle z, p_V'(y)\rangle for all z, y in \operatorname{principalForms}(D, V'), where p_V is the operator of p on \operatorname{principalForms}(D, V) and <, > the Petersson inner product.
