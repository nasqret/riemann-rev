lemma Filling.exists_besselS_mem_termsOver

What it saysUnder the standing hypotheses (ok), for filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), a class c and \sigma\in\mathsf{G} (implicit): if \sigma lies in \overline{S}= reducedImage(S,\mathsf{S}) (the premise), then there are a class d and a rational \gamma\in\mathsf{S}(E) with \gamma\in termsOver(S,c,\sigma,d).
