lemma Filling.enorm_amplitudeSum_le_classSize_top

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S admissible, and use the cut Bessel functions. Let T be a stable set of rational points (IsStable(S,T): u^{-1}\gamma u'\in T iff \gamma\in T for rational u,u'\in\mathsf{U}) with termDisc(S,\gamma)\ne0 for all \gamma\in T. Then for all classes c,c' and s\in\mathsf{G}: \lVert\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(S,T,c,s,c')\rVert\le\Sigma_{\gamma\in T\cap\mathtt{termsOver}(S,c,s,c')}[\mathtt{IsNearRep}(S,c,c',\gamma)]\cdot\nu\cdotclassSize(S,\top,\mathtt{termDiscNorm}(S,\gamma)), the sum over the near terms that are the chosen representatives of their double cosets \mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g)\gamma\mathsf{U}_c'^{0}(g),\nu= unipCovolume(S),\top=\varrho+1.
The argument in briefChange the weight to [\gamma= chosen representative of its \mathsf{U}_c(g)\times\mathsf{U}_c'^{0}(g) class]\cdot\omega(\gamma^{-1}y),\omega the box partition of c', the representative chosen near if possible; for a representative that is a near representative, the open-cell bound and the class-size bound (IsNear.le_classSize_and_cellMass_le) bound the integral by \nu\cdotclassSize(S,\top,\cdot); for other representatives the integral vanishes, since a nonzero integrand produces a near representative.
