lemma TwistingData.excessWeight_dotProduct_permStep

What it saysLet N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, K a field and T : TwistingData N W K (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to K, parity isEven), k \in \mathbb{N}, indices a, a' \in Fin k, a letter i and \nu \in \mathbb{R}^{Fin k \times \mathbb{Z}/N}: with \beta = permStep T.isEven P 1 \nu (signs by parity, trivial permutations, P = T.P as a real number) and u_i = rootVar T a a' i: \sum_{x} excessWeight T i a a' x\cdot \beta(x) =(1 - 1/q)\cdot(\beta(u_i) -(\nu(a, i) - \nu(a', i))).
The argument in briefExpand the dot product letter by letter; the contributions telescope around the cycle of letters, leaving the terms at the letter i.
