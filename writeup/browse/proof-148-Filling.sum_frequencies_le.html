lemma Filling.sum_frequencies_le

What it saysLemma. Let E carry Weil data and let T be stable under conjugation. Let B\ge1,k\in\mathbb{N},X a finite set of elements of E,c=(c_v) complex and x=(x_v) real numbers indexed by the infinite places with |c_v|^{-1}\le|x_v|\le B for all v, and suppose every y\in X is nonzero and integral outside T. Then \Sigma_{y\in X} (\prod_v dampedPower(k, |c_v\cdot\Phi_v(y)|^{-1})) \cdot\sqrt{y}_T^{k}/\operatorname{den}(y)^{6}\le(\prod_v|x_v|^{k}\cdot(b\cdot B^{2})^{3})\cdot(b\cdot\operatorname{rd}_{E})^{3f}, where dampedPower(k,r)=r^{k}\cdot\min(1,r)^{6},[y]_T= genIndex(\mathtt{ratToPlaces}(T)(y)),\operatorname{den}(y)= denomIndex(O,\mathtt{ratToPlaces}(T)(y)),\operatorname{rd}_{E} the root discriminant and f the number of infinite places.
The argument in briefEach y spans a line in E^{2} whose lattice discriminant d(y) satisfies d(y)\cdot[\det a]\le\prod_v(1+|y|_v^{2})\cdot[\det a] and controls [y]_T and \operatorname{den}(y); so each term is at most a constant times d(y)^{-3}, and the lines are distinct, so the sum is bounded by the count of lines \Sigma_L d(L)^{-3}\le(400f\cdot\operatorname{rd}_{E})^{3f} (tsum_sublatticeDisc_inv_le with u=1,\kappa= Fin 2,s=1).
