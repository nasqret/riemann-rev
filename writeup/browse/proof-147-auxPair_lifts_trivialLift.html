lemma LValueData.auxPair_lifts_trivialLift

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data, n a finite index type and X : LValueData E n L-value data. Then Lifts(auxPair(X), trivialLift(X), 1): the lift \Xi = trivialLift(X) of the trivial character from U(1) to U(I_n) lifts the constant system 1, i.e. at every finite place w with \overline{w} \ne w and w off the places of X and their conjugates, \Xi is spherical with Hecke polynomial T \mapsto trivialHecke(q_w, |n| - 1, \alpha(w)T)\cdotheckePolyEval(1, w, 1, q_w^{|n|-1}\alpha(w)T/\beta(w)), that is \prod_{l<|n|-1}(1 - q^{l} \alpha T)\cdot(1 - q^{|n|-1}\alpha \beta^{-1}T), where \alpha, \beta are the Frobenius values (AutChar.frobValue) of the characters a, b of auxPair(X) in ranks 1 and |n|.
The argument in briefHecke transfer for the equivariant map \Lambda = class sum of kernel values, applied to the datum \Upsilon; on the U(1) side the representation is trivial.
