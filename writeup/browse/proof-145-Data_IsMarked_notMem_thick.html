lemma Data.IsMarked.notMem_thick

What it saysFor data D, a marked set \Lambda (for the section's p , \iota_{p}) and an infinite place \sigma such that inducedPlace( \sigma ) or its conjugate lies in \Lambda: \sigma is not thick.
The argument in briefIf \sigma were thick (class \equiv - 1 mod N_{0}), then ( - p )^{i} \cdot \sigma is a top (class \equiv 1) for some i, and raising to the odd power o_{0} gives 1 \equiv - 1 mod N_{0}, impossible.
