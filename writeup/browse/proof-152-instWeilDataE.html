What it saysInstance. For parameters P and an exponent s (implicit): Weil data for E(P, s) (class WeilData): the stably-unramified structure, a splitting character \upsilon (from nonempty_splittingChar), j = -1/\sqrt{-d(P)} with \operatorname{conj}(j) = -j and \operatorname{Im} \Phi _w(j) > 0 at every infinite place, the idele \alpha (P, s) with v_w(j\cdot \alpha \cdot \overline{\alpha }) = \exp (m_w) at every finite place w, m_w the multiplicity of w in the different of E over \mathbb{Q} (.exp in the value group \mathbb{Z}^{m0}: additive order -m_w), (1 - j^{-1})/2 integral, and \upsilon (\pi _w) = -1 at the chosen uniformiser idele \pi _w (υ.atUnif w) of every finite place w with \overline{w} = w (\pi _w is local; it is not the global generator \varpi (P, s) of the different).
The argument in briefA splitting character exists because every root of unity \zeta (P, s)^k is \delta /\overline{\delta } for \delta = (\zeta (P, s)^m(\zeta (P, s) - 1))^k, m = N_1(P, s)/4, a unit of norm 1 over K; then j = -1/\sqrt{-d(P)} and \alpha (P, s) satisfy the conditions of Weil data, the self-duality coming from v_w(\alpha \overline{\alpha } relDifferentGen(P, s)) = 1 and the different (relDifferentGen(P, s)\cdot \sqrt{-d(P)}).
