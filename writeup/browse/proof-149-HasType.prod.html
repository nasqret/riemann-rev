lemma HasType.prod

What it saysExponents add under products. For a commutative ring O, an O-module V, an index type \iota, a submodule M \subseteq V, z \in O, a natural number s, endomorphisms T(l) of V and real numbers t(l) (l \in \iota), and a list L of indices: if HasType z M s (T l) (t l) for every l in L, then HasType z M s (\Pi_{l\in L} T(l)) (\sum_{l\in L} t(l)), where the product is the composition T(l_{1})\circ T(l_{2})\circ\cdots in the order of the list (the empty product is the identity) and the sum is the sum of the t(l) over the list.
The argument in briefInduction on the list; the step composes the head operator with the product of the rest by IndexDvd.comp, adding the exponents.
