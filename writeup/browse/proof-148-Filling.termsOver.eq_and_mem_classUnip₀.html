lemma Filling.termsOver.eq_and_mem_classUnip₀

What it saysUnder the standing hypotheses, for filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), \gamma,u\in G(S,E), classes c,c',d and r\in\mathsf{G} (implicit): if \gamma\in termsOver(S,c,r,c') (k), u\in termsOver(S,c,r,d) (h) and \gamma f=uf (e), then c'=d and \gamma^{-1}u\in\mathsf{U}_{c'}^{0}(g).
The argument in brief\gamma^{-1}u fixes the frame, lies in \mathsf{S}, and its position lies in K(g).
