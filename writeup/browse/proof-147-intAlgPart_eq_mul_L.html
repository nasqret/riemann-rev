lemma Data.Pair.intAlgPart_eq_mul_L

What it saysFor P, D, a pair p =(\nu, z) and n with H : HasLiftRank(p, n) (z \ge 2): there is A \ne 0 such that for every tame character \vartheta, intAlgPart(H, \vartheta) = A\cdot L((\nu \cdot \vartheta)^{-1}, \{chosen places\} \cup Q(\vartheta), z), the partial Hecke L-value at z without the Euler factors at the chosen places, the places of Q(\vartheta) and their conjugates.
The argument in briefThe squared norm of the lift is C\cdot(explicit counts)\cdot L (LValueData.inner_trivialLift_self_eq_L); the counts at Q cancel against the normalisation of intAlgPart, leaving a constant A independent of \vartheta.
