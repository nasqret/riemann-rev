lemma SieveSystem.pi

What it saysLet C be a commutative ring with a measure that is s-finite, U a topological space, \tau a finite type, and K= SieveSystem(u,\iota,n,J,\psi,En,\Lambda,\beta) a sieve system on C (with u:C\to U,\iota:U\to C,n,J,\psi,En,\Lambda,\beta implicit). Let \iota':\mathbb{C}^{\times}\to\mathbb{C} and \Lambda' be given (implicit), and for each v\in\tau forms J_v', a bound En_v' and a function \gamma_v:\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{R} such that H_v= SieveSystem(\mathtt{unitOrOne},\iota',n,J_v', wave, En_v',\Lambda',\gamma_v) is a sieve system on \mathbb{C} (unitOrOne(c)=c if c\ne0, else 1; \operatorname{wave}(c)=\exp(c-\overline{c})). Then the product is a sieve system on the ring (\tau\to\mathbb{C})\times C with its product measure: SieveSystem(u^{+},\iota^{+},n,J^{+},\psi^{+},En^{+},\Lambda^{+},\beta^{+}) holds for u^{+}(x)=((\mathtt{unitOrOne}(x_v))_v,u(x_C)),\iota^{+}(y)=((\iota'(y_v))_v,\iota(y_U)),J_i^{+}(p,q)=(\prod_v J_{v,i}'(p_v,q_v))\cdot J_i(p_C,q_C),\psi^{+}(x)=(\prod_v\operatorname{wave}(x_v))\cdot\psi(x_C),En^{+}=(\prod_v En_v')\cdot En,\Lambda_i^{+}(p)=(\prod_v\Lambda_i'(p_v))\cdot\Lambda_i(p_C) and \beta^{+}(x)=(\prod_v\gamma_v(x_v))\cdot\beta(x_C); here p_v and p_C are the sequences of v-components and of C-components of a sequence p in (\tau\to\mathbb{C}^{\times})\times U.
The argument in briefField by field: \psi^{+}(0)=1; the step property by Fubini over the finite product, with the bounds multiplied; positivity by the Schur product theorem; continuity of products; the two norm bounds by multiplying the factor bounds.
