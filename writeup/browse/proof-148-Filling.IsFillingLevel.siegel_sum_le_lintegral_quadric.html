lemma Filling.IsFillingLevel.siegel_sum_le_lintegral_quadric

What it saysLet E be a number field with a CM structure and Weil data (Field E, CMType E, WeilData E), \mathfrak{s} a shape, S admissible filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s} (ok : IsAdmissible), T a finite set of finite places of E stable under conjugation, with gIdele(S)^{-1} integral at every finite place outside T (an instance Fact), and (hT) latBasis(S) an integral invertible matrix outside T and N(S)^{-1} integral outside T; let P be an integral place structure on LocProd(T)=\prod_{w\in T}E_w (integers R= P.R, character \psi, modulus md) and F a level on LocProd(T) with hF : IsFillingLevel(S,T,F). Lemma (Siegel's formula at T, one-sided). Let f be a [0,\infty]-valued function on the vectors over LocProd(T) (indexed by BesselIndex(\mathfrak{s})), s a finite set of pairs z=(c_z,y_z) of a class of S and a vector y_z\in E^{\kappa},t\in E with (ht) N(S)^{2}<|\Phi_v(t)|^{2} for every infinite place v, and c : Classes(S)\times E^{\kappa}\to\mathbb{R}_{\ge0}. Put x(z)= (representative of c_z){}^{-1}\cdot y_z\in\mathbb{A}_f^{\kappa}. Suppose that for every z\in s: N(S)\cdot\overline{y}_z\cdot y_z=t\overline{t}-N(S)^{2}, every coordinate of x(z) is integral outside T, and for all e in a neighbourhood of tailVector(\overline{\pi}(S),t,x(z)) taken at T (evalPlaces T), every k\in\mathsf{H}\cap congGroup(F) (\mathsf{H}=\mathtt{besselH}) and every y with k\cdot y=e,c(z)\le f(y). Then for all sufficiently small sets L\subseteq LocProd(T) (eventually in the small-sets filter of the neighbourhoods of 0) that are clopen: (\sum_{z\in s}c(z))\cdot\operatorname{vol}(L)\le(1+b(S)^{-6})\cdot\#Classes(S)\cdotsiegelConst(S)\cdot(\prod_v(|\Phi_v(t)|^{2}-N(S)^{2}))^{k(\mathfrak{s})-1}\cdot\int1[(J_T\cdot e)_{f_{\mathrm{top}}}-t\in L]\cdot f(e)d floorQuadric(F, top)(e), where J_T is the Gram matrix of latMiddle(S,\mathtt{LocProd}T),f_{\mathrm{top}} the top frame index and t is taken at T (ratToPlaces T); siegelConst(S)=(\pi^{k}/(k-1)!/N^{k})^{f}\cdot|d_E|^{1/4}\cdot\operatorname{vol}(\hat{O})^{k}.
The argument in briefIf \sum c=0 there is nothing to prove. Otherwise build from the step function \Psi (\le f, \ge c(z) at the tail vectors, invariant under \mathsf{H}\cap K(g) and small translations) the locally constant compactly supported test function n(x)=1[x integral outside T]\cdot\Psi(\mathtt{tailVector}(\overline{\pi}_T,t_T,x_T)), and apply Siegel's formula (classSum_norm_eq) to n and \beta=(t\overline{t}-N^{2})/N: the class sum dominates \sum_{z\in s}c(z), so \sum c<r\cdot|C\cdot\int F| with r=(1+b^{-6})/(1+2^{-n}). The Fourier integral \int F, over the codifferent scaled by a large M, is continuous in (M-scale, t) by dominated convergence; evaluating it (enorm_integral_codifferent) turns it into the mass of n near the quadric, which off T is v times the mass at T (mass_near_quadric_off_places_le) and at T is at most \operatorname{md}(\pi)\cdotskewMeasure\cdot\int_{\mathrm{slab}}\Psi (eventually_mass_near_quadric_le). For small clopen L, averaging over L gives (\sum c)\cdot\operatorname{vol}(L)\le const\cdot\int1_{\mathrm{slab}}\cdot\Psi, and the constants combine to (1+b^{-6})\cdot\#\operatorname{Cl}\cdotsiegelConst\cdot(\prod(|t|^{2}-N^{2}))^{k-1}.
