lemma Data.exists_spaceY

What it saysFor admissible data D over P and a natural number j there is a hermitian space V over E(D) with basis index set Y(D, j) and signs \operatorname{signsY}(D, j) such that: if m(D) \le j then V is standard (all signs 1 and point 1); and for every proof h that j < m(D), the Gram matrix of V is \operatorname{gradedGram}(\operatorname{indexEquivY}(D, j, h), N(D)), and there is k in \mathrm{GL}_{Y(D, j)}(O-hat) (integral finite adelic matrices with integral inverse) whose reduction at every chosen place of D is 1, with x_f(V) = \operatorname{gradedLatticeBasis}(\operatorname{indexEquivY}(D, j, h), \operatorname{ideleA}(D), \operatorname{piIdele}(D), \operatorname{latticeScale}(D, j)) k, where x_f(V) is the finite part of the point of V.
The argument in briefFor j \ge m take the diagonal space of the signs, which are all 1. For j < m: at every infinite place a frame diagonalises the graded Gram matrix to \operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{signsY}); at the finite places the lattice basis B makes B^H J B unimodular, so some l in \mathrm{GL}(O-hat) makes it 1; the point is (frames, B l u) with u = liftResidue of the inverse reduction of l, and k = l u reduces to 1 at the chosen places.
