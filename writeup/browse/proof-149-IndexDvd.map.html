lemma IndexDvd.map

What it saysPush-forward. For a commutative ring O, O-modules U and V, submodules M, N \subseteq V and M', N' \subseteq U, z \in O and an O-linear map f : V \to U: if IndexDvd M N z (h), M' \subseteq f(M) + N', and f(N) \subseteq N', then IndexDvd M' N' z.
The argument in briefWrite each \Phi_k = f(x_k) + y_k with x_k \in M, y_k \in N'; the matrix that moves the x_k into N moves the \Phi_k into f(N) + N' \subseteq N'.
