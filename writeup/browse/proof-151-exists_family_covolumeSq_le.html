lemma Data.exists_family_covolumeSq_le

What it saysTheorems A and B together. For parameters P (N_{0} = P.N_{0}, d = P.d) and admissible data D for P (Lean's Data P): there are a natural number n and rows y : Fin n \to \operatorname{rows}(D) such that y is linearly independent over \mathbb{C}, (1 - 4/b_D^{2})\cdot\dim \operatorname{rows}(D) \le n, there is a real \delta \le 5\cdot\dim \operatorname{rows}(D)\cdot\log(1 + b_D^{2})\cdot\mathcal{B}(D) with IndexLe(D, y, \delta), and for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}, covolumeSq(\Theta_D(1)\circ y, \sigma) \le \exp(n\cdot(h_{\mathrm{main}}(D) - m_D\cdot\operatorname{rem}(D) + \log b_D\cdot\mathcal{B}(D))).
The argument in briefA basis of the span of the short translates of Theorems A and B, with Hadamard's inequality.
