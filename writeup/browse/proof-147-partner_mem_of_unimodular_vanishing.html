lemma IntegralFrame.partner_mem_of_unimodular_vanishing

What it saysLet F be a semilocal field (instance V) with compact open subring O and unit \varpi, a, b, m, n finite index types, \chi a tame multiplicative character of F (instance h\chi), e : b \sqcup a \simeq n, U a set of n \times b matrices, P a \mathrm{GL}_n(F)-stable space (hP) of Schwartz–Bruhat functions supported in U (hS), \Phi, \Psi partners for \chi over O with respect to e (Q) with \Psi locally constant (h\Psi), A : IntegralFrame F n m b an integral frame (blocks K, I, L; K', I', L'), \psi an additive character of F with conductor O (h\psi: \psi is continuous and \psi(tO) = 1 exactly when t \in O) and M a space of complex functions on n \times b matrices. For z \in M_b(F) put S_z(y) = stdColDatum(\chi, K')(y + L\cdot z\cdot(K'\cdot y)), so S_{0} = stdColDatum(\chi, K'). Suppose (1) every S_z lies in M, and (2) every \Psi ' \in M with the following property lies in P: \Lambda(\Psi ') = 0 for every b \times b matrix T and every linear functional \Lambda such that \Lambda(S_z) = \psi(\operatorname{tr}(T\cdot z))\cdot \Lambda(S_{0}) for all z and \Lambda(S_{0}) = 0 whenever T is primitive (unimodular). Then \Psi \in P.
The argument in briefApply partner_mem_of_stdColDatum to the translate of the Gauss-type sum \sum = \sum_{r} \eta(\det r)\cdot S_{\varpi l(r)} (r over the b \times b matrices over the residue ring): \sum \in P by hypothesis (2), since an admissible \Lambda with \Lambda(\sum) \ne 0 forces, by the matrix Gauss sum, T to be unimodular, where \Lambda(S_{0}) = 0; and the translate of \sum is a positive integer multiple of a standard datum.
