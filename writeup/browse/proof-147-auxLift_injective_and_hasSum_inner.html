lemma Data.auxLift_injective_and_hasSum_inner

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data for P and j a lift index (1 \le j \le m). There is a complex number A \ne 0 such that for every tame character \vartheta of D: the adjoint lift auxLift(D, \vartheta, j) (\theta_{j}^{\ast}, from forms of parameter \varsigma and principal level g on W_j to algebraic forms on V_j) is injective, and for all forms x, y in principalForms(D, spaceW(D, j)) the series over the cosets \gamma of A\cdotgramFactor(auxPair(D, \vartheta, j))\cdotrallisTerm(D, \vartheta, j, spaceW(D, j), x, y, \gamma) has sum \langleauxLift(D, \vartheta, j)(x), auxLift(D, \vartheta, j)(y)\rangle (HasSum). The constant A does not depend on \vartheta.
The argument in briefThe Rallis inner product formula for the edge of the auxiliary pair gives one constant C \ne 0 for every twist; the adjoint lift is (the algebraic form of) the theta lift by the sharp datum F \otimes vac, F = \gamma \cdotlevelIndicator, and the coset sum gives the formula with gramFactor.
