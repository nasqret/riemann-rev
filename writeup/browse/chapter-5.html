Unit 141The seesaw identity for the chain of theta lifts, and the index types of the data

note and structured proofs · 310 declarations · 83 structured proofs

The seesaw identity Data.seesaw: there is a unit c \in \mathbb{C}^\times, depending on D only, such that for every twist \vartheta some good datum \Psi has \iota_\vartheta(\mathcal{P}(c \cdot \varphi)) = B^\Psi_\vartheta(\varphi) for all ramified columns \varphi (\varphi = 0 included; no further condition on \varphi), i.e. the indefinite chain of m theta lifts Y_0 \to \cdots \to Y_m of the Poincaré series equals a finite admissible combination of definite chains X_0 \to \cdots \to X_m restricted to Y_m; this is the input of Theorem A (Data.short_forms). It is proved by unfolding the chain one lift at a time against Bessel periods (exists_isAdmissibleComb_unfolding), each unfolded period being a finite sum over a cut of cosets whose multiplicities divide powers of the conductor of \vartheta (exists_stage_cut, with the near-lines bounds of the lattices), evaluating pairs of lifts through zero terms of arrow pairs (AdmissibleEvaluation, EdgePair, DoubleLiftValue), and turning the resulting invariant admissible combination of reduced vacua into a good datum (GoodDatumExists). The folder also defines objects used across the proof: the places, characters, level prime N and index types of the data (IndexTypes), the lifts along the Y_j (GradingsOfY), the grid spaces (PairTensorData), and the Gauss-sum operator at the level prime used by the columns (NamesAtSplitLevel, LiftCharacters).

Unit 150Theta functions of the CM curve: periods and scales, tight frames, the integral vacuum, Galois conjugates of jets, and divisibility of jets at non-split primes

note and structured proofs · 89 declarations · 36 structured proofs

The folder New/Curve. It fixes a CM elliptic curve \mathcal{E} = \mathbb{C}/\Omega_0\mathcal{O}_K for K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d}) and the scales s_0, s_1 and \operatorname{per} at which Taylor series of theta functions are taken; builds tight frames (substitutes for orthonormal bases) of every large length in E = K(\zeta_{N_1}); shows that the scaled vacuum \kappa_0^n \cdot \operatorname{vac} of a standard hermitian space has p-adically integral values with bounded archimedean conjugates; and describes how \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{C}/K) acts on the jets (Taylor coefficients) of theta functions. At primes p inert or ramified in K it proves a normal form of theta series (an integral series times one exponential), the resulting lower bound for the p-adic valuation of Schur jets (divisibility, norm_schurJet_le) and its sharpness (exists_hasLargeSplit_of_pairs, CMLattice.katz_divisibility_limit), and it defines the asked divisibility (Data.localAsk, Data.gain), the allowed loss and the column counts \kappa used by the chain of theta lifts.

Unit 145Columns and the chain of theta lifts: levels and branching on the spaces X_j, short ramified columns, divisibility of the chain at each prime (marked primes and scaled Hecke operators), and size bounds

note and structured proofs · 105 declarations · 46 structured proofs

The folder New/Columns. Columns are algebraic forms on the definite space X_0 of weight (\kappa, e_0) at the level Data.columnLevel 0 (principal at the chosen places, last row \equiv (0, \ldots, 0, 1) \bmod \pi, \pi\overline{\pi} = N); the ramified columns are the image of \prod_{w \mid \pi}(1 - z_w), and they are spanned by short columns (integral everywhere, all conjugates of size \le \exp(\operatorname{fa}(\log b)^3)). For each prime p and each integer \beta < g(p) - \operatorname{err}(p) + 1 there are one exponent l \le \operatorname{err}(p) \cdot \dim \text{Column} and one O_\iota-submodule S of columns, of small index in the integral columns in the sense of IndexDvd (a determinant condition: every finite family of integral columns has a square matrix over O_\iota with determinant dividing p^l whose row combinations lie in S), such that for every twist \vartheta of conductor prime to p and every good \Psi for \vartheta the chain of theta lifts B_\mu(\vartheta, \Psi) maps the ramified columns of S to forms divisible by p^\beta (Data.exists_indexDvd_Bmu_divisible), using jet exponents along the chain, marked sets of primes at inert p and scaled Hecke operators of small elementary divisors; and the untwisted chain enlarges squared norms of all conjugates by at most an explicit factor (Data.weylNormSq_Bmu_le). The size bookkeeping (row lengths, factorials, class numbers, sum of allowed losses \le budget) is also here.

Unit 144Elementary divisors of a Frobenius-twisted Hecke operator: twisting data, Frobenius inequalities for least terms, interlacing-cone volumes, flag counts and an approximate projector

note and structured proofs · 78 declarations · 32 structured proofs

Twisting data T: letters \iota_s : W \to R (s \in \mathbb{Z}/N) of a local ring W with residue field of q = P^N elements, consecutive letters congruent up to the P-power Frobenius mod p, alternating parity, and w with w^2 = p. \mathrm{GL}_k(W) acts on forms of degree n in each letter through g_s = \iota_s(g) or \iota_s((g^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}}) (letterRep, polyAction). The unit proves hasType_twisted_hecke: a Frobenius-twisted Hecke operator has small elementary divisors (HasType at p^m with an explicit bound), from (i) the root lemma (a term least in all Levi frames obeys Frobenius inequalities, via Stepanov's count of roots and Gauss filtrations), (ii) dimension bounds mod p: forms whose exponents nearly obey those inequalities fill about one part in the Hecke degree (flag count against the volume of a cyclic interlacing cone), and forms deep in every frame are rare, (iii) an approximate projector from Levi-invariant means on polynomials in a generic matrix, and (iv) Smith/Hermite normal forms over the valuation ring.

Unit 149A crude bound for the class number of the standard definite hermitian genus, and divisibility of indices of submodules

note and structured proofs · 11 declarations · 5 structured proofs

Two independent pieces of bookkeeping. (1) Space.card_classes_le: for a CM field E with a nonzero natural number N in its different, the number of classes \Gamma_f\backslash G_f/K_f of the standard definite hermitian space of rank n is at most (1 + 2C^3)^{4fn^2}, C = 6N(fn)^2, by mapping each class injectively to the integral, archimedean-bounded entries of two matrices built from a short basis. (2) IndexDvd M N z, a divisibility form of 'the index of N in M divides z' (every finite family in M is moved into N by a square matrix whose determinant divides z), with its calculus: pull-back, push-forward, transitivity, spans, composition, products of operators of a given type (HasType: y(M) \subseteq M and y(M) + p^n M has index dividing p^l with l \le c), and the determinant criterion over a valuation domain (IndexDvd.of_det_dvd).
