What it saysLemma (filling, abstract form). Let E be a number field with a CM structure and Weil data, \mathfrak{s} a shape, S admissible filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s},V a finite-dimensional complex inner product space, g=(g_0,g_1) with g_i\in\mathbb{C}[\mathsf{G}_i],c\in\mathbb{R},\rho a representation of \mathsf{G} on V and Q:\ell^{2}(\mathtt{Classes}(S)\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}(S))\to V linear. Suppose (h\rho)\rho is unitary; (hQ) \langle\rho(s)Q(e_y),Q(e_x)\rangle=c\cdotamplitudeSum(S,G(E),x_1,s,y_1)(x_2,y_2) for all s\in\mathsf{G} and x,y (e_x the basis vectors); (hg) each g_i is a central idempotent; (hb) \operatorname{Re}(1-g_i)(1)\le b(S)^{-8}/2 for i=0,1; (hc) c>0; and (hD) \dim range \rho(e)\le\#\mathsf{G}\cdotbesselDegree(S)\cdot\operatorname{mass}(E,r(\mathfrak{s})), where e= piTensorElem(g)=g_0\otimes g_1\in\mathbb{C}[\mathsf{G}]. Then the family s\mapsto R_s=\rho(e)|^{\mathrm{range}}\circ\rho(s)\circ Q:\ell^{2}(Classes \times BesselCoord) \to range \rho(e) is spread with defect (19/2)\cdot b(S)^{-4} (IsSpread): for every subspace F of range \rho(e), with \varphi=\dim F/\dim range \rho(e), some s\in\mathsf{G} has \dim R_s^{-1}(F)\le((1+\varphi)/2 + (19/2)\cdot b(S)^{-4})\cdotcolumnDim(S). (Notation: \mathsf{G}= GRes(S)=\prod_{i\in\{0,1\}}\mathsf{G}_i,\mathsf{G}_i=\prod_{w\in\mathtt{chosenPlaces}(S,i)}\operatorname{GL}_{\mathtt{BesselIndex}(\mathfrak{s})}(\mathbb{F}_w); \iota_i(s)= Pi.mulSingle(i,s)\in\mathsf{G} for s\in\mathsf{G}_i; amplitudeSum(S,T,c,s,c')\in\ell^{2}(\mathtt{BesselCoord}\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}) is the sum of \operatorname{amplitude}(S,c,c',\gamma) over the rational \gamma\in T that are terms over (c,s,c'), with the full Bessel functions; \mathsf{S}(E)= besselS of the rational middle form; \nu(S)= unipCovolume(S); \operatorname{energy}(A,\Omega)=\sum_{c,c'}\sum_{s\in\Omega}\lVert A(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu(S)^{2}; \mathsf{d}(S)= mainEnergy(S)= columnDim(S)^{2}/(\mathtt{besselDegree}(S)\cdot\operatorname{mass}(E,r(\mathfrak{s}))); \mathsf{d}_1(S)= besselEnergy(S)= \#reducedImage(S,\mathsf{S})\cdotcolumnDim(S); columnDim(S)= \#Classes(S)\cdot\#BesselCoord(S).)
The argument in briefNormalise P= tQ with t^{2}=1/(c\nu), so \langle\rho(b)\mathtt{Pe\_y},\rho(a)\mathtt{Pe\_x}\rangle= amplitudeSum(G(E),x_1,a^{-1}b,y_1)/\nu. For each s, with U_s=R_s^{-1}(F) and A=F: \dim U_s-\varepsilon-\sum_x\lVert\rho(1-e)\rho(s)\mathtt{Pe\_x}\rVert^{2}\le\operatorname{Re}\langle\operatorname{dyad}(\rho(s)P),\operatorname{dyad}(A)\rangle,\varepsilon=b^{-4}columnDim bounding P^{*}P-1 (the off-\mathsf{S} amplitude sums at 1). Averaging over s, Cauchy–Schwarz and the second moment with constant one (\Theta(s)= energy at \{s\}, sum_le_of_positive_type) give \sum_s\operatorname{Re}\langle\ldots\rangle\le\#\mathsf{G}\cdotcolumnDim/2\cdot(1+9b^{-4}+\varphi); and, splitting 1-e into the single-prime terms, \sum_x\lVert\rho(1-e)\rho(s)\mathtt{Pe\_x}\rVert^{2}\le\sum_i (\mathsf{S}-trace + off-\mathsf{S} rows of 1-g_i) \le4b^{-4}columnDim. Hence \dim U_s\le((1+\varphi)/2+19/2\cdot b^{-4})columnDim for some s.
