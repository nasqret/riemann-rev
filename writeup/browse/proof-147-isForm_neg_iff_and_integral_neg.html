lemma isForm_neg_iff_and_integral_neg

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type, n a finite index type, s signs, W : Space(E, n, s) and S : Space(E, n, -s) a space with the opposite signs, and suppose (\Gamma) that the identification G.neg of the adelic unitary groups of signs s and -s carries the rational points of W onto those of S: G.neg(g) \in \Gamma(S) if and only if g \in \Gamma(W) (by default proved by simplification). Then (1) for every parameter l and every function F on U(S)(\mathbb{A}), the pull-back g \mapsto F(G.neg(g)) is a form of type typeKernel(l, s) on W if and only if F is a form of type typeKernel(l, -s) on S; and (2) for every function F, the integral of F over [U(S)] equals the integral over [U(W)] of g \mapsto F(G.neg(g)).
The argument in briefG.neg is a measure-preserving isomorphism of the groups and of their Haar measures compatible with the maximal compact subgroups; unfolding forms and quotient measures, both statements are transport along it.
