lemma Parameters.thetaProducts_normal_form

What it saysLet p be a prime with \chi_K(p) \ne 1 and a p-adic place \iota_p, P parameters and s an exponent. There are \varpi \in \mathbb{C}_p and \pi \in \mathfrak{o} with |\varpi | = p^{-\rho_p} and |\pi | = p^{(\chi_K(p)-1)/2} such that for every finite type I, every r \le 1 and every \Theta \in \operatorname{thetaProducts}(P, s, 1, r, I): (a) for every B \in \mathbb{N}, \operatorname{IsIntegralOnNullCone}(pr, S_B, \varpi, \pi, B, \Theta) holds with pr_j(v) = (-p^j)\cdot v and S_B = \{j \le \log_p B \colon jodd\text{ or } p = d}; and (b) if \chi_K(p) = -1 and r = 1, there are \kappa \in \mathbb{C}_p and integral series \Psi, V (over \mathfrak{o}) with |\kappa | = p^{1-1/(p-1)}, \Psi(0) = 0, \Theta(\varpi z) = \exp(\kappa \Psi)\cdot V, and for every variable x and exponent d: the coefficient of d in \partial_x\Psi reduced modulo the maximal ideal is nonzero iff d = p^j\cdot e_y for some odd j and some variable y = (place, copy) with ((-p^j)\cdot y.1, y.2) = x.
The argument in briefTake \varpi, \pi from the normal form exists_formal_parameter of \mathcal{E}. Each factor U_{y_i}\theta(\varpi \ell_i) is \exp(\kappa \psi)\cdot V_i after the change of variables of the frame (z_{v, i} from exists_frame_rows_frob), so \Theta(\varpi z) = \exp(\kappa \Psi)\cdot V with \Psi = \sum_i \psi(\ell_i). (a) For a null family Z, \Psi(Z) \equiv 0 modulo \pi up to degree B, because u_1\sum t_i^{p+1} + u_2\sum t_i^{p+p^e} vanish (sum_pow_eq_zero_of_null with the frame pairings) and u_0\sum L_i^2 vanishes; then the exponential series is integral since |\kappa \pi |^k \le |k!|. (b) Modulo the maximal ideal, \partial_x\Psi \equiv u_1\sum_i c_i(S)^p\cdot z_{x, i}, whose nonzero coefficients coeff_sum_frob_ne_zero_iff locates.
