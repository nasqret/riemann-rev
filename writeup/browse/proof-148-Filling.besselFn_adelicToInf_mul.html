lemma Filling.besselFn_adelicToInf_mul

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), x\in G(S,\mathbb{A}_E) (implicit), the side (full or cut, an instance), the hypothesis x\in\mathsf{U} (h) and an archimedean point y: besselFn(S,\mathtt{adelicToInf}(x)\cdot y)=(\psi(-\sigma(x)/(g\varpi))/\mathtt{unipotentChar}(S,\mathtt{adelicToFin}(x)))\cdotbesselFn(S,y), where \psi= stdAddChar is the standard additive character of \mathbb{A}_E and \sigma= simpleRootSum: left translation by the archimedean part of x multiplies W(y) by \psi(-\sigma(x)/(g\varpi)) divided by the finite character of the finite part of x.
The argument in briefWrite z=-(g\varpi)^{-1}\sigma(x)\in\mathbb{A}_E; the finite character is \psi_f(z_f); at each infinite place the rescaled x_v lies in \mathsf{U} with \sigma=-2\pi i z_v, and the Bessel function transforms by \operatorname{wave}(\sigma); the product over v is the infinite part of \psi(z).
