Chapter VII · Unit 147

The exchange identity: theta integrals of complementary rank agree up to a constant, and cruder forms complete a family to a basis in the lower bound

note and structured proofs · 305 declarations · 122 structured proofs · 3 notes

Unit 147 (New/Exchange) proves the exchange theorem: for hermitian spaces V, V' of complementary rank inside W, the theta integrals over U(V) and U(V') of matched ramified data agree up to a non-zero constant whose squared absolute value is a fixed number times residue counts and products of partial Hecke L-values (exchange_theorem); it uses it for one square of lifts and transposed lifts, for the grid of such squares, and for the cruder forms of Data.crude_rows, which complete a family of target forms to a basis with integral twisted lifts and bounded conjugates in the lower bound (Theorem C). Chunk a (this dossier) holds the analytic and local foundations: the Rallis inner product of the adjoint lift \theta_j^* and the L-value of the lift of the constant from U(1) (CharacterLiftNorm); local partner data and matched pairs (BlockPartners, PartnersMatch, StableSpaces); the vanishing of theta coefficients at forms that are not negative definite (NonnegativeVanishing); the equivariant functional of the difference of the two theta integrals and its vanishing on standard data (ExchangeDifference, StandardCoefficients); and the exchange identity at the top of the tower, from equal-rank constancy, matched real data and determinant vectors (LowRankIdentity, RealPair, DeterminantVectors).
