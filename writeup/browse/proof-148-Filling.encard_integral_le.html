lemma Filling.encard_integral_le

What it saysFor a number field E' with chosen complex embeddings (PlaceEmbedding E'), a type \alpha, a finite type \iota, a set A\subseteq\alpha,r with 0\le r (o) and f:\alpha\to E'^{\iota} such that for a\in A all f(a)_i are algebraic integers with |\Phi_v(f(a)_i)|\le r at every infinite place v (h), and f injective on A (j): \#A\le(1+2r)^{2\cdot|\iota|\cdot m},m the number of infinite places of E'.
The argument in briefPacking in \mathbb{C}^{\iota\times\mathrm{places}}: distinct points are 1-apart in the sup norm.

    Map a\in A to (\Phi_v(f(a)_i))_{i,v}\in\mathbb{C}^{\iota\times\mathrm{places}}. Distinct points are at sup-distance \ge1: otherwise every coordinate of f(a)-f(b), an algebraic integer, would have |\Phi_v|<1 everywhere and vanish (eq_zero_of_norm_\Phi), contradicting injectivity. All points have norm \le r. By the packing bound (encard_le_of_separated with m=1,R=r) \#A\le(1+2r)^{\dim_{\mathbb{R}}} with \dim_{\mathbb{R}}=2\cdot|\iota|\cdot m.
