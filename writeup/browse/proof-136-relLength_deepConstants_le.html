lemma Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.relLength_deepConstants_le

What it saysLet the chart be unramified (hk), S a side, i a letter, c, c' exponent functions, \eta \in \mathbb{N}, and n, r : letters \to \mathbb{N} such that, at every letter j, f_k\cdot\sum_{u\in[-\eta, \eta]} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le n(j) (hn) and f_k\cdot\sum_{u\in U_j} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le r(j) (hr) (f_k the residue degree of the chart, Y the lattice of S), and a, A, B \in \mathbb{N}, i' = \sigma^{-a}(i) (f). Suppose: no integer in the open interval (c(0, v), c(0, v) + p^{a}) is divisible by p, for every integer v (ha); for every b' and u \in U_{\sigma^{-b'}(i)}: -u \le c(b', u), and c'(b', u) \le c(b', u) if b' \ne a (hc); c'(a, \cdot) is antitone on U_{i'} (ho); and \sum_{n'\in[c(a,u), c'(a,u))} \operatorname{cost}(a, n') \le A + (B if u \in [-\eta, \eta], else 0) for every u \in U_{i'} (hA). Then the relative length relLength(deepConstants(S, i, 0, c), deepConstants(S, i, 0, c')) (the length of the first modulo its intersection with the second) is at most A\cdot r(i') + B\cdot n(i').
The argument in briefReduce to \sum over the steps (u, n') of f\cdot\mathrm{mult}(u)\cdot\mathrm{cost}(a, n') and induct over that finite set: raising c at one (a, u) costs at most f\cdot\mathrm{mult}(u)\cdot\mathrm{cost}(a, c(a, u)) in length, because among f\cdot\mathrm{mult}(u) + 1 constants with deep lifts a combination with a unit coefficient lifts deeper (exists_comb_of_jumpMultiplicity_le), its E-part being moved by a cost-zero operator from E\cdot M into E^{p^{a}}M \subseteq pM + M'.
