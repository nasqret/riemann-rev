lemmaSpace.card_classes_le

Theorem 1 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ClassCountBound.lean, lines 14–37

Let E be a CM field with a CM type (CMType E: a CM number field with a chosen complex embedding \Phi_w inducing each infinite place w), N a nonzero natural number (NeZero N) whose image in the ring of integers lies in the different ideal of E (h), and n a finite index type. Then the number of classes of the standard definite hermitian space of rank |n| at the level K_f, i.e. Nat.card of StdClasses (Kf E n) = \Gamma_f\backslash G_f/K_f (G_f the finite adelic unitary group of the identity form on E^{n}, \Gamma_f its rational points, K_f = G_f \cap \mathrm{GL}_n(\hat{O}) the stabiliser of the standard lattice \hat{O}^{n}), is at most (1 + 2\cdot C^{3})^{4\cdot f\cdot|n|^{2}}, where C = shortBasisConst E n N = 6\cdot N\cdot(f\cdot|n|)^{2} and f = numInfPlaces E is the number of infinite places of E.

defIndexDvd

Definition 2.1 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean, line 20

For a commutative ring O, an O-module V, submodules M, N \subseteq V and an element z \in O: IndexDvd M N z holds when for every natural number n and every family \Phi_1, \ldots, \Phi_n of elements of M there is an n\times n matrix m over O whose determinant divides z such that every combination \sum_k m_{ik}\cdot\Phi_k (i = 1, \ldots, n) lies in N. (A divisibility form of 'the index of N in M divides z'; when M \subseteq N, m = 1 works.) The definition takes M, N, z as explicit arguments (variable (M N z) in).

lemmaIndexDvd.comap

Lemma 4.2 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean, lines 24–25

Pull-back. For a commutative ring O, O-modules U and V, submodules M, N \subseteq V and M', N' \subseteq U, z \in O and an O-linear map f : U \to V: if IndexDvd M N z (h), f(M') \subseteq M, and every x \in M' with f(x) \in N lies in N', then IndexDvd M' N' z.

lemmaIndexDvd.trans

Lemma 4.3 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean, lines 31–33

Transitivity. For a commutative ring O, an O-module V, submodules M, N, P \subseteq V and z, a \in O: if IndexDvd M N z (h) and IndexDvd N P a (g), then IndexDvd M P (z\cdot a).

lemmaIndexDvd.map

Lemma 4.4 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean, lines 37–41

lemmaIndexDvd.sup

Lemma 4.5 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean, lines 46–48

For a commutative ring O, O-modules U and V, an index type \iota, O-linear maps f_i : V \to U (i \in \iota), a submodule M \subseteq V, a submodule N' \subseteq U and z \in O: if IndexDvd M (f_i^{-1}(N')) z for every i \in \iota (h), then for every finite set s \subseteq \iota, IndexDvd (span of \bigcup_{i\in s} f_i(M) \cup N') N' (z^{|s|}). (Induction on s with IndexDvd.map and IndexDvd.trans.)

lemmaIndexDvd.comp

Lemma 4.6 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean, lines 50–51

Composition. For a commutative ring O, an O-module V, submodules M, N \subseteq V, elements z, z', a \in O and an O-linear endomorphism f of V with f(M) \subseteq M (e): if IndexDvd M (f(M) + a\cdot M) z and IndexDvd M (N + a\cdot M) z', then IndexDvd M (f(N) + a\cdot M) (z\cdot z'). (IndexDvd.map along f, then IndexDvd.trans.)

defHasType

Definition 2.2 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean, lines 55–56

For a commutative ring O, an O-module V, p \in O, a submodule M \subseteq V, a natural number n, an O-linear endomorphism y of V and a real number c: HasType p M n y c means that y(M) \subseteq M and there is a natural number l \le c with IndexDvd M (y(M) + p^{n}\cdot M) (p^{l}) ('y has type at most c at p^{n} on M': the index of y(M) + p^{n}M in M divides p^{l}).

lemmaHasType.prod

Proposition 4.7 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean, lines 60–64

Exponents add under products. For a commutative ring O, an O-module V, an index type \iota, a submodule M \subseteq V, z \in O, a natural number s, endomorphisms T(l) of V and real numbers t(l) (l \in \iota), and a list L of indices: if HasType z M s (T l) (t l) for every l in L, then HasType z M s (\Pi_{l\in L} T(l)) (\sum_{l\in L} t(l)), where the product is the composition T(l_{1})\circ T(l_{2})\circ\cdots in the order of the list (the empty product is the identity) and the sum is the sum of the t(l) over the list.

lemmaIndexDvd.of_det_dvd

Theorem 2 of the note · unit 149 · New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean, lines 68–73

Determinant criterion.
