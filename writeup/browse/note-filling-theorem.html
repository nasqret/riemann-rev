Theorem (filling). For parameters P, admissible data D for P and a unit c\in\mathbb{C}^{\times}, put for s\in\mathsf{G}_D B_s= goodProjTranslate(D,s)\circ\operatorname{poincare}(D)\circ(c\cdot\mathtt{ramifyingOp}(D,\mathtt{piPlaces}(D))) : Column(D)\to\operatorname{rows}(D), where goodProjTranslate(D,s)(y)=\#\mathsf{G}_D\cdot e_0(D)\cdot(s\cdot y) (s acting on principalForms(D,Y_0(D)) through the principal representation, e_0(D)\in\mathbb{C}[\mathsf{G}_D] the good idempotent, whose image is \operatorname{rows}(D)), \operatorname{poincare}(D)=\mathcal{P}_D is the Poincaré series map, and ramifyingOp(D,\mathtt{piPlaces}(D))=\prod_{w|\pi}(1-z_w) on Column(D),z_w the translation by the scalar centralGen(D,w), the product over all finite places w dividing \pi=\pi(D).
