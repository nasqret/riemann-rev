lemma IntegralPlace.phase_eq

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. Lemma. If u\in\mathsf{U} and u^{-1}y\in K(g), then \operatorname{phase}(g,y)=\psi(g^{-1}\sigma(u)) (independent of the chosen u).
The argument in briefThe chosen u' and the given u differ by an element of K(g), so their simple-root sums differ by an element of gR, on which \psi(g^{-1}\cdot) is 1.
