lemma Data.descent_past_gridPathMap

What it saysA descent moves past a lifts. Let P be parameters, D admissible data, a, b natural numbers with b < m (h) and a \le b (h'), and z a grid. There are \kappa \in \mathbb{C} and a nowhere-zero function c of the m-fold twists costing a squares (Data.CostsSquares D a c) such that for every m-fold twist \vartheta, order n, finite set w of edges and datum \Lambda: if every edge of w avoids grid z or is rowEdge(z, i, b) with i \ge a, and \Lambda is tidy of order n on w \cup gridPath(z, a, b), then there is \Phi, tidy of order n + 2a on \{verticalEdge(z, a, b)\} \cup gridPath(z, a, b + 1) \cup w, with \Lambda(S \mapsto l(S)(gridPathMap(S, z, a, b)(F))) = \kappa /c(\vartheta)\cdot \Phi(S \mapsto l(S)(descentMove(S(verticalEdge(z, a, b)))(gridPathMap(S, z, a, b + 1)(F)))) for every form F at (0, m) and every family l(S) of linear functionals depending only on the values of S on w.
The argument in briefInduction on a: for a = 0 nothing moves; at a + 1, apply the induction hypothesis and then cross one square (Data.IsTidy.exists_square_move with the square law of Data.exists_squareLaw_grid); the denominators multiply, so their costs add (a + 1 in all), and the order of the short sum grows by 2 at each square.
