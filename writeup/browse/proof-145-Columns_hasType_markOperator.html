The argument in briefReduce to the abstract TwistingData.hasType_twisted_hecke: columns are functions on classes (freeLevelEquiv); the Hecke operator at l is a sum over the cosets of the parahoric P of pullbacks by d = \operatorname{diag} ( p \cdot 1_{\mathfrak{r}} , 1 ), which, after decomposing \rho_{x} \cdot \delta_{b} = \gamma \cdot \rho \cdot u with rational \gamma, act on values by the weight representation of \gamma: at the letters (tops inducing l or \overline{l}) this is the twisted letter representation, elsewhere an integral invertible matrix. Then check: the image is the integral columns, the operator formula, and the injectivity of the source map.
