lemma AuxPair.normSqAt_starProjection_le

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, r, n finite types, O an auxiliary pair over E with index types r \subset n, w a finite place of E with \overline{w} \ne w (hw) outside the places of O and their conjugates (hS), e_0' a central idempotent of \mathbb{C}[O.ChosenGL r] (he), Z' a complex subspace of O.FormV contained in (image of O.groupAlgAction e_0') \cap \ker(O.heckePolyOnForms hw hS (O.testValue w)) (hZ), and y \in O.FormV with O.IsTested e_0' Z'^\perp y (h). Then for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}: normSqAt (\operatorname{pr}_{Z'} y) \sigma \le normSqAt y \sigma, where \operatorname{pr}_{Z'} is the orthogonal projection onto Z' and normSqAt \varphi \sigma = \lVert \varphi \rVert ^{2}_\sigma is the class sum of the squared Weyl norms of the \sigma-conjugated values of \varphi.
The argument in briefWrite y = z + x with z \in Z' and x \in \ker e_0' + \operatorname{im} H_w(t); x is orthogonal to Z' at every \sigma, so \operatorname{pr}_{Z'} y = z and \lVert y\rVert ^{2}_\sigma = \lVert z\rVert ^{2}_\sigma + \lVert x\rVert ^{2}_\sigma \ge \lVert z\rVert ^{2}_\sigma.
