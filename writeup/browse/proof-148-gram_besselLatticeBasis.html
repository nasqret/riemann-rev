lemma gram_besselLatticeBasis

What it saysLet R be a commutative ring with involution, \mathfrak{s} a shape (implicit), p a unit of R and N\in R. If p\cdot\overline{p}=N, then B^{H}\cdot splitGram(\mathtt{middleGram}(N))\cdot B= splitGram(\mathtt{latMiddleGram}(\mathfrak{s},N)) for B= besselLatticeBasis(\mathfrak{s},p): in the lattice basis the form of H^{\varrho}\oplus X_0\oplus Ez with h(z,z)=-N has the Gram matrix built from latMiddleGram(\mathfrak{s},N).
The argument in briefUnfold the three matrices and compare entries; the identities follow from p p^{-1}=1, the rules of the involution and p\overline{p}=N.
