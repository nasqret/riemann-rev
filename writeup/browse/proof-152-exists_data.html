Structured proof · Unit 152

lemma exists_data

What it saysExistence of admissible data. For parameters P and a real number \varepsilon with e0 : 0 < \varepsilon /6 and e1 : \varepsilon < 1/100: for all sufficiently large real x (eventually along atTop) there are admissible data D over P with x(D) = x, \log x_1(D) = x^{\varepsilon /6}, (10^{6}\cdot \log x)^{2} \le \log x_1(D), \log \log b(D) = \varepsilon \cdot \log x, and towerDeg(s(D))/\log b(D) \le \#\operatorname{thick}(D).
The argument in briefSubstitute x = e^X and work at a large real X (written x below), with W = e^{\varepsilon x/6}. Take m = 2^{o+1} \approx e^{x/2}, r = 3^{e+1} \approx e^{5x}, s = 22(o + 1), \mathit{logX} = x, \mathit{logX}_1 = W, \mathit{logB} = W^{6}. A weak Siegel–Walfisz theorem gives primes \equiv 1 \bmod M\omega (P, s)^{2} in every window of length W in [W, W^n]; h_K + 1 such primes split in K, and the class-group pigeonhole gives two, g_0 \ne g_1, with an integer \alpha of K of norm g_0g_1. Behrend's construction in \Phi \cong (\mathbb{Z}/N_1)^\times gives the \operatorname{thick} places. All size conditions are inequalities between powers of x, W and 2^n that hold for large x.
