lemma TwistingData.exists_le_weight_of_mem_deepForms

What it saysLet N be a natural number, W a commutative local ring, K a field and T twisting data over them (T : TwistingData N W K, with prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to K for s \in \mathbb{Z}/N, parity isEven and w \in K), N \ne 0, K of characteristic p, natural numbers k, n, r, \tau and a coefficient vector F : (\mathbb{Z}/N \to Monomials (Fin k) n) \to K with 2r \le k (hk), 2r < N (hN), F \in deepForms T k n r \tau (hF: every translate \rho(g)F, g \in \mathrm{GL}_k(W), has only monomials of depth \ge \tau) and F \ne 0 (F0). Then some \beta has F(\beta) \ne 0 and \tau \le \sum_{(b,s)} \beta_{s}(b)\cdotdeepIndicator T r (b, s)/P^{e(b)} (in \mathbb{R}), with e(b) = 2r if b \ge 2r and e(b) = 1 if b < 2r: a nonzero form all of whose translates are deep has a monomial of weight at least \tau when deep variables weigh P^{-2r} (b \ge 2r) or 1/P (b < 2r) and the others 0. (Here \tau is a natural number, the depth bound, not a labelling.)
The argument in briefCombinatorial Nullstellensatz with multiplicity over the field of rational functions K(x): substitute constants for the non-deep variables, give a deep variable v the cost 1/\kappa(v), \kappa(v) = P^{2r} or P, and use the points z(y), one for each y : I \to residue field (I = Fin r \times Fin (m+1), k = 2r + m), coming from the unipotent matrices 1 + \sum_{t} y_t E_{\rho(t),\sigma(t)}. Frobenius twists make each u^{\kappa(u)} the value of a cheap polynomial at these points; the image of F vanishes to order \tau at each point, because the translate of F by the unipotent matrix is deep; and there are at least \Pi \kappa points. So the image of F has a monomial of cost \ge \tau, which comes from a monomial \beta of F with F(\beta) \ne 0.
