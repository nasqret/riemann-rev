lemma IsPolar.exists_window_sub_mul_le

What it saysA good second scale in every long window. For functions E, E' : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} and r, r' : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C} with IsPolar(E, r) (h) and IsPolar(E', r') (h'): for every sufficiently large real t and every real X with 0 \le X \le e^{t}, there is u with t \le u \le t^{2} and E(u) - X\cdot E'(u) \le e^{-t}.
The argument in briefIf E - XE' > e^{-t} on the whole window [t, t^{2}], the k-th moment with k \approx t^{2}/9, corrected by two exponentials that dominate outside the window, would be \ge 0; but the moment bounds make it negative.
