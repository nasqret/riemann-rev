lemma Data.endPeriod_mem

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data, \vartheta an m-fold twist, n a natural number and \Lambda \in GridDatum(\vartheta) tidy of order n on path(m) (h\Lambda); y a pair of forms at the nodes (0, m) with FixedByEndLevels(\vartheta, y) (hy); S a predicate S(z, j, g, v) on grids, indices, points and vectors; F a normed field, \sigma : \mathbb{C} \to F a ring homomorphism, M \in \mathbb{C} and R \in \mathbb{R}. Assume (\mathit{hs}) S is preserved by the kernels: for every tuple x, grid z, j < m, points g, h and vector v, S(z, j, g, v) implies S(z, j + 1, h, jetMatrix(endKernel(\vartheta, z, j))(\omega(g, h)(endDatum(x, z, j)))\cdot v); (hB) S holds at the start: S(z, 0, g, endToAlgForm(z)(y(z))(g)) for all z, g; (hA) for vectors v', v with S at the end node of the grid V at g and of the grid Z at endBlockPoint(\vartheta, g): \lVert \sigma(M\cdotendPairing(v', v))\rVert \le R. Then there is N \le tupleBound(\vartheta)^{n} such that endPeriod(\Lambda, y) is a sum of N\cdot \prod_{z} \prod_{j < m + [z = V]} \#StdClasses(endLevel(\vartheta, z, j)) complex numbers a, each with \lVert \sigma(M\cdot a)\rVert \le R.
The argument in briefTidiness makes the integrand of the end period invariant under the levels of the end nodes (J); expand \Lambda as a short sum, write the class integral as a sum over double cosets, and bound each chain value by the jointly invariant chain lemma.
