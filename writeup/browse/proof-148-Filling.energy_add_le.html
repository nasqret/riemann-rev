lemma Filling.energy_add_le

What it saysLemma. For a normed additive group V, families A,B : Classes(S)\times\mathsf{G}\times Classes(S)\to V, a set \Omega\subseteq\mathsf{G} and a real \varepsilon>0: \operatorname{energy}(A+B,\Omega)\le(1+\varepsilon)\cdot\operatorname{energy}(A,\Omega)+(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\cdot\operatorname{energy}(B,\Omega).
The argument in briefUnfold the energies and compare termwise: \lVert a+b\rVert^{2}\le(1+\varepsilon)\lVert a\rVert^{2}+(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\lVert b\rVert^{2}.

    Unfolding \operatorname{energy}(\cdot, \Omega) and collecting the right-hand side into one sum over c,c',s\in\Omega, it suffices to compare the sums termwise: \lVert A(c,s,c')+B(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu^{2} against (1+\varepsilon)\lVert A(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu^{2}+(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\lVert B(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu^{2}.

    Termwise (dividing by \nu^{2} is monotone): \lVert a+b\rVert^{2}\le(\lVert a\rVert+\lVert b\rVert)^{2}=\lVert a\rVert^{2}+2\lVert a\rVert\lVert b\rVert+\lVert b\rVert^{2}\le\lVert a\rVert^{2}+\varepsilon\lVert a\rVert^{2}+\varepsilon^{-1}\lVert b\rVert^{2}+\lVert b\rVert^{2}, using 2xy\le\varepsilon x^{2}+\varepsilon^{-1}y^{2} for \varepsilon>0 (hypothesis h\varepsilon).
