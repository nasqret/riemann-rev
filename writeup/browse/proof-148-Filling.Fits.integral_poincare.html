lemma Filling.Fits.integral_poincare

What it saysLemma (unfolding). For E stably unramified, S admissible, X : Fits(S,V,l, kw, Y,\eta),\varphi, a subgroup \Delta\le\Gamma and a form y on V of type q (V.IsForm(q,y)): with F=y\cdot\operatorname{poincare}(X,\varphi,\Delta,\cdot),F is integrable on \Delta\setminus G(\varepsilon) and \int_{\Delta\setminus G}F=\Sigma_a\varphi_a\int_{G(\varepsilon)}y(g)\cdotcutBessel(X,a,g)dg.
The argument in briefUnfold the Poincaré series over \Delta against y: for each a, the function g\mapsto y(g)\cdotcutBessel(a,g) is integrable on G (y bounded measurable, \Sigma_{\Gamma}|\mathtt{cutBessel}| bounded), so the unfolding lemma for \Delta=\Gamma\cap\Delta gives integrability and the integral; sum over a.
