lemma Parameters.norm_jet_jetSeries_le_one

What it saysIntegrality of jets. Let V be a standard hermitian space over E(P, s) of rank n, p a prime with a p-adic place \iota_p, \delta \in \mathcal{O}_K with \delta \ne 0, f \in \operatorname{standardData}(V, \delta), Q a polynomial with coefficients in O_\iota (the complex numbers integral at \iota_p) and Q' its image as a complex polynomial. Then |\iota_p(jet_{Q'}(T_{\overline{\delta}\cdot d\cdot per} f))| \le 1, where jet_{Q'}(F) = (Q'(\partial)F)(0) and T_{\overline{\delta}\cdot d\cdot per} = Space.jetSeries (\overline{\delta}\cdot d\cdot \operatorname{jetScale}(P, s)).
The argument in briefApply norm_jetSeries_le with L = jet along Q' and r = 1; for a theta product the jet is integral by CMLattice.jet_theta_prod_integral.
