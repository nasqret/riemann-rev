lemma Grading.IsAdaptedAt.ideleLogVal_add_rev_nonpos

What it saysLet E be a stably unramified field with a CM type, V a hermitian space with a grading Y (X plus a string of length s, norm N), x \in G_f(\iota) with an adapted basis b of string diagonal \beta (h), and N = \pi\overline{\pi} split and squarefree along P (q: IsSplitSquarefree(P, N, \pi)). Assume (hg) that, if s is odd, for no n \in B_f and w \in P is (nx)^{-1}\cdot K_w(X)\cdot(nx) \subseteq K_w(V), i.e. some a \in maxCompactAt(X, w) has (nx)^{-1}\cdotinclXFin(a)\cdot(nx) \notin maxCompactAt(\iota, w). Then for every string index i and finite place w: ideleLogVal(\beta_i)_{w} + ideleLogVal(\beta_{s-1-i})_{\overline{w}} \le 0, i.e. \operatorname{ord}_w(\beta_i) + \operatorname{ord}_{\overline{w}}(\beta_{s-1-i}) \ge 0.
The argument in briefThe Gram matrix of an adapted basis gives the bound except at a middle index above P with s odd; there, if the bound failed, a local change of basis at w and Witt's theorem (exists_mem_besselFin) would put the conjugate of K_w(X) inside K_w(V), contradicting hg.
