lemma Filling.energy_fixingSet_le

What it saysLemma (main-term estimate). Let E be a number field with a CM structure and Weil data, \mathfrak{s} a shape and S admissible filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s}. For every finite set Sg of chosen places of S above q_0(S) and frame-vector data D (for each chosen place w above q_0(S), a flag and a nonzero vector over \mathbb{F}_w): \operatorname{energy}(\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(S,G(E), \cdot, \cdot, \cdot), fixingSet(S,Sg,D))\le(1-b(S)^{-4})\cdot((1+9b(S)^{-4})\cdot\mathsf{d}(S)\cdot(b(S)^{6k(\mathfrak{s})})^{-\#Sg}). Here G(E) is the whole rational group (T= univ) and fixingSet(S,Sg,D) is the set of s\in\mathsf{G} that fix the vector of D_w at every w\in Sg (vectorFixer) and, unless Sg contains all chosen places above q_0(S), have all leading minors of frameGram(s_{0,w}) nonzero at some chosen w above q_0(S). (Notation: \mathsf{G}= GRes(S)=\prod_{i\in\{0,1\}}\mathsf{G}_i,\mathsf{G}_i=\prod_{w\in\mathtt{chosenPlaces}(S,i)}\operatorname{GL}_{\mathtt{BesselIndex}(\mathfrak{s})}(\mathbb{F}_w); \iota_i(s)= Pi.mulSingle(i,s)\in\mathsf{G} for s\in\mathsf{G}_i; amplitudeSum(S,T,c,s,c')\in\ell^{2}(\mathtt{BesselCoord}\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}) is the sum of \operatorname{amplitude}(S,c,c',\gamma) over the rational \gamma\in T that are terms over (c,s,c'), with the full Bessel functions; \mathsf{S}(E)= besselS of the rational middle form; \nu(S)= unipCovolume(S); \operatorname{energy}(A,\Omega)=\sum_{c,c'}\sum_{s\in\Omega}\lVert A(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu(S)^{2}; \mathsf{d}(S)= mainEnergy(S)= columnDim(S)^{2}/(\mathtt{besselDegree}(S)\cdot\operatorname{mass}(E,r(\mathfrak{s}))); \mathsf{d}_1(S)= besselEnergy(S)= \#reducedImage(S,\mathsf{S})\cdotcolumnDim(S); columnDim(S)= \#Classes(S)\cdot\#BesselCoord(S).)
The argument in briefApply the crude estimate (energy_le_of_linkEnergy_le) with T=G(E),X=q_0^{\#Sg} (s\in fixingSet fixes the vectors at the places of Sg), C=y\mathsf{d} and w=(1+b^{-4})y\mathsf{d},y=b^{-6k\#Sg},R=\{\text{links}:Sg\ne\text{all}\}; then (1+b^{-4})^{2}w+b^{-12}C\le(1-b^{-4})(1+9b^{-4})y\mathsf{d}. It remains to bound the cut link energy by w\nu^{2}: each nonzero link block is a limit of sums of coset amplitudes over growing T (exists_hasSum_cosetAmplitude), whose squares the top-kernel domination (sum_blocks_le_topKernel) and the sieve (with the finite sieve system of exists_finite_sieveSystem, bottom bound (1+b^{-6})/b^{6k\#Sg}) bound by (1+b^{-6})packetConst\cdot(sieve constant), and packetConst_mul_mass_le closes the numerics.
