lemma AuxPair.innerAt_formAction

What it saysFor a field E with a CM type, finite types r, n, an auxiliary pair O over E with index types r \subset n, an automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}, g \in O.ChosenGL r and forms x, y \in O.FormV: innerAt \sigma (O.formAction g x) (O.formAction g y) = innerAt \sigma x y; the \sigma-conjugate pairing is invariant under the action of the finite group \prod _i \operatorname{GL}_r(k_{w_i}).
The argument in briefApply the injective \sigma; the scalar \prod \psi (\det g) contributes |\prod \psi (\det g)|^{2} = 1, a root of unity having absolute value 1, and the pairing is invariant under translation.
