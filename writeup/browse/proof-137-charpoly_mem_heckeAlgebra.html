lemma BaseLevel.charpoly_mem_heckeAlgebra

What it saysIn the setting (\ell, \iota_\ell, the CM field E \subset \Omega, the nonempty rank type n with N = |n|, V standard, \kappa, J, c, the base datum D, Weil data for E), let L \colon J \to finite places of E be a level, a \colon \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to O a character and N a family of representations with N_x(g) = a(g)\cdot \rho_x(g) for every isotypic space x and g (hN). Then for every g \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) and i \in \mathbb{N}, the function on \operatorname{Eigensystem}(D, L) sending x to the coefficient of X^i in the characteristic polynomial of N_x(g) lies in the Hecke algebra \operatorname{heckeAlgebra}(D, L).
The argument in briefThe coefficient is a(g)^{N-i} times the coefficient for \rho_x(g); the Hecke algebra is a closed subalgebra, and the coefficient for \rho_x(g) is a limit, along most Frobenius data, of \iota of the coefficients of the Hecke polynomials at places off level, which are integers times Hecke eigenvalues; the eigenvalue function acts on forms of level L as the Hecke operator T_{w, j}, which preserves integral forms.

    The Hecke algebra is an O-subalgebra containing the scalar a(g)^{N-i}; it remains that x \mapsto \operatorname{coeff}_i \operatorname{charpoly} \rho_x(g) lies in it.

    1. 2.1reductionlines 69–71

      The Hecke algebra is closed in the functions on eigensystems (isClosed_heckeAlgebra). For every eigensystem x (with a nonzero form in it) \rho_x is attached to the Hecke polynomials of x (Isotypic.attached_galRep), so \operatorname{coeff}_i \operatorname{charpoly} \rho_x(g) is the limit, along most Frobenius data (w, \ldots) with Frobenius tending to g, of \iota_\ell of the coefficient of X^i of the Hecke polynomial of x at w (Attached.tendsto_charpoly_coeff); it suffices that these functions of x lie in the Hecke algebra for most Frobenius data.

      UsesBaseLevel.isClosed_heckeAlgebra unit 130, Isotypic.attached_galRep unit 102, Attached.tendsto_charpoly_coeff unit 5

    2. 2.2reductionlines 71–72

  3. 3computationline 73

    The coefficient of X^i of the Hecke polynomial at w is an integer (a sign times a power of q_w) times the j-th Hecke eigenvalue of x at w; the goal is that this integer times x \mapsto \iota_\ell(t_{x, w, j}) is in the Hecke algebra.

  4. 4factlines 74–77

    The integer is a scalar; and x \mapsto \iota_\ell(t_{x, w, j}) lies in the Hecke algebra because its operator (multiplication by \iota_\ell^{-1} of it on each eigensystem, diagOp) agrees on \operatorname{formsOfLevel}(D, L) with the Hecke operator T_{w, j} (heckeSplit; w is split as q_w is prime): this is checked on each eigensystem, whose forms are eigenforms of T_{w, j} with eigenvalue t_{x, w, j} (formsOfLevel_le_of_eigensystem, diagOp_apply); and T_{w, j} lies in the local group algebra at w (heckeFun_mem_localGroupAlgebra), which preserves integral forms since w \nmid \ell (localGroupAlgebra_le_endPreserving).
