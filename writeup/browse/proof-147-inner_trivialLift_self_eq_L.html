lemma LValueData.inner_trivialLift_self_eq_L

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data, n a finite index type, X : LValueData E n L-value data and k a natural number with 2k = |n| (hM) and k > 1 (hk), so |n| \ge 4 is even. Put C = \operatorname{vol}([U(I_n)])\cdot \operatorname{vol}([U(I_{1})]) / eulerProd(realZetaCoeff, |n|/2), the volumes being the total masses of the quotient measures and the Euler product that of the coefficients realZetaCoeff. Then C \ne 0, and \langle \Xi, \Xi \rangle for \Xi = trivialLift(X) equals C\cdot \prod_{i}(q(chosenPlace(X, i)) - 1)\cdothookNumber(\kappa(auxPair(X)), rankAt(n))\cdot \#WeylIndex(n, \kappa(auxPair(X)))\cdot L(\mu(X)^{-1}, places(X), k)\cdot(\prod_{i} q(twistPlace(X, i)))^{|n|-1}, where L(\mu^{-1}, S, k) = AutChar.L is the partial Hecke L-value L^{S\cup \overline{S}}(k, BC(\mu^{-1})) of the base change x \mapsto \mu^{-1}(x/\overline{x}) to the ideles of E, without the Euler factors at the places of X and at their conjugates (AutCharLFunction.lean 48–50); the product over the chosen places is the number d attached to the weight's level; C does not depend on X (only on E and n).
The argument in briefDoubling: \langle \Xi, \Xi \rangle is a doubling integral of the constant function on U(1); its partial integrals off the places of X are computed place by place (local doubling integrals give the Euler factors of L^{S\cup \overline{S}}(k, BC \mu^{-1})/\zeta-type factors), the archimedean part by the Fock pairing of the highest vector (hookNumber), and the finite part at the places of X by the sharp datum (indices of parabolic and vector stabilizers).
