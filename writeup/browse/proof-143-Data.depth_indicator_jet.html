lemma Data.depth_indicator_jet

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data over P and m a function from the infinite places \sigma of the field of D to \mathbb{N} with w(\sigma) \le m(\sigma) \le w(\sigma) + x^{42} for every \sigma (hm; w = D.nominalWeight, x = D.x). Then there is a number t \ne 0 with |\sigma(t)| \le \exp(f\cdot m\cdot b/k) for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C} (f = D.f, m = D.m, b = D.b, k = D.k: the data's), such that for every modulus \mathfrak{m} whose ideal is generated by \overline{\varpi} = D.piGBar and every prime p with a chosen embedding \iota_p \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_p (PadicPlace p), there are an exponent \alpha (finitely supported on triples (\sigma, i, ())) and a matrix x_0 modulo \mathfrak{m} with \sum_i \alpha(\sigma, i, ()) = m(\sigma) for every \sigma and p^{-F(p)} \le |\iota_p(\kappa_0^{12}/t\cdot \prod_\sigma m(\sigma)!\cdot a)|, where a is the coefficient at \alpha of the scaled Taylor series T_{c}(datum) (Space.jetSeries with scale c = \operatorname{conj}(\pi_g)\cdotper, \pi_g = D.piG, per = P.jetScale D.s) of the indicator datum: the function equal to 1 at x_0 and 0 elsewhere on matrices modulo \mathfrak{m}, tensored with the vacuum, on \operatorname{Hom}(I_1, I_{12}) (modDatum). Here F = D.forcedPart and \kappa_0 = P.vacuumScale D.s.
The argument in brieft is a power of g times the depth losses times a vacuum value. For a modulus (\overline{\varpi}) and a prime p, a tight frame turns a product of monomials into the jet of a finite datum; the products \Phi_\alpha of translated \wp'-series and theta series carry exponents (IsCarriedBy), and a large split of the exponent a (HasLargeSplit, by the Katz bound when p splits in K and by Frobenius packings otherwise) gives a coefficient of the required size; a pigeonhole over the finitely many translates picks x_0.
