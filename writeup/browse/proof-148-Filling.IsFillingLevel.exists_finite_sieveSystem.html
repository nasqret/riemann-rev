lemma Filling.IsFillingLevel.exists_finite_sieveSystem

What it saysLemma. Let E be a number field with a CM structure and Weil data, \mathfrak{s} a shape, S admissible filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s},T a finite set of finite places of E stable under conjugation, P an integral place structure on LocProd(T) (an implicit argument, also an instance), F a level with hF : IsFillingLevel(S,T,F),Sg a finite set of chosen places of S above q_0(S),d frame-vector data (for each chosen place w above q_0(S): a flag and a nonzero vector over \mathbb{F}_w), and suppose (an instance) gIdele(S)^{-1} is integral outside T. Then there is an intermediate group Z of F such that (i) toPlaces(S,T,k)\in Z for every k\in latStabilizer(S) with \operatorname{reduction}(S,k)\in fixingSet(S,Sg,d), and (ii) for every open additive subgroup L of LocProd(T) that is compact, the kernels J(j,p,q)= (floorForm(F,j,p,q,\mathtt{besselVector}(Z),1) if j\le\varrho, topKernel(L,Z,p,q)/\operatorname{vol}(L) if j=\varrho+1)/massRatio(Z) form a sieve system SieveSystem(\mathtt{unitOrOne},x\mapsto g(S)_T^{-1}\cdot x^{-1},\varrho,J,\mathtt{charOnPlaces}(S,T),(1+b(S)^{-6})/(b(S)^{6k(\mathfrak{s})})^{\#Sg},\mathtt{finHeadWeight}(n(\mathfrak{s})^{6}),\mathtt{frequencyGain}(S,T)). Unfolded, with \Lambda= finHeadWeight(n^{6}) and \beta= frequencyGain(S,T): \psi= charOnPlaces(S,T) has \psi(0)=1; for i\le\varrho and sequences p,q of units and \xi\in LocProd(T), the function c\mapsto\psi(\xi c)\cdot J(i+1,p[i\mapsto\mathtt{unitOrOne}(c)],q[i\mapsto\mathtt{unitOrOne}(c)]) is integrable, its integral is J(i,p,q) when \xi=0, and has norm \le\beta(\xi)\cdot\Lambda(i,p)\Lambda(i,q) when \xi is a unit; for every finite family of sequences with a common \varrho-th term the matrix of J(\varrho+1) is positive semidefinite; J(\varrho+1) is continuous; \lVert J(0,p,q)\rVert\le(1+b^{-6})/(b^{6k})^{\#Sg}; and for i<\varrho, with \xi=g_T^{-1}(p_i^{-1}-q_i^{-1}) a unit, \lVert J(i+1,p,q)\rVert\le\beta(\xi)\cdot\Lambda(i+1,p)\Lambda(i+1,q).
The argument in briefTake Z from exists_intermediateGroup_lines and verify the six axioms of a sieve system for the normalised kernels: \psi(0)=1; the step identities (the floor form at level i is the \psi-twisted integral of that at i+1 — integral_floorForm_succ below the top, and at the top the integral of the top kernel averaged over L, via floorForm_top_eq_integral and Fubini); positive semidefiniteness and continuity of the top kernel; the bottom bound (floorZeroForm(Z) against floorZeroForm(K_T), the inequality of Z and the main-term bound for K_T); and the frequency bounds from enorm_floorForm_le with K=n^{6}.
