lemma IsPrimitiveLabel.scaleRoots

What it saysLet k be a finite field, c in k[X], a in k^x, r and M natural numbers, and assume a^M = 1 and M^2 | |k| - 1. If \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c), then \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c.scaleRoots a), where c.scaleRoots a = \sum_i c_i a^{\deg c - i} X^i is the polynomial whose roots are a times the roots of c.
The argument in briefTake the root \xi of c that is a primitive (q^r - 1)-th root of unity; a \xi is a root of c.scaleRoots a, which is a label (scaling preserves labels) and irreducible (image under the multiplicative bijection \operatorname{scaleRootsEquiv}(a)); its order equals that of \xi because every prime p dividing \operatorname{ord}(a) has p \operatorname{ord}(a) | \operatorname{ord}(a)^2 | M^2 | q - 1 | q^r - 1.
