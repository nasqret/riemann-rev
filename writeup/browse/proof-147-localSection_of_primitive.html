lemma localSection_of_primitive

What it saysLet F be a semilocal field, b, n finite index types, \chi a multiplicative character of F and y an n \times b matrix over F with a left inverse (hy). There is g \in \mathrm{GL}_b(F) such that y\cdot g is primitive and, for every function \Psi that vanishes off the primitive matrices, localSection(\chi, y)(\Psi) = \chi(\det g)\cdot \Psi(y\cdot g).
The argument in briefTake the unique representative r_q with y\cdot r_q primitive; the defining sum has only that term.
