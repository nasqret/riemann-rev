lemma TwistingData.exists_depth_factorization

What it saysLet N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, O a valuation domain (a commutative integral domain whose ideals are totally ordered), T : TwistingData N W O (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to O, w \in O with w^{2} = p), and write Fam for the families \mathbb{Z}/N \to Monomials (Fin k) n, \mathfrak{m} for the maximal ideal of O, \kappa_{O} = O/\mathfrak{m} and T mod \mathfrak{m} = map (residue O) T, O of characteristic 0, k, n, c \in \mathbb{N}, a, r \in Fin k with a < r (ha), x \in \mathbb{N} with depth T k n r \beta \le x for every family \beta (hm), X, E finite, s : E \to X and g : E \to \mathrm{GL}_k(W) such that e \mapsto(s(e), g(e)^{-1}\cdot \mathcal{P}) is surjective onto X \times \mathrm{GL}_k(W)/\mathcal{P}, \mathcal{P} = parahoric W (twoBlock k r) (hs). Then there are matrices Q, d and R over O — Q with rows E \times Fam and columns X \times Fam, d square with rows and columns X \times Fam (only d is square: E and X may differ), and R with rows X \times Fam and columns E \times Fam (the type of Q^{\mathsf{T}}) — with: (i) Q\cdot d = J, where J_{(e,\alpha),(x,\beta)} = [s(e) = x]\cdot(\operatorname{diag}(w^{\text{depth} T k n r \cdot})\cdot \rho(g(e)))_{\alpha \beta}, \rho = letterRep T n; (ii) \det d divides w^{\sum_{\tau <x} \dim_{\kappa_{O}}(X \to \text{deepForms}(T \text{mod} \mathfrak{m}) k n r \tau)}; (iii) R\cdot Q = 1; and (iv) for every vector j, every e \in E and every real h \ge 2c, the block (Q\cdot j)(e, \cdot) lies in excessCond T n a r (w^{c}\cdot O) h. (The text reading also lists the section variables Q, R, \beta, which the statement binds itself.)
The argument in briefHermite form J = U_{\iota}\cdot D and Smith form D = K\cdot \operatorname{diag}(t)\cdot P over O. Q = U_{\iota} (not square), d = D (square) and R = rows \iota of U^{-1} give (i) and (iii). For (ii), each t_i divides w^{x}; put m_i = \#\{\tau < x : t_i \nmid w^{\tau}\}, the number of initial failures (divisibility by w^{\tau} only improves as \tau grows), so that t_i divides w^{m_i}. m_i is not the valuation of t_i in general (w = \pi^{2} and t_i = \pi in a discrete valuation ring give m_i = 1, while the valuation normalised by v(w) = 1 is 1/2). By double counting \sum_{i} m_i = \sum_{\tau <x} \#\{i : t_i \nmid w^{\tau}\}, and the columns i of P^{-1} with t_i \nmid w^{\tau} reduce to deep forms of level \tau (card_le_finrank_translatesIn). For (iv), J sends P^{-1}\cdot \operatorname{diag}(t')\cdot K^{-1}\cdot j to w^{x} times the blocks of Q\cdot j. With \omega = deepIndicator - \text{excessWeight}_i/2, choose for every family \gamma an order l(\gamma) such that w^{l(\gamma)} divides every translated coefficient at \gamma and l(\gamma) \le \lceil x - \omega \cdot \gamma \rceil_{+}, while some translated coefficient at \gamma is not divisible by w^{l(\gamma)+1} or \lceil x - \omega \cdot \gamma \rceil_{+} < l(\gamma) + 1; a \gamma minimising l(\gamma) + \omega \cdot \gamma has x \le l(\gamma) + \omega \cdot \gamma. In the first case x \le l(\gamma) + depth(\gamma) (by \mathit{hf}; equality allowed) and the root lemma, applied at the constant Levi labelling 0 and the adic order l(\gamma), gives \text{excess}_i(\gamma) \le 0, so \omega \cdot \gamma \ge depth(\gamma); in the second case directly.
