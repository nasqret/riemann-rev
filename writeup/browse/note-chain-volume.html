lemmanormDet_sq_eq_pow_mul_det

Lemma 3.1 of the note · unit 143 · New/Rows/RowVolume.lean, lines 15–19

Let U and V be complex inner product spaces, both finite-dimensional, u \colon U \to V a \mathbb{C}-linear map, S an endomorphism of U and a \in \mathbb{C} with \langle u x, u y\rangle = a\cdot \langle x, S y\rangle for all x, y \in U (h). Then \operatorname{normDet}(u)^2 = a^{\dim U}\cdot \det S, where \operatorname{normDet}(u) is the volume factor of a linear map u between complex inner product spaces (Mathlib's LinearMap.normDet), whose square is \det(u\cdot u).

defData.liftVolume

For parameters P, admissible data D over P, an m-fold twist \vartheta (D.MFoldTwist: a twist whose tame part has \psi_w^n \ne 1 at every place w of its modulus for 1 \le n \le m) and a lift index j: D.liftVolume \vartheta j = \operatorname{normDet}(D.\operatorname{goodLift} (\vartheta^j) j)^2, as a complex number: the volume \det(u\cdot u) of u = \theta_j^* for the twist \vartheta^j restricted to the good forms on U(W_j) (\operatorname{normDet}(u) is the volume factor of a linear map u between complex inner product spaces (Mathlib's LinearMap.normDet), whose square is \det(u\cdot u)).

lemmaData.volume_iota_eq_prod_liftVolume

Theorem A of the note · unit 143 · New/Rows/RowVolume.lean, lines 37–58

For parameters P and admissible data D over P there is a function c on m-fold twists such that c costs m\cdot m squares (D.CostsSquares (m\cdot m) c: for every family \theta of fit twists, |c(\theta(\iota))/c(1)|^{-2} has height at most 2m^2\cdot t\cdot x^{999}) and, for every m-fold twist \vartheta: D.𝔷 \vartheta \ne 0 and D.𝔷 \vartheta = |c(\vartheta)|^{\operatorname{rowDim}}\cdot \prod_{j=1}^{m} D.liftVolume \vartheta j, where rowDim = D.rowDim is the complex dimension of the rows.

lemmaData.range_tupleIdem_le

Lemma 3.2 of the note · unit 143 · New/Rows/RowVolume.lean, lines 62–65

Let U be a complex inner product space, P parameters, D admissible data, f a \mathbb{C}-algebra homomorphism from the group algebra (of \mathsf{G}) to \operatorname{End}_\mathbb{C}(U) and c a good tuple of labels (h: c \in D.goodTupleFamilies). Then the range of f(\operatorname{tupleIdem} c) is contained in the range of f(D.e_0).

lemmaData.groupAlgAction_auxLift

For parameters P, admissible data D, a tame character \vartheta (Data.TameChar, not necessarily a twist), a lift index j, an element x of the group algebra and a form y: D.auxLift \vartheta j ((D.principalRep _).\operatorname{asAlgebraHom} x y) = (D.auxPair \vartheta j).\operatorname{groupAlgAction} x (D.auxLift \vartheta j y): \theta_j^* intertwines the action of the group algebra on forms of principal level with its action on forms on V_j.

lemmaData.range_goodLift_le

Lemma 3.4 of the note · unit 143 · New/Rows/RowVolume.lean, lines 75–82

For parameters P, admissible data D, a tame character \vartheta (Data.TameChar, not necessarily a twist), a lift index j and a subspace N: if N contains \theta_j^* y (D.auxLift \vartheta j y) for every Hecke eigensystem p, every good form y in the p-eigenpart and every good tuple c such that \theta_j^* y lies in the range of the action of tupleIdem c (h), then the range of D.goodLift \vartheta j is contained in N.
