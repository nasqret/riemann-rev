lemma AuxPair.adjoint_theta_eq_formLift

What it saysLet E be a field (Field E) which is a CM number field with a CM type (CMType E) and carries Weil data (WeilData E); let r, n be finite index types (Fintype r, Fintype n), r nonempty (Nonempty r), and O : AuxPair E r n an auxiliary pair, with W = W(O) its space of rank |r| and V = I_n the definite space. Let l be a parameter on W that fits O (h : FitsParams(O, l)), let y be a form on W of the type that the statement infers, and let z be an algebraic form on V of the level, character and weight of O (an element of FormV(O)) such that (a) for every \Xi in FormV(O) the inner product \langle z, \Xi \rangle equals the Petersson product petersson(W, y, \theta(O)(\Xi)), \theta(O) the class-sum lift from V to W (AuxPair.θ). Then the function underlying z equals toAlgForm(I_n, isHCParam_paramV(O), formLift(pairThetaKernel(O), typeKernel(l, \cdot), \cdot, fockDatum(O, highestVector(\kappa(O), paramV(O))))(y)): the algebraic form, for the parameter paramV(O), of the theta lift of y to I_n by the datum v_{P_{\xi}} \otimes \Upsilon at the highest vector \xi of the weight of O.
The argument in briefThe datum of O is an edge, hence an arrow both ways; z and the algebraic form of the lift have the same inner product with every \Xi, by the hypothesis, the expression of \theta as a conjugated descent and the adjointness of lift and descent.

    Since l fits O, the real datum of O is an edge, so the finite datum \Upsilon of O gives an arrow A from the type of l on W to the type of V and, for the swapped pair, an arrow B back (AuxPair.FitsParams.isEdge, IsEdge.isArrow). The algebraic form of the lift of y is a member of FormV(O) (its values transform by the level of O, IsArrow.formRep_formLift, AuxPair.pairWeil_fockDatum). For every \Xi: \langle z, \Xi \rangle = petersson(W, y, \theta(O)(\Xi)) by (a); \theta(O)(\Xi) is a multiple of the conjugate of the descent of \operatorname{conj} \Xi (AuxPair.theta_eq_smul_star_lift_swap); the adjoint of the lift through A is the conjugate of the descent through B (IsArrow.inner_formLift_adjoint); and inner products of algebraic forms are multiples of Petersson products (LevelPlaces.LevelForm.inner_eq_petersson). So z and that algebraic form have equal inner products with every \Xi, hence are equal.
