lemma Data.omega_zpow_ne_one

What it saysFor parameters P and admissible data D over P (both implicit), an integer n with h : n \ne 0 and |n| \le 8\cdot m(D) (by default found by omega), and a finite place w of E(D): (\omega (D)^n)(w) \ne 1 if and only if IsChosen(D, w). Here \omega (D) is the family of residue characters (at a chosen place w of order exactly M\omega (P, s(D)) = (N_0(P)\cdot d(P))^{s(D)+6}, trivial elsewhere) and the power is pointwise.
The argument in briefAt a place w that is not chosen \omega (D)(w) = 1 and both sides fail; at a chosen place \omega (D)(w) has order M\omega (P, s(D)), so \omega (D)(w)^n = 1 iff M\omega (P, s(D)) \mid n, impossible for 0 < |n| \le 8\cdot m(D) < N_1(D)\cdot d(P) \le M\omega (P, s(D)).
