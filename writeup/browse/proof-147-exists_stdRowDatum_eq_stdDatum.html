lemma IntegralFrame.exists_stdRowDatum_eq_stdDatum

What it saysLet F be a semilocal field (instance V) with compact open subring O, b, m, n finite index types, \chi a tame multiplicative character of F (instance h\chi), A : IntegralFrame F n m b an integral frame and \sigma : b \sqcup (b \sqcup m) \simeq n. There is c \in \mathbb{C} with |c| = 1 such that stdRowDatum(\sigma, \chi, L') = stdDatum(A, y \mapsto c\cdotinvOnUnits(\chi, \det y)): for every (b \sqcup m) \times n matrix x, \chi^{-1} of the determinant of the square matrix with rows those of L' and x, ordered by \sigma, when that matrix lies in \mathrm{GL}_n(O) (and 0 otherwise), equals c\cdot \chi^{-1}(\det(x\cdot(K | I))) when x is integral and \det(x\cdot(K | I)) is a unit of O (and 0 otherwise).
The argument in briefMultiply the stacked matrix (L'; x) by the integral invertible (L | K | I): the product is block triangular with determinant \det(x\cdot(K | I)), so the two standard data differ by the constant \chi^{-1} of a unit determinant.
