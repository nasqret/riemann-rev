lemmaData.isEigenform_of_hasHeckePolyAt

Lemma 4.1 of the note · unit 139 · New/GaloisRep/LabelUniqueness.lean, lines 13–16

Let D be admissible data over P, n a finite type, M a type, f a function from G_f(E(D), n) to the functions M \to \mathbb{C}, f nonzero, and Q a family of complex polynomials indexed by the finite places of E(D). Assume that at every finite place w split off the level (IsSplitOffLevel(D, w): w split, neither w nor its conjugate a chosen place) f has Hecke polynomial Q(w) at w (HasHeckePolyAt: the maximal compact subgroup at w fixes f, each Hecke operator T_{w,j} acts on f by a scalar t_j, and Q(w) = \sum_j (-1)^j q_w^{j(j-1)/2}\, t_j\, X^{|n|-j}). Then IsEigenform(f, Q): f is nonzero, and for all but finitely many finite places w of prime norm, w is split and f has Hecke polynomial Q(w) at w.

lemmaData.occurringLabels_subsingleton

Theorem 1 of the note · unit 139 · New/GaloisRep/LabelUniqueness.lean, lines 23–59

Let D be admissible data over P, s signs on RowIndex(D), V a hermitian space over E(D) with signs s, n a nonempty finite type, v : RowIndex(D) \to n an embedding, and L a linear map from principalForms(D, V) to the algebraic forms LevelForm(S, J, chi', kappa, nu) on I_n (S a system of places, J a level, \chi' a level character, \kappa a weight and \nu a determinant exponent). Let p be an eigensystem, F a family indexed by the finite places, x a chosen place of D, m a natural number, b an assignment of blocks, \chi = (\chi_l) for l < m characters of the residue field k_x and \psi a character of k_x. Here F assigns to every finite place w of E(D) a function F(w) : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C} (one scalar F(w)(X) for every complex number X, not a list of eigenvalues). Assume: (hL) L is injective; (hF) for every y in eigenPart(D, V, p) and every finite place w split off the level, IsSphericalWith(rightRegular, w, F(w), smooth form of L(y)): the smooth form of L(y) is fixed by the part of K_0 above w and \operatorname{conj}(w), and for every complex number X the Hecke polynomial operator heckePolyOp(w, X) = \sum_{i=0}^{|n|} (-X)^i q_w^{\binom{i}{2}}\, T_{w,i} acts on it by the scalar F(w)(X); (hk) for every y in principalForms(D, V), the smooth form of L(y) has parahoric type at x (HasParahoricType) with embedding v, blocks b, characters \psi and \chi, and the function g \mapsto L(principalRep(D, V)(g at x)(y)) of g in \mathrm{GL}_{\text{RowIndex}(D)}(k_x), the general linear group of the residue field at x; (hchi) \chi is injective; (hpsi) \psi^{M_\omega} = 1; (hM) \chi_l^{M_\omega} = 1 for every l, where M_\omega = Mω(P, s(D)). Then occurringLabels(D, V, p, x) has at most one element. (No existence is asserted: a label occurs only through some y in eigenPart(D, V, p) that its idempotent does not kill, so if eigenPart(D, V, p) = 0 no label occurs.)

lemmaIsPrimitiveLabel.scaleRoots

Input 5 of the note · unit 140 · New/Trace/BadShare.lean, lines 35–41

Let k be a finite field, c in k[X], a in k^x, r and M natural numbers, and assume a^M = 1 and M^2 | |k| - 1. If \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c), then \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c.scaleRoots a), where c.scaleRoots a = \sum_i c_i a^{\deg c - i} X^i is the polynomial whose roots are a times the roots of c.

lemmaIsPrimitiveLabel.isPrimitiveRoot_prod_roots

Input 5 of the note · unit 140 · New/Trace/BadShare.lean, lines 45–51

Let k be a finite field, c in k[X] and r a natural number with \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c). Then the product of the roots of c in the algebraic closure of k, counted with multiplicity, is a primitive root of unity of order |k| - 1; and there is a natural number e such that \xi^e equals this product for every root \xi of c in the algebraic closure.
