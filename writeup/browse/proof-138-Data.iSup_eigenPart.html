lemma Data.iSup_eigenPart

What it saysFor admissible data D, signs s and a hermitian space V over E(D) with index set RowIndex(D) and signs s such that principalForms(D, V) is finite-dimensional: the sum over all systems p of the p-parts D.eigenPart V p is the whole space principalForms(D, V).
The argument in briefBy the spectral theorem for the Hecke operators at the places split off level (IsUnitary.le_span_hecke_eigenvectors) — right translation is unitary for the Petersson product, so the adjoint of T_{w,j} is T_{\overline{w},j} and the operators form a commuting family closed under adjoints (a normal family; the Hecke operators themselves need not be unitary) — the principal forms are spanned by joint eigenvectors; each lies in the p-part for its system of eigenvalues.
