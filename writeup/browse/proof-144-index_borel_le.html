lemma index_borel_le

What it saysFor a finite field K with q = Nat.card K elements and k \in \mathbb{N}: the index in \mathrm{GL}_k(K) of the subgroup of lower triangular matrices (the parabolic subgroup for the labelling b \mapsto b in the order dual: g_{\mathit{ij}} = 0 whenever i < j) is at most \Pi_{l=1}^{k}(q^{l} - 1)/(q - 1) (in \mathbb{R}), the number of full flags of K^{k}.
The argument in briefindex\cdot |B| = |\mathrm{GL}_k(K)| = \Pi q^{i}(q^{i+1} - 1), and |B| \ge(q - 1)^{k}\cdot q^{k(k-1)/2} through an injective map from tuples (diagonal unit, entries left of the diagonal) to B; dividing gives the flag count.
