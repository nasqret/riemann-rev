    Moving the factor b(\det (1, z)) across the inner product conjugates it; being a value of a unitary character it has absolute value 1, so its conjugate is b(\det (1, z))^{-1} = b(\det (1, z^{-1})). The translation by the central scalar z is unitary for the pairing of forms (smoothPairing_formTranslation applied to centralRep(z^{-1})x and y): \langle x, R(z)y\rangle = \langle R(z)R(z^{-1})x, R(z)y\rangle = \langle R(z^{-1})x, y\rangle.
