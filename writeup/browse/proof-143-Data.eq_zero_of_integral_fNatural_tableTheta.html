lemma Data.eq_zero_of_integral_fNatural_tableTheta

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D, a lift index j and a good form F on U(W_j): if \int_{[U(W_j)]} \operatorname{conj}(F(g)\cdot F^{\natural}_i(g))\cdot \operatorname{tableTheta} j \zeta g dg = 0 for every choice i and every \zeta (h), then F = 0.
The argument in briefIf F \ne 0, take a point g where |F| is near its maximum (exists_near_max: away from the cusps, first minimum bounded below); there the F^{\natural} combinations approximate K_0 (exists_fNatural_approx) and the reproducing formula writes the needed kernels through tableTheta; the hypothesis makes the integrals vanish, and isolation gives |K_0(1)| \le 4\varepsilon B^{7\nu } + \varepsilon, a contradiction.
