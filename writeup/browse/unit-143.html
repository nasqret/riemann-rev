The rows: target forms on an indefinite U(r), the volume of the chain of lifts as a product of lift volumes, and the height of each lift volume

note and structured proofs · 219 declarations · 85 structured proofs · 4 notes

The rows are the good forms on the indefinite space Y_0 (target forms on U(r)). The volume \mathfrak{z}(\vartheta) = \det(\Theta^*\Theta) of the chain \Theta_\vartheta of the m theta lifts restricted to the rows equals |c(\vartheta)|^{\operatorname{rowDim}} times the product of the volumes \lambda_j(\vartheta^j) of the single lifts, with |c(\theta(\iota))/c(1)|^{-2} of controlled height (Data.volume_iota_eq_prod_liftVolume); and for a fit family of twists each ratio \lambda_j(1)/\lambda_j(\theta(\iota)^j) has height at most \operatorname{rowDim} \cdot k \cdot (B_0 \cdot \log b - \mathsf{U}) (Data.heightLe_liftVolume_ratio): the rows repay the cost of the idle directions. The second bound rests on integral comparison forms built from auxiliary dual pairs, glued lattices and the carrier (an explicit theta lift of 1), whose descents see the twist only through one number made of Hecke L-values (exists_comparison_forms), and on height bounds for those Hecke value ratios.
