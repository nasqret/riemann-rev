lemma IsSplitLevel.pairWeilFinite_name

What it saysFor a field E with a CM type and Weil data, a split level P for the prime N (instance h), g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\prod_{w\in P} E_w), k \in \mathrm{GL}_{n'}(\prod E_w), a function f on n' \times n matrices, characters a, b of U(1)(\mathbb{A}) and a finite datum v: the pair Weil operator with characters a, b of (embedGL g, embedGL k) sends name(liftFn f)(v) to (detCharAt(b.spaceChar(|n'|), |n'|)(g)^{-1}\cdotdetCharAt(a.spaceChar(|n|), |n|)(k))\cdot\mathrm{name}(S \mapsto \widetilde{f}(k^{-1}\cdot S\cdot g))(v), the factors in this order, where \widetilde{f} = liftFn(f) is the local lift of f (\widetilde{f}(S) = f(S mod N) for integral S over \prod_{w\in P} E_w and 0 otherwise). The change of variables S \mapsto k^{-1}\cdot S\cdot g acts on the lifted function \widetilde{f}, not inside the residue function f: g and k are general elements of the local general linear groups and can move a matrix into or out of the integral locus, so S \mapsto \widetilde{f}(k^{-1}\cdot S\cdot g) is in general not liftFn(S \mapsto f(k^{-1}\cdot S\cdot g)).
The argument in briefRewrite g, k through atPlaces \circ embedGL and apply the action of pair Weil operators on names.
