lemma Data.crude_rows

What it saysCrude rows: integral twisted lifts and bounded conjugates. For parameters P and admissible data D there is a function c of the m-fold twists to \mathbb{C}^{\times} costing 2m^{2} squares (Data.CostsSquares D (2m²) c) such that the forms y of level K(g) on Y_{0} satisfying (1) for every m-fold twist \vartheta, prime l (Fact l.Prime), l-adic place \iota (an embedding of \mathbb{C} into \mathbb{C}_{l}, PadicPlace l) and l coprime to the norm of the modulus of \vartheta: (c(\vartheta)/c(1))\cdotiotaAtLevel(D, \vartheta)(y) lies in the l-integral forms (LevelPlaces.LevelForm.integralForms); and (2) for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C} and point g: weylNormSq(\sigma \circ(\iota(1)(y))(g)) \le \exp(x_{1}^{2}\cdot b^{2}), span the whole space (the span is \top).
The argument in briefThe period of the chain of lifts of r(\varphi) against delta forms is \alpha \cdot(grid period) (Data.exists_isTidy_integral_eq_gridPeriod), which climbs to the end period with cost 2m^{2} (Data.gridPeriod_climb, Data.gridPeriod_eq_endPeriod); the end period is integral after N (Data.exists_mul_endPeriod_mem_embValRing) and bounded (Data.endPeriod_le); a dual pairing of the weights turns the period into the coordinates of the lift. The images r(\varphi) of short algebraic forms span (span_isShort_eq_top), so suitable multiples of them are the crude rows.
