lemma Data.exists_isLevelTidy_iotaPlus_eq

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data, \vartheta an m-fold twist and b \le m (hb). There is \Phi \in GridDatum(\vartheta), level-tidy of order 2(m - b) on legEdges(false, b) \cup legEdges(true, b) (Data.IsLevelTidy), such that for every bilinear form B and forms \Xi, \varphi at the nodes (0, m) of the grids V and Z: B(iotaPlus(\vartheta, false, b)(\Xi), iotaPlus(1, true, b)(\varphi)) =(\prod_{b \le i < m} triangleOrder(false, \vartheta, i)\cdottriangleOrder(true, \vartheta, i))\cdot \Phi(S \mapsto B(legDescents(S, false, b)(\Xi), legDescents(S, true, b)(\varphi))).
The argument in briefDecreasing induction on b from m: at each step bind \Phi with the inclusion data of the two new edges (point descents are sums of descents with indicator data over the matrices reducing to the inclusion), which keeps level-tidiness with one more edge per grid.
