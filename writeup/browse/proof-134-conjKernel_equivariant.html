lemma AuxPair.conjKernel_equivariant

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data and O an auxiliary pair over E (W of rank r, V = I_n). For \gamma in U(I_n)(E), k in levelGroup(O), a coordinate vector v of weight (\kappa(O), \operatorname{detExp}(O)), g in the adelic group of W and x in G_f of V: conjKernel(O, g, gamma x k)(levelChar(O)(k) weightRep(stdSpace(E, n), kappa(O), detExp(O))(gamma)(v)) = conjKernel(O, g, x)(v), where \gamma acts on G_f through stdEmbFin.
The argument in briefconjKernel is the conjugate of a theta value; moving k through the datum costs levelChar(k)^{-1} = \operatorname{conj}(levelChar(k)) (pairWeil_fockDatum), and the rational \gamma is absorbed by the invariance of theta under rational points.
