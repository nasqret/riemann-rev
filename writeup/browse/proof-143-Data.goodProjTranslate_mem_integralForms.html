lemma Data.goodProjTranslate_mem_integralForms

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D, a prime l with an embedding j \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_l (PadicPlace l), a twist \vartheta, a \in \mathbb{C}, a form z on Y_0 (principal forms) with a\cdot \iota_\vartheta(z) integral at j (h: in LevelForm.integralForms j, i.e. the Weyl gauge of each value is at most 1; \iota_\vartheta = iotaAtLevel \vartheta), and s \in \mathsf{G}: a\cdot \iota_\vartheta(D.\operatorname{goodProjTranslate} s z) is integral at j.
The argument in briefThe value is a sum of translates of a\cdot \iota(z) with coefficients |\mathsf{G}|e_0(t), which are integral; the gauge of a sum is at most the maximum.
