lemma Data.finrank_rows_le_mass

What it saysLemma. For parameters P and admissible data D for P: \dim_{\mathbb{C}}\operatorname{rows}(D)\le\#\mathsf{G}_D\cdot besselDegree(\operatorname{filling}(D))\cdot\operatorname{mass}(E(D),r(D)). Here \operatorname{rows}(D)= goodForms(D,Y_0(D)) is the image of the good idempotent e_0(D) on the forms of principal level on Y_0(D); \mathsf{G}_D=\prod_x\operatorname{GL}_{\mathtt{RowIndex}(D)}(\mathbb{F}_{\operatorname{chosen}(D,x)}) is the finite group of the chosen places above g_0 and g_1; besselDegree(\operatorname{filling}(D))=\prod_v\deg(\mathtt{besselSys}(\operatorname{filling}(D),v)) is the product over the infinite places of the degrees of the Bessel systems of the filling data of D; and \operatorname{mass}(E(D),r(D))= classSum(\mathtt{stdSpace}(E(D), Fin r(D)).\Gamma f,K_f,1) is the mass of the genus of the standard hermitian lattice of \operatorname{rank}r(D).
The argument in briefdim \operatorname{rows}(D)=\dim goodForms(D,W_j(D))\le\dim of all forms of that type and level; the forms are algebraic forms (algFormEquiv), whose level space has dimension #classes\cdot\dim(rep) (trivial class automorphisms); #classes = classSum(K_{\mathrm{level}},1)=[K_f:K_{\mathrm{level}}]\cdot\operatorname{mass}(E,r) and [K_f:K_{\mathrm{level}}]=\#\mathsf{G}_D; \dim(rep) =\prod_v formal degree =\prod_v\deg(Bessel system) (Harish-Chandra).
