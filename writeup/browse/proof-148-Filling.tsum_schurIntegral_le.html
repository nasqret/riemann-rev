lemma Filling.tsum_schurIntegral_le

What it saysLemma (Schur test on a thin class). Let E be stably unramified and S admissible; let j\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\}, classes c,c',s\in\mathsf{G} and \gamma\in G(E) with \gamma\in termsOver(S,c,s,c') and HasLevel(S,j,\gamma). Then \Sigma_{\delta\in\mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g)\gamma\mathsf{U}_c'^{0}(g)} schurIntegral(S,c,c',\delta)\le\nu\cdot \#BesselCoord(S)\cdot\tau^{2}\cdot(b^{4}g)^{-\min(\varrho,j)\cdot k\cdot f}\cdot(i/\sqrt{D})^{k+1}, where i= xRowsIndex(S,j,\mathtt{termPosition}(S,c,c',\gamma)) (the index of the \hat{O}-span of the conjugated first \min(j,\varrho) rows of Z(h_c^{-1}\gamma h_c') in that of its first \varrho rows), D= minorNorm(S,j,\gamma) (|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(D_j(Z(\gamma)))|, divided by N^{2f} when j=\varrho+1), \tau= tailScale(S)=b^{\operatorname{kf}/4},\nu= unipCovolume(S),g=q_0q_1,k=|\kappa| and f the number of infinite places.
The argument in briefAverage the Schur integrals over unipotent translates s(x)= unipotentOf(\iota(\mathtt{x\_vR\_v})^{H},0),x in a unit ball (lintegral_boxCutoff_le_of_average); colour the terms of the class by their middle rows modulo g\cdot(row lattice of Z) (index i^{k+1} colours); in one colour class the frames differ by g\cdot l\cdot\xi with the same l on the support, so the translates by L(l)=(b/\pi)l are disjoint and the frame-volume ratios are dominated by the volume kernel K_v (exists_kernel_frameVolume_le); the frame count bounds the overlap by \tau, and the integral of K_v gives the factor (b^{4}g)^{-\min(\varrho,j)\operatorname{kf}}/\sqrt{D^{k+1}}.
