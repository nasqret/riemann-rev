lemma IsLabelledColumn.isRow

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, an auxiliary pair O, labels c apt for a twist \vartheta, an \ell-adic representation R : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \operatorname{GL}_r(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell ) of dimension |r|, k \in \mathbb{N} and jumps J: if R is a labelled column for O with the twisted labels of c and empty extra level (IsLabelledColumn O k J (twistedLabel O c) \varnothing R) and the jumps satisfy IsRowType D |r| k J, then R is a row: IsRow D k J R.
The argument in briefIsColumn and IsRowType are given. With a prime g_l \ne \ell and the chosen place w_i over g_l carrying a primitive label (or X - 1 in rank one): a tame element s has eigenvalues \iota \ell T(\xi ^{q^a}) that stay residually distinct, so every lattice spans all matrices and a power of Frobenius acts by a scalar; s^e acts by a scalar of large order. At the places above g the inertia acts with eigenvalues that are roots of unity of orders dividing g_i^d - 1, whose \ell-adic distances to 1 are at most \operatorname{cr}_\ell.
