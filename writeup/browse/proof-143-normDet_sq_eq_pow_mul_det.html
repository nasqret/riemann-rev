lemma normDet_sq_eq_pow_mul_det

What it saysLet U and V be complex inner product spaces, both finite-dimensional, u \colon U \to V a \mathbb{C}-linear map, S an endomorphism of U and a \in \mathbb{C} with \langle u x, u y\rangle = a\cdot \langle x, S y\rangle for all x, y \in U (h). Then \operatorname{normDet}(u)^2 = a^{\dim U}\cdot \det S, where \operatorname{normDet}(u) is the volume factor of a linear map u between complex inner product spaces (Mathlib's LinearMap.normDet), whose square is \det(u\cdot u).
The argument in briefu\cdot u = a\cdot S, so \det(u\cdot u) = a^{\dim U}\cdot \det S.
