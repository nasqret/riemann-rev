lemma bad_share_le

What it saysLet n and \iota be finite types, k = (k_w), w in \iota, a family of finite fields, q, M, r, f natural numbers with M nonzero, and \varepsilon, L real numbers. Assume |k_w| = q for every w, |n| = r, |\iota | = f, 1 < r, r < q, \varepsilon \le 1, (1 + M + 2r)/q \le \varepsilon and 2r(2r \log q + 1)L \le f. Let e be the sum, over the families c in \operatorname{goodTuples}(M, r), of \operatorname{tupleIdem}(n, c) in the group algebra of \prod_w \mathrm{GL}_n(k_w). Then the real part of the coefficient of 1 - e at the identity is at most f \varepsilon + \exp(-L).
The argument in briefWith m = (1 - r/q)/q^r, every good tuple contributes at least m^f to the real part of the coefficient of e at 1 (labelChar_degree_bounds factorwise). Good tuples include all families in \prod_w A_w that are not in \prod_w B_w, where A_w are the monic polynomials of degree r with no root in {0} or \mu_M and no square factor, and B_w the non-primitive ones. Counting: |A_w| m \ge 1 - \varepsilon (at most (1 + M) q^{r-1} + r q^{r-1} excluded polynomials), and |B_w| m \le 1 - L/f (the minimal polynomials of the \phi(q^r - 1) primitive roots give at least \phi(q^r - 1)/r primitive labels, and n \le \phi(n)(2 \log n + 1)). Then \operatorname{Re} e(1) \ge(1 - \varepsilon)^f - (1 - L/f)^f \ge 1 - f \varepsilon - \exp(-L).
