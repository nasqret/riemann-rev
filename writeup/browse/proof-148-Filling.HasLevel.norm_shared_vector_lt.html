lemma Filling.HasLevel.norm_shared_vector_lt

What it saysLemma (shared vectors are short). Let S be admissible, j\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\},\gamma\in G(E) near and of level j (IsNear(S,\gamma)\wedge HasLevel(S,j,\gamma)), u\in G(E) with u\in\mathsf{U}^{0}, an index b\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho\} with j\le b, and Y= paddedLeading(j,Z(\gamma)) (the matrix whose rows a<j are those of Z(\gamma)= frameProducts(S,\gamma) and whose other rows are those of the identity). Then for every infinite place v and every a\in\{1,\ldots,\varrho\}: 2\cdot|\Phi_v(((u\gamma)f\cdot\det(Y)\cdot(Y^{-1})_{\cdot,b})_{e_a})|<q_0^{(\varrho+1)^{4}}, i.e. the e_a-coordinate of the vector u\gamma f\cdot(\det Y\cdot column b of Y^{-1}) is small, q_0= levelPrime(S,0).
The argument in briefCramer's rule: the e_a-coordinate of u\gamma f\cdot(\det Y\cdot Y_{\cdot,b}^{-1}) is the determinant of a block matrix built from the frame data of the boxed points y,\gamma^{-1}y (bounded by b^{5n^{2}} entrywise) and the rows of Y; Hadamard's bound and q_0\ge b^{10} give the estimate.
