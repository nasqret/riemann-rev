lemma IsPartner.mem_matchingPairs

What it saysLet a, b, n be finite index types, F a semilocal field with compact open subring O, \chi a multiplicative character of F which is tame (h\chi), e : b \sqcup a \simeq n, and \Phi, \Psi complex functions (on a \times n and n \times b matrices) which are partners for \chi over O with respect to e (h : IsPartner(O, e, \chi, \Phi, \Psi): \Phi vanishes off the matrices invLastRows(e, k), \Psi off firstCols(e, k), k \in \mathrm{GL}_n(O), and \Phi(invLastRows(e, k)) = \chi(\det k)\cdot \Psi(firstCols(e, k))), with \Psi locally constant (h\Psi). Then (\Phi, \Psi) lies in matchingPairs(\chi, e).
The argument in briefBoth functions are Schwartz–Bruhat with primitive supports; at each k \in \mathrm{GL}_n(F) the two local sections reduce to single values (localSection_of_primitive), which agree by the partner identity at an element of \mathrm{GL}_n(O).

    \Phi is Schwartz–Bruhat on surjective matrices: the set where \Phi takes a given non-zero value is clopen, being the image under k \mapsto (invLastRows(e, k))^{\mathsf{T}} of an open and closed subset of the compact group \mathrm{GL}_n(O) (on it \Phi(x_k) = \chi(\det k)\cdot \Psi(y_k) is locally constant in k: \Psi is, and \chi is on units, TameLocalChar.isLocallyConstant_invOnUnits), and the supports consist of primitive matrices (?l), so schwartzBruhat.of_support_primitive applies after transposing; \Psi likewise (?r). The matching identity at k \in \mathrm{GL}_n(F): x_k^{\mathsf{T}} has a left inverse and y_k = firstCols(e, k) too, so both local sections are single values \chi(\det g)\cdot(\Phi \circtranspose)(x_k^{\mathsf{T}} g) and \chi(\det u)\cdot \Psi(y_k u) (localSection_of_primitive); these agree by the partner identity at some j \in \mathrm{GL}_n(O) with firstCols(e, j) = y_k\cdot u (exists_firstCols_eq_mul), whose invLastRows equals that of an element i \in \mathrm{GL}_n(O) (the factorization through b\cdot u, IsPrimitiveMatrix.mul_iff).
