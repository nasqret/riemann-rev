lemma Gauge.hasFibreBound_floorQuadric_of_le

What it saysLet A be a place (class Place: a locally compact Polish commutative ring with involution and Haar measure \,\mathrm{d}x, with an element \tau with \tau + \tau^{*} = 1, a skew unit \delta, a Haar measure \nu_{\mathrm{skew}} on the skew elements, a multiplicative modulus \operatorname{md} : A \to [ 0 , \infty ] such that v \mapsto Mv carries \,\mathrm{d}x to \operatorname{md} ( \det M )^{- 1} \,\mathrm{d}x whenever \det M is a unit, a unitary additive character \psi and a Plancherel constant c); the instances CommRing A, StarRing A, TopologicalSpace A, MeasureSpace A and P : Place A are arguments. Let \mathfrak{s} be a shape, T an \mathfrak{s}-form over A whose Gram matrix J_{T} = splitGram( T ) of V is hermitian and invertible (IsHermUnit), \iota a finite type and m \in \mathbb{N}; G_{T} = U ( J_{T} ), H_{l} = GT ( l ) (fixing e_{a} , e_{a}^{*} for a < l), Q_{l} = unitriGroup( l ), \mathcal{N}_{< l} = unipotentBelow( l ), f_{b} = besselFrame( b ) the frame (f_{b} = e_{b} for b < \varrho, f_{\varrho} = z). Let F be a level (Level \iota T m: measures F.θ, F.μH, unit g_{F}, ball \mathfrak{B}_{F}, box Bx_{F}, positions Cm_{F}) and \Gamma a gauge for F (sets B_{j}^{\Gamma} = \Gamma .ofSize( j ), constant \Lambda_{\Gamma}); E_{b}^{F} = floorBasis( F , b ) is the basis matrix of V_{b} ( b \le \varrho ) made of F's basis of V_{\varrho} and the pairs ( e_{a} , e_{a}^{*} ) , a \ge b. If the quadric measure of the hyperbolic Gram matrix on FloorPairs( b , m ) (with empty middle block) at 0 gives vecsOfSize( \Gamma , j + 5 ) mass at most M (hypothesis e), then HasFibreBound( \Gamma , b, floorQuadric( F , b ) , j , M ).
The argument in briefThe quadric measure is the flat-chart image of a product; the fibres in the skew direction are translates, and their total mass is the quadric mass of the size-( j + 5 ) vectors.
