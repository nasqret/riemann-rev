lemma stdClassSum_det_modKernel_eq

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, m a natural number with m \ne 0 (instance NeZero m) and m even (he), and c : InfinitePlace E \to \mathbb{N}. Then there is C such that for every modulus \mathfrak{m}, character \alpha and multiplicative character \chi of O_E/\mathfrak{m} with \alpha tame for (\mathfrak{m}, -c, \chi), there is \gamma with |\gamma |^2 = C / \operatorname{lValueProd}(\operatorname{places} \text{ of } \mathfrak{m}, \alpha^{-1}\cdot \upsilon^{m/2}, m/2) and, for every point g, \gamma \cdot \sum_h \theta^{I_m, I_m}_{\alpha, \alpha }[\chi \circ \det, \prod_\tau \det(x_\tau)^{c_\tau }](g, h) = 1, the sum being the class sum over U(I_m) at level K_0 (Kf E (\operatorname{Fin} m)).
The argument in briefExplicit matching with W = I_m, V' the zero space and no negative signs: the integral over the trivial group is its volume times the kernel 1, which gives the constant \gamma.
