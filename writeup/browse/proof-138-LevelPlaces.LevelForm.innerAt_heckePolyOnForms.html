lemma LevelPlaces.LevelForm.innerAt_heckePolyOnForms

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, r and n finite types, O an auxiliary pair over E with index types r \subset n, \sigma an automorphism of the field \mathbb{C}, \varphi and \varphi ' forms in O.FormV (algebraic forms on V = I_n of the weight, level and character of O), w a finite place of E with \overline{w} \ne w (hw), such that neither w (hS) nor \overline{w} (hS') lies in the set of places of O and their conjugates, and X \in \mathbb{C}. Then, for the \sigma-conjugate pairing innerAt \sigma of forms on V (LevelPlaces.LevelForm.innerAt: the class sum of the Gram pairing of the \sigma-conjugated values, conjugate-linear for \sigma ^{-1}\circ \operatorname{conj}\circ \sigma in the first variable): innerAt \sigma (O.heckePolyOnForms hw hS X \varphi) \varphi ' = innerAt \sigma \varphi (O.heckePolyOnForms hw' hS' X' \varphi '), where hw' : \operatorname{conj}(\overline{w}) \ne \overline{w} follows from hw and X' = \sigma ^{-1}(\operatorname{conj}(\sigma (X))); that is, the adjoint of the Hecke polynomial operator H_w(X) = \sum _{i\le |n|} (-X)^i q_w^{i(i-1)/2} T_{w,i} for innerAt \sigma is H_{\overline{w}}(\sigma ^{-1}(\operatorname{conj} \sigma X)).
The argument in briefThe general adjointness of Hecke polynomials for an invariant pairing, applied to innerAt \sigma.
