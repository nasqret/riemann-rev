lemma centreCoeff_eq_zero_of_not_negative

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type, with Weil data H (WeilData E; \jmath = H.j, \upsilon = H.upsilonU1 its splitting character). Let a, b, m, n be finite index types; X : Space(E, m, r), Y : Space(E, n, s), Z : Space(E, a, t) hermitian spaces with signs r, s, t, and D a splitting of X over Z along b (Space.Splitting X Z b: X =(\mathrm{lE}^{b} \oplus \mathrm{kE}^{b}) \perp iZ with isotropic frames l, k, l^{*}Jk = 1, so \mathit{rk} X = \mathit{rk} Z + 2|b|). Let \nu be a character of U(1)(\mathbb{A}) with weights \mu and tame part \psi (AutChar.HasWeight ν μ ψ), \delta a character, f a datum of the pair (X, Y), g a point of U(X)(\mathbb{A}) and T an n' = b \times b matrix over E. Assume: i is an index of Y with s(i) = 1 (Y has a positive sign); (h\gamma) \mu(\tau) < negCount(-t) for every infinite place \tau (the weights of \nu are below the number of positive signs of Z); (\mathit{ht}) |b| + negCount(t) = negCount(s) (Y has |b| more negative signs than Z); (d) \delta = \nu \cdot \upsilon^{|b|}; (h_{0}) T is invertible; (hR) T, read as an adelic matrix, is not represented by the form -1, i.e. at some infinite place \tau there is no c with -c^{*}c = T_{\tau} (T_{\tau} is not negative semidefinite); (hi) the theta terms of D for (Y, \delta, \delta, f, g) are integrable (Space.Splitting.HasIntegrableTerms D Y δ δ f g). Then the Fourier coefficient of the theta integral thetaIntegral(X, Y, \delta, \delta, f) at \jmath T along the centre of the unipotent radical of D vanishes at g: centreCoeff(D, \jmath \bullet T, thetaIntegral(X, Y, \delta, \delta, f), g) = 0.
The argument in briefThe coefficient is a sum over rational x with x^{*}J_Y x = T; if there is none it is 0. Otherwise it is an integral over the quotient by the stabilizer H of x, and averaging the integrand over a compact subgroup C of H gives 0: at a real place \tau where T is not negative semidefinite, Witt's theorem moves a vector of norm 1 to a positive basis line, and then no functional on the Fock space is invariant under the rotations of a remaining negative line (circle averages and the weight bound).
