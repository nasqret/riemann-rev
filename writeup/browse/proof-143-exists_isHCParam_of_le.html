lemma exists_isHCParam_of_le

What it saysLet \Phi and n be finite types, s \colon n \to \{\pm 1\}, \kappa \colon \Phi \to \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}, e \colon \Phi \to \mathbb{Z}, c \in \mathbb{R} and l \colon \Phi \to n \to \mathbb{Z} such that every l_w, read as a parameter for the signs s, has gap c (hc: toHCParam s (l w) has HasGap c: the entries l_w(i)/2 are distinct, in (|n| - 1)/2 + \mathbb{Z}, and differ by at least c where the signs differ), and e(w) \le l(w, i)/2 + |n|/2 for all w and i (hg; integer division). Then there is f \colon \Phi \to \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} such that IsHCParam f (w \mapsto e(w) + f(w, |n|)) l and f(w, k) = \kappa(w, k) for every w and every k > |n|.
The argument in briefFor each w build the column counts by induction over the values of l_w in increasing order, then check the HC parameter condition.
