lemma exists_corepresenting_coords

What it saysFor a number field E embedded in \Omega, a finite type n, a commutative ring \Lambda , m \ne 0, a function \rho : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to M_n(\Lambda ), a neighbourhood W of 1 in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E), a finite set S of finite places and a set L of relations: there are a finite type \iota, coordinates \gamma : \iota \to \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \times n and a \Lambda-submodule R of \Lambda ^\iota containing m\cdot \Lambda ^\iota such that for every \Lambda-module B killed by m on whose n-th power W acts trivially through \rho (\rho (g)\cdot b = b for g \in W, b \in B^n), the map c \mapsto (c(g_k)_{i_k})_{k\in \iota } (\gamma _k = (g_k, i_k)) is a bijection from the Selmer module of \rho with values in B^n (locally constant cocycles unramified outside S obeying L) onto the w \in B^\iota killed by R (\sum _k r_k\cdot w_k = 0 for every r \in R).
The argument in briefChoose an open subgroup V such that all unramified-off-S cocycles with values in a finite auxiliary module vanish on V; then Selmer cocycles with m-torsion values factor through \operatorname{Gal}/V, and are determined by their values at representatives of \operatorname{Gal}/V, subject to the evaluated relations.
