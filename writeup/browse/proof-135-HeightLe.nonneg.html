Structured proof · Unit 135

lemma HeightLe.nonneg

What it saysProduct formula. For a real number c: if the constant family 1 (equal to 1 at every (\ell, \iota)) has height at most c, that is HeightLe c 1, then 0 \le c.
The argument in briefLet H witness HeightLe c 1. H is algebraic (bounded at all 2-adic isomorphisms), so q = N_{\mathbb{Q}(H)/\mathbb{Q}}(H) is a nonzero rational. At every place, archimedean or \ell-adic, the bound on the conjugates of H bounds \log |q|/\deg from above by \alpha resp. e(\ell); summing over \ell < M and using c \ge \alpha + \sum e(\ell), the product formula for q gives c \ge 0.
