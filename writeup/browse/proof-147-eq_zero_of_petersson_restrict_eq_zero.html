lemma eq_zero_of_petersson_restrict_eq_zero

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data, n a finite non-empty type, s signs on n and V : Space(E(D), n, s); A \in \mathbb{N}, L, N \in \mathbb{N}, \beta, m \in \mathbb{R}; t signs on n \sqcup \{\ast \} with t = s \oplus 1 (e); l(w) Harish-Chandra parameters for s and l'(w) for t at the infinite places w. Assume: (hg) each l(w) has a large gap 4A and (\mathit{hg}') each l'(w) a large gap 2A; (\mathit{hl}) |l(w, i)| \le L; (hA) 8(|n| + 1)^{2} < A; (hN) N < A; (hA') 2f(|n| + 1) + 2 \le A; (\mathit{hc}) g(D)\cdotcuspConst(E, n, cycloConductor)^{2} \le \beta; (h\beta) 2000(|n| + L + 1)^{6} \le \beta; (f\beta) f \le \beta; (\mathit{hm}) \beta^{20A(f + 1)} \le m; (hR) m^{3|n| + 2} \le \lVert \Phi_{w}(gridLevelGen)\rVert for every w; and (H) for every w there is \omega in the real unitary group of s \oplus 1 with \lVert \omega \rVert^{2} \le \beta and \operatorname{vol}\{r : \lVert r - 1\rVert \le 1\} \le \beta^{N}\cdot \lVert \int_{r} dsCoeff(l(w), r)\cdot \operatorname{conj}(dsCoeff(l'(w), \omega^{-1}\cdot(r \oplus 1)\cdot \omega))\rVert (a matrix-coefficient lower bound). Then there is a real point g such that every form y on V of type l and level chosenLevel vanishes if it is orthogonal to x \mapsto \varphi((x \oplus 1)\cdot g) for every form \varphi on V \oplus \langle 1\rangle (reindexed by e) of type l' and principal level gridLevelGen.
The argument in briefTake g = \omega. If y is orthogonal to all restrictions, the Poincar\acute{e}-series periods (the kernels of \varphi) show that y is small near every point where the first minimum is large, by the Laplace-method lower bound H; near the cusp y is small by the cusp-step bound; a halving argument then gives y = 0.
