The finite test datum of an auxiliary pair and the theta lift as a conjugate descent

note and structured proofs · 16 declarations · 7 structured proofs · 1 note

For an auxiliary pair O (a space W of rank r, V = I_n, places M and Q, a character \mu): the finite datum \Upsilon = auxDatum(O) is a sum of translates of the level indicator (upsilon_eq_sum) and transforms under the level by the characters \psi and auxLevelChar (pairWeil_upsilon). The doubled Harish-Chandra parameter paramV(O, \cdot, \cdot) of the weight of O on V; FitsParams(O, l): a parameter l on W matching it. Under FitsParams the polynomial P of the highest vector is a product over the infinite places of joint harmonics with staircase lowest K-types, hence an edge between the discrete-series kernels. Finally the class-sum lift \theta(\Xi) of a form on V to W is a fixed multiple of the conjugate of the theta descent of \operatorname{conj}(F_\Xi) with datum P tensor \Upsilon.
