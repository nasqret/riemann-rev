lemma realPairWeil_fockLetters

What it saysLet E be a CM field, r and n finite types, s : r \to \{+1, -1\} signs, \operatorname{ord} : r \to \mathbb{N}, \gamma' an integer for each infinite place, y a real point of U(I_n) (a unitary matrix of rank n at each infinite place), c a sign and F a polynomial in the matrix variables. Then realPairWeil with weights (negCount(s) at every place, \gamma'), at (1, y), sends the Fock vector of fockLetters(s, ord, c, F) to the Fock vector of fockLetters(s, ord, c, rowSubst(z, F)), where z_\tau = (y_\tau)^{T} if c = 1 and z_\tau = (y_\tau)^{-1} otherwise.
The argument in briefUnfold the action of realPairWeil on Fock vectors of polynomials and the two algebra maps; the two sums agree after exchanging the order of summation.
