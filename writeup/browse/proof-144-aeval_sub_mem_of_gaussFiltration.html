lemma aeval_sub_mem_of_gaussFiltration

What it saysLet R be a commutative ring, A an R-algebra, F =(F_t) a family of additive subgroups of R indexed by \mathbb{R}, c : \sigma \to \mathbb{R} weights, t \in \mathbb{R}, \chi, \psi : R[x_{\sigma}] \to A homomorphisms of R-algebras (\psi an optional argument, by default \chi), G a decreasing multiplicative real filtration of A, and f \in R[x_{\sigma}]. Assume f \in gaussFiltration F c t (h); \chi(x_i) \in G_{c(i)} for every variable i (u); \psi(x_i) - \chi(x_i) \in G_{c(i) + 1/20} for every i (v; proved by simp by default, e.g. when \psi = \chi); and the image of every x \in F_s in A lies in G_s, for all s \in \mathbb{R} and x \in R (y; proved by default when the target is again a Gauss filtration). Then \psi(f) \in G_t and \psi(f) - \chi(f) \in G_{t + 1/20}.
The argument in briefInduction on f: constants, adding a monomial, and multiplying by a variable; the filtration is multiplicative.
