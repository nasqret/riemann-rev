Unit 143The rows: target forms on an indefinite U(r), the volume of the chain of lifts as a product of lift volumes, and the height of each lift volume

note and structured proofs · 219 declarations · 85 structured proofs

The rows are the good forms on the indefinite space Y₀ (target forms on U(r)). The volume 𝔷(ϑ) = det(Θ*Θ) of the chain Θ_ϑ of the m theta lifts restricted to the rows equals |c(ϑ)|^{rowDim} times the product of the volumes λ_j(ϑ^j) of the single lifts, with |c(θ(ι))/c(1)|^{−2} of controlled height (Data.volume_iota_eq_prod_liftVolume); and for a fit family of twists each ratio λ_j(1)/λ_j(θ(ι)^j) has height at most rowDim·k·(B₀·log b − 𝖴) (Data.heightLe_liftVolume_ratio): the rows repay the cost of the idle directions. The second bound rests on integral comparison forms built from auxiliary dual pairs, glued lattices and the carrier (an explicit theta lift of 1), whose descents see the twist only through one number made of Hecke L-values (exists_comparison_forms), and on height bounds for those Hecke value ratios.

Unit 147The exchange identity: theta integrals of complementary rank agree up to a constant, and cruder forms complete a family to a basis in the lower bound

note and structured proofs · 303 declarations · 122 structured proofs

Unit 147 (New/Exchange) proves the exchange theorem: for hermitian spaces V, V′ of complementary rank inside W, the theta integrals over U(V) and U(V′) of matched ramified data agree up to a non-zero constant whose squared absolute value is a fixed number times residue counts and products of partial Hecke L-values (exchange_theorem); it uses it for one square of lifts and transposed lifts, for the grid of such squares, and for the cruder forms of Data.crude_rows, which complete a family of target forms to a basis with integral twisted lifts and bounded conjugates in the lower bound (Theorem C). Chunk a (this dossier) holds the analytic and local foundations: the Rallis inner product of the adjoint lift θ_j^* and the L-value of the lift of the constant from U(1) (CharacterLiftNorm); local partner data and matched pairs (BlockPartners, PartnersMatch, StableSpaces); the vanishing of theta coefficients at forms that are not negative definite (NonnegativeVanishing); the equivariant functional of the difference of the two theta integrals and its vanishing on standard data (ExchangeDifference, StandardCoefficients); and the exchange identity at the top of the tower, from equal-rank constancy, matched real data and determinant vectors (LowRankIdentity, RealPair, DeterminantVectors).

Unit 134The finite test datum of an auxiliary pair and the theta lift as a conjugate descent

note and structured proofs · 16 declarations · 7 structured proofs

For an auxiliary pair O (a space W of rank r, V = I_n, places M and Q, a character mu): the finite datum Upsilon = auxDatum(O) is a sum of translates of the level indicator (upsilon_eq_sum) and transforms under the level by the characters psi and auxLevelChar (pairWeil_upsilon). The doubled Harish-Chandra parameter paramV(O, ., .) of the weight of O on V; FitsParams(O, l): a parameter l on W matching it. Under FitsParams the polynomial P of the highest vector is a product over the infinite places of joint harmonics with staircase lowest K-types, hence an edge between the discrete-series kernels. Finally the class-sum lift theta(Xi) of a form on V to W is a fixed multiple of the conjugate of the theta descent of conj(F_Xi) with datum P tensor Upsilon.

Unit 146Vanishing of theta descents: reflection sums at the level, and climbing the theta tower

Unit 142Degree-uniform decay of Heisenberg matrix coefficients of homogeneous Fock vectors

note and structured proofs · 1 declaration · 1 structured proof

One explicit estimate in the Fock model of the Weil representation: for a homogeneous polynomial P of degree d in |iota| variables, v = v_P its Fock vector, sigma in U(eps) and a nonzero translation y, the matrix coefficient <v, rho(y) omega(sigma) v> decays like |y|^(-2n), with a constant (4(A+n))^n |sigma|^(|iota|+2n) <v, v> uniform in the degree once A >= d + |iota|. Proved by writing the coefficient as an integral, comparing v(z) with the dilated v(z/c), c = sqrt(n/A + 1), at both arguments z = x and z = sigma x + y (both dilated integrals equal c^(2|iota|) <v, v>), and the elementary inequalities 2ab <= a^2 + b^2, 1 + t <= e^t, e t <= e^t and (a + b)^2 <= 2(a^2 + b^2).

Unit 139At most one primitive label per Hecke eigensystem at a chosen place (via Galois representations of lifts)

note and structured proofs · 2 declarations · 2 structured proofs

If an injective linear map L sends the forms of principal level on V to definite algebraic forms, makes every form of a Hecke eigensystem p spherical with fixed eigenvalues at the split places off the level, and intertwines the parabolic action at a chosen place x up to psi(det) and distinct characters chi_l(det) of orders dividing M_omega (parahoric type), then at most one primitive label occurs in the p-part at x (no existence is asserted: if the p-part vanishes, no label occurs). Each occurring label c gives, by Frobenius reciprocity, a product label of L(y); L(y) is an eigenform for one family Q of Hecke polynomials that depends only on the eigenvalue functions F(w), not on c or y, so it has an attached 2-adic Galois representation r, and every representation attached to Q is labelled at x by the twist of c and the chi_l (Attached.isLabelledAt, which the authors call local-global compatibility at tame level; its identification with the published theorems is open, see that import's literature record). Hence one r attached to Q is labelled by both twisted labels; at a tame inertia element s, every eigenvalue of r(s) is then an M_omega-th root of unity (distinct irreducible labels have no common root), so the product of the roots of the first twisted label, a primitive (q - 1)-th root of unity with q - 1 > M_omega, would have M_omega-th power 1.
