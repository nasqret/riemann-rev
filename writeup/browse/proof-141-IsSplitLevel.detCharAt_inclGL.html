lemma IsSplitLevel.detCharAt_inclGL

What it saysFor a field E with a CM type and Weil data, a finite index type n, a finite set P of places, a character a of U(1)(\mathbb{A}) whose base change a(x/\overline{x}) is unramified at every w \in P (c), i, k \in \mathbb{N} and g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(O): detCharAt(a.spaceChar(i), k)(inclGL g) = 1, where a.spaceChar(i) is the Hecke character x \mapsto a(x/\overline{x})\cdot\upsilon(x)^{-i}.
The argument in brief\det g is a unit at each w \in P, where the character is unramified and |\det g|_{w} = 1.
