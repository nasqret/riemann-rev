lemma stdClassSum_rowStabilizer_eq

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, n and b finite types, \mathfrak{m} a modulus, r a finite type, c a type, S a commutative ring, s a sign vector, \alpha, \beta characters, \gamma a weight, \chi a multiplicative character of O_E/\mathfrak{m} with \alpha tame for (\mathfrak{m}, \gamma, \chi) (hα), P a polynomial whose Fock vector is fixed by the real Weil representation of the definite group (hP: realPairWeil \gamma 0 (1, u) v_P = v_P for all u), g a point, W a space with signs s whose hom-matrices to I_b split into x = (x' | x'') with x' a b\times n and x'' a b\times b block, \varphi \colon O_E/\mathfrak{m} \to S a ring homomorphism, w a c\times b matrix over S, e \colon r \hookrightarrow b, and F a complex function on b\times n matrices over O_E/\mathfrak{m} with F(A\cdot y) = F(y) for every A in H = rowStabilizer \varphi w e (hF). Then the class sum at the level 𝔪.levelOfSubgroup H (the elements of K_0 whose reduction modulo \mathfrak{m} lies in H) of h \mapsto \theta^{W, I_b}_{\alpha, \beta }[\Psi_H, P](g, h), \Psi_H(x) = columnDatum \chi \varphi w e (x''_e)\cdot(F(x') if FixesColsOff e x'', else 0), equals the class sum at level K_0(b) of h \mapsto \theta^{W, I_b}_{\alpha, \beta }[\Psi, P](g, h) with \Psi(x) = \chi(\det x'')\cdot F((x'')^{-1}x').
The argument in briefWrite both class sums as class integrals; the integral at the smaller level H is the index times the integral at K_0 of the sum over the cosets of H; with columnDatum_mul_ite_eq the coset sum of the datum \Psi_H is the datum \Psi, coset by coset.
