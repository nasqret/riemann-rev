lemma Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.exists_extend_form

What it saysFor a side S, a B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{+}-submodule W of B_{\mathrm{dR}} and an invariant form \mu \in forms(kerLattice(S)) (h) with \mu(1 \otimes t) \in W for every t \in Y (m): there is an invariant form \nu \in forms(extLattice(S)) mapping ainfLattice(extLattice(S)) into W with \nu \circ inclKer(S) = \mu.
The argument in briefExtend t \mapsto \mu(1\otimes t) B_{\mathrm{cris}}-linearly to \Lambda; each crystalline cocycle \alpha(c) is a coboundary gv_c - v_c, so \Lambda(v_c) is invariant modulo W and congruent to a constant a_c; these are choices for each c, with no asserted linearity in c, so \nu is defined on a \mathbb{Z}_p-basis (e_j) of C by \nu(1\otimes e_j) = \Lambda(v_{e_j}) - a_{e_j} and extended linearly; take \mu\circwittFst + \nu\circprojTriv, and check invariance by expanding an arbitrary c in the basis.
