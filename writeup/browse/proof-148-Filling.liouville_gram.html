lemma Filling.liouville_gram

What it saysLiouville inequality for Gram determinants. Let E be a stably unramified CM field, \sigma,\tau finite, Q a \tau\times\tau matrix over E that is totally positive definite (\Phi_w(Q) positive definite at every infinite place w; hQ), a,b\tau\times\sigma matrices over E with \det(a^{H}Qb)\ne0 (h), v,v'\in\operatorname{GL}_{\sigma}(\mathbb{A}_f), and x a real number with x\ge0 (hx) such that x\le[\delta] whenever \delta=\det(v^{H}\cdot(a^{H}Qb)\cdot v') is a unit of \mathbb{A}_f (i). Then x^{2}\le[\det v]^{2}\cdot|N(\det a^{H}Qa)|\cdot|N(\det b^{H}Qb)|\cdot[\det v']^{2},N the norm to \mathbb{Q} and [\cdot]= genIndex.
The argument in briefReplace x by [\delta]; the module of a rational determinant is a norm, a product over the infinite places; compare place by place by Cauchy–Schwarz for determinants.
