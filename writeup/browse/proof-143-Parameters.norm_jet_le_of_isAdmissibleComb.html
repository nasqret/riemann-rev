lemma Parameters.norm_jet_le_of_isAdmissibleComb

What it saysLet P be parameters, s a natural number (the exponent of the field E = P.E s), n and n' finite types, a and b characters with weights ea, eb and tame parts \psi, \psi' (instances AutChar.HasWeight a ea \psi and hb: AutChar.HasWeight b eb \psi'), B a real number and f a datum of the Weil representation of the pair (I_n, I_{n'}) that is an admissible combination of size B of finite Heisenberg translates of the integral vacuum (H: IsAdmissibleComb (finHeisOp \cdot intVacuum) univ 0 0 True B f), \sigma an automorphism of \mathbb{C}, c \in \mathbb{C}, and g, h finite adelic points of U(I_n) and U(I_{n'}). Put u = \log(|\sigma(c)|^2/\Omega_s) (\Omega_s = P.bigOmega s), M(L, w) = B\cdot \exp(P.c\cdot(N_1(s)\cdot |n'\times n| + 1)^2 + |\Phi_s \times n' \times n|\cdot \log(2L + 2) + w) (\Phi_s the infinite places of P.E s, N_1(s) = P.N₁ s), and F = the scaled Taylor series T_{c\cdot per}(\omega_{a, b}((1, g), (1, h)) f) (jetSeries with scale c\cdot P.\operatorname{jetScale} s); u, M and F are the statement's lets, and the statement runs to line 59 (the census cut it at line 50). Then: (i) for every polynomial Q, natural number L, real w and real A \ge 0: if, for all z and all real R with \sum_i |\sigma(z_i)|^2 \le e^u\cdot(2L + 2)\cdot R, |\sigma(Q(z))| \le A\cdot \exp(w + L\cdot R), then |\sigma(jet_Q F)| \le M(L, w)\cdot A; and (ii) for every positive natural number L, every weight (\kappa, e) with IsLiftWeight n n' \kappa eb e, and every coordinate vector \xi: \sqrt{\operatorname{weylNormSq}(\sigma \circ (\operatorname{schurJet}\ \kappa F\cdot \xi))} \le M(L, w_\kappa)\cdot \sqrt{\operatorname{weylNormSq}(\sigma \circ \xi)}, where w_\kappa = (1/2)\cdot \sum_\tau \sum_{i<|n|} (\lambda_{\tau, i}/L + \log(\lambda_{\tau, i}!) + \lambda_{\tau, i}\cdot u) and \lambda_{\tau, i} = \operatorname{diagramWeight}(\operatorname{rankAt} n', \kappa)(\tau, i).
The argument in briefReplace \sigma by an element of \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{C}/K) with the same absolute values; derive the Schur bound (ii) from (i) by duality and the norm bound for Schur matrices; prove (i) by expanding f into translates, conjugating jets with the semilinear map of exists_semilinear_isJetConjugate, writing a jet as a Gaussian integral of the polynomial against the conjugated vacuum, and bounding the integrand.
