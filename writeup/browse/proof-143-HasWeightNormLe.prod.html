lemma HasWeightNormLe.prod

What it saysLet \Phi and n be finite types, p a proposition, \iota a type, s a finite subset of \iota, and for each i \in \iota a weight \kappa i, a polynomial F i and a real number b i. If HasWeightNormLe p (\kappa i) (F i) (b i) holds for every i \in s, then HasWeightNormLe p (\sum_{i\in s} \kappa i) (\prod_{i\in s} F i) (\prod_{i\in s} b i) holds: the product of the polynomials is the polynomial of a coordinate vector of the summed weight, whose squared norm is, when p holds, at most the product of the bounds.
The argument in briefInduction over the finite set s: the empty product is the polynomial of the vector of values at 0 of the weight-0 basis, and a product with one more factor is again the polynomial of a vector, with the norm bound of the Cartan product.
