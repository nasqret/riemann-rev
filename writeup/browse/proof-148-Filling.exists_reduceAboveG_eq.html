lemma Filling.exists_reduceAboveG_eq

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit) and any prescribed residues t_x\in\mathbb{F}_w, one for each x=((i,w),b) (w a chosen place, b a Boolean): there is y\in\hat{O} with reduceAboveG(S,x)(y)=t_x for every x (Chinese remainder theorem at the chosen places and their conjugates).
The argument in briefTake the sum over x of lifts of t_x times the idempotent of w, conjugated when b is true; all other terms vanish modulo w.
