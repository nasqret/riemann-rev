What it saysFor P, D, a pair p, n with H : HasLiftRank(p, n) (|n| = 2z \ge 4), a tame character \vartheta and a finite point h of U(2z): trivialLift(H, \vartheta)(h) = \sum_{(u, A)} pointKernel(H, \vartheta)(u\cdottwistLift(A), h), the sum over representatives u of the classes of U(1) at the principal level at the chosen places above g (StdClasses of D.chosenLevel) and A \in \prod_{w\in Q} k_w^{\times} (TwistGL), with twistLift(A) the chosen lift (LevelPlaces.twistLift).
