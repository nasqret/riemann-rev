lemma AuxPair.theta_eq_smul_star_lift_swap

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data and O an auxiliary pair over E (W of rank r, V = I_n). For every form \Xi in FormV(O) (algebraic forms on V of weight (\kappa(O), \operatorname{detExp}(O)), level levelGroup(O) and character auxLevelChar(O)) and every point h of the adelic group of W: theta(O)(Xi)(h) = c\,\operatorname{conj}(Space.lift(swap of pairThetaKernel(O), phi, conj(F_Xi), h)) = c\,\operatorname{conj}\bigl( integral over g in [U(I_n)] of \theta(\omega(g, h)(\phi))\, \operatorname{conj}(F_\Xi(g))\, dg\bigr), where c = |WeylIndex(n, kappa(O))| stdLevelIndex(levelGroup(O)) (stdLevelIndex(K) = [K_0 : K intersect K_0]); the integration variable g runs over the quotient [U(I_n)] of the definite source V with its quotient measure, and h is the point of the target W of the descent; \omega and \theta are the Weil representation and theta functional of the swap of pairThetaKernel(O) (the group of V first); \phi = fockDatum(O)(highestVector(kappa(O), paramV(O, ., .))), the Fock vector of P_{v+} tensor \Upsilon; and F_\Xi = ofAlgForm(paramV(O, ., .), 1)(Xi), F_\Xi(g) = \langle v+, \rho(g_\infty^{-1})\, \Xi(g_f) \rangle. Both conjugations matter: the lift is linear in its input, which is \operatorname{conj}(F_\Xi), and its output is conjugated again. theta(O) itself is defined (AuxPair.θ) as the finite class sum over the level of x \mapsto conjKernel(O, h, x)(Xi(x)); this integral description with the scalar c is the theorem.
The argument in briefEvaluate at a point y of the adelic group of W: the conjugate of the lift is an integral over g in [U(I_n)] of an automorphic theta function of g against F_\Xi(g) (the conjugation inside the lift cancels against the one applied to its output); the class-integral formula for algebraic forms turns it into (\dim)^{-1} times a class integral of conjKernel, which by the level equivariance (H) is the class sum defining \theta(\Xi).
