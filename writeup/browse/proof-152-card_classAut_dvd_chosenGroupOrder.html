lemma Data.card_classAut_dvd_chosenGroupOrder

What it saysFor parameters P and admissible data D over P (both implicit), a finite type n with an instance Fintype n, a subgroup K of Gf(E(D), n), hn : \#n \le 2\cdot n(D, m(D)), hK : K \le Kf(E(D), n) (K stabilises the standard lattice) and g \in Gf(E(D), n): the order of classAut(stdRatPoints, K, g), the stabiliser in U_n(E(D)) of the coset gK, divides chosenGroupOrder (of D) (Minkowski's lemma).
The argument in briefMinkowski: reduction modulo the chosen places, after conjugating by g, embeds the stabiliser \Gamma \cap gKg^{-1} in \prod \operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_x) (its kernel lies in a stabiliser for chosenLevel, which is trivial), and the order of that product divides chosenGroupOrder.
