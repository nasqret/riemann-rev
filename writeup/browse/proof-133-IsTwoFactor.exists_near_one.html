lemma IsTwoFactor.exists_near_one

What it saysLet n , m , X , S , \varepsilon , l , \eta , k , j , \tau , B , M , e , \varphi be as in IsTwoFactor and assume IsTwoFactor( l , k , j , \tau , B , e , \varphi ) ( H ). Let r \in \mathbb{R} with 1 \le B (hB), 0 < r ( e0 ) and r \le 1 / 100 (he), and let s \in S with \lVert j_{1} ( s ) - a_{\varepsilon} ( j_{1} ( s ) ) \rVert^{2} + \lVert j_{2} ( s ) - a_{\eta} ( j_{2} ( s ) ) \rVert^{2} \le r^{2} ( h ) (\lVert \cdot \rVert is the Frobenius norm, the norm in force in this file). Then there is t \in S with \theta_{l , k , j , \varphi} ( s ) = \theta_{l , k , j , \varphi} ( t ), \lVert j_{1} ( t ) - 1 \rVert \le 10 r and \lVert j_{2} ( t ) - 1 \rVert \le 10 r, where \theta_{l , k , j , \varphi} ( s ) = laplaceIntegrand( l , k , j , \varphi , s ).
The argument in briefNear the block-diagonal locus, the part c of j_{1} ( s ) - 1 outside the coordinates e ( X ) is small (\lVert c \rVert^{2} \le 6 r^{2}, from the steep weights and the real phase); correcting j_{1} ( s ) by the element s_{0} of the diagonal U ( X ) that undoes the X-block (a unitary u built from c) gives t = s_{0} s with \theta ( t ) = \theta ( s ) and both components of j ( t ) - 1 of norm at most 10 r.
