lemma Data.finrank_rows_ne_zero

What it saysLemma. For parameters P and admissible data D for P: \dim_{\mathbb{C}}\operatorname{rows}(D)\ne0.
The argument in briefIf dim \operatorname{rows}(D)=0, the filling theorem with c=1 and F=\operatorname{rows}(D) (=\top) gives s with dim Column(D)\le(1/2+b_D^{-2})\cdot\dim Column(D) (\varphi=0/0=0), impossible since Column(D)\ne0 and b_D^{-2}<1/2.
