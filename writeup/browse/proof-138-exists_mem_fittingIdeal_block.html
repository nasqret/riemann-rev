lemma exists_mem_fittingIdeal_block

What it saysFor a discrete valuation ring O, a finite linearly ordered J, a finite n, a finite subset s of G, an O-linear \Phi : O^n \to (s \to O[X_J]), z : G \to O^n, a submodule N of (s \to O[X_J]), p \ne 0 (hp), \tau , \varphi : J \times Bool \to G, C, I, b with HasTriangleCost (formsMod \Phi z N p) \tau \varphi I C b (h), \Phi (p\cdot v) \in N for every v (hN), \Phi (z(g)) - e_g \in N for g \in s (hs), \Phi (z(\tau k)) - X_{k.1}\cdot \Phi (z(\varphi k)) \in N for every k (hτ), and, for j \in I and i < j, i \in I or X_i\cdot (s \to O[X_J]) \subseteq N (hI): there is G \in O[X_J] with v(G(0)) \le b + (|I| + |s|)\cdot v(C) and G\cdot \prod _{j\in I} X_j in the Fitting ideal of N (fittingIdeal N).
The argument in briefTwo cost steps and a triangle step for Fitting ideals: pass from N to K (cost e bounded by the relLength of the forms modulo the functions vanishing on L), then the triangle with r_j = C\cdot X_j, then the C-torsion cost f; an explicit determinant gives d_4 with the right constant term.
