lemma coeff_sum_frob_ne_zero_iff

What it saysLet p be a prime, Q a commutative ring of characteristic p, J, \Phi finite index types, I a type, r \colon \Phi \to J \to Q, and power series t \colon J \to Q[[\Phi \times I]] with t_i(0) = 0 for all i; let x \in \Phi and c \in I with t_i - t_i^{p^2} = \sum_v r_{v, i}\cdot X_{(v, c)} for all i. Then for every exponent d: the coefficient of d in \sum_i t_i^p\cdot r_{x, i} is nonzero iff there are n \in \mathbb{N} and y \in \Phi with \sum_i r_{y, i}^{p^{2n+1}}\cdot r_{x, i} \ne 0 and d = p^{2n+1}\cdot(the exponent of the single variable X_{(y, c)}).
The argument in brieft_i = \sum_l (\sum_v r_{v, i}X_{v, c})^{p^{2l}} up to terms of high order, so \sum_i t_i^p r_{x, i} = \sum_{l, v} (\sum_i r_{v, i}^{p^{2l+1}} r_{x, i})\cdot X_{(v, c)}^{p^{2l+1}}; distinct (l, v) give distinct monomials.
