lemmaAuxPair.descentsVanish_of_reflectionSum

Theorem 1 of the note · unit 146 · New/SiegelWeil/AuxPairVanishing.lean, lines 59–158

Let E be a CM field with Weil data, O an auxiliary pair over E (W(O) of rank r, V = I_n), y a form in FormV(O) and u a linear functional on coordinate vectors of weight \kappa(O). Assume (hQ) \psi(O, w) is nontrivial at every twist place w of O; (hM) for every chosen place i and every family v = (v_j), v_j in k_{w_j}^{r} for the chosen places j, with v_i nonzero, the element piTensorElem((reflectionSum(\psi(O, w_j), v_j))_{j}) of the group algebra of \prod_{j} \mathrm{GL}_r(k_{w_j}) kills y through formAction(O); and (h0) for every polynomial P, the descent Space.lift(swap of pairThetaKer(W(O), stdSpace(E, n), a(O), 1), v_P tensor auxDatum(O), \operatorname{conj}(algFormCoeff(1, u)(y)), g) is 0 for every point g, where v_P is the Fock vector of P. Then DescentsVanish(W(O), stdSpace(E, n), a(O), S, \operatorname{conj}(algFormCoeff(1, u)(y))) for S the set of finite places outside the places of O and their conjugates: every such descent with a datum that is the vacuum at the places of S and their conjugates is 0.

lemmaeq_zero_of_heckePolyOp_eq_zero

Proposition 5.1 of the note · unit 146 · New/SiegelWeil/DescentsVanishPlanes.lean, lines 17–28

Let E be a CM field, m a finite type, t signs on m, F a complex function on the adelic unitary group G(E, t) of rank m, w a split finite place (w differs from its conjugate) and X a complex number. Assume F is spherical at w (fixed by right translation by the elements of K_0 that are 1 away from w and its conjugate), the Hecke polynomial operator heckePolyOp(finRightRep(E, t), w, X) (sum over i of (-X)^{i} q^{i(i-1)/2} T_{w,i}, q the norm of w) kills F, q^{|m|+1} |X| \le 1, and |F| is bounded. Then F = 0.

lemmaDescentsVanish.integral_sourceTerm_eq_zero

Lemma 6.1 of the note · unit 146 · New/SiegelWeil/DescentsVanishPlanes.lean, lines 35–44

Let W' = W + H^{p} be split by an adapted splitting D over W (p hyperbolic planes), V a definite hermitian space (signs 1) of rank n, a a character, S a set of places, Y a function on the group of V, and assume DescentsVanish(W, V, a, S, Y). Then for every datum f on \operatorname{Hom}(W', V) with projOn(S) f = f (the vacuum at the places of S and their conjugates) and every g in the adelic group of W': the integral over [U(V)] of \theta(sourceTerm(D, V, 0)(\omega_{a \upsilon^{|p|}, 1}(g, h) f)) Y(h) dh is 0, where sourceTerm(D, V, 0) is the zero term along the maps killing the orthogonal of the frame k.

lemmadescentsVanish_add_planes

Theorem 2 of the note · unit 146 · New/SiegelWeil/DescentsVanishPlanes.lean, lines 49–109

Let W' = W + H^{p} be split by an adapted splitting D over W, V a definite hermitian space of rank n, r the rank of W, a a character, S a set of places and Y a function on the group of V with DescentsVanish(W, V, a, S, Y). Assume: Y is automorphic; w is a split place with \operatorname{conj}(w) in S; the base change of a is unramified at \operatorname{conj}(w) (finite part); Y is spherical at w; the Hecke polynomial operator heckePolyOp(finRightRep, w, X) kills Y, where |X| = (\sqrt{q_w}^{r} q_w^{n - r - 1})^{-1}; and 2r + 2|p| + 3 < n. Then DescentsVanish(W', V, a \upsilon^{|p|}, S, Y), \upsilon = upsilonU1 the splitting character on U(1).
