lemma isLabelledAt_avatar

What it saysLet D be admissible data, \ell a prime with \iota \ell fixed, a a character of U(1)(\mathbb{A}_E) trivial on E with weight e and tame part \psi (h), k, j \in \mathbb{Z}, w a finite place of E that is split (\overline{w} \ne w) with \psi _{\overline{w}} = 1, A a place of \Omega and f \in \mathbb{N}; let T be the chosen faithful character of the algebraic closure of the residue field of w and \mu the \ell-adic avatar of h.toAlgebraic k j, as a 1\times 1 representation. Then IsLabelledAt A T f (PolyKillsAt \mu A) (X - x) holds with x = charName T \psi _w \in \mathbb{F}_w^\times: for every s with tame exponent a' at A (IsTameExponent w A T f s a'), the polynomial X - \iota \ell (T(x)^{a'}) kills s on \mu at A (PolyKillsAt). So inertia at A acts on \mu through the tame character labelled by \psi _w.
The argument in briefFor s with a tame exponent at A, the local compatibility of the avatar at w (stably unramified E) gives the polynomial X - \iota \ell (\theta _w(x)) killing s; \theta _w(x) is computed through the label of \psi _w.
