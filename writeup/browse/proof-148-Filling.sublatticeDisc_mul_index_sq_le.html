lemma Filling.sublatticeDisc_mul_index_sq_le

What it saysLet E be a stably unramified CM field, \sigma finite, V a subspace of E^{\sigma},s=\dim V (hypothesis c), x a \sigma\times s matrix over E whose columns lie in V (e), w\in GL_s(\mathbb{A}_f),D=[\det(x^{H}x)]\cdot[\det w]^{2} (d) with D\ne0 (h), and (with the matrices H,Y and the lattice K of the file) n=H\cdot x\cdot w. If n is integral and every column of Y\cdot n lies in K, then with i=[M:M\cap K], the index of M\cap K in M (submoduleIndex(K,M); 0 if infinite; M\subseteq K is not assumed), where M is the \hat{O}-span of the columns of Y\cdot H\cdot m (m=\mathtt{sublatticeBasis}(u,V)), and d= sublatticeDisc(u,V): (d\cdot i)^{2}\le D,d>0 and i\ge1.
The argument in briefWrite x= yB in the chosen basis y of V, so that x w=b A with b= sublatticeBasis and A=a^{-1}Bw; then D=[\det b^{H}b]\cdot[\det A]^{2}=d^{2}[\det A]^{2},A is integral because Hb has an integral left inverse, and i divides the index [\det A] of A's column lattice.
