lemma trivialLift_mem_range_tupleProj_rank_one

What it saysFor admissible data D, a twist \vartheta and a pair p of rank one for j (H): the lift \Xi _p of the trivial character, (p.lValueData H.1 \vartheta).trivialLift (the class sum of the kernel form over U(1) at principal level at the chosen places), lies in the range of tupleProj ((p.lValueData H.1 \vartheta).auxPair) (i \mapsto X - 1), the image of the idempotent with all labels X - 1.
The argument in briefThe trivial lift is invariant under the action of \prod \operatorname{GL}_1(\mathbb{F}_w) on forms (adjointness and the theta descent); the label idempotent of X - 1 in dimension one averages the trivial character, so it fixes the trivial lift, which is therefore in the range.
