lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.coeff_mem_constantsModE

What it saysLet L be a lattice datum, l > 0 (hl), A \in \mathbb{N}, i a letter, w : S \to \Lambda and t : S \to S with s - w(s) = E\cdot t(s) for every s \in S (e), v_1, \ldots, v_m \in constantsModE(L, l, A, i) (hv), c \in constantsModE(L, l, A, i) (hc), n \in \mathbb{N}, s : \{1, \ldots, m\} \to S, f \in \mathbb{Z}[X] and o : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z} with o(u) \le A for all u (ho), such that \sum_a s_a\cdot v_a - f(E)\cdot c \in withExponents(L, i, shiftedStd(l, o)) (H). Then d = \sum_a w(t^{n}(s_a))\cdot v_a - f_n\cdot c (f_n the coefficient of X^{n}, t^{n} the n-th iterate) lies in constantsModE(L, l, A, i) and in withExponents(L, i, shiftedStd(l, u \mapsto 0 if o(u) \le n else A)).
The argument in briefStrong induction on n: split s_a = w(s_a) + E\cdot t(s_a) and f = X\cdotdivX(f) + f(0); the constant part has exponents \min(1, o) at E and the invariance of constants, which improves to the claim by mem_withExponents_of_deep; for n + 1 divide the E-part by E and apply the hypothesis to t(s_a), divX(f).
