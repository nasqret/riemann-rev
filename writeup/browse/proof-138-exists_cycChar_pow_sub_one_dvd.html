lemma exists_cycChar_pow_sub_one_dvd

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D for P and a prime \ell (ThePrime \ell): there is a natural number h with h \le v_\ell (Mk), where Mk = D.chosenGroupOrder (the product, over the chosen places w of E = D.E, of the orders of the groups \operatorname{GL}_{r\cdot t}(\mathbb{F}_w)) and v_\ell = padicValNat \ell, such that \ell \le h_2^{2} or h = 0 (h_2 = D.h₂ = r^{8}, r the rank of the data), and there is \gamma \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) (\Omega the fixed algebraic closure of \mathbb{Q}) such that for every natural number a with 0 < a < t (t = D.t = 5r) the element \operatorname{cyc}_\ell (\gamma )^a - 1 of \mathbb{Z}_\ell divides \ell ^h in \mathbb{Z}_\ell , \operatorname{cyc}_\ell the \ell-adic cyclotomic character.
The argument in briefTwo cases. If h_2^{2} < \ell, take h = 0 and for \gamma an element of an inertia group above \ell whose cyclotomic character is a generator b of (\mathbb{Z}/\ell )^\times: it fixes E \subset \mathbb{Q}(\zeta _N) as N < \ell, and \operatorname{cyc}(\gamma )^a - 1 is a unit because b^a \ne 1 for 0 < a < t < \ell - 1. Otherwise take h = v_\ell (Mk) and \gamma a Frobenius at a chosen place v of norm g_i not above \ell: \operatorname{cyc}(\gamma )^a - 1 = g_i^a - 1 divides Mk, which divides \ell ^{v_\ell (Mk)} in \mathbb{Z}_\ell.
