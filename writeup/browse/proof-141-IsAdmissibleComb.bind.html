lemma IsAdmissibleComb.bind

What it saysLet \alpha, \alpha' be types, Z a complex vector space, v : \alpha \to Z and v' : \alpha' \to Z, A \subseteq \alpha and A' \subseteq \alpha', n, d, d', e natural numbers, p a proposition, B, B', C real numbers and z \in Z. Assume h: IsAdmissibleComb(v, A, n, d, p, B, z); H: IsAdmissibleComb(v', A', n, d', p, B', v(x)) for every x \in A; hd: d\cdot d' \mid e (default proof: simp); hB: if p then B\cdot|B'| \le C (default proof: simp). Then IsAdmissibleComb(v', A', n, e, p, C, z): substituting combinations into a combination multiplies denominators and sizes.
The argument in briefWrite z = \sum c(x)v(x) and v(x) = \sum r_x(y)v'(y); the coefficients \sum_{x} c(x)r_x(y) have support in A', are integral away from n up to e, and have size \le B\cdot|B'| \le C under p.
