lemma Data.exists_isAdmissibleComb_reducedVacuum

What it saysFor admissible data D there is C \in \mathbb{C} such that for every tame character \vartheta (Data.TameChar; the statement binds its own \vartheta, whose type is fixed by levelNorm and evaluatedDatum, both defined for tame characters: it is not the section's twist, and its tame part may be trivial at places of Q) with q = levelNorm(\vartheta) = \prod_{w\in Q} N(w), and every tuple y = (y_k) of finite adelic unitary points with y_k in localLevel(away from q) — integral at every place not dividing q — for all k: the pure tensor \otimes_{i\le\varrho} evaluatedDatum(\vartheta, y, i) (TensorChain.pure) satisfies IsAdmissibleComb(t \mapsto C\cdotD.reducedVacuum(t), admissibleTranslations(q), q, cutBound^{2m}, (Q(\vartheta) = \emptyset), 1, \cdot): it is a combination of C times the reduced vacua over admissible translations (\pi_g\cdot t_k integral away from q), with coefficients integral away from q up to the denominator cutBound^{2m}, m = 2\varrho + 2, and, if Q is empty, of size at most 1. One C serves all tame characters and all such tuples; the non-triviality of the tame part at the places of Q (Data.Twist) enters only later, in the good datum.
The argument in briefEach moved datum \Upsilon_k is an admissible combination of vacuum translates (named indicators, moved by integral points); pair them by arrow products, apply the admissible evaluations of the pairs (isAdmissibleEval_pairEvalY) and tensor the factors (IsAdmissibleComb.pure); the constants and denominators multiply to C and cutBound^{2m}.
