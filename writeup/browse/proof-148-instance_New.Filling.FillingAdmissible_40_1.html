The argument in briefCollect the size facts of D (\log-bounds) and the computations of the counts, then verify the fields of IsAdmissible: the column level and the surjectivity of the reduction directly; every size inequality, after taking logarithms, is a linear inequality in the log-scales, closed by linear arithmetic.
