lemma exists_root_of_unity_eval_large

What it saysFor a prime \ell, a finite type \kappa, positive reals y_k (k \in \kappa ) (hy), a real r with 0 < r < 1 (h0, h1) and \lVert \ell \rVert < r^{\ell -1} in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell (e): for all sufficiently large N, for all sufficiently large coefficient rings O, every family F : \kappa \to O[X] such that for each k some coefficient satisfies y_k \le \lVert \operatorname{coeff}_i(F_k)\rVert \cdot r^i (the Gauss norm of radius r of F_k is at least y_k) admits \zeta \in O with \zeta ^{\ell ^N} = 1 and r\cdot y_k \le \lVert F_k(\zeta - 1)\rVert for every k.
The argument in briefBy contradiction. If at every \ell ^N-th root of unity \zeta some |F_k(\zeta - 1)| < r y_k, the Gauss norms g_j of P = \prod _k F_k at the radii \rho _j = r^{1/\ell ^j} increase by a fixed factor per step relative to 1/(j+1): an identity through the roots \eta ^i - 1 of (1 + X)^{\ell ^{j+1}} - 1 and the ultrametric inequality show it. Summing over j < N, the harmonic series exceeds the bounded total change.
