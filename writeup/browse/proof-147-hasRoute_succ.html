lemma Data.hasRoute_succ

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D and j < m (h): HasRoute(D, j \le m, j + 1 \le m, 4j + 2): tidy data of order n on path(j) give tidy data of order n + 4j + 2 on path(j + 1), and if the new periods are constant multiples of T, the old are constant multiples of T/d.
The argument in briefMove the descent of each grid past j lifts (Data.descent_past_gridPathMap: a denominator of cost j and an increase 2j of the order of the short sum, for each grid), cross the square at (j, j) (Data.IsTidy.exists_square_move, with the Fourier square law of the flipped kernel of the line), and compare the periods with the seesaw identity for the subdiagonal space (seesaw_periods), the flip costing a power of the residue count.
