lemma norm_gram_det_le_of_operators

What it saysLet \ell be a prime with \iota \ell : \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell fixed, V and W complex vector spaces, A \subseteq V a set whose span Z is finite-dimensional, of dimension N, j : V \to W a linear map, \eta _1, \ldots , \eta _N linear forms on V, x_1, \ldots , x_N vectors of Z (x : \operatorname{Fin} N \to V with every x_b \in Z), \gamma \in \mathbb{R} and m \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell. Assume that for every nonzero p \in A there is a linear map G : V \to W such that (i) for every v \in A, G v = l\cdot j v for some l \in \mathbb{C}; (ii) for every l \in \mathbb{C} with G p = l\cdot j p, e^{-\gamma } \le \lVert \iota \ell (l)\cdot m\rVert; and (iii) for all a, b and every w \in Z with j w = G(x_b), \lVert m\cdot \iota \ell (\eta _a(w))\rVert \le 1. Then \lVert \iota \ell (\det (\eta _a(x_b))_{a,b})\rVert \le e^{N\cdot \gamma }.
The argument in briefChoose a basis \omega of Z inside A, the operators G_p and scalars l_{p,k} with G_p(\omega _k) = l_{p,k}\cdot j(\omega _k), and an integral point d making the linear forms \sum _p \iota \ell (l_{p,k})\cdot m\cdot X_p large; the matrix (\iota \ell \eta _a(\omega _k))\cdot \operatorname{diag}(\sum _p \iota \ell (l_{p,k}) m d_p)\cdot (\iota \ell P_{b,k})^{T} is integral, so its determinant has norm \le 1, and the diagonal factors are \ge e^{-\gamma }.
