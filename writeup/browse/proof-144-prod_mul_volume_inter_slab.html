lemma prod_mul_volume_inter_slab

What it saysLet N \ne 0, k \in \mathbb{N}, v : \mathbb{Z}/N \to Bool, P > 1 real (hP), D : \mathbb{Z}/N \to \mathbb{R} and \Omega \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{Fin k \times \mathbb{Z}/N} with permStep v P 1 \nu \in \Omega iff \nu \in pieriCone v P, for every \nu (h\Omega). Then (\Pi_{l=1}^{k}(q^{l} - 1)/(q - 1))\cdot \operatorname{vol}(\Omega \cap slab k D) = \operatorname{vol}(slab k D), q = P^{N} (volumes as real numbers). This is an identity of volumes for every real P > 1 and every D, and it is written multiplicatively: it also holds when the slab has volume zero. Only when q = P^{N} is a prime power, as in the application (P = p^{o_{0}}), is the factor \Pi_{l=1}^{k}(q^{l} - 1)/(q - 1) the number of full flags in a k-dimensional space over a field with q elements, so that the cyclic interlacing cone fills the share 1/(that number) of a slab of positive volume; for other P it need not be an integer (N = 1, P = 3/2, k = 2 give 5/2).
The argument in briefBy volume_inter_slab_eq_signed_sum, \operatorname{vol}(\Omega \cap slab k D) = C\cdot \operatorname{vol}(slab k D) with a constant C = \Delta(id)/k!\cdot \sum_{\tau} \operatorname{sgn}(\tau)/\Delta(\tau) that depends only on k and q = P^{N}; nothing is divided by \operatorname{vol}(slab k D), which may be 0, so the identity stays multiplicative. C is evaluated in the special case of one letter with parity true, P \coloneqq q and D = 1: there the cone is an orthant in the coordinates \beta_{l} - q\beta_{l+1}, and the linear map F with these coordinates rescaled by d_l =(q^{l+1} - 1)/(q - 1) maps its part of the slab onto the slab (it preserves the coordinate sum), so C\cdot \operatorname{vol}(slab k 1) = \operatorname{vol}(slab k 1)/\det F. The only volume cancelled is that of this one-letter test slab, shown positive and finite; hence C = 1/\det F = 1/\Pi d_l, for every real P > 1.
