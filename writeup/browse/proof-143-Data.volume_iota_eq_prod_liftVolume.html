lemma Data.volume_iota_eq_prod_liftVolume

What it saysFor parameters P and admissible data D over P there is a function c on m-fold twists such that c costs m\cdot m squares (D.CostsSquares (m\cdot m) c: for every family \theta of fit twists, |c(\theta(\iota))/c(1)|^{-2} has height at most 2m^2\cdot t\cdot x^{999}) and, for every m-fold twist \vartheta: D.𝔷 \vartheta \ne 0 and D.𝔷 \vartheta = |c(\vartheta)|^{\operatorname{rowDim}}\cdot \prod_{j=1}^{m} D.liftVolume \vartheta j, where rowDim = D.rowDim is the complex dimension of the rows.
The argument in briefBoth volumes are computed from Gram forms: the Rallis inner product formula gives the Gram form of each single lift as a constant times a Hecke operator, the triangle of lifts gives that of the chain as a constant times an operator whose determinant is the product of the same Hecke determinants; normDet_sq_eq_pow_mul_det turns both into determinants, and the constants form c.
