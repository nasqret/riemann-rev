lemma Grading.HasNearLines.step

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data, Y a grading of V (X, string s) and Y' of V' (X', string s') with s' + 1 = s (hs), m = m' + (0 if s is odd, 1 if s is even) (hm). Assume: HasNearLines(Y, a, Q, m, k, x_f) (hx); v(c_w) = v((a\overline{a})_{w}) for all w, c the constant of Y (ha); the constant term constTermAtT_{Y,Y'}(\mu, t \otimes f)(x, x') \ne 0 (n); b a finite idele with ideleLogVal(\operatorname{conj}(\alpha/a))_{w} \le ideleLogVal(b)_{w} + [w \in Q] for all w (hb); \varrho_f(b\cdot y)f = f for all y \in \hat{O} (hf); and every adapted basis of Y' at x'_{f} satisfies ideleLogVal(\beta_i)_{w} + ideleLogVal(\beta_{s'-1-i})_{\overline{w}} \le 0 (hg). Then HasNearLines(Y', a, Q, m', k + 1, x'_{f}): one lift moves the lines of the lattice by at most one step above Q.
The argument in briefThe non-vanishing constant term localises the frequency T on the lattice (zeroTermFin_support); reading the relation between the two lattice bases entrywise along the string gives one integrality per string index of Y' (claim H), which with hb, hx, ha and hg is the near-lines bound with k + 1.
