lemma ClassMean.exists_invariant_diagonal_form

What it saysLet O be a valuation domain of characteristic zero, \sigma a finite type, k a prime number (named k here) whose image in O is not a unit (hp), and f \in \mathbb{N}. There is q : (\sigma \to_{0} \mathbb{N}) \to O such that (i) for every exponent vector \gamma of total degree |\gamma | < k^{f}: k^{|\sigma |\cdot f} = multinomial(\gamma)\cdot q(\gamma) in O, multinomial(\gamma) = |\gamma |!/\Pi_{b} \gamma_{b}!; and (ii) for every square matrix A \in Mat_{\sigma}(O) and all exponent vectors \alpha, \beta: (coefficient of x^{\alpha} in linSubst A (x^{\beta}))\cdot q(\alpha) = q(\beta)\cdot(coefficient of x^{\beta} in linSubst A^{\mathsf{T}} (x^{\alpha})).
The argument in briefMultinomial coefficients of degree < k^{f} divide k^{|\sigma |f} in O (the k-part of a binomial coefficient C(m, j) with m < k^{f} is at most k^{f}, the rest is a unit), so q(\gamma) = k^{|\sigma |f}/multinomial(\gamma) exists (0 in higher degree). The transposition identity follows from the Fock pairing \langle f, g\rangle = \sum \alpha!f_{\alpha}g_{\alpha}, for which linSubst A and linSubst A^{\mathsf{T}} are adjoint, multiplied through by |\alpha |!.

      Write C = k^{v_k(C)}\cdot C' with C' prime to k. C' is a unit of O: k is not a unit, so it lies in the maximal ideal of the local ring O, and C' coprime to k cannot lie there too. And v_k(C) \le \log_k(\sum \alpha) < f since \sum \alpha < k^{f} (h); so C divides k^{f}.
