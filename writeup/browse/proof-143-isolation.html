lemma isolation

What it saysLet E be a number field, n a finite nonempty type, s signs on n, m \in \mathbb{N} and B \in \mathbb{R} with f\cdot(2|n|^3) \le m (hn; f the number of infinite places), m \le B (hm) and 10^{13} \le B (hB); o an order positive-first for s (ho); w and k weights that are K-dominant (hu, hd), with holomorphic gap 300m (hw, hk), and |k(\tau, i)| \le B for all \tau, i (hk'). Let further E be a CM field, F a continuous function (hF), adelic holomorphic of order o and weight k (c), z a point with F(z) \ne 0 (ha) and |F(x)| \le 2|F(z)| for all x (h2), K_0 with |K_0(h)| \le C for all h (g), and \varepsilon, such that (H) if mh_w is integrable, then for every x with |F(z)| \le |F(x)| there is K, holomorphic of weight k - w, with |K(h) - K_0(h)| \le \lVert h\rVert^{6m}\cdot \varepsilon for all h and \int \operatorname{conj} F(x\cdot(h, 1))\cdot K(h)\cdot mh_w(h) dh = 0. Then |K_0(1)| \le 4\varepsilon \cdot B^{7m} + \varepsilon.
The argument in briefAverage over the circle of rotations z\cdot(torus element): holomorphy of weight k turns the circle mean of \operatorname{conj} F(z\theta \cdot)\cdot mh_w into a multiple of mh_k; the orthogonality at each rotated point and the approximation |K - K_0| \le \varepsilon \lVert h\rVert^{6m} then bound |K_0(1)| through integral_mul_holMinorsPi_le.
