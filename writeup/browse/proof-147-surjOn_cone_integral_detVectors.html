lemma surjOn_cone_integral_detVectors

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, n', r, r' finite index types; signs s, t on n, r and s', t' on n', r'; natural numbers k and m(w); W : Space(E, n, s), W' : Space(E, n', s'), V : Space(E, r, t), V' : Space(E, r', t'); A a base-adapted splitting of W' over W along one line and X a base-adapted splitting of V' over V along one line (so W', V' are W, V plus a hyperbolic plane). Let \beta be a parameter and z a real number with z \ge 1 (hz), and suppose \mathit{rk} W' = 2(k + negCount(s')) (hn). Then the cone integral at z with weights k + m, v' \mapsto \int \int coneIntegrand(A, X, k + m, \beta, z, v', p) d(Haar \times Haar)(p), is surjective from detVectors(k, m) (for (s', t')) onto detVectors(k, m) (for (s, t)) in the sense of Set.SurjOn: every determinant vector v of the smaller pair is the cone integral of some determinant vector v' of the larger pair (the direction used when planes are removed). It is not asserted that the cone integral maps every determinant vector v' into the determinant vectors of the smaller pair (no MapsTo).
The argument in briefFor v = detVectorFn(s, t, k, m, c, F, \cdot), take v' = R(x) = the Fock vector of x\cdot \prod_{w} d(w, 1)^{m_w}\cdot d(w, -1)^{m_w+2k}, d the determinants of linear forms built from G = -s(E)^{-1}(K_A K_X^{*} + L_A L_X^{*}) + I_A F I_X^{*}; v' is a determinant vector (Weil action computed on polynomial vectors), and the cone integrand of R(\mathit{dc}) at each point q is e^{\varphi(q)}\cdot d\cdot v, so a suitable x gives cone integral v.
