What it saysLet N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, K a field and T : TwistingData N W K (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to K, parity isEven), K of characteristic p, k \in \mathbb{N}, indices a, a' \in Fin k with a + 1 = a' (ha), natural numbers n, \ell and reals h, \kappa, z with HeckeSizes P o_{0} N k n a' \ell \kappa h z (big; r = a' and y = h). [\mathrm{GL}_k(W) : \mathcal{P}]\cdot [L : L\cap \mathcal{I}] \le [\mathrm{GL}_k(W) : \mathcal{I}] \le [\mathrm{GL}_k(\kappa_{W}) : B], \mathcal{I} being the preimage of the lower Borel subgroup B of \mathrm{GL}_k over the residue field \kappa_{W} of W (a relative-index inequality), and [\mathrm{GL}_k(\kappa_{W}) : B] is at most the number of full flags \Pi_{l}(q^{l} - 1)/(q - 1); flags \times \operatorname{vol}(excessCone a' 0 \cap slab k D) = \operatorname{vol}(slab k D) (prod_mul_volume_inter_slab). The slab volume is at most a product over the N letters of simplex volumes, each at most |(e^{1/h}n)^{k-1}/(k-1)!| because D(j) \le e^{1/h}\cdot n by the size condition on n/h (the shift, the enlarged slab and this condition are matched), and the size conditions turn the product into (1 + \kappa)\cdot m^{N}, m = \#Monomials (Fin k) n.
