lemma Filling.link_big_cell_eq_iff

What it saysFor a shape \mathfrak{s}, a field A with involution, a Gram matrix T, sequences p,q:\mathbb{N}\to A^{\times} and \sigma,\tau\in U(\mathtt{T.splitGram}) with \sigma,\tau\in\operatorname{GT}_{\varrho} (h\sigma,h\tau; they fix all e_a and e_a^{*}): \operatorname{link}(w_p\sigma)=\operatorname{link}(w_q\tau) iff \sigma=\tau, where w_p= weylElem(\varrho,p).
The argument in briefThe link of w_p\sigma is the graph of the middle block of \sigma, independent of p.

  2. 2computationlines 99–100

    By mem_link and the matrix of w_p\sigma (coe_weylElem_mul): for (p_1,p_2) to lie in \operatorname{link}(w_p\sigma), the e^{*}-part of w_p\sigma(a,p_2,0)=(0,\sigma_{\mathrm{mid}}p_2,(p_b a_b)_b) must vanish, forcing a=0, and then p_1=\sigma_{\mathrm{mid}}p_2. So \operatorname{link}(w_p\sigma) is the graph of the middle block of \sigma (whatever p), and the links agree iff the middle blocks of \sigma and \tau agree, i.e. iff \sigma=\tau (both are the identity on the e_a and e_a^{*}).
