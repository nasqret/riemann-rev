lemma IsAdmissibleComb.smul_mem

What it saysLet \alpha be a type, Z a complex vector space, v : \alpha \to Z, A \subseteq \alpha, n, d natural numbers, p a proposition, B real, z \in Z with h: IsAdmissibleComb(v, A, n, d, p, B, z); let l be a prime with a chosen ring homomorphism \iota_{p} : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_l (PadicPlace l), coprime to n (hypothesis hq), and M an O_{\iota}-submodule of Z, O_{\iota} = \{x \in \mathbb{C} : |\iota_{p}(x)|_{l} \le 1\} (embValRing), containing v(x) for every x \in A (hypothesis hv). Then d\cdot z \in M.
The argument in briefd\cdot z = \sum (d\cdot r(x))\cdot v(x) is an O_{\iota}-combination of vectors of M.

  2. 2deductionline 67

    d\cdot z = \sum_{x \in \operatorname{supp} r} (d\cdot r(x))\cdot v(x). Each d\cdot r(x) lies in integralAway(n), so |\iota_{p}(d\cdot r(x))|_{l} \le 1 because l is a prime coprime to n (hq): d\cdot r(x) \in O_{\iota}. Each v(x) with x in the support lies in A, hence in M by hv. So every summand lies in M, and so does the sum.
