lemma le_of_isotypic_mem

What it saysFor an auxiliary pair O, subspaces Z and N of the forms on V: if Z is invariant under every Hecke operator H_w(X) (all split w off the places of O and their conjugates, all X \in \mathbb{C}) (hT), and every v \in Z whose smooth form is nonzero and lies in an isotypic space x (a space of eigenforms with the eigenvalues t_w at almost all split w) belongs to N (h), then Z \subseteq N.
The argument in briefZ (as smooth forms) is fixed by the level and stable under the Hecke operators, so it is spanned by Hecke eigenvectors off the places of O; each lies in an isotypic space, hence in N.
