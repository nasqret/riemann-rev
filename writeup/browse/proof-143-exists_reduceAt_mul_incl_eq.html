Structured proof · Unit 143

lemma exists_reduceAt_mul_incl_eq

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r and n finite types, O : AuxPair E r n, Y a family of n\times r matrices Y_i over the residue fields at the chosen places w_i of O with injective columns (hY), and e \colon r \hookrightarrow n. Then there is k \in K_0(n) supported on the places of O (component 1 at all other places and their conjugates) such that at every chosen place w_i the reduction of k times I_{incl} equals Y_i (the columns O.incl of k mod w_i are those of Y_i), and at every twist place q_j the transposed reduction of k times I_e equals I_{incl} (I_e, I_{incl} the inclusion matrices of e and O.incl).
The argument in briefExtend each Y_i, and each inclusion matrix at the twist places, to an invertible matrix; then lift the family of residues to K_0.
