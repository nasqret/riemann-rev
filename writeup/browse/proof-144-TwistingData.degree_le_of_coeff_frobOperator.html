lemma TwistingData.degree_le_of_coeff_frobOperator

What it saysLet N be a natural number, W a commutative local ring, O a commutative ring and T : TwistingData N W O (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to O, parity isEven, w \in O with w^{2} = p), N \ne 0, natural numbers k, r, \ell, n, families \beta, \gamma and an exponent \nu on the generic variables: if the coefficient of x^{\nu} in the (\beta, \gamma) entry of frobOperator T r \ell n (frobGenericMatrix T) is nonzero (e), then for every letter j the letter-j part of \nu has degree at most N\cdot n\cdot k\cdot(\ell +2)\cdot P^{\ell}.

    Apply frobOperator_mem with the Gauss filtration (weight -1 on the variables of letter j, 0 on the others) over the trivial filtration, \alpha = 0, x = 0 and u = P^{\ell} (the entries of frobGenericMatrix are P^{d}-th powers of variables, d \le \ell, at level \ge -P^{\ell}): the entry lies at level -N\cdot n\cdot k(\ell +2)P^{\ell}, so a monomial x^{\nu} with nonzero coefficient has letter-j degree at most N\cdot n\cdot k(\ell +2)P^{\ell} (otherwise its coefficient would be divisible by a positive power of 0).
