lemma Filling.energy_le_of_linkEnergy_le

What it saysLemma (crude estimate). Let E be stably unramified and S admissible. Let 0\le C,0\le w,\Omega\subseteq\mathsf{G},T stable, X\ne0 with FixesNormAtLeast(S,s,X) for all s\in\Omega,\mathsf{d}_1\le C,\tau^{20}\mathsf{d}_1\le C if some \gamma\in T over some (c,s,c') with s\in\Omega is in normal form of level 0; let R be a set of links with \tau^{12}/X^{k}\cdot\mathsf{d}\le C if some link is not in R, and assume that the link energy of the terms of T with \mathsf{g}\ne0, for the cut Bessel functions, is linkEnergy(T\cap\{\gamma:\mathtt{termDisc}(S,\gamma)\ne0\},\Omega,R)\le w\cdot\nu^{2}. Then for the full Bessel functions \operatorname{energy}(\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(S,T),\Omega)\le(1+b^{-4})^{2}\cdot w+b^{-12}\cdot C.
The argument in briefWrite A^{\mathrm{full}}=(A^{\mathrm{full}}-A^{\mathrm{cut}})+A^{\mathrm{cut}} and split A^{\mathrm{cut}} along \{\mathsf{g}\ne0\}: the cut difference costs b^{-30}\mathsf{d}_1, the part with \mathsf{g}\ne0 (level \varrho+1) is bounded by energy_le_of_level_pairs with j=\varrho+1, and the part with \mathsf{g}=0 by energy_le_of_level_lt with n=\varrho+1 (agreeing terms with \mathsf{g}=0 have a level \le\varrho); energy_add_le with \varepsilon=b^{-4} twice gives the constants.
