defIntegralPlace.phase

Definition 2.2 of the note · unit 148 · New/Filling/TopFloor.lean, lines 23–24

At a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. Definition. For a unit g and z in the unitary group U(J): \operatorname{phase}(g,z)=\psi(g^{-1}\cdot\sigma(u)) if z\in u\cdot K(g) for some u\in\mathsf{U} (u a chosen such element; \sigma(u)= simpleRootSum(u), the sum of the entries at the simple roots of the frame block; K(g)= unitaryLevel(R,g,J)), and 0 if z\notin\mathsf{U}\cdot K(g). This is the finite Bessel function \chi_g.
