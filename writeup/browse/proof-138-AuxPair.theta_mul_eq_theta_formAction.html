lemma AuxPair.theta_mul_eq_theta_formAction

What it saysFor a field E with a CM type and Weil data, finite types r, n, an auxiliary pair O over E with index types r \subset n, an element k of the stabiliser K_0 of the standard lattice of W whose reductions at the chosen places of O are s = (s_i) \in O.ChosenGL r (reduceFamily k = s, hk), a form \Xi \in O.FormV and a point g of U(W)(\mathbb{A}): (O.\theta \Xi )(g\cdot (1, k)) = (O.\theta (O.formAction s \Xi))(g), where (1, k) has infinite component 1 and finite component k; the auxiliary theta lift intertwines right translation by k on W with the action of the reduction s on V.
The argument in briefWrite \theta through the theta descent of the kernel; the descent is equivariant, and the Weil representation at k acts on V through the reduction s.
