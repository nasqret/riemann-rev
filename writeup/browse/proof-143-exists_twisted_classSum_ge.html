lemma exists_twisted_classSum_ge

What it saysLet l be a prime, E a field with a CM type, \kappa a type, w \colon \kappa \to (finite places of E) with every w i in a set S of places (hS), \iota \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_l a ring homomorphism, s a complex function on G_f(I_1), \Gamma and K subgroups of G_f(I_1) with the rational points in \Gamma (hΓ) and the principal level at w in K (hK: levelOf w \perp \le K), the double cosets \Gamma \backslash G_f/K finite (HasFiniteType \Gamma K), and every stabiliser classAut \Gamma K g trivial (a). Then there are a point z and a character \chi in tameChars S (weight 0, tame part trivial outside S) with |\iota(s(1))| \le |\iota(\operatorname{classSum} \Gamma K (g \mapsto \chi(\det(gz))\cdot s(gz)) / classSum \Gamma K 1)|.
The argument in briefThe classes form a finite abelian group; Fourier inversion writes s(1)\cdot \#classes as a sum over its characters, which come from tame characters \chi; by the ultrametric inequality one term is at least as large.
