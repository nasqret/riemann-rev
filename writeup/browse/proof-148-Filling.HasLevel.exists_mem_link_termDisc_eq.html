lemma Filling.HasLevel.exists_mem_link_termDisc_eq

What it saysLemma. Let E be a number field with a CM structure, S filling data, \gamma\in G(E) and j with HasLevel(S,j,\gamma) (h). Then there are a\in E^{\kappa} and y\in E^{\mathrm{Unit}}=E such that the vector ((a,-1),(0,y))\in(X_0\oplus Ez)^{2}, i.e. the pair (a-z,y\cdot z), lies in \operatorname{link}(\gamma), and \mathsf{g}(\gamma)=D_j(\gamma)\cdot\overline{D}_j(\gamma)\cdot(1-\overline{y}y). (Notation: T= ratMiddle(S) is the Gram matrix of the rational middle form X_0\oplus Ez with z of square h(z,z)=-N(S) (T= middleGram(N(S)): 1 on X_0 and -N(S) at z), J= ratGram(S) its split Gram matrix on BesselIndex(\mathfrak{s}); Z(\gamma)= frameProducts(S,\gamma); D_j(\gamma)= leadingMinor(Z(\gamma),j); \mathsf{g}(\gamma)= termDisc(S,\gamma); \operatorname{link}(\gamma)\subseteq(X_0\oplus Ez)^{2} is the image under linkMatrix(\gamma) of the J-orthogonal of the isotropic frame pair (f_a,\gamma f_a); zFormPair(T)=\operatorname{diag}(T_z,T_z) takes the z-pairing in both halves; linkMetric(S)=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\operatorname{diag}(T,T)+N(S)^{-1}\cdotzFormPair(T)^{*}zFormPair(T), the diagonal matrix with 1/2 at the X_0-coordinates and -N(S)/2+N(S)=N(S)/2 at the two z-coordinates; linkDiscSq(S,v,e)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(\det(v^{*}\cdot\mathtt{linkMetric}(S)\cdot v))|\cdot[\det e]^{2},[\cdot] the generalised index of a finite adele; minorNorm(S,j,\gamma)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(D_j(\gamma))|/N(S)^{2(j-\varrho)_+f}; termDiscNorm(S,\gamma)=\prod_v|\Phi_v(\mathsf{g}(\gamma))|; HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m): m= linkMatrix(\gamma)\cdot t\cdot w for a rational t with (isotropic frame pair of \gamma)*\cdot J\cdot t=0 and adelic w with classCoord(S,c)\cdot t\cdot w integral; f=f(E) the number of infinite places.)
The argument in briefFind x with \gamma's frame image of x in the link-pair form and the stated \mathsf{g}: for j=\varrho+1 take y with Z(\gamma)y=\det\cdot e_{\mathrm{top}} scaled by N/\det (norm_sq_middle_part_eq gives the z-coordinate and the identity for \mathsf{g}); for j\le\varrho take the shared vector of \gamma^{-1} at -1 (normal form) and use that the top coordinates have equal norms and \mathsf{g}(\gamma)=0 for j\le\varrho.
