lemma AuxPair.realPairWeil_harmonicPoly

What it saysFor an auxiliary pair O over E (W of rank r with signs signs(O), V = I_n), a weight \gamma', a real point y of U(I_n) and a vector \xi on WeylIndex(n, kappa(O)): realPairWeil with weights (negCount(signs(O)), \gamma') at (1, y) sends the Fock vector of harmonicPoly(O)(conj xi) to that of harmonicPoly(O)(conj(conjWeylRep(kappa(O), detExp(O))(y)(xi))).
The argument in briefWrite harmonicPoly through the mixed Cartan embedding, which intertwines the action of y on coordinate vectors with the actions on the two tensor factors; expand the vector in the standard basis and apply realPairWeil_fockLetters to each letter polynomial.
