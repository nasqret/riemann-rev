lemma Filling.tsum_invPowWeight_le_of_codim_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S admissible, c a class, j\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\},t an element of the reduced Bessel image \overline{S},s\in\mathbb{N},A a set of pairs p=(c',\gamma)\in Classes(S)\times G(E) with IsLabelledTerm(S,j,c,t,p) for all p\in A (\gamma is the near representative of its \mathsf{U}^{0}-double coset, has level j and lies over (c,s',c') for some s' with \overline{S}-component t), T an E-subspace of X_0 with k\le\dim T+s, and n a function to \mathbb{N} on the finite adelic group such that for every p\in A, frameValueIndex(S,c,\mathtt{termElem}(S,c,p),\mathtt{gIdele}(S),T) divides n(\mathtt{termElem}(S,c,p)) (termElem(S,c,p)=h_c^{-1}\gamma h_c'). Then \Sigma_{p\in A} invPowWeight(S,c,n,p)\le b^{2000(\varrho+1)^{6}(s+1)f}, where invPowWeight(S,c,n,p)=n(\mathtt{termElem}(S,c,p))^{-k} (1/0 read as 0).
The argument in briefGroup the pairs by Z(\gamma) (few values: integral matrices with entries \le b^{5n^{2}} at every place); in one group apply the congruence count modulo d=M\cdot g\cdot\det(YY^{H}),Y= paddedLeading(j,Z): each congruence class holds at most one labelled term, because two such terms p,q give an element r\in\mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g) with (r u_p p)f=(u_q q)f (shared vectors are short, so the matrix X\cdot\operatorname{adj}(Y) vanishes in the high columns); the count and the index [\hat{O}:d\hat{O}] give the power of b.
