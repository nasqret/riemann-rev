lemma Filling.exists_lifts_linkDiscSq_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S filling data, c,c' classes, t\in\mathsf{G},\gamma\in G(E) a term over (c,t,c') (hk) of level j (hj), \tau a finite type and v a rational matrix with \tau columns that has a left inverse (hv) and whose columns span \operatorname{link}(\gamma) (hL). Put k= termPosition(S,c,c',\gamma) and w= xRowsIndex(S,j,k)\cdotxRowsIndex(S,j,k^{-1})\cdotsaturationIndex(S,k) (indices of lattices attached to the frame Gram matrix and the isotropic frame pair of k). Then there is e\in\operatorname{GL}_{\tau}(\mathbb{A}_f) with HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,v\cdot e), linkDiscSq(S,v,e)\cdot w^{2}\le minorNorm(S,j,\gamma)^{2}, and w\ne0. (Notation: T= ratMiddle(S) is the Gram matrix of the rational middle form X_0\oplus Ez with z of square h(z,z)=-N(S) (T= middleGram(N(S)): 1 on X_0 and -N(S) at z), J= ratGram(S) its split Gram matrix on BesselIndex(\mathfrak{s}); Z(\gamma)= frameProducts(S,\gamma); D_j(\gamma)= leadingMinor(Z(\gamma),j); \mathsf{g}(\gamma)= termDisc(S,\gamma); \operatorname{link}(\gamma)\subseteq(X_0\oplus Ez)^{2} is the image under linkMatrix(\gamma) of the J-orthogonal of the isotropic frame pair (f_a,\gamma f_a); zFormPair(T)=\operatorname{diag}(T_z,T_z) takes the z-pairing in both halves; linkMetric(S)=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\operatorname{diag}(T,T)+N(S)^{-1}\cdotzFormPair(T)^{*}zFormPair(T), the diagonal matrix with 1/2 at the X_0-coordinates and -N(S)/2+N(S)=N(S)/2 at the two z-coordinates; linkDiscSq(S,v,e)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(\det(v^{*}\cdot\mathtt{linkMetric}(S)\cdot v))|\cdot[\det e]^{2},[\cdot] the generalised index of a finite adele; minorNorm(S,j,\gamma)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(D_j(\gamma))|/N(S)^{2(j-\varrho)_+f}; termDiscNorm(S,\gamma)=\prod_v|\Phi_v(\mathsf{g}(\gamma))|; HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m): m= linkMatrix(\gamma)\cdot t\cdot w for a rational t with (isotropic frame pair of \gamma)*\cdot J\cdot t=0 and adelic w with classCoord(S,c)\cdot t\cdot w integral; f=f(E) the number of infinite places.)
The argument in briefLift the basis v of the link to a rational u with (f_a,\gamma f_a)^{*}Ju=0, linkMatrix(\gamma)u=(0|v),(e_a^{*})^{*}Ju= (1 | 0) (a\ge j), complete classCoord(c)\cdot u to a primitive matrix with diagonal blocks a,e (so v\cdot e has integral lifts), and apply Jacobi's identity to the bordered matrix M of abs_norm_bordered_det: [\det M]=(N^{2f}\mathtt{minorNorm})^{2} equals [\det a]^{2}[\det e]^{2}\cdot|N\det(v^{*}\cdot\mathtt{linkMetric}\cdot v)|\cdot[\text{index}]^{2}\cdot N-power. The three indices xRowsIndex(k), xRowsIndex(k^{-1})\cdotsaturationIndex(k) divide [\det a] and [index], which gives linkDiscSq(v,e)\cdot w^{2}\le minorNorm{}^{2} and w\ne0.
