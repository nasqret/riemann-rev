lemma Filling.energy_le_of_level_pairs

What it saysLemma (incoherent and degenerate pairs are negligible). Let E be stably unramified and S admissible, and use the cut Bessel functions. Let C,w be reals with 0\le C and 0\le w,\Omega\subseteq\mathsf{G},T a stable set of rational points, X\ne0 a natural number such that every s\in\Omega satisfies FixesNormAtLeast(S,s,X) (s fixes, modulo chosen places of total norm \ge X, a nonzero vector of the span of the frame), \mathsf{d}_1\le C (\mathsf{d}_1=\mathtt{besselEnergy}(S)), and \tau^{20}\mathsf{d}_1\le C if some \gamma\in T over some (c,s,c') with s\in\Omega is in normal form of level 0. Let j\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\} with HasLevel(S,j,\gamma) for all \gamma\in T,R a set of links such that \tau^{12}/X^{k}\cdot\mathsf{d}\le C whenever \varrho<j and some link is not in R (\mathsf{d}=\mathtt{mainEnergy}(S)), and assume linkEnergy(T,\Omega,R)\le w\cdot\nu^{2}. Then \operatorname{energy}(\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(S,T),\Omega)\le w+C/b^{30}/4^{\varrho+1-j} (\tau=\mathtt{tailScale}(S),\nu=\mathtt{unipCovolume}(S)).
The argument in briefSplit each block by the links of its terms; the diagonal part with links in R is the link energy; every other product \lVert A_{\Lambda}\rVert\lVert A_{\Lambda}'\rVert is bounded by the block bounds (enorm_amplitudeSum_le_classSize) and compared, pair by pair, with C/\tau^{3}\cdot\nu^{2}\cdotpairWeight via the pair invariants; the pair weights sum to at most 2\tau^{2} (sum_pairWeight_le), which gives C/b^{30}/4^{\varrho+1-j}.
