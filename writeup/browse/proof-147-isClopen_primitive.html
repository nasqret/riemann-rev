lemma TameLocalChar.isClopen_primitive

What it saysLet F be a semilocal field, a, b, n finite index types, \chi a multiplicative character of F which is tame (TameLocalChar χ: a finite residue ring R, a surjection O \to R with kernel \varpi O, and a primitive character \eta of R with \eta \circ \pi = \chi^{-1} on units of O and 0 on non-units), and e : b \sqcup a \simeq n. Then the set of primitive n \times a matrices over F is closed and open (clopen).
The argument in briefClosed: the primitive matrices are the column blocks of the compact group \mathrm{GL}_n(O). Open: around a primitive matrix the standard datum is non-zero and locally constant, and its non-vanishing forces primitivity.

    Closed: a matrix is primitive exactly when it is the block of columns indexed by a of some k \in \mathrm{GL}_n(O) (IsValuationPair.exists_firstCols_iff), so the set is the image of the compact group \mathrm{GL}_n(O) (isCompact_intGL) under a continuous map, hence compact and closed. Open: for a primitive y with left inverse s, the standard datum stdColDatum(\chi, s) is locally constant (it is Schwartz–Bruhat, stdColDatum_mem_schwartzBruhat), non-zero at y, and every matrix where it is non-zero is primitive (isPrimitiveMatrix_of_stdColDatum_ne_zero): so the preimage of \mathbb{C}\setminus \{0\} is an open neighbourhood of y inside the set.
