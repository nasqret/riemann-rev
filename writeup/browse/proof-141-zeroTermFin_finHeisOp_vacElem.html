lemma zeroTermFin_finHeisOp_vacElem

What it saysLet E, n, n_{0}, p, V, V_{0} (signs s, s_{0}) and D be as in zeroTermFin; let g be in the finite adelic unitary group of V_{0}, c a p \times n_{0} matrix over \mathbb{A}_f, and B = \iota_f\cdot g + k_f\cdot c (hypothesis e) integral (hB), \iota_f, k_f the finite parts of the adelic frames of D. Then there is C \in \mathbb{C} such that for every finite adelic vector y of V there is \zeta \in \mathbb{C}, zero or a root of unity, with zeroTermFin(D)(\varrho_f(y)\cdot\operatorname{vac}) = \zeta\cdot C\cdot\omega(1, g)(\varrho_f(B^{H}y)\cdot\operatorname{vac}_{0}), \operatorname{vac} and \operatorname{vac}_{0} the vacua of V and V_{0}.
The argument in briefTake C with zeroTermFin(D)(\operatorname{vac}) = C\cdot\omega(1, g)\operatorname{vac}_{0}. For y, if the zero term of \varrho_f(y)\operatorname{vac} is non-zero, y = l + \iota_f w + k_f b with l \in \hat{O}^{n} (zeroTermFin_support); moving \varrho_f(y) through the zero term gives \zeta\cdot C\cdot\omega(1, g)\varrho_f(B^{H}y)\operatorname{vac}_{0} with \zeta a product of two values of the finite additive character.
