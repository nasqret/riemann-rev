lemma exists_three_term_free

What it saysBehrend's construction. For commutative groups G and H, a group homomorphism \varphi : G \to H, elements c, g \in G, natural numbers n, M, L with hL : 0 < L and h : (L\cdot M)^n \le orderOf(\varphi (g)): there is a finite set T \subseteq G such that (1) every \sigma \in T equals c\cdot g^e for some natural number e; (2) M^n/(n\cdot M^{2}) \le \#T (a real quotient with Lean's division, x/0 = 0: for n\cdot M = 0 the bound is 0 \le \#T); and (3) for all natural numbers i, j with 0 < i < j \le L and all \sigma , \tau , \rho \in T, \varphi (\tau /\sigma )^j = \varphi (\rho /\sigma )^i implies \varphi (\tau /\sigma ) = 1.
The argument in briefBehrend: T is the image of a sphere {x \in [0, M)^n : \sum x_l^{2} = k} under x \mapsto c\cdot g^{\langle x\rangle }, \langle x\rangle the number with base-LM digits x. A relation \varphi (\tau /\sigma )^j = \varphi (\rho /\sigma )^i becomes the vector identity j\cdot v = i\cdot w + (j - i)\cdot u among points of the sphere, which strict convexity forces to be trivial.
