lemma norm_schurJet_le

What it saysDivisibility of Schur jets. Let p be a prime, \Phi, n, n' finite index types, \kappa column counts fitting the ranks (FitsRanks n n' \kappa), pr : \mathbb{N} \to \Phi \to \Phi, S \subset \mathbb{N} finite, \rho, \omega \in \mathbb{R}, \varpi \in \mathbb{C}_p with |\varpi | = p^{-\rho }, \theta \in \mathfrak{o} with |\theta | = p^\omega, a power series \Theta over \mathbb{C}_p, ev \subset \Phi finite, and charges g \colon \Phi \to \mathbb{N} \to (index) \to \mathbb{N} such that for e \in S, \sigma, h, b: if \sigma \in ev or (h = 1 \text{ and } pr_e(\sigma) \in ev) then g(\sigma, 1, b) \ne 0 or g(pr_e(\sigma), h, b) \ne 0. Let i', i be Weyl indices, N = |n'|, B = \operatorname{boxes}(\kappa, N) and m = \sum_\tau \sum_{h<N}\sum_j\sum_b g(\tau, h, (i'_\tau)_{h, j, b}) (the total charge of the boxes of row i'). If \operatorname{IsIntegralOnNullCone}(pr, S, \varpi, \theta, B, \Theta), then |\operatorname{schurJet}_\kappa(\Theta)_{i', i}| \le p^{m - ((1/(p-1) - \rho)\cdot B + \omega \cdot \sum_{e\in S}\sum_\sigma \operatorname{couplingCount}(p, \kappa, N, ev, \sigma, pr_e(\sigma), e) - |\Phi |\cdot N^3\cdot(\lfloor \log_p B\rfloor + 1))}, where \lfloor \log_p B\rfloor is Lean's Nat.log p B (0 \text{ at } B = 0).
The argument in briefThe Schur jet is a combination, with integral coefficients y, of jets along products of minors (sum_schur_smul_nablaBasis), and is controlled by a jet of \Theta composed with linear forms. Choose the integral frames M of exists_null_coupling_frames for the coefficients y: the substituted family z = p^g\cdot M\cdot y is null, so by IsIntegralOnNullCone the coefficients of \Theta(\varpi z) of degree \le B are integral. The jet along the product of minors equals a jet of \Theta(\varpi z) times a factor whose norm is \ge |\theta |^{\operatorname{coupling}}\cdot |\varpi |^B\cdot p^{-m}. A jet of \Theta(\varpi z) along a monomial of exponent d (degree B) is d!\cdot(an integral coefficient), and for B > 0 the factor from \prod_x d_x! has norm at most p^{-B/(p-1) + |\Phi |N^3(\lfloor \log_p B\rfloor +1)} (Legendre: v_p(d_x!) \ge d_x/(p-1) - (\lfloor \log_p B\rfloor +1), at most |\Phi |N^3 variables): the factorials gain p^{-B/(p-1)} and lose at most p^{|\Phi |N^3(\lfloor \log_p B\rfloor +1)}. (At B = 0 the bound of the statement, with Lean's Nat.log p 0 = 0, holds as well.)
