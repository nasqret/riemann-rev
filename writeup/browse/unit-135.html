Chapter IV · Unit 135

Heights of families indexed by ℓ-adic embeddings, the product formula, and the height of one Gram determinant

note and structured proofs · 18 declarations · 7 structured proofs · 1 note

A family y of complex numbers indexed by the pairs (\ell, \iota) of a prime \ell and a ring isomorphism \iota : \mathbb{C} \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell has height at most c (HeightLe c y) when one nonzero H \in \mathbb{C}, a real \alpha and reals e(\ell) bound |\sigma H| \le e^\alpha for all \sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{C}), \lVert\iota(H \cdot y(\ell, \iota))\rVert_\ell \le \exp e(\ell), and \alpha + \sum_{\ell < M} e(\ell) \le c for all large M. Height bounds are monotone, add under pointwise products and powers, and the product formula for \mathbb{Q} (applied to the norm of H) gives HeightLe.nonneg: a bound for the constant family 1 is \ge 0. The second file proves the linear algebra behind the lower bound: a Fourier/positive-type estimate for singular minors (card_mul_sum_det_le), a Hilbert–Schmidt inequality (finrank_sub_le_re_inner_dyadSum), the greedy 'few translates' lemma (exists_few_translates), basis extension, and one_determinant, which bounds the height of the ratio of squared volumes \operatorname{normDet}(u_\theta)^2/\operatorname{normDet}(u_o)^2 by \alpha + 2\delta + k \cdot g from integral recombinations at every \ell-adic place.

  • defHeightLelines 21–23

    For a real number c and a family y : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C} (one complex number y(\ell, \iota) for every prime \ell and every ring isomorphism \iota : \mathbb{C} \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}), HeightLe c y ('y has height at most c') holds when there exist a complex number H, a real number \alpha and a function e : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R} such that (i) H \ne 0; (ii) |\sigma(H)| \le \exp(\alpha) for every ring automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}; (iii) \lVert \iota(H\cdot y(\ell, \iota))\rVert_{\ell} \le \exp(e(\ell)) for every (\ell, \iota) \in PrimePadicIso, where \lVert \cdot \rVert_{\ell} is the norm of \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell} extending the \ell-adic norm of \mathbb{Q}_{\ell} (\lVert \ell \rVert_{\ell} = 1/\ell); (iv) \alpha + \sum_{\ell < M, \ell \text{prime}} e(\ell) \le c for all sufficiently large natural numbers M (eventually along M \to \infty). Nothing requires e \ge 0, so c may be negative a priori; HeightLe.nonneg excludes that for the constant family 1.

    Product formula for \mathbb{Q}, in logarithms. For every natural number M and every rational number q with \max(|numerator of q|, denominator of q) < M: \log q + \sum_{p < M, p \text{prime}} \log |q|_{p} = 0, where \log is Lean's real logarithm of the real number q (\log x = \log |x| for x \ne 0, \log 0 = 0) and |q|_{p} = padicNorm p q is the p-adic absolute value (p^{(}-v_p(q)) for q \ne 0, and 0 for q = 0). For q \ne 0 this is the product formula |q|\cdot \Pi_{p} |q|_{p} = 1, the primes below M including every prime that divides the numerator or the denominator; for q = 0 both terms are 0 by the conventions.

    Hilbert–Schmidt inequality. Let H be a finite-dimensional complex inner product space with an orthonormal basis b indexed by a finite type m, n a finite type, \mathbb{C}^{n} = EuclideanSpace \mathbb{C} n with standard basis (e_x), P : \mathbb{C}^{n} \to H and R : H \to H linear, U \subseteq \mathbb{C}^{n} and A \subseteq H subspaces, and \varepsilon a real number.   • lemmaone_determinantlines 129–149argument

    One determinant. Let W be a finite-dimensional complex inner product space, \Theta a type (of twists), \theta : PrimePadicIso \to \Theta, and for each \vartheta \in \Theta a complex inner product space H(\vartheta) and a linear map u_{\vartheta} : W \to H(\vartheta); let o \in \Theta with u_o injective (hu). Let \alpha, \delta, g be real numbers with |\sigma(\det \operatorname{Gram}(u_o\circ x))| \le \exp(\alpha) for every ring automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C} (h\alpha), \operatorname{Gram}(v) =(\langle v_s, v_t\rangle)_{s,t\in S}. Let \gamma : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C} with \gamma(\iota) \ne 0 for all \iota (\gamma 0) and HeightLe g (\iota \mapsto |\gamma(\iota)|^{-2}) (h\gamma); let e : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R} with \sum_{l\in U} e(l) \le \delta for every finite set U of natural numbers (he); let \Lambda assign to every (\ell, \iota) \in PrimePadicIso and every ring homomorphism j : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_{\ell} a subset \Lambda(\iota, j) \subseteq H(\theta(\iota)); and let k \in \mathbb{N}.
