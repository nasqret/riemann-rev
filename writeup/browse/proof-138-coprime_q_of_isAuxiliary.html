lemma coprime_q_of_isAuxiliary

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell and a finite set I of places auxiliary for badPlaces D \ell: \ell is coprime to \prod _{w\in I} q_w, and so is g_0g_1\cdot N.
The argument in briefEach q_w (w \in I) is a prime not dividing \ell g_0g_1N, since w is not a bad place.
