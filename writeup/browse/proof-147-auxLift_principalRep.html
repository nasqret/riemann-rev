lemma Data.auxLift_principalRep

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data for P, \vartheta a tame character of D and j a lift index (1 \le j \le m). For every s in the group \prod_{w} \mathrm{GL}_r(k_w) over the chosen places w above g (s \in G(D)) and every form y of principal level on W_j: auxLift(D, \vartheta, j)(principalRep(D, spaceW(D, j))(s)(y)) = formAction(auxPair(D, \vartheta, j))(s)(auxLift(D, \vartheta, j)(y)); \theta_{j}^{\ast} intertwines the representation Data.principalRep on forms on W_j with AuxPair.formAction (s acting by \prod \psi_{w}(\det s_w) times translation by \operatorname{diag}(s, 1)) on forms on V_j.
The argument in briefTest both sides against formAction(s)\Xi for all \Xi (formAction(s) is bijective); adjointness and the equivariance of \theta reduce this to the invariance of the inner products.

    Two algebraic forms on V_j are equal when their inner products with every \Xi agree, and since formAction(auxPair(D, \vartheta, j))(s) is bijective it suffices to test against formAction(s)(\Xi). By adjointness (Data.inner_auxLift), \langleauxLift(principalRep(s) y), formAction(s)\Xi \rangle is a Petersson product on W_j with \theta(formAction(s)\Xi); \theta(auxPair(D, \vartheta, j))(formAction(s)\Xi)(g) = \theta(auxPair(D, \vartheta, j))(\Xi)(g\cdot(1, k)) for a lift k of s (AuxPair.theta_mul_eq_theta_formAction, reduceFamily_liftResidue); the inner products are invariant under the two actions (Space.inner_formRep, AuxPair.innerAt_formAction), and adjointness again (Data.exists_theta_to_principalForms) gives \langleformAction(s)(auxLift y), formAction(s)\Xi \rangle.
