What it saysFor parameters P (N_{0} = P.N_{0}, d = P.d) and admissible data D for P (Lean's Data P) and a family \theta = (\theta_{\iota}) of m-fold twists of D indexed by the pairs \iota = (l, \iota) of a prime l and a field isomorphism \iota : \mathbb{C} \cong \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_l (Lean's PrimePadicIso) that is fit (o: Data.IsFitFamily D θ): the family \iota \mapsto \mathfrak{z}_D(1)^{2}/\mathfrak{z}_D(\theta_{\iota})^{2} has height (HeightLe) at most 2\cdot\dim \operatorname{rows}(D)\cdot(2\cdot\log b_D\cdot\mathcal{B}(D) - h_{\mathrm{main}}(D)); here \mathfrak{z}_D(\vartheta) = D.\mathfrak{z} \vartheta = \det(u\ast u) for u = \Theta_D(\vartheta) on the rows (the square of its normDet, a volume) and 1 is the trivial m-fold twist.
