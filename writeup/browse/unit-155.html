Chapter I · Unit 155

The five final theorems: the Riemann hypothesis and the generalised Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions

note and structured proofs · 5 declarations · 1 note

The root module. Five one-line theorems: Mathlib's RiemannHypothesis; the same as RiemannZetaZerosOnLine; non-vanishing of every Dirichlet L-function on \operatorname{Re} s > 1/2 (the pole s = 1 of a principal character excepted); zeros of Dirichlet L-functions in the critical strip lie on \operatorname{Re} s = 1/2, for all characters and for primitive ones. Everything is New.grh (unit 154) and its two consequences by the functional equation, New.zeros_on_line and New.zeta_zeros_on_line.

    The proposition: every zero s of ζ with s ∉ {−2, −4, …} and s ≠ 1 has Re s = 1/2.

  • DirichletLNonvanishingRightOfHalf unit 153

    The proposition: for N ≠ 0, χ a Dirichlet character mod N, and Re s > 1/2 with χ ≠ 1 or s ≠ 1, L(s, χ) ≠ 0.

  • DirichletLZerosOnLine unit 153

    The proposition: for N ≠ 0, χ a Dirichlet character mod N, and 0 < Re s < 1 with L(s, χ) = 0, Re s = 1/2.

  • PrimitiveDirichletLZerosOnLine unit 153

    The proposition: for N ≠ 0, χ a primitive Dirichlet character mod N, and 0 < Re s < 1 with L(s, χ) = 0, Re s = 1/2.

    Mathlib's statement of the Riemann hypothesis: every zero s of ζ that is not −2(n + 1) for a natural n and is not 1 has real part 1/2.
