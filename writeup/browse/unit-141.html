The seesaw identity for the chain of theta lifts, and the index types of the data

note and structured proofs · 310 declarations · 83 structured proofs · 4 notes

The seesaw identity Data.seesaw: there is a unit c \in \mathbb{C}^\times, depending on D only, such that for every twist \vartheta some good datum \Psi has \iota_\vartheta(\mathcal{P}(c \cdot \varphi)) = B^\Psi_\vartheta(\varphi) for all ramified columns \varphi (\varphi = 0 included; no further condition on \varphi), i.e. the indefinite chain of m theta lifts Y_0 \to \cdots \to Y_m of the Poincaré series equals a finite admissible combination of definite chains X_0 \to \cdots \to X_m restricted to Y_m; this is the input of Theorem A (Data.short_forms). It is proved by unfolding the chain one lift at a time against Bessel periods (exists_isAdmissibleComb_unfolding), each unfolded period being a finite sum over a cut of cosets whose multiplicities divide powers of the conductor of \vartheta (exists_stage_cut, with the near-lines bounds of the lattices), evaluating pairs of lifts through zero terms of arrow pairs (AdmissibleEvaluation, EdgePair, DoubleLiftValue), and turning the resulting invariant admissible combination of reduced vacua into a good datum (GoodDatumExists). The folder also defines objects used across the proof: the places, characters, level prime N and index types of the data (IndexTypes), the lifts along the Y_j (GradingsOfY), the grid spaces (PairTensorData), and the Gauss-sum operator at the level prime used by the columns (NamesAtSplitLevel, LiftCharacters).
