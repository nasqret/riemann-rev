lemma Lift.exists_tameChart

What it saysLet p be prime, \Gamma a group over G_p with j : \Gamma \to G_p surjective (hj), f a positive integer with p \nmid f (hf) and e \in \mathbb{N} with e \mid p^{f} - 1 (he). Then there is a tame chart d for \Gamma whose index is e.
The argument in briefTake \varpi with \varpi^{e} = p and a chart with coordinate [u], u^\sharp = \varpi; let \Gamma' be the preimage of the open subgroup U fixing \Lambda (cyclotomic character mod N = p^{f} - 1 trivial) and \varpi — normal because an element fixing \varpi and \mu_N fixes every conjugate g\varpi \in \mu_N\cdot\varpi (the fixer of \varpi alone need not be normal), of index prime to p because G_p/U has exponent dividing f\cdot N; inertia moves [u] to [\zeta u] for a primitive e-th root \zeta, which gives the tame generator.
