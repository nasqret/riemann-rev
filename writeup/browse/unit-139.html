Chapter VII · Unit 139

At most one primitive label per Hecke eigensystem at a chosen place (via Galois representations of lifts)

note and structured proofs · 2 declarations · 2 structured proofs · 1 note

If an injective linear map L sends the forms of principal level on V to definite algebraic forms, makes every form of a Hecke eigensystem p spherical with fixed eigenvalues at the split places off the level, and intertwines the parabolic action at a chosen place x up to \psi(\det) and distinct characters \chi_l(\det) of orders dividing M_\omega (parahoric type), then at most one primitive label occurs in the p-part at x (no existence is asserted: if the p-part vanishes, no label occurs). Each occurring label c gives, by Frobenius reciprocity, a product label of L(y); L(y) is an eigenform for one family Q of Hecke polynomials that depends only on the eigenvalue functions F(w), not on c or y, so it has an attached 2-adic Galois representation r, and every representation attached to Q is labelled at x by the twist of c and the \chi_l (Attached.isLabelledAt, which the authors call local-global compatibility at tame level; its identification with the published theorems is open, see that import's literature record). Hence one r attached to Q is labelled by both twisted labels; at a tame inertia element s, every eigenvalue of r(s) is then an M_\omega-th root of unity (distinct irreducible labels have no common root), so the product of the roots of the first twisted label, a primitive (q - 1)-th root of unity with q - 1 > M_\omega, would have M_\omega-th power 1.

  • Attached unit 80

    Attached(P, ρ, j) takes a family of complex polynomials P_w indexed by the finite places of the CM field E, a representation ρ of a group H with a topology on an n-indexed vector space over the algebraic closure of ℚ_ℓ, and a map j from H to Gal(Ω/E). For H = Gal(Ω/E), j defaults to the identity. The representation is continuous, the trace pairing (X,Y) ↦ tr(XY) is nondegenerate on the linear span of ρ(H), and j is open. For every real η > 0, for most geometric Frobenius data (w,A,s), for every x in H with j(x) = s and every index i, the ℓ-adic distance between coefficient i of the characteristic polynomial of ρ(x) and coefficient i of ι_ℓ(P_w) is at most η. Here most means that each nonempty open subset of Gal(Ω/E) contains a nonempty open subset O such that, outside finitely many w, every Frobenius datum at w with s in O satisfies this condition.
