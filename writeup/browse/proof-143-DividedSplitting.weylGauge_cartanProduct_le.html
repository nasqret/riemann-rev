lemma DividedSplitting.weylGauge_cartanProduct_le

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r, n, N, m and l finite types, a a type, O : AuxPair E r n, z \colon r \hookrightarrow m, V a divided splitting (DividedSplitting N m a O z), p a prime with a p-adic place \iota(\iota_p \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_p), and M \ne 0 a natural number in the ideal of the product of the chosen places of O (hM). Let s be an a\times N matrix over O_E/M^2, L a divided lattice glued for s (H), h = (h₁, h₂) a pair of points of the finite unitary groups of I_N and I_m, y a coordinate vector of weight k^+ = O.colsPlus and \eta one of weight k^- = O.colsMinus. Then \operatorname{weylGauge}(O.\kappa, O.detExp, U.block V.ε h\cdot \gamma_L, cartanProduct V.ε 1 y \eta) \le |M|_p^{-V.\operatorname{loss}}\cdot(\operatorname{weylGauge}(k^+, 0, h₁, y)\cdot \operatorname{weylGauge}_{\iota \circ \operatorname{conj}}(k^-, 0, h₂, \overline{\eta})), the last gauge taken for the conjugate embedding \iota_p \circ \operatorname{conj}.
The argument in briefThe Weyl representation of a Cartan product is the product of the two Schur actions; bound each by norm_schur_inv_cols_le_weylGauge (for \eta with the conjugate embedding).
