lemma IsSplitLevel.norm_detCharAt_diagN_anti

What it saysFor a split level P for the prime N (instance h), finite index types n, n', a finite set S of places, \iota : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_N, a Hecke character \chi and k \le k' (e): |\iota(detCharAt(\chi, k')(diagN(n, S)))|_{N} \le |\iota(detCharAt(\chi, k)(diagN(n', S)))|_{N}; the N-adic size of the character value at \operatorname{diag}(1, \ldots, 1, N) on S does not increase from k to k' (and does not depend on the rank).
The argument in briefThe character value at \operatorname{diag}(1, \ldots, 1, N) on S is \chi-part (a unit) times N^{k/2} per place; its N-adic size decreases in k.
