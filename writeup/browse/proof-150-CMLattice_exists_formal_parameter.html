lemma CMLattice.exists_formal_parameter

What it saysNormal form of theta series at non-split primes. Let d be a prime \equiv 7 mod 8, C a CM datum for d with theta function \theta and lattice \Lambda, and p a prime with a p-adic place \iota_p. There are e \in \mathbb{N}, \varpi \in \mathbb{C}_p, \pi \in \mathfrak{o} and series S, E \in \mathfrak{o}[[X]] such that: e \ne 0, |\varpi | = p^{-\rho_p} (\rho_p = nonsplitWeight d p), 1/p \le |\pi | < 1, S(0) = 0, S - S^{p^e} = X + \pi \cdot E, and T(\varpi X) = \varpi \cdot S for some T \in \mathfrak{o}[[\varphi]] (CMLattice.phiSeriesRing); and, if \chi_K(p) \ne 1 and p \ge 13, there are u_0, u_1, u_2 \in \mathfrak{o}, R \in \mathfrak{o}[[X]] and \psi \in \mathbb{C}_p[[X]] with |\pi | = p^{(\chi_K(p)-1)/2} (that is p^{-1/2} if p = d is ramified and p^{-1} if p is inert), (e = 1 \text{ and } p = d) or (e = 2, u_0 = 0 \text{ and } |u_1| = 1), \psi = u_0X^2 + u_1S^{p+1} + u_2S^{p+p^e} + \pi \cdot R (as series over \mathbb{C}_p), \psi(0) = 0, and for every natural n prime to p there are N, \kappa \in \mathbb{C}_p with |N| = 1 and |\kappa | = p^{(1-\chi_K(p))/2 - 1/(p-1)} such that for every y \in \mathbb{C} with n\cdot y \in \Lambda there is V \in \mathfrak{o}[[X]] with (\iota_p-Taylor series of U_y\theta)(\varpi X) = (\exp(\kappa \psi)\cdot V)(NX). (The first part holds for every prime p; the second needs both \chi_K(p) \ne 1 and p \ge 13. N and \kappa may depend on n, and one choice serves every y with n\cdot y \in \Lambda; the integral factor V may depend on y and is not asserted to be a unit.)
The argument in briefLet q = p^e be the norm of the prime of K above p and m = q - 1. The part T of the formal parameter \varphi in degrees \equiv 1 mod m generates \mathfrak{o}[[\varphi]]; its inverse Z has only degrees \equiv 1 mod m, linear coefficient 1 and X^{m+1} \mid Z - X, and the coefficient of X^{m+1}, Z_{m+1} = -(\varpi^m)^{-1} (the first nonlinear coefficient), defines \varpi; the endomorphism a = \sqrt{-d} or p of degree q, with exists_unit_coeff_degree_le and the height of the formal group, gives |\varpi^m| = |a|. Then \varpi^{-1}Z(\varpi X) is integral and \equiv X - X^{m+1} mod \varpi^m; its inverse S satisfies S - S^q = X + \pi E with \pi = \varpi^m. For the second part, \log \theta = -L, and the part of L\circ Z in degrees \equiv p + 1 mod m is controlled by norm_coeff_le_of_deriv. A torsion translate, rescaled by \delta = nm, is \theta^{(nm)^2} times an integral series u \in \mathfrak{o}[[\varphi]] (rescale_translate_eq_theta_pow_mul; u is integral, not asserted to be a unit). The norm pair used for a rotation a = \zeta^i is (a, a^{p+1}), so each integral factor is \exp(\zeta^{-i(p+1)}\cdot L(\zeta^i z))\cdot \theta(z): the average of the rotations weighted by the character a \mapsto a^{-(p+1)} extracts the degrees \equiv p + 1 mod m of L (an unweighted average would extract the degrees \equiv 0 mod m).
