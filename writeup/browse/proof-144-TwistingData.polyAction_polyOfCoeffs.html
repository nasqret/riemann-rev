lemma TwistingData.polyAction_polyOfCoeffs

What it saysLet N be a natural number, W a commutative local ring, R a commutative ring and T twisting data over them (T : TwistingData N W R, with prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to R for s \in \mathbb{Z}/N, parity isEven and w \in R), N \ne 0, natural numbers k, n, g \in \mathrm{GL}_k(W) and a coefficient vector F : (\mathbb{Z}/N \to Monomials (Fin k) n) \to R: polyAction T g (polyOfCoeffs F) = polyOfCoeffs (\rho(g)\cdot F), where polyOfCoeffs F = \sum_{\beta} F(\beta)\cdot x^{\beta} and \rho = letterRep T n; i.e. \rho(g) is the matrix of g acting on forms of degree n in each letter.
The argument in briefExpand both sides in monomials; Sym^{n}(g_s) is by definition the matrix of linSubst g_s on degree-n monomials.

    polyOfCoeffs F = \sum_{\alpha} F(\alpha)\cdot \Pi_{s} x_s^{\alpha_{s}} (x_s^{\alpha_{s}} the monomial of letter s, renamed into the variables (\cdot, s)); g acts on the letter-s variables by linSubst g_s, which sends a degree-n monomial to a homogeneous form of degree n equal to \sum_{\beta} (coefficient)\cdot x^{\beta} (sum_monomials_eq_of_isHomogeneous), the coefficients being the entries of Sym^{n}(g_s); multiplying over s gives the entries of \rho(g), so the image is \sum_{\beta}(\rho(g)F)_{\beta} x^{\beta} = polyOfCoeffs(\rho(g)F).
