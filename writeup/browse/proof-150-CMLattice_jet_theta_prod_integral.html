lemma CMLattice.jet_theta_prod_integral

What it saysLet d be a prime \equiv 7 mod 8, C a CM datum for d (lattice \Lambda, theta function \theta), p a prime with a p-adic place \iota_p, I and \sigma finite index types, c \colon I \to \sigma \to \mathbb{C}_p (the coefficients of the substituted linear forms lie in \mathbb{C}_p), \delta \in \mathbb{C} with \delta \in \operatorname{quadInt}(d) (the image of \mathcal{O}_K), \delta \ne 0, |\iota_p(\overline{\delta})^{-1}\cdot c_{i, x}/d|_p \le 1 for all i, x, and y \colon I \to \mathbb{C} with \delta \cdot y_i \in \Lambda for all i (the CM integer \delta and the translation parameters y_i are complex). Then for every polynomial Q in the variables \sigma with coefficients in \iota_p^{-1}(\mathfrak{o}) (the complex numbers whose image under \iota_p is integral), the jet along \iota_p(Q) (jet: (Q(\partial)F)(0)) of the series \prod_i (Taylor series of U_{y_i}\theta read through \iota_p) substituted at the linear form \sum_x c_{i, x} z_x lies in \mathfrak{o} (the valuation ring of \mathbb{C}_p). Here U_y = CMLattice.twistedTranslate 1 y.
The argument in briefLet S be the \mathfrak{o}-algebra generated by the substitutions, into the rescaled linear forms \iota(\overline{\delta})^{-1}\cdot \ell_i, of the series in \mathfrak{o}[[\varphi]], of L' and of \exp(-L) (\theta = \exp(-L)). Every element of S has integral constant term and S is closed under \partial_x; hence jets along integral polynomials of elements of S are integral. Each factor of the product lies in S, because U_y\theta(\overline{\delta}z) = \theta(z)^N\cdot u(z) with u \in \mathfrak{o}[[\varphi]] (\overline{\delta} is an isogeny).
