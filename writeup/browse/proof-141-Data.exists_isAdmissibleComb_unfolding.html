lemma Data.exists_isAdmissibleComb_unfolding

What it saysFor admissible data D, k \le 2\varrho + 2 (l), a grading Y of Y_k (X plus a string of length s = 2\varrho + 1 - k, hs) and a function a of the real points \xi, there is \kappa \in \mathbb{C} such that for every tame character \vartheta (n = levelNorm \vartheta), every complex vector space M and every map S from tuples of cosets (y_i\cdotlevelY(\vartheta, i))_{i} to M there is w \in M with: (i) IsAdmissibleComb(y \mapsto S(cosets of y), all tuples y of points integral away from n, n, 1, (Q(\vartheta) = \emptyset), cutBound^{k}, w); (ii) \omega(w) = w for every b \in \prod_{i} D.columnLevel(i) and every linear \omega with \omega(S(q)) = S(i \mapsto inclXFin_{\mathrm{gradingY}(i)}(b_i)\cdot q_i for i < k, q_i otherwise) for all q; and (iii) for every form z on Y_k of the conjugate type, every ramified column \varphi and every linear L: M \to \mathbb{C}: if for all \xi, y with y_{2\varrho+2} = 1 and y_{0} \in chosenLevel, with F = unfoldStage(\vartheta, \varphi, \xi, y, k, Y)\cdot z (the statement's let), F integrable on B(E^{+})\backslash G implies \int_{B(E^{+})\backslash G} F = a(\xi)\cdot L(S(cosets of y)), then \int_{[U(Y_k)]} z\cdotliftChainY(\vartheta, k)(\mathcal{P}\varphi) = \kappa\cdot L(w), \mathcal{P} the Poincaré series (Data.poincare). The hypothesis of (iii) is, at each such (\xi, y), the implication from integrability of F to the period identity; it does not assert that F is integrable. The constant \kappa comes before the quantifiers over \vartheta, M and S.
The argument in briefInduction on k. Stage k + 1 is reached by moving the last lift across the integral (lift and descent are transposes) and unfolding one step (integral_besselGroup_unfold); the period over B(E^{+})\backslash G is a finite sum over the cut T with weights 1/coverMultiplicity (exists_integral_eq_sum_over_cut), which divide powers of n (exists_stage_cut); so w for k + 1 is built from w for k with S'(y) = \sum_{t} S(update y k t)/coverMultiplicity(t).
