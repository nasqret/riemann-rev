lemma exists_null_coupling_frames

What it saysLet p be a prime, \Phi and n finite index types, pr : \mathbb{N} \to \Phi \to \Phi, S \subset \mathbb{N} finite, N \in \mathbb{N}, \kappa \colon \Phi \to \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} (column counts), ev \subset \Phi finite, Y \colon \Phi \to n \to \mathbb{N} \to \mathfrak{o} and \theta \in \mathfrak{o}. There are matrices M_{\sigma, h} \in M_h(\mathfrak{o}) (for \sigma \in \Phi and heights h) such that (i) |\theta |^{\sum_{e\in S}\sum_\sigma \operatorname{couplingCount}(p, \kappa, N, ev, \sigma, pr_e(\sigma), e)} \le \prod_{(\sigma, h<N)} |\det M_{\sigma, h}|^{\kappa(\sigma, h)}, and (ii) for every commutative ring R of characteristic p and ring map g \colon \mathfrak{o} \to R with g(\theta) = 0, all j \in S, \sigma \in \Phi, heights h, h' and row indices i, i': unless (h = 1 \text{ and } (\sigma \in ev or (h' = 1 \text{ and } pr_j(\sigma) \in ev))), g(\sum_a (M_{\sigma, h}\cdot Y_{\sigma, a})_i^{p^j}\cdot(M_{pr_j\sigma, h'}\cdot Y_{pr_j\sigma, a})_{i'}) = 0, where Y_{\sigma, a} is the column vector (Y_{\sigma, a, k})_k.
The argument in briefFor each (\sigma, h) build M_{\sigma, h} from the divisibility of a diagonal matrix with entries \rho_e(\theta) = \theta^{1/p^e} (p^e-th roots of \theta in \mathfrak{o}): its rows are chosen (IndexDvd.of_det_dvd) so that the couplings with the partner rows vanish modulo \theta, at the cost of a determinant that divides the product A_{\sigma, h} of the selected roots (\det M_{\sigma, h} \mid A_{\sigma, h}, hence |A_{\sigma, h}| \le |\det M_{\sigma, h}|). The exponents of |\theta | in the products A_{\sigma, h} add up exactly to the coupling counts (an equality of exponents, valid also for \theta = 0).
