If u is a lattice in R, then for every g \in G_\ell the jumps of v at coeffEmb O g are J(g), and v lies in the class InClass F K U with F = \perp if w lies above g_0 or g_1 and \top otherwise, K(s, Q) = (w lies above g_0 or g_1, and for every R' \in \mathcal{R} some P kills s on R' at placeOver w with Q vanishing at the roots of P), and U the jump sets J as finite sets.
The argument in briefConjugate the lattice into R by the matrix P (IsLatticeIn); the local clauses of the column at the place above \ell (finite type, jumps, goodness off g) transfer to the lattice by jointly injective comparison maps and base change of jumps.
