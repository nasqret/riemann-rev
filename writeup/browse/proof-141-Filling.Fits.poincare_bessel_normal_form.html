lemma Filling.Fits.poincare_bessel_normal_form

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data, S admissible filling data (Filling.IsAdmissible) fitting V (h), \psi a column of level K_c, n in the unipotent radical of the Bessel group of Y (hn), a \in U(X_{0})(\mathbb{A}) and x with x_f \in stdPrincipalLevel (hx). Then the Poincaré series of h.columnCoords(\psi) over the rational Bessel points, at n\cdot\mathrm{inclX}_Y(a)\cdot x, equals \psi_{\mathrm{std}}(-\sigma(n)/(g\varpi))\cdot(A(conjWeylRep(a_\infty^{-1})\cdot\psi(a_f\cdot\eta)) \cdot W(x_\infty)), where \sigma = simpleRootSum of n (read through adelicEquiv), A = weylBesselEquiv, W = besselFn (at realHomeo(x_\infty)), \psi_{\mathrm{std}} the standard additive character, and u \cdot v = \sum_{i} u_i\cdot v_i the bilinear dot product (no complex conjugation of either vector). Here h.columnCoords is the coordinate map of the arbitrary fitting data h (Filling.Fits.columnCoords: values at class representatives in Bessel coordinates), not the specialized D.columnCoords, which first translates the column by the central element D.latticeScale(0).
The argument in briefWrite a_f\cdot\eta = \gamma\cdot r_d\cdot k; in the Poincaré series only the class d contributes, its terms sum to 1, and moving the rational and unipotent elements out produces the character of n and the Bessel function at x.
