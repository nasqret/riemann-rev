lemma Filling.finite_classUnip_inter_compact

What it saysLet S be filling data (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), c a class, m a finite index type (implicit), f:m\to (basis indices of V) and C a compact set of tuples (indexed by the infinite places v) of complex m\times m matrices (hypothesis hC). Then only finitely many points of C have the form (submatrix on rows and columns f of ratToInf(S)(u)_v){}_v with u\in\mathsf{U}_c(g): the set C\cap {that tuple : u\in classUnip(S,c)} is finite.
The argument in briefThe entries of such points are rational numbers of E whose infinite components lie in a compact set (C) and whose finite components lie in a compact set (h_c K(g)h_c^{-1} in the lattice basis); E meets a compact subset of \mathbb{A}_E in a finite set.
