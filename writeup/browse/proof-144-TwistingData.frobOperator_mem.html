The argument in briefEntry of a Kronecker product = product over letters; each factor, an entry of Sym^{n}(B_s), is a coefficient of linSubst(B_s) applied to a monomial, which lies in a Gauss filtration once each entry (B_s)_{\mathit{bc}} lies in G_{x(b,s) - k(\ell +2)u}. For even letters this is the product bound (\psi(s,m-1)\cdots \psi(s,0))_{\mathit{bc}} \in G_{\alpha(s,m,b) -(m+1)u} (induction on m); for odd letters the adjugate entry is a signed sum of products of k - 1 such entries, and \sum_{b} \alpha(s, m, b) = 0 turns the levels into -\alpha(s, m, b).
