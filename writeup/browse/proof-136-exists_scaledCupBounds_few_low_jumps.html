lemma Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.exists_scaledCupBounds_few_low_jumps

What it saysFew low jumps. Let the chart be unramified (hk), p \ge 5 (hp), S a side, a, \eta, W \in \mathbb{N} with \eta \ge 1 (h\eta; only this lemma of the file assumes it), b, n, r : letters \to \mathbb{N} and \varepsilon : letters \to \mathbb{Z}^\times such that for every letter j: b(j) + W + \eta \le (p - 3)\cdot a (ha); 6(a + \eta + W) < b(j) (hb); NoMultipleNear(p, 6(a + \eta + W), b(j)) (hs); the jump set U_j of S lies in [-\eta, \eta] \cup \varepsilon(j)\cdot[b(j), b(j) + W] (hU); f\cdot\sum_{u\in[-\eta, \eta]} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le n(j) (hn); and f\cdot\sum_{u\in U_j} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le r(j) (hr) (f the residue degree of the chart, Y the lattice of S). Then there is a \mathbb{Z}_p-submodule V of C with relLength(C, V) \le 11\cdot\sum_j (n(j)(a + 1) + r(j)(W + 1)) such that for every side T whose jump sets lie in the opposite windows (IsInJumpWindow(\eta, -\varepsilon(j), b(j), W, U^{T}_{j}) for every j), every pairing P of T and S, every c in the lattice of T and every d \in V: ScaledCupBounds(P, 0, c, d).
