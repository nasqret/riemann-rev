lemma exists_isPencil

What it saysFor a prime \ell, a stably unramified CM field E, a finite family of finite types n_i (i \in \iota ), finite sets S \subseteq S_0 (hS) of places, z in the inertia above the places of S_0 not above \ell (hz), a ring O of integers of a finite extension of \mathbb{Q}_\ell, representations \rho _i : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to M_{n_i}(O) balanced up to \beta _i (IsBalanced \ell S \rho _i L_i \gamma _i \beta _i M) (h), a neighbourhood W of z (hW) and a \in O with a \mid \ell ^M: there is J_0 such that for every N there are a helper place P_0, a finite set J of places with |J| \le J_0 and data x forming a pencil IsPencil \ell S_0 (i, g \mapsto \rho _i(g) \equiv 1 \bmod \ell ^M) W N P_0 J x, such that for every i the Selmer module of \rho _i modulo a for the places S \cup J \cup \overline{J} and the relations L_i \cup tieRelations J x i \cup {vanishing at \gamma _i} has O-length at most \beta _i.
The argument in briefInduction on a over irreducible factors. For a unit nothing is needed. From a to \varpi a: start from the pencil for a, and choose among pencils (with the same helper) one minimising the total length modulo \varpi a; if some Selmer module were longer than \beta _i, the mirror lemma gives a new prime P at which the values at Frobenius are not values at tame generators; tilting gives a slope t at which adding P with slope t strictly lowers the total length: contradiction with minimality.
