lemma Filling.amplitudeAbs_sq_le

What it saysLemma (Cauchy–Schwarz with Schur weights). For S admissible, classes c,c' and rational \gamma, for the cut Bessel functions: amplitudeAbs(S,c,c',\gamma)^{2}\le schurIntegral(S,c,c',\gamma)\cdot schurIntegral(S,c',c,\gamma^{-1}), where amplitudeAbs(S,c,c',\gamma)=\int\beta_c(y)\beta_c'(\gamma_{\infty}^{-1}y)\lVert W(y)\otimes W(\gamma_{\infty}^{-1}y)^{*}\rVert dy.
The argument in briefCauchy–Schwarz for the integral of \beta_c(y)\beta_c'(\gamma^{-1}y)\lVert W(y)\rVert\lVert W(\gamma^{-1}y)\rVert, splitting the integrand as \sqrt{f\cdot g} with the Schur weights; the frame-volume factors of the two weights cancel.
