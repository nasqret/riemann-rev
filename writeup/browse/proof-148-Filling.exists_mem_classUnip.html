lemma Filling.exists_mem_classUnip

What it saysLet S be filling data over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit), c a class, m a finite type (implicit), p:m\to\{0,\ldots,\varrho-1\},x the matrix with columns e_{p(b)} (e), u a matrix over E with rows the basis of V and columns m whose e_a^{*}-entries vanish for a\le\max p (h_0) and with classCoord(c)\cdot u integral (hu), and s an m\times m matrix over E with s^{H}=-s (h) and integral (hs). Then there is \gamma\in\mathsf{U}_c(g) and \tau\in E with \gamma=1+g\cdot(x\cdot u^{H}J-(u+x(\tau\cdot u^{H}Ju-s))\cdot x^{H}J) (J=\mathtt{ratGram}), and \gamma\in\mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g) if moreover the columns of u are orthogonal to the frame. (The census also lists the section variable \tau of the file; the statement binds its own \tau.)
The argument in briefWith x isotropic and orthogonal to u, and \omega with \omega+\overline{\omega}=1, the matrix is unitary; it lies in \mathsf{U} and its position is congruent to 1 modulo g.
