lemma Filling.IsFillingLevel.card_reducedImage_mul

What it saysFor the filling data S over E and a finite set T of finite places of E (stable under conjugation where stated). Let \prod_{w\in T}E_w carry an integral place structure P and F be a filling level (IsFillingLevel(S,T,F)), and assume g^{-1} is integral outside T. Lemma. Let B be a subgroup of GFin(S) and \mu an s-finite measure, carried by toPlaces of elements of B integral outside T and right invariant under toPlaces(B). Then \#reducedImage(S,B)\cdot\mu(\mathtt{congGroup}(F))=\mu(\mathtt{unitaryLevel}(R,1,\cdot)).
The argument in briefThe translates of K(g)_T by the representatives of the reduced image are disjoint and cover (\mu-a.e.) the integral group at T; by right invariance each has measure \mu(K(g)).
