lemma Data.ramifiedColumns_fill_rows

What it saysTheorem (filling). For parameters P, admissible data D for P and a unit c\in\mathbb{C}^{\times}, put for s\in\mathsf{G}_D B_s= goodProjTranslate(D,s)\circ\operatorname{poincare}(D)\circ(c\cdot\mathtt{ramifyingOp}(D,\mathtt{piPlaces}(D))) : Column(D)\to\operatorname{rows}(D), where goodProjTranslate(D,s)(y)=\#\mathsf{G}_D\cdot e_0(D)\cdot(s\cdot y) (s acting on principalForms(D,Y_0(D)) through the principal representation, e_0(D)\in\mathbb{C}[\mathsf{G}_D] the good idempotent, whose image is \operatorname{rows}(D)), \operatorname{poincare}(D)=\mathcal{P}_D is the Poincaré series map, and ramifyingOp(D,\mathtt{piPlaces}(D))=\prod_{w|\pi}(1-z_w) on Column(D),z_w the translation by the scalar centralGen(D,w), the product over all finite places w dividing \pi=\pi(D). Then the family (B_s)_{s\in\mathsf{G}_D} is spread with defect b_D^{-2} (IsSpread): for every \mathbb{C}-subspace F of \operatorname{rows}(D), with \varphi=\dim F/\dim\operatorname{rows}(D) (read as 0 when dim \operatorname{rows}(D)=0), there is s\in\mathsf{G}_D with \dim_{\mathbb{C}}\{\psi\in\mathtt{Column}(D):B_s(\psi)\in F\}\le((1+\varphi)/2+b_D^{-2})\cdot\dim_{\mathbb{C}} Column(D).
The argument in briefApply the abstract filling lemma (Fits.exists_translate_finrank_comap_le) to the principal representation of \mathsf{G}_D on principalForms(D,Y_0(D)) (restricted scalars), Q=\mathcal{P}_D\cdotcolumnCoords{}^{-1}-coordinates (Petersson products =\operatorname{vol}(\text{level})\cdotamplitude sums, petersson_poincare) and the good-label idempotents g_i (bad share \le b^{-8}/2 by bad_share_le), with the rank bound finrank_rows_le_mass: some translate keeps (1+\varphi+19b^{-4})/2 of the coordinates outside F. Composing with c\cdotramifyingOp costs \#piPlaces\cdot(N-1)^{-1}\cdot\dim Column \le\ldots in the preimage, and 19/2\cdot b^{-4} + \#piPlaces/(N-1)\le b^{-2} by the sizes of the data.
