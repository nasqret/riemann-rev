lemma detPeriod_insert

What it saysLet \alpha have weight - c and tame part \psi, with \psi_{w} = 1 for every w outside the places of \mathfrak{m}, and let \mathfrak{m}' be a modulus whose places are those of \mathfrak{m} together with one more place v \notin \operatorname{places} ( \mathfrak{m} ). Then detPeriod( n , \alpha , c , \mathfrak{m}' , \psi ) = \prod_{l < | n |} ( 1 - q_{v}^{l} \cdot X ) \cdotdetPeriod( n , \alpha , c , \mathfrak{m} , \psi ) with X = frobValue( \alpha , n , v )^{- 1} (trivialHecke q_{v} | n | X; frobValue( \alpha , n , v ) = \chi ( \varpi_{v} ) \cdot q_{v}^{| n | / 2} for the Hecke character \chi = spaceChar of \alpha).
The argument in briefThe datum for \mathfrak{m}' is obtained from that for \mathfrak{m} by the Hecke polynomial at v acting through the pair Weil representation (sum_heckePolyCoeff_smul_pairWeil); on class sums this becomes a sum over the coset representatives of the basic double cosets at v, which the q-binomial count turns into \prod_{l < n} ( 1 - q_{v}^{l} X ).
