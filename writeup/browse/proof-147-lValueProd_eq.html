What it saysFor P, D, a pair p =(\nu, z), a sign s = \pm 1 and a tame character \vartheta of modulus Q: lValueProd(\{chosen\} \cup Q, (\nu \cdot \vartheta^{s})^{-1}, z) = N(Q)^{z^{2}}\cdot(lValueProd(\{chosen\}, \nu^{-1}, z)\cdot \prod_{1\le z'\le z} ratio(lowerPoint(p, z'), \vartheta, s)^{2}), where lValueProd(S, \mu, e) =(\prod_{w\in S} Nw)^{e^{2}}\cdot \prod_{z=1}^{e} |L^{S\cup \overline{S}}(z, BC \mu)|^{2}, L^{S\cup \overline{S}}(\cdot, BC \mu) = \mu.L(S, \cdot) the Hecke L-function of the base change x \mapsto \mu(x/\overline{x}) without the Euler factors at S and its conjugate places, and N(Q) = levelNorm(\vartheta).
The argument in briefUnfold lValueProd over the union of disjoint sets of places, and write each |L|^{2} for (\nu \vartheta^{s})^{-1} as the ratio squared times |L|^{2} for \nu^{-1}.
