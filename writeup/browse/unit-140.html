Chapter VII · Unit 140

Good idempotents, labels and the transfer of traces between unitary groups

note and structured proofs · 46 declarations · 16 structured proofs · 2 notes

The objects of the proof's spaces: the spaces Y_j and W_j, forms of principal level at the chosen places above g = g0 g1, the good idempotent e0 of the group algebra of G = prod_w GL_r(k_w) and the good forms (its image), the rows (good forms on Y_0) and their dimension rowDim, the j-th auxiliary pair. Then three results: bad label tuples have small share in the regular representation (bad_share_le); a primitive cuspidal label has a cubic twist that avoids two given labels and agrees with it off irreducible characteristic polynomials; and for two spaces on which every Hecke eigensystem meets at most one primitive label at each chosen place (HasRigidLabels, proved for W_j and Y_0) every Hecke operator has the same trace and determinant on their good forms (by a comparison of simple trace formulas), so that rowDim equals the dimension of the good forms on each W_j.
