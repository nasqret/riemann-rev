lemma Data.range_goodLift_le

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D, a tame character \vartheta (Data.TameChar, not necessarily a twist), a lift index j and a subspace N: if N contains \theta_j^* y (D.auxLift \vartheta j y) for every Hecke eigensystem p, every good form y in the p-eigenpart and every good tuple c such that \theta_j^* y lies in the range of the action of tupleIdem c (h), then the range of D.goodLift \vartheta j is contained in N.
The argument in briefThe good forms are the sum of their Hecke eigenparts; on each, e_0 is the sum of the tuple idempotents, and each term lies in N by hypothesis.
