lemma Filling.volume_nonunits_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be a number field with a CM structure, \mathfrak{s} a shape, S filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s},T a finite set of finite places of E stable under complex conjugation (fT), and P an integral place structure on LocProd(T)=\prod_{w\in T}E_w with ring of integers R= P.R and unit group R^{\times}= P.intUnits. Suppose (k) that every place of T is a chosen place p of S (above q_0(S) or q_1(S)) or the complex conjugate \overline{p} of one: T\subseteq\bigcup_{i,p\in\mathtt{chosenPlaces}(S,i)}\{p,\overline{p}\}. Then 2\le b(S)^{9} and the Haar measure of the non-units satisfies \operatorname{vol}(R\setminus R^{\times})\le b(S)^{-9} (both read in [0,\infty] through ENNReal.ofReal).
The argument in briefAlmost every element of LocProd(T) is a unit, and such an element of R that is not a unit of R is divisible, at some w\in T, by the uniformiser \varpi_w; so R\setminus R^{\times}\subseteq\bigcup_{w\in T}\{x:\varpi_w^{-1}x\in R\} up to a null set. Each of these sets has measure \operatorname{md}(\varpi_w^{-1})^{-1}=q_w^{-1}\le b(S)^{-10} (q_w is a level prime), and \#T\le2\cdot\#(chosen places) =4f(E)\le b(S)/250, whence the bound b(S)^{-9}.

    The first claim, 2\le b(S)^{9}, follows from 1000\le b(S) (bound: 2\le1000^{9}). For the second, by monotonicity of the measure up to null sets it suffices that almost every x\in R\setminus R^{\times} lies in \bigcup_{w\in T}\{x:\varpi_w^{-1}\cdot x\in R\} (\varpi_w the unit of LocProd(T) equal to the uniformiser at w and to 1 elsewhere): a.e. x is a unit of LocProd(T) (P.ae_isUnit); if no \varpi_w^{-1}x were integral, every component x_j would have valuation 1, so x\in R^{\times}, a contradiction (simp_all with v(\varpi_j^{-1}x_j)\le1\iff v(x_j)<1).
