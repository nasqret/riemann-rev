lemma DividedSplitting.genResidue_smul_eq_iff

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r, n, N, m finite types, a a type, O : AuxPair E r n, z \colon r \hookrightarrow m and V a divided splitting (DividedSplitting N m a O z); let k and c be types, S a k\times c matrix over R_1 = O_E/M^2Q (the residue ring of O.levelModulus 2) and u \colon c \to k. Then \overline{t}\cdot(S reduced to R_0) = \overline{t}\cdot I_u in R_0 = O_E/M^2 (\overline{t} = V.genResidue, reduction by \operatorname{reduceToR}_0, I_u = inclMatrix u) if and only if for every chosen place w_i of O the reduction of S to the residue field k_{w_i} equals I_u.
The argument in briefWrite S = I_u + (reduction of an adelic matrix X); then \overline{t}\cdot(\overline{S} - I_u) = 0 modulo M^2 says that X vanishes modulo M, which by the Chinese remainder theorem is the vanishing at each chosen place.
