lemma IsRow.eventually_isBalanced

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, a finite type n, a row R (IsRow D t J R) (r), a chosen place w (hG) above g_0 or g_1 and not above \ell (h\ell ), M \in \mathbb{N}, and h the finiteness of the places above \ell or g (default argument): for all sufficiently large coefficient rings O there are a lattice u of R, \gamma and \beta such that normLength(\beta + \sum _b v(\det (u(\sigma _b) - 1))) \le 10\cdot f\cdot |n|\cdot \operatorname{cr}_\ell , \sigma _b \in \{\sigma _w, (\sigma _w^c)^{-1}\} with \sigma _w the tame generator at the chosen place over w, and u is balanced: IsBalanced \ell S u (crysRels u) \gamma \beta M holds for the finite set S = h.toFinset of places above \ell , g_0 or g_1. Its finiteness follows from S \subseteq badPlaces D \ell, whose definition also includes the places dividing the level N.
The argument in briefTake a lattice u of R; the row clauses give the spanning, the scalar elements and the inertia conditions above g; conjugate self-duality relates u at g and at (g^c)^{-1}; the local length at \ell is bounded by the rank of the crystalline cocycles, at the places above g by the inertia orders, and the determinant terms by \operatorname{cr}_\ell.
