lemma Filling.IsFillingLevel.exists_intermediateGroup_lines

What it saysLemma. Let E a number field with a CM structure (Field E, CMType E), \mathfrak{s} a shape, S filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s} (Filling), T a finite set of finite places of E stable under complex conjugation (fT) (the section's second set T_0 belongs to exists_intermediateGroup_one_prime and is no argument here: the hypothesis hT is about T), P an integral place structure on LocProd(T)=\prod_{w\in T}E_w (ring of integers R= P.R, Haar measure, modulus md, character \psi), F a level on LocProd(T) (Level (Fin (k(𝔰) − (6ϱ(𝔰) + 13))) (latMiddle(S, LocProd T)) (ϱ(𝔰) + 1 + 2ϱ(𝔰) + 5)) with the instance h : IsFillingLevel(S,T,F) (F is a standard level in the sense of IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel, P.\psi= charOnPlaces(S,T), and the unit F.g of F is the image of gIdele(S)=g(S) at T), and Sg a finite set of chosen places of S above q_0(S); assume S is admissible and E carries Weil data (WeilData E). Let d be frame-vector data (for each chosen place w above q_0(S): a flag and a nonzero vector over \mathbb{F}_w), and suppose (hT, an instance) that gIdele(S)^{-1} is integral at every finite place outside T. With K_T= unitaryLevel(R,1,J_T),\mathsf{M}= groupMeasure(F) and \varkappa(Z)= floorZeroForm(F,Z): there is an intermediate group Z (K(g)_T=\mathtt{congGroup}(F)\le Z\le K_T) such that (i) toPlaces(S,T,k)\in Z for every k\in latStabilizer(S) with \operatorname{reduction}(S,k)\in fixingSet(S,Sg,d), and (ii)\varkappa(Z)\cdot\mathsf{M}(K_T)\cdot(b(S)^{6k(\mathfrak{s})})^{\#Sg}\le\varkappa(K_T)\cdot\mathsf{M}(Z).
The argument in briefPartition T by the place of Sg (if any) that a place equals or is conjugate to: T=\sqcup_o T_o',o\in Sg\cup\{\text{nothing}\}. Each T_o' is conjugation-stable and carries a filling level F_o' (exists_isFillingLevel), to which the one-prime lemma applies (giving Z_o and the factor b^{6k} for each o=w, and 1 for o= nothing). The unitary group at T is the product of those at the T_o' (\varphi is a measurable group isomorphism onto the product), and under it the levels, \mathsf{U}, the phase and the Haar measures factor (\mathsf{M}=t\cdot\prod\mathsf{M}_o' by uniqueness of Haar measure). Hence \varkappa and \mathsf{M} of Q(Z)=\varphi^{-1}(\prod Z_o) are t^{2}\cdot t\cdot\prod and t\cdot\prod of those of the parts, and the inequalities of the parts multiply.
