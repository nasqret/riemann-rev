lemma Filling.amplitudeSum_eq_zero_of_not_charsAgree

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S admissible, \sigma a side, and T a stable set of rational points (IsStable(S,T)) such that no \gamma\in T satisfies CharsAgree(S,\gamma) (CharsAgree(S,\gamma): for all rational u,u'\in\mathsf{U} with u\gamma=\gamma u', unipotentChar(S,\mathtt{ratToFin}(S)(u))= unipotentChar(S,\mathtt{ratToFin}(S)(u'))). Then amplitudeSum(S,T,c,s,c')=0 for all classes c,c' and s\in\mathsf{G}.
The argument in briefChange the weight to \beta_c'(\gamma_{\infty}^{-1})\cdot[\gamma is the representative of its \mathsf{U}_c(g)-orbit of cosets]\cdot\varphi_{\gamma}(y), with \beta_c' taken at the point \gamma_{\infty}^{-1}= ratToInf(S)(\gamma)^{-1} (a constant in y; only \varphi_{\gamma} depends on y), \varphi_{\gamma} a cutoff for the stabiliser of \gamma\mathsf{U}_c'(g) in \mathsf{U}_c(g); each integral is then an integral over the stabiliser quotient of a kernel that transforms by the ratio of the unipotent characters, nontrivial because \gamma does not satisfy CharsAgree; so every integral vanishes.
