lemma exists_isEquivariantLift

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, a, b finite index types; W : Space(E, n, s) of even rank (\mathit{hn}: 2 divides |n|), V : Space(E, a, t), V' : Space(E, b, t') hermitian spaces; \mathfrak{c} a split tame character of weight \mu, with places S and local character \chi_{\mathfrak{c}} at S; \sigma : b \sqcup a \simeq n; v, v' rapid real data (\mathit{hv}, \mathit{hv}': all weighted norms finite, IsRapidDatum); and c \in \mathbb{C}. There are a space M of complex functions on n \times b matrices over the local product at S and a linear map \mathcal{D} from such functions to functions on U(W)(\mathbb{A}) such that (1) M, \mathcal{D} form an equivariant lift for \mathfrak{c}, W and the local character \rho = localChar(S, \upsilon^{|b|}, |b|)^{-1} (IsEquivariantLift 𝔠 W ρ M 𝒟: M consists of Schwartz–Bruhat functions supported on matrices with a left inverse; each \mathcal{D}\Psi, \Psi \in M, is an automorphic function on U(W); for every finite adelic g supported at S, the translate \Phi = \Psi((g_S)\cdot) lies in M and \mathcal{D}\Phi(x g) = \rho(\det g_S)\cdot \mathcal{D}\Psi(x) for all x; and \mathcal{D}\Psi is invariant under the elements of K_f trivial at S); and (2) for all partners \Phi, \Psi for \chi_{\mathfrak{c}} over O with respect to \sigma (IsPartner O σ χ_𝔠 Φ Ψ) with \Psi locally constant: \Psi \in M and \mathcal{D}(\Psi) = thetaLarge(W, V, \mathfrak{c}, v, \Phi) - c\cdotthetaSmall(W, V', \mathfrak{c}, v', \Psi).
The argument in briefExtend both theta integrals to linear functionals of the datum; the first, as a function of \Phi, factors through the matched partner \Psi (matchingPairs.exists_linear_factor); \mathcal{D} is that factor minus c times the second, on the space of second components of matched pairs; the laws come from the Weil action on local data.
