lemma inner_adjoint_comparisonForm

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D over P and a lift index j (1 \le j \le m) and a twist \vartheta, and \kappa \in \mathbb{C} such that for every choice i and point g, F^{\natural}_i(g) = \kappa \cdot \operatorname{cosetKernelSum} (D.auxPair \vartheta j) (bigChar \vartheta j) ((splittingV j \vartheta).\operatorname{stackOverGenScalar} i.1) (inl \circ inl) (lineMultiplier \vartheta j i) g (e; F^{\natural} = D.fNatural j). Then for every good form F on U(W_j) and every table \zeta: \operatorname{conj}(\kappa)\cdot \langle F, (goodLift \vartheta j)^*(\operatorname{comparisonForm} j \vartheta \zeta)\rangle = |\Gamma |\cdot g^{\operatorname{loss}}\cdot \operatorname{conj}(\beta_1)\cdot \sum_i \operatorname{conj}(\beta_{L_i})\cdot \int_{[U(W_j)]} \operatorname{conj}(F(g)\cdot F^{\natural}_i(g))\cdot \operatorname{tableTheta} j (\zeta(i, \cdot)) g dg, where \Gamma = (splittingV j 1).\operatorname{congruenceGroup}, loss = (splittingV j 1).\operatorname{loss}, \beta_1 = lineScale j, \beta_{L_i} = (glueLattice j 1 i).\operatorname{weilScalar}.
The argument in briefMove the adjoint across (inner_auxLift), expand \theta of the comparison form by theta_stdClassSum_productForm, use e to replace the coset kernel sums by F^{\natural}, and collect the sums over the congruence group and the classes into tableTheta.
