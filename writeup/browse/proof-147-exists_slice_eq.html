lemma PositivePart.exists_slice_eq

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, n', r finite index types; signs s, s'; W : Space(E, n, s), W' : Space(E, n', s') and D a splitting of W over W' along r, adapted at 1 with signs t (h). For all a and u (vectors of coordinates) there is U with P_{+}(s)\cdot U = L\cdot(-a) + I\cdot P_{+}(s')\cdot u + K\cdot(-a) (L, I, K the blocks of the mixed model of h, P_{+} the projections to positive coordinates) such that for every polynomial P in the positive coordinates, with v the Fock vector of P renamed into all coordinates: (slice(h, a, v))(u) = v(U).
The argument in briefU = J(-a, u, -a) for J(a, z, b) = L a + I z + K b; the projection to positive coordinates and the inner products of J are computed with the unitary frame; the value identity is checked on vacuum translates.
