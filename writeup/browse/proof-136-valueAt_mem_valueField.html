lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.valueAt_mem_valueField

What it saysFor a lattice datum L, a \in \mathbb{N}, \mu \in \operatorname{forms}(L) (hμ) and x \in kisinLoc(L) (hx): valueAt(L, a, \mu, x) = \mu(\varphi^{-a}x) lies in the field \mathcal{K}_a = valueField(a).
The argument in briefWrite \varphi^{-a}x with a denominator r and expand \tau^{h}\cdot r\cdot\varphi^{-a}x = \sum_l t_l\cdot v_l in the span of kisinLoc; apply \varphi^{a} to this relation first, then an S-linear coordinate \lambda_i to its coefficients (frame theorem): \lambda_i(\varphi^{a}(\tau^{h} r))\cdot x and \sum_l \lambda_i(\varphi^{a} t_l)\cdot\varphi^{a}(v_l) agree under valueAt, so \mu(\varphi^{-a}x) is a quotient of images of S-elements and values \mu(v_l), all in \mathcal{K}_a. (\lambda_i need not commute with \varphi, so \varphi^{a} must be applied before \lambda_i.)
