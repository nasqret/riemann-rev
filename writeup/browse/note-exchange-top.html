lemmaexists_common_coset_reps

Lemma 3.1 of the note · unit 147 · New/Exchange/BlockPartners.lean, lines 20–29

Let F be a semilocal field with compact open subring O, and b, n finite index types. There are a linear functional \ell on the complex functions on \mathrm{GL}_b(F) and a choice of representatives r_q \in \mathrm{GL}_b(F) of the cosets q \in \mathrm{GL}_b(F)/\mathrm{GL}_b(O) such that: (1) for every finite set t of cosets and every function f with f(r_q) = 0 for all q \notin t, \ell(f) = \sum_{q\in t} f(r_q); and (2) for every n \times b matrix y over F having a left inverse, there is exactly one coset q with y\cdot r_q primitive (IsPrimitiveMatrix O: entries in O and a left inverse with entries in O), and exactly one coset q with y\cdot r_q^{-1} primitive.

deflocalSection

Definition 3.2 of the note · unit 147 · New/Exchange/BlockPartners.lean, lines 33–34

For a semilocal field F, finite index types b, n, a multiplicative character \chi of F and an n \times b matrix y over F, localSection(\chi, y) is the linear functional \Psi \mapsto \ell(g \mapsto \chi(\det g)\cdot \Psi(y\cdot g)) on the complex functions \Psi on n \times b matrices, \ell the functional chosen by exists_common_coset_reps; by its property (1) it is \sum_{q} \chi(\det r_q)\Psi(y\cdot r_q) over the representatives r_q whenever only finitely many terms are non-zero.

defmatchingPairs

Definition 3.3 of the note · unit 147 · New/Exchange/BlockPartners.lean, lines 39–41

For a semilocal field F, finite index types a, b, n, a multiplicative character \chi of F and an identification e : b \sqcup a \simeq n, matchingPairs(\chi, e) is the space of pairs (\Phi, \Psi) with \Phi a Schwartz–Bruhat function on the a \times n matrices supported on those whose transpose has a left inverse (surjective matrices) and \Psi a Schwartz–Bruhat function on n \times b matrices supported on matrices with a left inverse (injective matrices), such that for every k \in \mathrm{GL}_n(F): localSection(\chi, (invLastRows(e, k))^{\mathsf{T}})(\Phi \circ transpose) = \chi(\det k)\cdotlocalSection(\chi, firstCols(e, k))(\Psi); that is, the \chi(\det)-averages \Phi, \Psi over \mathrm{GL}_a(F), \mathrm{GL}_b(F) satisfy \Phi(x_k) = \chi(\det k)\Psi(y_k), x_k the rows of k^{-1} indexed by a, y_k the columns of k indexed by b.
