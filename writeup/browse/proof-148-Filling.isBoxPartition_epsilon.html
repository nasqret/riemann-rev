lemma Filling.isBoxPartition_epsilon

What it saysUnder the standing hypotheses (IsAdmissible S), for filling data S over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit) and a class c: the function chosen by Hilbert's \varepsilon among those with IsBoxPartition(S,c,\cdot) has the property: IsBoxPartition(S,c,\varepsilon(\mathtt{IsBoxPartition}(S,c))) — that is, a box partition for c exists.
The argument in briefConstruct \omega: for q in \mathsf{U}_c(g)/\ker\theta (\theta the frame matrices at infinity) let I(y,q) be the indicator that the frame of y moved by q is bounded by b^{60\varrho+92}\cdotbesselSize; \omega=I(\cdot, 1)/\sum_q I(\cdot, q). A finite induction over the entries of the frame matrix, with integral Eichler elements and rational approximation, shows \sum_q I>0; finiteness comes from discreteness.
