lemma IsSplitLevel.exists_embedGL_scalar_eq_scalarFin

What it saysFor a split level P for the prime N (instance h), a finite index type n, w \in P and a norm-one finite adele \nu that is integral and equal to 1 at every v \notin \{w, \overline{w}\} (a): there is an integral unit x of \prod_{v\in P} E_v with \operatorname{reduction}(x)_{v} = 1 for every v \in P other than w and embedGL(inclGL(x\cdot1)) = scalarFin(n, \nu).
The argument in briefx = the components of \nu at P; it is a unit since \nu\cdot\overline{\nu} = 1, \equiv 1 off w, and both sides agree at P.
