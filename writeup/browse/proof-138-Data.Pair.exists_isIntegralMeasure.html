lemma Data.Pair.exists_isIntegralMeasure

What it saysFor admissible data D and a ramified pair p = (\nu , z) of the data (the tame part of \nu is nontrivial exactly at the chosen places): there are a function F from the tame characters of D to \mathbb{C}, a finite set K \subseteq \operatorname{Fin} 2 and m \in \mathbb{N} such that (1) F(1) \ne 0; (2) for every tame character \vartheta , F is an integral measure at the modulus of \vartheta (IsIntegralMeasure: F(\vartheta ') = \sum _a c_a \vartheta '(u_a) for every tame \vartheta ' with the same set Q, finitely many points u_a of U(1)(\mathbb{A}) and coefficients c_a integral at every prime l prime to Q); (3) for tame characters \vartheta , \vartheta ' and a place v \notin Q(\vartheta ) with Q(\vartheta ') = Q(\vartheta ) \cup \{v\} and the same character and tame part, F(\vartheta ') = F(\vartheta )\cdot \prod _{k\in K} \prod _{\varepsilon =\pm 1} (1 - y_v^\varepsilon / q_v^{z-k}), y_v the value of the base change of \nu \vartheta at the uniformiser of v; (4) for every twist \vartheta , F(\vartheta ) = F(1)\cdot N(Q)^m\cdot \rho _p(\vartheta )^{m+1}, \rho _p = Data.Pair.ratio p \vartheta and N(Q) = ϑ.levelNorm.
The argument in briefFor z \ge 2, F is the normalised squared norm of the lift (intAlgPart), K = \{0\}, m = 0; adding a place multiplies the L-value by its Euler factors. For z = 1, F = \lVert Mk\cdot N\cdot \Lambda \rVert ^{2}, \Lambda the determinant period of rank 2, K = \{0, 1\}, m = 1; integrality comes from the class-sum expansion with stabilisers dividing Mk, and the twist relation from the L-value product formula.
