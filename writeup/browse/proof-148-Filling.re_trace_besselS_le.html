lemma Filling.re_trace_besselS_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be a number field with a CM structure and Weil data, \mathfrak{s} a shape, S admissible filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s},i\in\{0,1\}, and x\in\mathbb{C}[\mathsf{G}_i] a central idempotent (IsCentralProj: x^{2}=x and its coefficients form a class function) with \operatorname{Re}x(1)\le b(S)^{-8}/2. Then \operatorname{Re}\sum_{s\in\mathsf{G}_i}x(s)\cdot\sum_{(c,a)\in\mathtt{Classes}(S)\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}(S)} amplitudeSum(S,\mathsf{S}(E),c,\iota_i(s)^{-1},c)(a,a)/\nu(S)\le b(S)^{-4}\cdotcolumnDim(S). (Notation: \mathsf{G}= GRes(S)=\prod_{i\in\{0,1\}}\mathsf{G}_i,\mathsf{G}_i=\prod_{w\in\mathtt{chosenPlaces}(S,i)}\operatorname{GL}_{\mathtt{BesselIndex}(\mathfrak{s})}(\mathbb{F}_w); \iota_i(s)= Pi.mulSingle(i,s)\in\mathsf{G} for s\in\mathsf{G}_i; amplitudeSum(S,T,c,s,c')\in\ell^{2}(\mathtt{BesselCoord}\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}) is the sum of \operatorname{amplitude}(S,c,c',\gamma) over the rational \gamma\in T that are terms over (c,s,c'), with the full Bessel functions; \mathsf{S}(E)= besselS of the rational middle form; \nu(S)= unipCovolume(S); \operatorname{energy}(A,\Omega)=\sum_{c,c'}\sum_{s\in\Omega}\lVert A(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu(S)^{2}; \mathsf{d}(S)= mainEnergy(S)= columnDim(S)^{2}/(\mathtt{besselDegree}(S)\cdot\operatorname{mass}(E,r(\mathfrak{s}))); \mathsf{d}_1(S)= besselEnergy(S)= \#reducedImage(S,\mathsf{S})\cdotcolumnDim(S); columnDim(S)= \#Classes(S)\cdot\#BesselCoord(S).)
The argument in briefPass to T= the chosen places and their conjugates, with a filling level F. Lift each s\in\mathsf{G}_i to \sigma(s)\in K with reduction \iota_i(s). Pointwise on K_T,\sum_s x(s)\sum_{(c,a)} amplitudeSum(\mathsf{S},c,\iota_i(s)^{-1},c)(a,a)/\nu= columnDim\cdot\sum_s x(s)\cdot\operatorname{phase}(g,y\sigma(s)y^{-1}) (if y\sigma(s)y^{-1}\in\mathsf{U}\cdot K(g) the amplitude sum is \nu\cdotunipotentChar\cdotidentity, otherwise no term of \mathsf{S} lies over the class pair and both vanish, by freeness). Integrating over K_T and Cauchy–Schwarz in s give \mathsf{M}(K_T)^{2}\cdot|\text{LHS}|^{2}\le columnDim{}^{2}\cdot(\sum|x(s)|^{2})\cdot\sum_s|\int_{K_T}\operatorname{phase}(y\sigma(s)y^{-1})|^{2}, with \sum|x(s)|^{2}=\operatorname{Re}x(1)\le b^{-8}/2 and, by the bottom-value bound for K_T split over the disjoint translates \sigma(s)^{-1}K(g),\sum_s|\int phase|{}^{2} \le2\mathsf{M}(K_T)^{2}.
