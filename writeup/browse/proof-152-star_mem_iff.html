lemma Parameters.star_mem_iff

What it saysFor parameters P, an exponent s, a prime p (instance hp), a finite place w of E(P, s), g : p \equiv 1 \bmod N_1(P, s)\cdot d(P), hw : p \in w, an integer a of E(P, s) and ha : a\cdot \operatorname{conj}(a) = p\cdot c for a natural number c coprime to p: \operatorname{conj}(a) \in w if and only if a \notin w (w divides exactly one of a and \overline{a}).
The argument in briefa\overline{a} = pc lies in w, so a or \overline{a} does; not both, for then p\cdot c \in w^{2} with c \notin w, i.e. p \in w^{2}, but p is unramified.
