lemma integral_mul_holMinorsPi_le

What it saysLet E be a number field, n a finite nonempty type, s signs on n, m \in \mathbb{N} and B \in \mathbb{R} with f\cdot(2|n|^3) \le m (hn; f the number of infinite places), m \le B (hm) and 10^{13} \le B (hB); o an order positive-first for s (ho); w and k weights that are K-dominant (hu, hd), with holomorphic gap 300m (hw, hk), and |k(\tau, i)| \le B for all \tau, i (hk'). Then \int |mh_k(h)|^2 dh > 0, where mh_k = holMinorsPi s k is the product of the holomorphic minors of weight k; and for every continuous A, every C \in \mathbb{R} and N \le 6m with |A(h)| \le C\cdot \lVert h\rVert^N for all h (\lVert h\rVert = maxOpNorm h): A\cdot mh_w is integrable and |\int A(h)\cdot mh_w(h) dh| \le C\cdot B^{7m}\cdot \int |mh_k(h)|^2 dh.
The argument in briefThe holomorphic minors are discrete series coefficients; their L^2 norms are inverse formal degrees, positive; the tail bounds of the coefficients integrate the polynomial growth of A.
