lemma SquareLaw.sum_eq_nextDatum

What it saysLet R be a finite commutative ring, a, b, n finite index types, \chi a multiplicative character of R, c \in \mathbb{C} and L, L' functions satisfying SquareLaw(\chi, swapFirstTwo, c, L, L') for the identification a \sqcup (n \sqcup b) \simeq n \sqcup (a \sqcup b) (h). Then for every complex function p on n \times a matrices: \#\mathrm{GL}_a(R)\cdot \sum_{y} p(y)\cdot L'(fromRows(y, inclMatrix(R, inl))) = c\cdot \#\mathrm{GL}_{n\sqcup b}(R)\cdot \sum_{x} nextDatum(p, x)\cdot L(fromCols(inclMatrix(R, inl), x)), where inclMatrix(R, inl) is the standard inclusion matrix 1_{0} in the other block.
The argument in briefApply the partner identity of the square law to the block partners of the point function at (x; 0) (partner_block), and use the \mathrm{GL}-equivariance of L and L' to move the blocks to the standard ones.
