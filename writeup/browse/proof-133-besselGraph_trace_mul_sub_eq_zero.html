lemma besselGraph_trace_mul_sub_eq_zero

What it saysFor a shape \mathfrak{s} , s = ( g , h ) \in besselGraph( \mathfrak{s} ) and a complex matrix M indexed by the basis of V that is block upper triangular for the levels FI.level ( h ): \operatorname{tr} ( M \cdot ( g - embedH( h ) ) ) = 0.
The argument in briefWrite g - embedH( h ) = embedH( h ) ( w - 1 ) with w = embedH( h )^{- 1} g \in \mathsf{U}; the trace of M \cdotembedH( h ) \cdot ( w - 1 ) vanishes term by term because M \cdotembedH( h ) is block upper triangular and w - 1 is strictly block upper triangular.
