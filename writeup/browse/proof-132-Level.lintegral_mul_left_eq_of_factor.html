What it saysLet A be a place (class Place: a locally compact Polish commutative ring with involution and Haar measure \,\mathrm{d}x, with an element \tau with \tau + \tau^{*} = 1, a skew unit \delta, a Haar measure \nu_{\mathrm{skew}} on the skew elements, a multiplicative modulus \operatorname{md} : A \to [ 0 , \infty ] such that v \mapsto Mv carries \,\mathrm{d}x to \operatorname{md} ( \det M )^{- 1} \,\mathrm{d}x whenever \det M is a unit, a unitary additive character \psi and a Plancherel constant c); the instances CommRing A, StarRing A, TopologicalSpace A, MeasureSpace A and P : Place A are arguments. Let \mathfrak{s} be a shape, T an \mathfrak{s}-form over A whose Gram matrix J_{T} = splitGram( T ) of V is hermitian and invertible (IsHermUnit), \iota a finite type and m \in \mathbb{N}; G_{T} = U ( J_{T} ), H_{l} = GT ( l ) (fixing e_{a} , e_{a}^{*} for a < l), Q_{l} = unitriGroup( l ), \mathcal{N}_{< l} = unipotentBelow( l ), f_{b} = besselFrame( b ) the frame (f_{b} = e_{b} for b < \varrho, f_{\varrho} = z). Let \nu , \rho be s-finite measures on G_{T} , s \in G_{T}, S a subgroup of G_{T} and \Phi : G_{T} \to [ 0 , \infty ]. The argument in briefExpress \int \Phi d \nu' through \mathsf{M}^{F} for every factorization \mathsf{M}^{F} = \rho \ast \nu', and apply it to \nu and to \delta_{s} \ast \nu.
