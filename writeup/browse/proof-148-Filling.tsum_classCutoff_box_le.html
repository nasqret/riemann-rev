lemma Filling.tsum_classCutoff_box_le

What it saysFrame count. Under the standing hypotheses, for filling data S over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit), a class c,s\in\mathsf{G}, an index type \alpha, archimedean points y,y',x\in[0,\infty] and maps k (classes), \gamma,\delta (rational points), w (weights in [0,\infty]) on \alpha (all implicit): if p\mapsto(k_p,\delta_p) is injective (hi), if for every p with w_p\ne0: \gamma_p\in termsOver(c,s,k_p),\delta_p^{-1}\gamma_p\in\mathsf{U}_{k_p}(g),\gamma_p^{-1}y\in boxedPoints and w_p\le\beta_{k_p}(\delta_p^{-1}y')\cdot x (H), and if V is a real subspace (implicit) containing the differences F_p-F_q of the moved frames F_p= (frameVector(\mathtt{ratToInf}(\gamma_p)_v^{-1}y_v)){}_v for p,q in the support (hV), then \sum_p w_p\le b^{6n^{2}\cdot\dim V}\cdot x.
The argument in briefColour p by its moved frame F_p\in V: distinct frames are 1/(N\cdot\mathtt{boxSize})-separated and bounded, so there are at most b^{6n^{2}\dim V} colours; within a colour the cutoffs \beta_{k_p} sum to at most 1. Separation comes from integrality: close frames force equal frames, by a descending induction over the entries.
