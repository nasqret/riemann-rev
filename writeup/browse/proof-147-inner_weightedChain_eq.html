lemma IsTriangle.inner_weightedChain_eq

What it saysIn a triangle (h) with non-zero weights and G (hG), let U be a complex vector space, s : U \to V and S(t, i) endomorphisms of U with w(t, 0)\cdot \langle a(t, i, 0)(j(i)x), a(t, i, 0)(j(i)(s y))\rangle = \langle x, s(S(t, i)y)\rangle for i < m and all t, x, y (hS). Then for all n, i with i + n = m there is \kappa with M(n\cdot n, \kappa) such that every t has an endomorphism T of U with \det T = \prod_{i\le k<m} \det S(t, k) and \langleweightedChain(w, a, t, i, n)(j(i)x), weightedChain(w, a, t, i, n)(j(i)(s y))\rangle = \kappa(t)\cdot(w(t, 0)\cdot w(t, 1)^{n}/w(t, n))\cdot \langle x, s(T y)\rangle for all x, y.
The argument in briefInduction on n (Nat.rec): move the first arrow of row i down by IsTriangle.weightedChain_descent and use the formula for row i + 1.
