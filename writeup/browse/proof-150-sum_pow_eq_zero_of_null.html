lemma sum_pow_eq_zero_of_null

What it saysLet p be a prime, Q a commutative ring of characteristic p, J a finite index type, B, e \in \mathbb{N} and \rho a proposition with e = 2, or e = 1 and \rho. Let t, L \colon J \to Q with t_i - t_i^{p^e} = L_i and t_i^{p^n} = 0 for all i and all n \ge B. If \sum_i L_i^{p^j}\cdot L_i = 0 for every j such that j is odd or \rho holds, then \sum_i t_i^{p+1} = 0 and \sum_i t_i^{p+p^e} = 0.
The argument in brieft_i = \sum_{l<B} L_i^{p^{el}}; expanding the two sums gives sums \sum_i L_i^{p^a}\cdot L_i^{p^b} with a + b odd (or \rho), each of which is a Frobenius power of a vanishing coupling.

    In characteristic p, t_i = \sum_{l<B} L_i^{p^{el}} + t_i^{p^{eB}} = \sum_{l<B} L_i^{p^{el}} (eq_sum_pow_of_sub_pow_eq, t_0). Expanding t_i^{p+1} = t_i^p\cdot t_i and t_i^{p+p^e} = t_i^p\cdot t_i^{p^e} over the indices l, l' of the two factors and exchanging sums, each \sum_i t_i^{p+1}, \sum_i t_i^{p+p^e} becomes a sum of terms \sum_i L_i^{p^a}\cdot L_i^{p^b} whose sum of Frobenius exponents is a + b = 1 + e(l + l') for t_i^p\cdot t_i and a + b = 1 + e(l + l' + 1) for t_i^p\cdot t_i^{p^e} (both odd when e = 2; when e = 1, \rho is used); every such term vanishes, by induction on (a, b) (Nat.recDiagAux): \sum_i L_i^{p^a}L_i^{p^b} is the p^{\min(a, b)}-th power (Frobenius is additive) of \sum_i L_i^{p^{|a-b|}}\cdot L_i, which is 0 by hq.
