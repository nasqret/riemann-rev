lemma card_le_torsion_of_isotropic

What it saysMirror lemma. Let O be a commutative ring, G a group, n a finite type, I an ideal of O with O/I finite, \rho : G \to M_n(O) a homomorphism whose O-span is all of M_n(O) (hρ), \chi : G \to O a homomorphism, c an endomorphism of G with c(c(g)) = g (_{hc}), H and U subgroups of G with H normal, \gamma , g_0, z \in G, s \in O, y : G \to (O/I)^n, a type W and additive maps x_w(g) : (O/I)^n \to \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} (w \in W, g \in G), such that: (hi) w is determined by the restriction of x_w to U; (hU) \rho (g) acts trivially on (O/I)^n for g \in U; (hH) c(H) \subseteq H and H \subseteq U; (\kappa ) every commutator [g, \gamma ] lies in H; (p, s_2) s - 1 and s + 1 are units; (hγ) \rho ((c\gamma )^{-1}) = s\cdot 1, \rho (\gamma ) = s\cdot 1, \chi (c\gamma ) = \chi (\gamma ) = 1 and y(\gamma ) = 0; (hz) z \in U and c(z) \in U; (hy) y is a cocycle, y(gh) = y(g) + \rho (g)\cdot y(h); (hu) some coordinate y(g_0)_i is a unit; (hW) x_w(a\cdot g b g^{-1})(m) = x_w(a)(m) + x_w(b)(\chi (g)\cdot \rho (g)^{-1}\cdot m) for a, b \in U; (hq) x_w(zh)(t\cdot y(zh)) = x_w(c(zh))(t\cdot y(c(zh))) for h \in H and t \in O (isotropy on the coset zH); and (hδ) \rho (g) = \alpha \cdot 1 and \rho ((cg)^{-1}) = \alpha '\cdot 1. Then |W| is at most the number of m \in (O/I)^n with (\alpha - \chi (cg)\cdot \alpha ')\cdot m = 0.
The argument in briefUse the commutator \tau = [g_0, \gamma ] \in H, at which y has a unit coordinate. Isotropy on zH and equivariance show that each x_w(c\tau ) vanishes on the image of multiplication by \alpha - \chi (cg)\alpha ' (applied to the vectors A\cdot y(\tau ), which span all of (O/I)^n), so x_w(c\tau ) factors through the cokernel; w \mapsto x_w(c\tau ) is injective, and the dual of the cokernel has the size of the torsion.
