lemma Parameters.exists_semilinear_isJetConjugate

What it saysConjugates of jets. Let V be a standard hermitian space over E(P, s) of rank n, R a set of finite places with R \cap \overline{R} = \emptyset (Set.IsConjDisjoint), and \sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{C}/K). There are t \in \mathbb{C}, c \in \mathbb{C}, a finite idele z of norm one and a \sigma-semilinear endomorphism L \colon \mathcal{S}(V) \to \mathcal{S}(V) of the ambient Schwartz space (the map constructed takes values in \mathcal{W}_f and is composed with the inclusion \mathcal{W}_f \subseteq \mathcal{S}(V)) such that: |t|^2 = \Omega^{-1} (\Omega = \operatorname{bigOmega}(P, s)); c \ne 0; for every f \in \mathcal{W}_f, \sigma applied to the coefficients of T_{\mathrm{per}} f equals T_t(L f) with every variable (\tau, k) renamed to (\pi^{-1}(\tau), k), \pi = \operatorname{placePerm}(s, \sigma) (IsJetConjugate); for f \in \mathcal{W}_f and every finite point g: L(\omega(1, g)f) = (\sigma(\Delta(g))/\Delta(g))\cdot \omega(1, g)(L f), \Delta = finMultiplier (multiplierRatio); for all finite adelic x, finite points g and all points y: |L(\varrho(\operatorname{finVec} x)\omega(1, g)\theta^{\circ})(y)| \le \exp(c_P\cdot(N_1|n| + 1)^2), \theta^{\circ} = \operatorname{intVacuum}(V), c_P = Parameters.c P; and for every Schwartz–Bruhat function \Psi above R: L(\operatorname{name}_R(\Psi)(vac)) = c\cdot \omega(1, z)(\operatorname{name}_R(\sigma \circ \Psi)(vac)).
The argument in briefFrom exists_isJetConjugate_generator: F, F', t and B_\sigma. Define L as \sigma \circ T_{\mathrm{per}} followed by the inverse of the injective map g \mapsto T_t g (places renamed) on \mathcal{W}_f, a \sigma-semilinear map \mathcal{S}(V) \to \mathcal{W}_f; the witness is L followed by the inclusion \mathcal{W}_f \subseteq \mathcal{S}(V) (V.finitePart.subtype ∘ₛₗ L). Then L(\varrho(x)F) = \varrho(B_\sigma x)F'. semilinear_intertwines_weil turns the character identity \sigma \psi_0(z) = \psi_0(B \overline{B} z) and the translation identity into the vacuum, multiplier and name identities. The size bound comes from writing \omega(1, g)vac as a theta series over a lattice and bounding it by a packing estimate for separated points.
