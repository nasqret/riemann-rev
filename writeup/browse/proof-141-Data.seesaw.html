lemma Data.seesaw

What it saysThe seesaw identity. For admissible data D there is a unit c \in \mathbb{C}^{\times} (a non-zero complex number) such that for every twist \vartheta there is a finitely supported family \Psi : \operatorname{Translations}(D) \to_{0} \mathbb{C} with IsGoodDatum(\vartheta, \Psi) and \iota_{\vartheta}(\mathcal{P}(c\cdot\varphi)) = B^{\Psi}_{\vartheta}(\varphi) for every ramified column \varphi \in D.ramifiedColumns, the image of the ramifying operator \prod_{w\mid\pi}(1 - z_w) on \operatorname{Column}(D) (\varphi is any element of that subspace, \varphi = 0 included, read in \operatorname{Column}(D) through the inclusion ramifiedColumns.subtype; no shortness, integrality or non-vanishing condition on \varphi). Here \mathcal{P} = Data.poincare is the Poincaré series (columns on X_{0} \to forms of principal level on Y_{0}), \iota_{\vartheta} = Data.ι \vartheta is the chain of the m = 2\varrho + 2 theta lifts through Y_{0}, \ldots, Y_m (each lift liftY carrying its level-index scalar [K_{0} : levelY(\vartheta, j)]) followed by the identification with algebraic forms on Y_m, and B^{\Psi}_{\vartheta} = Data.Bμ \vartheta \Psi is the combination \sum_{x} \Psi(x)\cdot(chain of m definite lifts X_{0} \to \cdots \to X_m with the data translateDatum(x)) followed by the restriction to Y_m (Data.restrictToY: restriction to the points \operatorname{diag}(h, 1), the branching matrix, and the factor \prod_{\tau} \tau(\overline{\pi})^{-\mathrm{D.branchExp}(\tau)}). IsGoodDatum(\vartheta, \Psi) (unit 145) says: \Psi is supported on admissibleTranslations(levelNorm(\vartheta)\cdot N) (\pi_g\cdot t_k integral at every place not dividing levelNorm(\vartheta)\cdot N), coefficients \Psi(t) in integralAway(levelNorm(\vartheta)) (denominator 1), \sum_{t} |\sigma(\Psi(t))| \le \exp(x^{60}) for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C} when Q(\vartheta) = \emptyset, joint invariance, and chain_at_N (the definite chain of \Psi is the combination of the paired chains with the data pairTranslates of the projections at the places above \pi); the two moduli, levelNorm(\vartheta)\cdot N and levelNorm(\vartheta), differ by the level prime N. The unit c is chosen before the quantifier over twists, so it depends on D only, not on \vartheta or \varphi; one \Psi (depending on \vartheta) serves all ramified columns at once. Lean gives no bound on |c|.
The argument in briefRun the unfolding (exists_isAdmissibleComb_unfolding) up to the definite space Y_m with S(q) = the pure tensor of the evaluated data at the cosets q; the last stage is a matrix coefficient of the paired chain (unfoldStage_even) and pairs with the restriction map R (exists_last_stage_map). The resulting w, scaled by c\cdot a, is an admissible, invariant combination of reduced vacua (exists_isAdmissibleComb_reducedVacuum), whose paired chain is the definite chain of a good datum (exists_isGoodDatum_of_invariant). Here c = normUnit(a\cdot C/cutBound^{2m}), a unit of \mathbb{C} (normUnit takes unit values, also at 0); when a\cdot C \ne 0 it clears the constant a\cdot C and the denominator cutBound^{2m}.
