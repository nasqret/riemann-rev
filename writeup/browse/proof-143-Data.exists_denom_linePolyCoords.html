lemma Data.exists_denom_linePolyCoords

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D over P and a lift index j: there are a natural number d \le 31m\cdot \sum_\tau |\operatorname{DetIndex} j \tau | and an integer H with 0 < H \le(d^d)^3 such that for every line choice i, matrix Y over E and index l, Q = H\cdot \operatorname{linePolyCoords} j i Y l is homogeneous of degree d (Q(c\cdot X) = c^d\cdot Q \text{ for } c \ne 0), and Q has coefficients in every subring A of \mathbb{C} that contains all \tau(Y_{ab}).
The argument in briefH is the hook number; by the dual isometry, H times the coordinates is an integral combination of the coefficients of the determinant product, which are polynomials in the entries; homogeneity from the substitution law of firstColsCoord.
