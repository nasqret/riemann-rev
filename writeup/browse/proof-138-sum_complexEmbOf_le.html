lemma sum_complexEmbOf_le

What it saysFor a prime \ell with \iota \ell : \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell fixed, a stably unramified CM field E and a : (E \to \mathbb{C} embeddings) \to \mathbb{N}: \sum _{v | \ell } \sum _{\sigma \in S_v} a(\sigma ) \le \sum _\sigma a(\sigma ), the outer sum over the finite places v of E above \ell, where S_v is the set of complex embeddings complexEmbOf (e_v \circ (E \to \Omega )) g, g \in G_\ell , e_v : \Omega \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell the embedding of the local chart localChart \ell E v. (The sets S_v are pairwise disjoint.)
The argument in briefThe double sum is the sum over the union of the sets S_v, which are pairwise disjoint because the local chart at v induces the place over v; a sum of natural numbers over a subset is at most the sum over all embeddings.

    The double sum \sum _v \sum _{\sigma \in S_v} a(\sigma ) equals the sum of a over the union of the S_v once the S_v are pairwise disjoint, and this is \le \sum _\sigma a(\sigma ) (a \ge 0, final gcongr of step 2). Disjointness: suppose \sigma = complexEmbOf e_v g = complexEmbOf e_{v'} g'; it is shown that v = v' by comparing their prime ideals.
