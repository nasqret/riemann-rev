lemma isConjSelfDual_of_attached

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime j with \iota j : \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_j fixed, a finite type n, a type M, a function f : G_f(E, n) \to M \to \mathbb{C} (a form), a system Q of Hecke polynomials at the finite places, and a representation r : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \operatorname{GL}_n(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_j): if f is an eigenform for Q (IsEigenform f Q) and r is attached to Q (Attached Q r), then r is conjugate self-dual for |n|: IsConjSelfDual |n| r, i.e. r is continuous, has nondegenerate trace form, and \operatorname{tr} r(g^c) = cycInv(g)^{|n|-1}\cdot \operatorname{tr} r(g^{-1}) for all g.
The argument in briefContinuity and nondegeneracy are part of Attached. For the trace identity, the Hecke polynomials at w and \overline{w} are dual (heckeSplit_dual); passing to the limit along Frobenii approaching g gives c_0(r(g))\cdot c_k(r(g^c)) = c_1(r(g))\cdotcycInv(g)^k for the coefficients of characteristic polynomials (|n| = k + 1), which is the trace identity.
