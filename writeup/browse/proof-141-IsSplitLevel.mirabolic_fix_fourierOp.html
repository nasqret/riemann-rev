lemma IsSplitLevel.mirabolic_fix_fourierOp

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, P a split level for the prime N (instance, taken by Lean), n a finite index type, \pi a representation of \mathrm{GL}_{n+1}(\prod_{w\in P} E_w), \varphi a vector of \pi, T a linear endomorphism of \pi and g \in mirabolic (G). Assume h: \pi(a)(T(\pi(b)\varphi)) = \varphi for all a, b \in \mathrm{GL}_{n+1}(\prod E_w) with a\cdot g\cdot b \in mirabolic. Then T(F \varphi) = F \varphi for F = fourierOp(\pi).
The argument in briefWrite u_{0}^{-1}gu_{0} = inclGL(k) with the last row of \overline{k} equal to e; reindex the Gauss sum by y \mapsto \overline{k}y and check each term with the hypothesis h.
