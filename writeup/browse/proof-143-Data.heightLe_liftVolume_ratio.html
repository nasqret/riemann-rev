lemma Data.heightLe_liftVolume_ratio

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D, a family \theta of m-fold twists indexed by the pairs \iota of a prime and an isomorphism \mathbb{C} \cong \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell (PrimePadicIso) that is fit (O: D.IsFitFamily \theta), and a lift index j: the family \iota \mapsto D.liftVolume 1 j / D.liftVolume (\theta \iota) j has height (HeightLe) at most \operatorname{rowDim}\cdot(k\cdot(\log b\cdot B_0 - \mathsf{U})), where rowDim = D.rowDim (the dimension of the rows), k = D.k, \log b = D.logB, B_0 = D.B₀ = f\cdot a\cdot(\log b)^3/(m\cdot k) and \mathsf{U} = D.dirCost (the cost of one direction).
The argument in briefWrite each lift volume through the comparison forms: \operatorname{normDet}(\operatorname{goodLift})^2\cdot \det(\operatorname{compressedGramOp}) = \mathsf{h}^{\dim }\cdot |\gamma |^{2\dim }\cdot \operatorname{normDet}(A)^2; with \eta = (\theta \iota)^j the ratio u(\eta)/u(1) of determinants of compressedGramOp has height at most 2\cdot \dim \cdot r\cdot B_0 (archimedean bound of the common multiplier u(1)) plus \dim \cdot r\cdot B_0 (local exponents \dim \cdot r\cdot \beta_\ell, \sum_\ell \beta_\ell \le B_0 over every finite set of primes), and |\gamma(\eta)/\gamma(1)|^2 is the Hecke value ratio, whose inverse has height at most 10m x^{999} + k(6B_0 - \mathsf{U}).
