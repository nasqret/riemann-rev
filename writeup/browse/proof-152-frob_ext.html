lemma Parameters.frob_ext

What it saysFor parameters P, an exponent s, a prime p (instance hp), a commutative ring R of characteristic p, an infinite place v of E(P, s), h : p \nmid N_1(P, s), a ring homomorphism \varphi : \mathcal{O}_E \to R, a natural number n, a ring automorphism c of \mathcal{O}_E and an integer e with he : (c = \operatorname{id} and e = 1) or (c = \operatorname{conj} and e = -1): [\varphi(galOfPlace(P, s)(v) x) = \varphi (c x)^{p^n} for every x \in \mathcal{O}_E] if and only if [\varphi(galOfPlace(P, s)(v) \zeta (P, s)) = \varphi(galOfPlace(P, s)(u) \zeta (P, s)), where u = placeOfInt(P, s, e\cdot p^n) and \zeta (P, s) = zetaInt(P, s), and \chi _K(p)^n\cdot e\cdot \varphi (\sqrt{-d(P)}) = \varphi (\sqrt{-d(P)}), \sqrt{-d(P)} = sqrtNegDInE(P, s)]; here galOfPlace(P, s)(x)(\cdot ) = galOfPlaceInt(x)(\cdot ).
The argument in briefBoth sides of the left condition are ring homomorphisms in x (Frobenius in characteristic p), and \mathcal{O}_E is generated by \zeta (P, s) and c_0; at \zeta (P, s) the condition is the one on places, at c_0 it is the one on \sqrt{-d(P)} (eq_map_c₀_pow_iff).
