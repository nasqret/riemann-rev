lemma TwistingData.finrank_excessCond_le_volume

What it saysLet N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, K a field and T : TwistingData N W K (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to K, parity isEven), K of characteristic p, k \in \mathbb{N}, indices a, a' \in Fin k with a + 1 = a' (ha), n \in \mathbb{N} and h \in \mathbb{R}: \dim_K excessCond T n a a' \perp h \le [L : L \cap \mathcal{I}]\cdot \operatorname{vol}(excessCone T a' (h + N(k+1)) \cap slab k (fun \_ \mapsto n + k + 1)), where L = levi W (twoBlock k a') (block diagonal for the blocks \{b < a'\}, \{b \ge a'\}), \mathcal{I} = parahoric W (\uparrow) the parahoric subgroup for the labelling b \mapsto b (the lower Iwahori subgroup), [L : L \cap \mathcal{I}] = relIndex of \mathcal{I} in L, and the slab asks \sum_{b} \beta(b, s) \in [n + k, n + k + 1] for every letter s (volume as a real number).
The argument in briefVolume as the limit of lattice-point counts (the set is convex and bounded) along m = q^{t}. For such m, Frobenius amplification p(F) = \sum_{u} x^{\gamma(u)}\cdot F_u(x^{m}) sends m^{\text{kN}} copies of excessCond injectively to polynomials, and the coefficients at the points \lfloor m\lambda \rfloor (\lambda a lattice point) of the translates by representatives R of L/(L \cap \mathcal{I}) determine p(F): a monomial of least w-weight among all translates is m\cdot \lambda for a lattice point \lambda of the excess cone — at b' = a' because F_u is in excessCond, at the other pairs by the root lemma.
