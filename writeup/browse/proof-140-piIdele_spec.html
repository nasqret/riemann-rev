lemma Data.piIdele_spec

What it saysFor admissible data D over P: \operatorname{IsSplitSquarefree}(\operatorname{piPlaces}(D), N(D), \operatorname{piIdele}(D)); that is, N(D) = \operatorname{piIdele}(D) \operatorname{conj}(\operatorname{piIdele}(D)) as finite ideles, and for every finite place w the valuation of the w-component of \operatorname{piIdele}(D) is \exp(-1) if w is in \operatorname{piPlaces}(D) (the primes dividing \pi(D)) and 1 otherwise, and if w is in \operatorname{piPlaces}(D) then its conjugate is not. Here the valuation is written multiplicatively, \exp(-1) being the element of the multiplicatively written value group Z^{m0} (WithZero (Multiplicative Z)) that marks a uniformiser, not the real number 1/e: in the usual additive normalisation, \operatorname{ord}_w(\operatorname{piIdele}(D)_w) = 1 for w in \operatorname{piPlaces}(D) and 0 for w outside it.
The argument in briefThe three parts of IsSplitSquarefree: the norm, the valuations, and the conjugates.
