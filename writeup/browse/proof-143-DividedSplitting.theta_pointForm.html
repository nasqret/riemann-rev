lemma DividedSplitting.theta_pointForm

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r, n, N, m and a finite types, O : AuxPair E r n, z \colon r \hookrightarrow m and V a divided splitting (DividedSplitting N m a O z), E with Weil data, L : DividedLattice O, e \colon r \hookrightarrow n, v a coordinate vector of weight O.κ, g a point of the adelic group of W and h \in G_f(I_n). Then O.θ (pointForm (h\cdot L.\gamma) (the inclusion matrices of O.incl) e v) g = \operatorname{conj}(L.\operatorname{weilScalar}\cdot \theta^{W, I_n}_{O.a, O.b}[\Psi_L, O.harmonicPoly \overline{v}](g, h)), where \theta is modKernelI modulo O.levelModulus 2 and \Psi_L(S) = \prod_j \psi_{q_j}(\det of the rows e of S mod q_j) if G\cdot(S reduced to O_E/M^2) = \overline{\varpi}\cdot I_{incl} (G = L.reducedInverse, \overline{\varpi} = V.genResidue), and 0 otherwise.
The argument in briefApply theta_pointForm at h\cdot \gamma, move \gamma (supported on M and \overline{M}) from the point into the datum by the Weil representation at those places, which multiplies by the Weil scalar and transforms the residue datum into \Psi_L.
