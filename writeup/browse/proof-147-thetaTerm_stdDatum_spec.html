lemma thetaTerm_stdDatum_spec

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, a, b, m finite index types; signs s, t, r; X : Space(E, m, r), W_{1} : Space(E, n, s), D a base-adapted splitting of X over W_{1} along b, V : Space(E, a, t). Let P be a finite set of places with non-trivial local product LocProd(P) (Nontrivial), A a set of places and \pi a reading map with h : ReadsAt(A, \pi); \Phi a Schwartz–Bruhat function supported in a set U (h\Phi) which equals the standard datum of the adapted frame, \Phi = stdDatum(adaptedFrame(h, D), y \mapsto \varepsilon \cdotinvOnUnits(\chi, \det(y\cdot N))) (e), for a character \chi, a constant \varepsilon and a (b \sqcup n) \times a matrix N over LocProd(P); v a rapid real datum (hv); characters \beta, \beta ', \gamma with \beta ' = \beta \cdot \upsilon^{|b|} (h\beta); and c, z, q, g (the coordinates of a point). Put f = localDatum(X, V, v, \Phi, h) and p = radicalElt(D, c, z)\cdotlevi(D, (q, g)). Then: (1) the theta terms of D for (V, \beta ', \gamma, f) at p are integrable; (2) for all rational x, x' = x_V^{-1}\cdot x, adelic Y with x'\cdot q = scaleIdele(E)\cdot Y and every k, writing y = k_f^{-1}\cdot Y_f, the theta term thetaTerm(D, V, \beta ', \gamma, f, p, x, k) equals \psi(\tfrac12 \cdot \operatorname{tr}(z\cdot(x^{*}J_V x))) \cdot \gamma_{a}(\det q)^{-1} \cdot |\det q|^{|a|/2} \cdot [y integral]\cdotvacuumFactor(D, V) \cdot thetaOnRealData(W_{1}, V, \beta, \gamma, namedVector(W_{1}, V, y' \mapsto [y' integral]\cdot \varepsilon \cdotinvOnUnits(\chi, \det((y mod \pi | y')\cdot N)), h), x'c^{*}\cdotstdForm(s), (g, k))(realSlice(D, V, k^{-1}Y, v)), \gamma_{a} the character attached to \gamma in rank |a|; and (3) for signs t' and T not represented by t' (as an adelic matrix: at some infinite place v there is no complex c with c^{*}\cdot \operatorname{diag}(t')\cdot c = T_v) such that every non-zero real slice realSlice(D, V, x, v) has its \operatorname{Gram} matrix x^{*}\cdotstdForm(t)\cdot x represented by t', the coefficient centreCoeff(D, \jmath T, thetaIntegral(X, V, \beta ', \gamma, f), p) vanishes.
The argument in briefIntegrability from the named Schwartz–Bruhat datum; the explicit form by moving the source term through the radical and Levi elements and reading the local datum on the adapted frame; the vanishing because each term is a multiple of thetaOnRealData at a real slice that would have a represented \operatorname{Gram} matrix.
