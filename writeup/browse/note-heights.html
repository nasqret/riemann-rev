abbrevPrimePadicIso

Definition 1.1 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/Heights.lean, line 17

PrimePadicIso is the type of pairs (\ell, \iota) of a prime number \ell (an element of Nat.Primes) and a ring isomorphism \iota : \mathbb{C} \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell} from the complex numbers onto Mathlib's algebraic closure PadicAlgCl \ell of the field \mathbb{Q}_{\ell} of \ell-adic numbers (a dependent pair, \sum \ell, \mathbb{C} \simeq_{+*} PadicAlgCl \ell). Families of complex numbers indexed by it are the objects whose heights are bounded in this unit. (Such isomorphisms exist only by choice: both fields are algebraically closed of characteristic 0 and of the cardinality of the continuum; the source never constructs one.)

defHeightLe

Definition 1.2 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/Heights.lean, lines 21–23

For a real number c and a family y : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C} (one complex number y(\ell, \iota) for every prime \ell and every ring isomorphism \iota : \mathbb{C} \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}), HeightLe c y ('y has height at most c') holds when there exist a complex number H, a real number \alpha and a function e : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R} such that (i) H \ne 0; (ii) |\sigma(H)| \le \exp(\alpha) for every ring automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}; (iii) \lVert \iota(H\cdot y(\ell, \iota))\rVert_{\ell} \le \exp(e(\ell)) for every (\ell, \iota) \in PrimePadicIso, where \lVert \cdot \rVert_{\ell} is the norm of \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell} extending the \ell-adic norm of \mathbb{Q}_{\ell} (\lVert \ell \rVert_{\ell} = 1/\ell); (iv) \alpha + \sum_{\ell < M, \ell \text{prime}} e(\ell) \le c for all sufficiently large natural numbers M (eventually along M \to \infty). Nothing requires e \ge 0, so c may be negative a priori; HeightLe.nonneg excludes that for the constant family 1.

lemmalog_product_formula

Lemma 3.4 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/Heights.lean, lines 28–30

Product formula for \mathbb{Q}, in logarithms. For every natural number M and every rational number q with \max(|numerator of q|, denominator of q) < M: \log q + \sum_{p < M, p \text{prime}} \log |q|_{p} = 0, where \log is Lean's real logarithm of the real number q (\log x = \log |x| for x \ne 0, \log 0 = 0) and |q|_{p} = padicNorm p q is the p-adic absolute value (p^{(}-v_p(q)) for q \ne 0, and 0 for q = 0). For q \ne 0 this is the product formula |q|\cdot \Pi_{p} |q|_{p} = 1, the primes below M including every prime that divides the numerator or the denominator; for q = 0 both terms are 0 by the conventions.

lemmaHeightLe.mono

Lemma 3.1 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/Heights.lean, lines 36–37

Monotonicity. For real numbers c, c' and families y, y' : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C}: if HeightLe c y, c \le c', and \lVert \iota(y'(\ell, \iota))\rVert_{\ell} \le \lVert \iota(y(\ell, \iota))\rVert_{\ell} for every (\ell, \iota) \in PrimePadicIso, then HeightLe c' y' (the same witnesses H, \alpha, e serve).

lemmaHeightLe.of_le

Lemma 3.2 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/Heights.lean, lines 42–43

For a real number c, a family y : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C} and a function e : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}: if \sum_{\ell \in T} e(\ell) \le c for every finite set T of natural numbers all of whose elements are prime (he), and \lVert \iota(y(\ell, \iota))\rVert_{\ell} \le \exp(e(\ell)) for every (\ell, \iota) \in PrimePadicIso (h), then HeightLe c y (witnesses H = 1, \alpha = 0 and the given e).

lemmaHeightLe.mul

Proposition 3.3 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/Heights.lean, lines 47–50

Heights add in products. For real numbers c, c' and families y, y' : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C}: if HeightLe c y and HeightLe c' y', then HeightLe (c + c') (y\cdot y'), where (y\cdot y')(\ell, \iota) = y(\ell, \iota)\cdot y'(\ell, \iota) is the pointwise product.

lemmaHeightLe.prod

Proposition 3.3 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/Heights.lean, lines 54–55

Heights add in finite products. For an index type \iota, a finite set s of indices, real numbers c_i and families y_i : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C} (i \in \iota): if HeightLe (c i) (y i) for every i \in s, then HeightLe (\sum_{i\in s} c_i) (\Pi_{i\in s} y_i), the product taken pointwise (for s = \varnothing: the constant family 1 has height at most 0).

lemmaHeightLe.nonneg

defdyadSum

Definition 6.3 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/OneDeterminant.lean, line 58

For a complex inner product space H with an orthonormal basis b =(b_j)_{j\in m} (m finite) and a finite family v =(v_x)_{x\in n} in H: dyadSum b v = \sum_{x\in n} \overline{v}_{x} \otimes v_x \in \mathbb{C}^{m} \otimes_{\mathbb{C}} H, where \overline{v}_{x} \in \mathbb{C}^{m} (EuclideanSpace \mathbb{C} m) has coordinates (\langle v_x, b_j\rangle)_{j\in m}, the complex conjugates of the coordinates \langle b_j, v_x\rangle of v_x in the basis b.

Proposition 6.5 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/OneDeterminant.lean, lines 67–82

Hilbert–Schmidt inequality. Let H be a finite-dimensional complex inner product space with an orthonormal basis b indexed by a finite type m, n a finite type, \mathbb{C}^{n} = EuclideanSpace \mathbb{C} n with standard basis (e_x), P : \mathbb{C}^{n} \to H and R : H \to H linear, U \subseteq \mathbb{C}^{n} and A \subseteq H subspaces, and \varepsilon a real number. If \sum_{x\in n} \lVert(P^{*}P - 1)e_x\rVert \le \varepsilon (k) and P v - R(P v) \in A for every v \in U (h), then \dim U - \varepsilon - \sum_{x\in n} \lVert R(P e_x)\rVert^{2} \le \operatorname{Re} \langledyadSum b (x \mapsto P e_x), dyadSum b (a_j)_{j}\rangle, where (a_j) is Mathlib's standard orthonormal basis of A (stdOrthonormalBasis \mathbb{C} A). By inner_dyadSum the right side is \sum_{x} \sum_{j} |\langle a_j, P e_x\rangle |^{2} = \sum_{x} \lVert p_A(P e_x)\rVert^{2}, the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of p_A\circ P, p_A the orthogonal projection onto A. lemmaone_determinant

Theorem 2 of the note · unit 135 · New/Heights/OneDeterminant.lean, lines 129–149

One determinant. Let W be a finite-dimensional complex inner product space, \Theta a type (of twists), \theta : PrimePadicIso \to \Theta, and for each \vartheta \in \Theta a complex inner product space H(\vartheta) and a linear map u_{\vartheta} : W \to H(\vartheta); let o \in \Theta with u_o injective (hu). Let S be finite and x : S \to W linearly independent (i) with \dim W = |S| (hk), so x is a basis of W. Let \alpha, \delta, g be real numbers with |\sigma(\det \operatorname{Gram}(u_o\circ x))| \le \exp(\alpha) for every ring automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C} (h\alpha), \operatorname{Gram}(v) =(\langle v_s, v_t\rangle)_{s,t\in S}. Let \gamma : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C} with \gamma(\iota) \ne 0 for all \iota (\gamma 0) and HeightLe g (\iota \mapsto |\gamma(\iota)|^{-2}) (h\gamma); let e : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R} with \sum_{l\in U} e(l) \le \delta for every finite set U of natural numbers (he); let \Lambda assign to every (\ell, \iota) \in PrimePadicIso and every ring homomorphism j : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_{\ell} a subset \Lambda(\iota, j) \subseteq H(\theta(\iota)); and let k \in \mathbb{N}. Assume (w): for all \iota, j, all x' \in \Lambda(\iota, j\circ \operatorname{conj}) and y' \in \Lambda(\iota, j), \lVert j(\langle x', y'\rangle)\rVert_{\ell} \le 1; and (l): for all \iota =(\ell, \iota) and j there is a complex S\times S matrix m with \exp(-e(\ell))\cdot \lVert j(\gamma(\iota))\rVert^{k} \le \lVert j(\det m)\rVert and \sum_{l\in S} m_{\mathit{il}}\cdot u_{\theta(\iota)}(x_l) \in \Lambda(\iota, j) for every i \in S. Then HeightLe (\alpha + 2\delta + k\cdot g) (\iota \mapsto normDet(u_{\theta(\iota)})^{2} / normDet(u_o)^{2}), where normDet(u) = \sqrt{\det}(u^{*}u) \ge 0 is the volume factor of a linear map (Mathlib's LinearMap.normDet), the quotient taken in \mathbb{C}.
