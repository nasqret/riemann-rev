lemma Lift.TameChart.exists_z_smul_eq_sum_tameGen

What it saysFor a tame chart d for \Gamma, a lattice datum L over O, q = \sum_k q_k X^{k} \in \mathbb{Z}_p[X] with q(\beta_{s,0}) = 0 on B_{\mathrm{cris}} \otimes L (r; \beta_{s,0} = linearFrob(L, tameGen, 0)) and x in the Kisin module kisinModule(L) (hx): there is y \in kisinAinf(L) with z\cdot y = \sum_{k \le \deg q} q_k\cdot s^{k}\cdot x (s = tameGen acting as (0, s)).
The argument in briefBy the frame theorem, divisibility by z in M is tested with crystalline forms; for such a form, q(\beta) = 0 makes \sum q_k \mu\circ s^{k} vanish on the crystalline periods, so \mu(\sum q_k s^{k} x) is a combination of Galois differences s^{k} y - y of elements of the bicommutant, each divisible by z.
