Lifting crystalline extension classes in large weight: Kisin modules with exponents, cokernel killers and cup products that vanish up to a power of p

note and structured proofs · 141 declarations · 69 structured proofs · 3 notes

For crystalline lattices over a Kummer chart of p-adic Hodge theory, the unit measures, at each Frobenius translate φ^a(E) of the Eisenstein element, the exponents of elements of Kisin modules (withExponents), deepens them by Galois operators (g − 1)/(zτ) at an explicit cost in p, and finds annihilators p^κE^s of the cokernels of Kisin modules of crystalline extensions (KillsCokernel). A walk along Frobenius orbits then makes the cup product of two crystalline classes a coboundary after multiplication by an explicit power of p independent of the rank (ScaledCupBounds), for weights in a low and a far window and through a long-gap lemma (chunk a); chunk b (FewLowJumps, SparseThickLetters, ExponentTheorems) turns this into the length bound for classes with few low weights and the exponent for tame types used in the Selmer bound.
