lemma Data.reflectionSum_mul_e₀

What it saysLet D be admissible data over P, x = (x_y) a family indexed by the chosen places y of D with x_y in C[\mathrm{GL}_r(k_y)], i a chosen place, \chi a multiplicative character of the residue field k_i with \chi^{M_\omega } = 1, where M_\omega = M\omega(P, s(D)) = (N0 d)^{s(D)+6}, and l a nonzero vector in k_i^r. If x_i = \operatorname{reflectionSum}(\chi, l) (the sum of \chi(\det h)^{-1} h over the invertible h = 1 + u l^T), then \operatorname{piTensorElem}(x) e_0(D) = 0 in C[G(D)].
The argument in briefe_0 is a sum of tensor products of label idempotents of good families; at the place i the label has no root with x^{M_\omega } = 1, in particular not \operatorname{charName}(\chi), so the reflection sum kills that factor.
