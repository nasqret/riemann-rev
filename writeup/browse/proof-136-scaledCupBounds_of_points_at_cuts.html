lemma Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.SidePairing.scaledCupBounds_of_points_at_cuts

What it saysThe walk. Let S, S' be sides, P a pairing, c \in C, c' \in C', \kappa, e, e' \in \mathbb{N}, cut and Q predicates on letters, and l(j) for every letter j the least a > 0 with \sigma^{-a}(j) a cut (hl). Let o, o', z, z' be letter-indexed exponent functions such that for every cut j (H): HasDeepPoint(S, S', c, j, e, cut, l(j), o, o', z, Q(j)); HasDeepPoint(S', S, c', j, e', cut, l(j), o', o, z, \neg Q(j)); pointsOver(thirdSide(P), 1, j, \kappa, z(j), z'(j)) is nonempty; and z(j, a, \cdot) is antitone for every a. Then for every E with e + e' + \kappa \le E (he; default proof omega), ScaledCupBounds(P, E, c, c') holds.
The argument in briefMove the points from the nearest cut before each letter along the Frobenius orbit (\varphi^{d}), sum over the letters, and verify the four safety conditions of ScaledCupBounds.of_isSafe letter by letter (isSafe_sum), by isSafe_smul_sub and isSafe_of_exponents at the cuts, in the cases cut/not cut and Q/not Q.
