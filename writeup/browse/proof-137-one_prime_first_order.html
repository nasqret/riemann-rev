lemma one_prime_first_order

What it saysLet \Gamma be a finite group, R a ring, m a natural number, \rho \colon \Gamma \to R a homomorphism into the multiplicative monoid of R, H a subgroup of \Gamma, S a ring and \sigma \colon R \to S a ring homomorphism with m = 0 in S (hm). Let a \colon H \to \mathbb{Z}/m be a homomorphism (\mathbb{Z}/m written multiplicatively; a(p) also denotes its additive value), B an additive map from integer-valued functions on \mathbb{Z}/m to R with B(f)\cdot \rho(p) = B(x \mapsto f(x - a(p))) for all f and p \in H (hB), \mathcal{A} a subring of R containing every \rho(g) (hρ) and every B(f) (h\mathcal{A}), A a type and ac : A \to R a function whose values commute with every element of \mathcal{A} (hc). Let \Psi, L \in R, \gamma \in \Gamma and n, k \in \mathbb{N} satisfy: \sum_{p\in H} \rho(p^{-1})\Psi \rho(p) = n (hΨ); \rho(p)\cdot B(1) = B(1) for p \in H, 1 the constant function (hX); L\cdot B(1) = 0 (h); L\cdot \rho(g) = L for all g \in \Gamma (hL); multiplication by |\Gamma | is injective on R (tf); k\cdot \operatorname{Tr}_a(\gamma) = k in \mathbb{Z}/m, \operatorname{Tr}_a \colon \Gamma \to \mathbb{Z}/m the transfer of a (hn). Then there is F \in \mathcal{A} such that (i) for every g \in \Gamma there is Z \in R with \sigma(n\cdot F\cdot(\rho(g) - 1)) = \sigma((\sum_{g'\in \Gamma } \rho(g'))\cdot Z) and L\cdot Z = 0; and (ii) for all t, t' \in A and r \in R: if B(f(\cdot - k) - f)\cdot ac(t) = \operatorname{weightedSum}(\rho |_H, a)(f(\cdot - k) - f)\cdot ac(t') for every f \colon \mathbb{Z}/m \to \mathbb{Z}, and \operatorname{weightedSum}(\rho, \operatorname{Tr}_a)(f)\cdot ac(t') = (\rho(\gamma) - 1)\cdot \operatorname{weightedSum}(\rho, \operatorname{Tr}_a)(f)\cdot r for every f, then (2k)\cdot \sigma(F)\sigma(ac(t)) = (2k)\cdot \sigma(\sum_{g\in \Gamma } \rho(g))\sigma(r).
The argument in briefTake F = T\cdot B(-v)\cdot U with T, U the sums of \rho over coset representatives and their inverses and v the least absolute residue. Conjugating by the representatives turns sums over H into sums over \Gamma (key); with hΨ and hX this gives (\sum \rho)\Psi B(1) = n\cdot T\cdot B(1), whence (i) with Z_g built from the H-components of g\cdot \widehat{\eta}, and L\cdot Z_g = 0 by tf. For (ii), the weight f(x) = -v(x)(v(x)+k) has f(x-k) - f(x) \equiv 2k\cdot v(x); conjugation sums weights over the cosets into the transfer, and k\cdot \operatorname{Tr}_a(\gamma) = k closes the computation modulo m.
