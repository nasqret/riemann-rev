lemma firstMinimum_mul_le_hom

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, n and n' finite nonempty types, s and s' signs, S a space of rank |n| with signs s and T one of rank |n'| with signs s', and N \ne 0 a natural number in the different of E (hd). Then \lambda_S\cdot \lambda_T/(c\cdot |n'|\cdot |n|) \le \lambda_{\operatorname{Hom}(S, T)}, where \lambda denotes the first minimum of the lattice cut off at 1 (Space.firstMinimum, MinkowskiSecond.lean 73–76: the infimum of 1 and the lengths of the nonzero lattice vectors, so \lambda \le 1) and c = shortBasisConst E n N (= 6N(f\cdot |n|)^2).
The argument in briefA short basis Y of S turns a nonzero vector z of \operatorname{Hom}(S, T) into vectors z\cdot Y_k of T, one of them nonzero; its length is at least \lambda_T and at most |z|\cdot |Y|.
