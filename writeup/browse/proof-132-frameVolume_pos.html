lemma frameVolume_pos

What it saysLet \mathfrak{s} be a shape and \gamma \in G_{1} ( \mathfrak{s} ). Then frameVolume( \gamma ) > 0.
The argument in briefA kernel vector would give a vector w = \gamma^{- 1} F_{\mathfrak{s}} v with h ( w , w ) \ge 0 by the Euclidean orthogonality and \le 0 by unitarity; so w = 0.
