lemma norm_coeff_le_of_deriv

What it saysLet p \ge 13 be a prime, e \in \{1, 2\}, Q \in \mathbb{N} with p^e = Q + 1, \varpi \in \mathbb{C}_p with |\varpi^Q|^2 = p^{-e} and \varpi \ne 0, and let m, n, Z, \mu be one-variable power series over \mathbb{C}_p such that n' and Z' have coefficients in \mathfrak{o}, n(0) = 0, the coefficient of X^{j+1} in Z is 0 whenever Q \nmid j, the coefficient of X^{Q+1} in Z is -(\varpi^Q)^{-1}, the coefficients of X^0, X^1, X^2 in m are 0, m' = n\cdot Z', and \mu = (\varpi^{p+1}/p)^{-1}\cdot \widetilde{m}(\varpi X), where \widetilde{m} is the part of m in the degrees \equiv p + 1 mod Q (congruencePart Q (p+1) m). Then |\mu_{p+1}| \le 1, |\mu_{p+p^e}| \le 1, and (\varpi^Q)^{-1}\cdot(\mu - \mu_{p+1}X^{p+1} - \mu_{p+p^e}X^{p+p^e}) has coefficients in \mathfrak{o}.
The argument in briefShow |\mu_k| \le 1 for k \in \{p+1, p+p^e\} and |\mu_k| \le |\varpi^Q| otherwise. Only k = p + 1 + Q\cdot I contribute; k\cdot \mu_k is p\cdot \varpi^{QI}\cdot(m')_{k-1} = p \varpi^{QI}\sum n_{i+1}\cdot(j+1)Z_{j+1}; only j = QJ contribute (hypothesis R), and the p-adic valuations of k and of i + 1 (exists_norm_eq_inv_pow_bounds) balance the powers of \varpi.
