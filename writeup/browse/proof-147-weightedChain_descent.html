lemma IsTriangle.weightedChain_descent

What it saysLet (m, w, M, a, d, j, G) be a triangle (h) with all weights non-zero, i, n natural numbers with i + n < m (hi). There are \beta, \kappa such that (1) for all t, y: weightedChain(w, a, t, \cdot, n)(d(t, i, 0)(a(t, i, 0)y)) =(w(t, n + 1)/w(t, 1)/w(t, 0)/\operatorname{conj}(\beta(t)))\cdot d(t, i, n)(weightedChain(w, a, t, i, \cdot)y); and (2) under G: M(n, \kappa \cdot \beta) and \langle z, a(t, i, n)(weightedChain(w, a, t, i, n)(j(i)y))\rangle = \kappa(t)\cdot \langle d(t, i, n)z, weightedChain(w, a, t, \cdot, n)(j(\cdot)y)\rangle for all t, y, z.
The argument in briefInduction on n: the case n = 0 is the first-arrow law; the step moves one more square with the square law.
