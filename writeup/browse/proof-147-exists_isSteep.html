lemma Data.exists_isSteep

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data and z a grid. There is a bijection e : InitIndex(D, 0) \sqcup Fin(2\varrho + 2 + [z]) \simeq GridIndex(z, 0, 0) (an ordering of the indices of the node (0, 0) of grid z) such that at every infinite place \tau the parameter gridParam(z, 0, 0, 0, \tau) of that node is steep with bound w_{0}(\tau) = w(D, 0, \tau) along e (IsSteep), with the signs of the node and offset 0.
The argument in briefOrder the indices by the numbering of the base index set; compute the signs and doubled parameters along it, bound w at the top by 2w_{0}, and compute the lowest K-type entries (by induction over the rank); steepness is then a list of inequalities.
