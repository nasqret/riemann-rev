lemma Filling.IsFillingLevel.exists_intermediateGroup_one_prime

What it saysLemma. Let E a number field with a CM structure (Field E, CMType E), \mathfrak{s} a shape, S filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s} (Filling), T and T_0 finite sets of finite places of E with T stable under complex conjugation (fT), P an integral place structure on LocProd(T)=\prod_{w\in T}E_w (ring of integers R= P.R, Haar measure, modulus md, character \psi), F a level on LocProd(T) (Level (Fin (k(𝔰) − (6ϱ(𝔰) + 13))) (latMiddle(S, LocProd T)) (ϱ(𝔰) + 1 + 2ϱ(𝔰) + 5)) with the instance h : IsFillingLevel(S,T,F) (F is a standard level in the sense of IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel, P.\psi= charOnPlaces(S,T), and the unit F.g of F is the image of gIdele(S)=g(S) at T), and Sg a finite set of chosen places of S above q_0(S); assume S is admissible (IsAdmissible). Let o be either nothing or a place w\in Sg, let d assign to every chosen place w above q_0(S) a pair d_w= (a flag, a nonzero vector v_w over the residue field \mathbb{F}_w) (the data of vectorFixer), suppose (hT) that g(S)^{-1}= gIdele(S)^{-1} is integral at every finite place outside T_0, and suppose (f) that if o=w then T=T_0\cap\{w,\overline{w}\} (for every finite place j: j\in T\iff j\in T_0 and (j=w or \overline{j}=w)). Write K_T= unitaryLevel(R,1,J_T) for the integral unitary group at T (J_T the Gram matrix of latMiddle(S,\mathtt{LocProd}T)), \mathsf{M}= groupMeasure(F) and \varkappa(Z)= floorZeroForm(F,Z)=\int|\int_Z\operatorname{phase}(\mathtt{F.g},y x y^{-1})d\mathsf{M}(y)|^{2}d\mathsf{M}(x). Then there is an intermediate group Z (a subgroup with congGroup(F)=K(g)_T\le Z\le K_T) such that (i) for every k in the integral group K= latStabilizer(S) with \operatorname{reduction}(S,k)\in fixingSet(S,Sg,d), toPlaces(S,T,k)\in Z, and (ii)\varkappa(Z)\cdot\mathsf{M}(K_T)\cdot c_o\le\varkappa(K_T)\cdot\mathsf{M}(Z), where c_o=1 if o is nothing and c_o=b(S)^{6k(\mathfrak{s})} if o is a place.
The argument in briefIf o is nothing, Z=K_T works with equality. If o=w, let H be the subgroup of K= latStabilizer(S) of the k whose reduction lies in vectorFixer(S,w,d_w), and Z= toPlaces(H). Its index in K_T is the index of the stabiliser of a nonzero vector in \operatorname{GL}_{2\varrho+k+1}(\mathbb{F}_w),q_0^{2\varrho+k+1}-1\ge b(S)^{9(t+1)} (t=k-10^{10}n^{4}); with the bottom value \varkappa(Z)\le\mathsf{M}(Z)\mathsf{M}(K(g))(\mathsf{M}(Z)+\operatorname{vol}(R\setminus R^{\times})^{t+1}2^{-n}),\operatorname{vol}(R\setminus R^{\times})\le b^{-9} (T\subseteq\{w,\overline{w}\}),2^{-n}\le\mathsf{M}(K_T) and \varkappa(K_T)\ge\int_{K(g)}=\mathsf{M}(K_T)^{2}\mathsf{M}(K(g)), the inequality follows.
