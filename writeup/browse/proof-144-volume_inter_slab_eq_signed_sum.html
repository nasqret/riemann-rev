What it saysLet k, m \in \mathbb{N}, P > 1 real (hP), v : Fin (m+1) \to Bool (letters in Fin (m+1) with its cyclic addition), D : Fin (m+1) \to \mathbb{R}, and \Omega \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{Fin k \times Fin(m+1)} with permStep v P 1 \nu \in \Omega iff \nu \in pieriCone v P, for every \nu (h\Omega). The argument in briefSorting each column \nu(\cdot, j) of a point turns the cyclic interlacing conditions into a product over the letters of interlacing-indicator determinants, which expand (Leibniz) into a signed sum over tuples \sigma of permutations of indicators of the sets S(\sigma, 1, univ).
