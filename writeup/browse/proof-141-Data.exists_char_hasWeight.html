Structured proof · Unit 141

lemma Data.exists_char_hasWeight

What it saysFor admissible data D and natural numbers a, n: there is a unitary character \mu of U(1)(\mathbb{A}), trivial on U(1)(E), of weight \sigma \mapsto -a\cdot w_{0}(\sigma) (w_{0} = Data.w 0) and tame part (\omega_w^{n})_{w} (AutChar.HasWeight).
The argument in briefExistence of a Hecke character with prescribed infinity type and tame part reduces to a compatibility on the global units of norm one, which are roots of unity \zeta^{k}; there the infinite factors and \omega^{n} cancel because the orders divide M_{\omega} and the weights are adapted.

    By exists_hasWeight_of_prod_eq_one (with the finite set of chosen places, outside which \omega^{n} is trivial), it suffices that for every global norm-one unit u (integral, U) the product of the infinity-type factors u^{-a\cdot w_{0}} and of \omega^{n}(u mod w) over the chosen places is 1.

  2. 2factlines 91–92

    Such a unit is a root of unity of E, hence a power \zeta^{k} of the chosen primitive root \zeta of order N_{1}.

  3. 3computationlines 93–95

    For u = \zeta^{k} (\zeta^{N_{1}} = 1): the tame factors are 1, because \omega_w = T_w^{M_{\omega}} with T_w the chosen residue character of order M_{\omega}^{2}, so \omega_w(\zeta^{k})^{n} = T_w(\zeta^{k\cdot n\cdot M_{\omega}}) = T_w(1) = 1, as N_{1} \mid N_{1}\cdot d \mid M_{\omega} (N₁_mul_d_dvd_M_omega) — not because of the order of \omega_w(\zeta). In the infinity factors \Phi_v(\zeta^{k})^{-a\cdot w_{0}(v)}, w_{0}(v) = b_{1} or b_{2} (thick or not) + h_{2}, and b_{1}, b_{2} are multiples of N_{1} (D.roundUp), so they drop out and every place carries the same exponent -a\cdot k\cdot h_{2}; what remains is a power of \prod_{v} \Phi_v(\zeta), treated in step 4.

  4. 4computationlines 96–97

    The remaining factor is the norm of \zeta, which is 1 (N_{1} \ne 2, cyclotomic polynomial irreducible).
