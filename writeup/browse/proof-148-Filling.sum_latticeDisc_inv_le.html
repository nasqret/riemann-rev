lemma Filling.sum_latticeDisc_inv_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified and S admissible, and let s\in\mathbb{N}. Then \Sigma_{c\in\mathtt{Classes}(S)}\Sigma_T latticeDisc(S,c,T)^{-(k+1)}\le \#Classes(S)\cdot N^{-\operatorname{kf}}\cdot\tau^{s+1}, the inner sum over all E-subspaces T of X_0=E^{\kappa} with \dim T+s=k, where latticeDisc(S,c,T) is the discriminant (sublatticeDisc) of L_c\cap T,L_c the X_0-part of the lattice of the class c,\tau= tailScale(S),k=|\kappa| and f the number of infinite places (sums in [0,\infty]; each term is ENNReal.ofReal of the real inverse latticeDisc(S,c,T)^{-(k+1)}, so a discriminant 0 contributes 0, not \infty).
The argument in briefAverage over the finite set A=K_f/(K_f\cap K) of local translations of a class: for each u,\Sigma_{\varphi\in A}f([u\varphi])\le\#A\cdot b with f(c)=\Sigma_T d_c(T)^{-(k+1)} and b=\tau^{s+1}/N^{\operatorname{kf}} (the discriminant of a class transported to u\hat{O}^{\kappa}, Siegel's lemma bound, a congruence count modulo \overline{\pi}), and the class integral turns this average into \Sigma_c f(c)\le\#\operatorname{Classes}\cdot b.
