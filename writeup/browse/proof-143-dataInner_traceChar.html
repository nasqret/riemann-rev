lemma dataInner_traceChar

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, n, n' finite types, s, s' signs, S a space of rank |n| with signs s and T one of rank |n'| with signs s', \mathfrak{m} a modulus, t a matrix (the trace parameter), v, w Fock vectors and z an adelic vector of \operatorname{Hom}(S, T). Then the pairing dataInner of the data v \otimes \Psi_t \otimes vac and \varrho(z)(w \otimes \Psi_t \otimes vac) equals \mathfrak{m}.\operatorname{liftFn}(\Psi_t \circ \operatorname{vecToMat})(z_f)\cdot(\operatorname{realInner}(v, \operatorname{realHeisOp}(z_\infty) w)\cdot \operatorname{vacuumFn}(0, z_f)), where \Psi_t(y) = \psi(\operatorname{tr}(y\cdot t)) modulo \mathfrak{m} (traceChar t), z_f and z_\infty the finite and real coordinates of z.
