What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, a ring map e : E \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell , g, g' \in G_\ell and a, n \in \mathbb{N} with (g'g^{-1})(e(\zeta )^{\ell ^a}) = (e(\zeta )^{\ell ^a})^{\ell ^n} (h), an infinite place y of E (an element of the group of infinite places, identified with (\mathbb{Z}/N_1)^\times) with w(complexEmbOf e g')\cdot y = w(complexEmbOf e g) (hy, by default y is their quotient), and a divisor m of N_1 (d) with \ell ^a coprime to m (c): the class of y modulo m, placeClass d y \in (\mathbb{Z}/m)^\times, equals cmSign(complexEmbOf e g)\cdotcmSign(complexEmbOf e g')\cdot \ell ^n.
