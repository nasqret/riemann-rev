lemma Size.row_mem_ofSize_of_dual_frame

What it saysLet A be a commutative ring with involution (CommRing A, StarRing A ) , \mathfrak{s} a shape and T an \mathfrak{s}-form over A; V is the free A-module on the basis e_{0} , \ldots , e_{\varrho - 1} , ( x_{i} )_{i \in \kappa} , z , e_{\varrho - 1}^{*} , \ldots , e_{0}^{*} with Gram matrix J_{T} = splitGram( T ) and U ( J_{T} ) its unitary group; let S be a size for T , c , d , j \in \mathbb{N}, X a matrix with rows indexed by l and columns by the basis indices of V, and m a row index. Assume ( h ): for every pair index a < j there is a vector w with w ( e_{b}^{*} ) = 1 if b = a and 0 if b < a (for all pair indices b \le a), w \in vecsOfSize( S , c ) and Xw \in vecsOfSize( S , d ); and ( z ): X_{mk} \in B_{d}^{S} for every basis index k with j \le floorOf( k ) or \operatorname{level} ( k ) \le \varrho. Then for every i \in \mathbb{N} and every basis index k with j \le floorOf( k ) + i or \operatorname{level} ( k ) \le \varrho, X_{mk} \in B_{d + ( c + 3 ) i}^{S}.
The argument in briefInduction on i: an index not yet covered is e_{a}^{*} with a < j, and the dual-frame vector w expresses X_{mk} through entries covered at the previous stage.
