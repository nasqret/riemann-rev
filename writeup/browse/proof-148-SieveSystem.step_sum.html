lemma SieveSystem.step_sum

What it saysLet K= SieveSystem(u,\iota,n,J,\psi,En,\Lambda,\beta) be a sieve system on C (C,U,\tau,u,\iota,n,J,\psi,En,\Lambda,\beta as in SieveSystem, \tau finite), i\le n a natural number (hypothesis hi) and a:\tau\to(\mathbb{N}\to U) a finite family of sequences. Put F(c)=\sum_{p,q\in\tau}J_{i+1}(a_p[i\mapsto u(c)],a_q[i\mapsto u(c)]). Then F is integrable, \int F=\sum_{p,q}J_i(a_p,a_q), and for every unit \xi of C, |\int\psi(\xi c)F(c)dc|\le\beta(\xi)\cdot(\sum_p\Lambda_i(a_p))^{2}.
The argument in briefSum the step property of K over the pairs (p,q).
