lemma isPolar_primeError

What it saysFor a prime d (instance Fact (Nat.Prime d)) with d \bmod 8 = 7 (hd) and a natural number M \ne 0 (instance NeZero M) coprime to d (h): the function E(v) = R_{d,M}(e^{v}) of a real v is polar with residues r(s) = (\sum_{\xi} (\operatorname{ord}_s \widetilde{L}(\cdot, \xi) - \operatorname{ord}_s \widetilde{L}(\cdot, \xi\cdot\chi_d)))/\varphi(M)/(s(1 - s)) for s \notin \{0, 1\}, and r(0) = r(1) = 0 (Lean's division by zero gives 0; of these only r(1) = 0 is asked for, residues being asked at \operatorname{Re} \rho > 0), \xi over the Dirichlet characters modulo M, \operatorname{ord}_s the order of vanishing at s (Mathlib's analyticOrderNatAt, a natural number read in \mathbb{C}). Unfolding IsPolar E r: E is measurable, and there are a real C and a meromorphic \Phi : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C} with |E(v)| \le C\cdot e^{v} for v \ge 0, \Phi(s) = \int_{0}^\infty E(v)e^{(1-s)v} dv for \operatorname{Re} s > 2, s \mapsto (s - 1)\Phi(s) analytic near every point of \operatorname{Re} s \ge 1 (\Phi is a function defined at s = 1, so this product vanishes at s = 1, and \Phi has no pole at 1: off s = 1 it agrees with a function holomorphic near 1), and (z - \rho)\Phi(z) \to r(\rho) as z \to \rho, z \ne \rho, for every \rho with \operatorname{Re} \rho > 0.
The argument in brief\Phi(s) = (G(s)/s - \kappa)/(s - 1): its Laplace identity comes from the integral representation of the Dirichlet series of \lambda and the explicit formula through LSeriesHasSum_mul_vonMangoldt; G(1) is real by analytic continuation of the imaginary part from (2, \infty), which makes (s - 1)\Phi analytic at 1; the residues are the orders of the \widetilde{L} at \rho.
