lemma exists_stdExchange_removing_planes

What it saysDescent. Let E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, n', r, r', p, b finite index types with p non-empty; signs s, s', t, t', u; W : Space(E, n, s) of even rank (\mathit{hn}: 2 divides |n|), W' : Space(E, n', s'), U : Space(E, b, u), V' : Space(E, r', t'); A a base-adapted splitting of W' over W along p; \mu weights, \ell a real function of split tame characters, k, j natural numbers, V : Space(E, r, t) with negCount(t') = |p| + negCount(t) (hs), and \sigma, \sigma ' identifications for standard row data. There is a map f on real data such that (1) for all x, y: StdExchange(W', V', U, \mu, \sigma ', x, y, \ell) implies StdExchange(W, V, U, \mu, \sigma, f(x), realSlice(A, U, 0, y), \ell); and (2) if |n| = 2(k + negCount(s)), |n'| = 2(k + negCount(s')) and \mu = k + j(\cdot), then f maps detVectors(k, j) onto detVectors(k, j).
The argument in briefStrong induction on the number of planes. One plane: f is the cone integral of the tower property (exists_constant_term_thetaLarge); the exchange constant becomes c\cdotvacuumFactor\cdot a/\gamma, and the small side is identified through the constant term along the line, where only the zero term survives. Several planes: remove one plane, then the rest, and compose.
