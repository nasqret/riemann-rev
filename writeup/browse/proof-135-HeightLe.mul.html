lemma HeightLe.mul

What it saysHeights add in products. For real numbers c, c' and families y, y' : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C}: if HeightLe c y and HeightLe c' y', then HeightLe (c + c') (y\cdot y'), where (y\cdot y')(\ell, \iota) = y(\ell, \iota)\cdot y'(\ell, \iota) is the pointwise product.
The argument in briefMultiply the witnesses: H\cdot K, \alpha + \beta and e + f witness the bound c + c' for y\cdot y'; the eventual bound holds beyond the sum of the two thresholds.
