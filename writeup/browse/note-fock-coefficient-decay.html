Theorem 1 of the note · unit 142 · New/Weil/FockCoeffDecay.lean, lines 13–35

Let \iota be a nonempty finite type, \mathit{eps} signs on \iota , \sigma in U(\mathit{eps}) (the unitary group of \operatorname{diag}(\mathit{eps})), P a complex polynomial in the variables \iota , v a vector of the Fock space with v = polyVector(\mathit{eps}, P) (v_P(x) = P(\zeta (x)) \exp (-|x|^2/2)), d, A, n natural numbers and y in C^{\iota }. Assume P is homogeneous of degree d (P(c x) = c^d P(x) for every real c and every x), d + |\iota | \le A, A is nonzero, and |y| > 0 (Euclidean norm). Then |realInner(v, realHeisOp(y)(realWeil(\sigma )(v)))| \le |\sigma |^{(|\iota | + 2n)} \operatorname{Re}(realInner(v, v)) (4(A + n))^n |y|^{(-2n)}, where |\sigma | is the l2 operator norm, realInner the inner product of the Fock space, realHeisOp(y) the Heisenberg translation by y and realWeil the Weil representation of U(\mathit{eps}) on the Fock space. Remark: the hypothesis A \ne 0 follows from the others (\iota is nonempty, so |\iota | \ge 1 and d + |\iota | \le A gives A \ge 1); Lean takes it as an argument all the same (h0).
