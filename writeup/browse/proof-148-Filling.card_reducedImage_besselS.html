What it saysFor the filling data S over E and a finite set T of finite places of E (stable under conjugation where stated). Lemma. If E carries Weil data: \#reducedImage(S,\mathsf{S})= \#reducedImage(S,\mathsf{H})\cdotunipOrder(S).
