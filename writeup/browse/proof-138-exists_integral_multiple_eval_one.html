lemma exists_integral_multiple_eval_one

What it saysFor a prime \ell and polynomials A, A', B, C over \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell with B monic (m), C integral (all coefficients of norm \le 1) (z), A\cdot B \equiv C coefficientwise modulo the maximal ideal (\lVert \operatorname{coeff}_i(AB - C)\lVert < 1) (a), C \equiv A'\cdot B coefficientwise modulo the maximal ideal (h), and \lVert A'(1)\rVert \ge 1 (u): there is a polynomial e with coefficients in the integers of \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell such that A divides e (read in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell [X]) and e(1) = 1.
The argument in brief\lVert A(1)\rVert \ge 1 because A and A' agree modulo the maximal ideal (B monic); then e = A/A(1) has integral coefficients (A\cdot B \equiv C integral, B monic) and e(1) = 1.
