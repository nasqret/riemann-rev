lemma Data.norm_det_compressedGramOp_arch_le

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data, j a lift index, \vartheta a twist, gl a \mathbb{C}-linear map from tables (Tables j) to forms on V_j ((D.auxPair \vartheta j).\operatorname{FormV}), L a subspace of the forms on V_j and \beta \in \mathbb{R}, such that gl* is injective on L (sep) and gl(gl\cdot(z)) is tested for every z \in L (tst: AuxPair.IsTested e_0 L^\perp — orthogonal to every form of L^\perp that is good and killed by the Hecke polynomial at the test value of some split place off the level). Suppose L is the range of D.goodLift \vartheta j (e), and the size bound S holds: \mathsf{h}\cdot \lVert gl \zeta \rVert^2_\sigma \le \exp(\beta)\cdot \sum_a \operatorname{weylNormSq}(\sigma \circ \zeta_a) for every table \zeta and automorphism \sigma (\mathsf{h} = smallHookNumber j, \lVert \cdot \rVert^2_\sigma = normSqAt \sigma). Then for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}: |\sigma(\det(\operatorname{compressedGramOp} \mathsf{h} L gl))| \le \exp(\dim L\cdot \beta).
The argument in briefThe determinant is the product of the eigenvalues; for an eigenvector z with eigenvalue \alpha, pairing gl(gl\cdot z) with the \sigma-twisted table of gl\cdot z and using that projection to L does not increase the \sigma-norm of tested forms gives |\sigma \alpha | \le \exp \beta.
