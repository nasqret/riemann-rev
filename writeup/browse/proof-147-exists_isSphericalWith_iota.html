lemma Data.exists_isSphericalWith_iota

What it saysLet P be parameters and D admissible data for P. For every eigensystem p (a complex number p(w, i) for each finite place w of E(D) and each i) there is a family F, assigning to each finite place w a function F(w) : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}, such that for every form y in eigenPart(D, Y_{0}, p) (forms of principal level g on Y_{0} on which each Hecke element heckeOperator(D, x) acts by p(x)) and every finite place w with IsSplitOffLevel(D, w) (w split, i.e. \overline{w} \ne w, and neither w nor \overline{w} a chosen place above g), the chain of lifts \iota(1)(y) of y for the trivial twist 1 is spherical at w with Hecke polynomial F(w): IsSphericalWith(rightRegular, \overline{w} \ne w, F(w), \iota(1)(y)), i.e. it is fixed by the maximal compact subgroups at w and \overline{w} and the Hecke polynomial operator at each X acts on it by F(w)(X).
The argument in briefInduction along the chain Y_{0} \to Y_m: the eigenpart is spherical at split places off the level, and each lift transfers sphericity (Hecke transfer for theta lifts).
