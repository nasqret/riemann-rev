lemma Filling.energy_sub_cut_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified and S admissible. For every set U of rational points of G(E) and every \Omega\subseteq\mathsf{G}, the family A_U-A\flat_U, the difference of the amplitude sums amplitudeSum(S,U, \cdot, \cdot, \cdot) for the full Bessel functions and for their cuts, has \operatorname{energy}(A_U-A\flat_U,\Omega)\le b^{-30}\cdot\mathsf{d}_1, where b= S.b and \mathsf{d}_1= besselEnergy(S)=|\overline{S}|\cdotcolumnDim(S) (\overline{S} the reduction of the Bessel subgroup).
The argument in briefWrite each block A_U-A\flat_U as the sum over terms of the integrals of \beta_c\beta_c'(K-K^{\mathrm{cut}}), bound it by the cut error of amplitudeAbs_lt_top_and_cut_le (\nu\cdot b^{-k^{3}f}/2 for fixed c,s), sum the squares over at most \#\operatorname{Classes}\cdot\#\mathsf{G} blocks and compare with b^{-30}\mathsf{d}_1 using \#\mathsf{G}\le g^{r^{2}f}\le b^{23r^{2}f} and k large.
