defIsPrimitiveLabel

Definition 2.2 of the note · unit 140 · New/Trace/BadShare.lean, lines 29–30

Let k be a field, r a natural number and c in k[X]. \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c) holds iff \operatorname{IsLabel}(r, c) (c is monic, squarefree, of degree r, with nonzero constant term), c is irreducible, and some root \xi of c in the algebraic closure of k is a primitive root of unity of order |k|^r - 1, where |k| = Nat.card k.

lemmaIsPrimitiveLabel.scaleRoots

Lemma 3.1 of the note · unit 140 · New/Trace/BadShare.lean, lines 35–41

Let k be a finite field, c in k[X], a in k^x, r and M natural numbers, and assume a^M = 1 and M^2 | |k| - 1. If \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c), then \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c.scaleRoots a), where c.scaleRoots a = \sum_i c_i a^{\deg c - i} X^i is the polynomial whose roots are a times the roots of c.

lemmaIsPrimitiveLabel.isPrimitiveRoot_prod_roots

Lemma 3.2 of the note · unit 140 · New/Trace/BadShare.lean, lines 45–51

Let k be a finite field, c in k[X] and r a natural number with \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c). Then the product of the roots of c in the algebraic closure of k, counted with multiplicity, is a primitive root of unity of order |k| - 1; and there is a natural number e such that \xi^e equals this product for every root \xi of c in the algebraic closure.

defgoodTuples

Definition 2.5 of the note · unit 140 · New/Trace/BadShare.lean, lines 55–57

Let \iota be a finite type, k = (k_w), w in \iota, a family of finite fields, and M, r natural numbers. \operatorname{goodTuples}(M, r) is the finite set of families c = (c_w), c_w a monic polynomial of degree r over k_w (an element of the product of the sets \operatorname{monicsOfDegree}(k_w, r)), such that (i) for every w, \operatorname{IsLabel}(r, c_w) holds and no x in k_w with x^M = 1 is a root of c_w, and (ii) \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(r, c_w) holds for at least one w.

lemmaexists_labelIdem_eq_off_irreducible

Theorem 2 of the note · unit 140 · New/Trace/BadShare.lean, lines 64–86

Let k be a finite field, n a finite type and a a natural number with |n| = 3^{a+1} and 9 | |k| - 1; let c in k[X], and let S, T be sets of polynomials over k with at most one element each. Then there is P in k[X] such that (1) if \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(|n|, c), then \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(|n|, P) and P is not in the union of S and T; and (2) for every g in \mathrm{GL}_n(k) whose characteristic polynomial is not irreducible, the coefficients at g of \operatorname{labelIdem}(n, c) and of \operatorname{labelIdem}(n, P) in the group algebra C[\mathrm{GL}_n(k)] are equal.

lemmabad_share_le

Theorem 1 of the note · unit 140 · New/Trace/BadShare.lean, lines 92–136

Let n and \iota be finite types, k = (k_w), w in \iota, a family of finite fields, q, M, r, f natural numbers with M nonzero, and \varepsilon, L real numbers. Assume |k_w| = q for every w, |n| = r, |\iota | = f, 1 < r, r < q, \varepsilon \le 1, (1 + M + 2r)/q \le \varepsilon and 2r(2r \log q + 1)L \le f. Let e be the sum, over the families c in \operatorname{goodTuples}(M, r), of \operatorname{tupleIdem}(n, c) in the group algebra of \prod_w \mathrm{GL}_n(k_w). Then the real part of the coefficient of 1 - e at the identity is at most f \varepsilon + \exp(-L).
