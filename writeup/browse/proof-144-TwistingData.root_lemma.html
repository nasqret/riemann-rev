lemma TwistingData.root_lemma

What it saysRoot lemma: a term least in all frames obeys Frobenius inequalities. Let R be a commutative ring, N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, T : TwistingData N W R (with w \in R, w^{2} = p), k \in \mathbb{N}, a \ne a' in Fin k (ha), a letter i, a labelling \tau of Fin k with \tau(a) = \tau(a') (h\tau), weights c : Fin k \times \mathbb{Z}/N \to \mathbb{R} with c(u_s) - c(\overline{u}_{s}) \in [0.4, 1.6] for every letter s, where u_s = rootVar T a a' s and \overline{u}_{s} = rootVar T a' a s (hc), a polynomial f \in R[x_{b,s}], an exponent \alpha and l \in \mathbb{N}. Assume that for every g in the Levi subgroup of \tau, polyAction T g^{-1} f lies in gaussFiltration (adicFiltration w) c (l + c\cdot \alpha), i.e. the coefficient of every Levi translate of f at x^{\beta} is divisible by w^{\lceil l + c\cdot \alpha - c\cdot \beta \rceil_{+}} (h), and that w^{l+1} does not divide the coefficient of x^{\alpha} in f (h_{0}). Then the excess of \alpha at the letter i is \le 0: \sum_{(b,s)} \alpha_{(b,s)}\cdotexcessWeight T i a a' (b, s) \le 0. (The text reading also lists the section variable w : R; the statement only mentions the field T.w.)
The argument in briefSpecialise f along x_z \mapsto x_{\pi(z)}\cdot t^{m(z)} (moving the variable u_i onto \overline{u}_{i}) and its perturbation by \eta, then twist by g(r) = swap(a, a')\cdot(1 \pm r\cdot E_{\mathit{aa}'}), which lies in the Levi subgroup as \tau(a) = \tau(a'), and Taylor-shift the auxiliary variable t by \iota_{i}(r). Gauss-filtration bookkeeping (aeval_sub_mem_of_gaussFiltration) shows that the coefficient \Phi(t) of x^{\pi(\alpha)}, reduced mod w^{l+1}, has degree \le m\cdot \alpha, is nonzero (its top coefficient is f_{\alpha}, h_{0}), and vanishes to order e\cdot \alpha at every \iota_{i}(r). Stepanov's count gives (e\cdot \alpha)\cdot q \le m\cdot \alpha, which is the claim: the excess of \alpha at i is (e\cdot \alpha - m\cdot \alpha /q)/q.
