lemma sum_graphKernel_mul

What it saysFor R, a, b, n, \chi, e as above and a complex function \Psi on n \times b matrices with \Psi(y\cdot g) = \chi^{-1}(\det g)\cdot \Psi(y) for all y and g \in \mathrm{GL}_b(R) (h): for every k \in \mathrm{GL}_n(R), \sum_{y} graphKernel(\chi, e, invLastRows(e, k), y)\cdot \Psi(y) = \chi(\det k)\cdot \Psi(firstCols(e, k)).
The argument in briefThe non-zero terms are the y = firstCols(e, k)\cdot g, g \in \mathrm{GL}_b(R); each contributes \chi(\det k)/\#\mathrm{GL}_b\cdot \Psi(firstCols(e, k)).
