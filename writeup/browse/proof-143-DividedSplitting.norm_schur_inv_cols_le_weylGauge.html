lemma DividedSplitting.norm_schur_inv_cols_le_weylGauge

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r, n, N, m and l finite types, a a type, O : AuxPair E r n, z \colon r \hookrightarrow m, V a divided splitting (DividedSplitting N m a O z), p a prime with a p-adic place \iota(\iota_p \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_p), and M \ne 0 a natural number in the ideal of the product of the chosen places of O (hM). Let L be a divided lattice with point \gamma_L, \kappa a weight, h and g points of the finite unitary groups, v a coordinate vector of weight \kappa, S a set of columns and f \colon l \to n with HasColumnsBelow n l \kappa (hl), such that some K \in \mathrm{GL}_l(O_E/M^2) makes \overline{\varpi}\cdot((\operatorname{columns} f \text{ of } L.\operatorname{reducedInverse})\cdot K)_{ij} = 0 for all i and all j \notin S (hS), and the columns f of g^{-1} equal I_f\cdot h^{-1} (e). Then \lVert S_\kappa(\operatorname{columns} f \text{ of } (g\cdot \gamma_L)^{-1}, mapped at each place \tau by the weight embedding of \iota_p)\cdot \iota_p(v)\lVert \le |M|_p^{-\sum_\tau |S|\cdot \sum_{i<|l|} \kappa(\tau, i)}\cdot \operatorname{weylGauge}(\kappa, 0, h, v), S_\kappa the Schur matrix.
The argument in briefInsert the diagonal matrix with M^{-1} on S and 1 elsewhere: the product of the inverse columns with K and with \operatorname{diag}(M \text{ on } S) is integral, so its Schur matrix has norm \le 1, and the Schur matrix of the diagonal costs the power of |M|_p.
