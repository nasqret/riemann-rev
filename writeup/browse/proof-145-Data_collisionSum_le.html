lemma Data.collisionSum_le

What it saysFor data D, a prime p (instance q), a finite set S of infinite places and o \in \mathbb{N} such that every collision ( \tau , e ) outside S has e \ge o: collisionSum( D , p , S ) \le 2 \cdot f \cdot a / p^{o}.
The argument in briefShift e = o + e' and compare with a geometric series: a / p^{o + e'} \le ( a / p^{o} ) \cdot 2^{- e'}.
