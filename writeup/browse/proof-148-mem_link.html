lemma mem_link

What it saysFor F,\mathfrak{s},T,\gamma (implicit) and a pair p=(p_1,p_2) of middle vectors (implicit): p\in\operatorname{link}(\gamma) iff there is a\in F^{\varrho} such that \gamma\cdot w, for w the vector with e-part a, middle part p_2 and e^{*}-part 0, has middle part p_1 and e^{*}-part 0.

    Every vector t is \gamma w for a unique w (\gamma is invertible), so p\in\operatorname{link}(\gamma) iff some w satisfies: \gamma w\bot X (e^{*}-part of \gamma w is 0), w=\gamma^{-1}(\gamma w)\bot X (e^{*}-part of w is 0), middle part of \gamma w=p_1 and middle part of w=p_2. Splitting w into its e-part a, middle part and e^{*}-part, and the existential over the disjoint union of index sets, these are exactly the stated conditions.
