lemma IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel.unipotentMass_mul_inner_besselVector

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma. For a standard level, Z\in IntermediateGroup(F) and y,x\in U(J): unipotentMass(F)\cdot\langle W_Z(y),W_Z(yx)\rangle=\int1_Z(u y)\cdot\operatorname{phase}(\mathtt{F.g},(uy)x(uy)^{-1})d(\eta_0\ast\cdots\ast\eta_{\varrho+1})(u),\eta_l= heisenbergMeasure(l).
The argument in briefExpand the inner product as a sum over cosets c; for each c the term equals the \mathsf{U}-measure of \{u:uya_{c}{}^{-1}\in K(g)\} (unfold_besselU with \theta=\delta_1) times the product of coordinates; pointwise in u, the sum of indicators equals 1_Z(uy)\cdot\operatorname{phase}(g,(uy)x(uy)^{-1}) since exactly one coset contains uy.
