lemma Filling.exists_cutoff_classUnip

What it saysLet S be filling data over E for \mathfrak{s},c a class (implicit) and K a subgroup of \mathsf{U}_c(g)= classUnip(S,c). Let \Gamma be the image of K in \prod_v G_1 under ratToInf(S). Then \Gamma is discrete, and there is a measurable \beta:\prod_v G_1\to[0,\infty] with \sum_{u\in K}\beta(\mathtt{ratToInf}(u)\cdot y)=1 for every y and which is a cutoff for \Gamma with counting measure (IsCutoff: \int_{\Gamma}\beta(\gamma y)d count =1).
The argument in brief\Gamma is discrete because only finitely many points of \mathsf{U}_c(g) lie over a compact set; a countable discrete group with counting measure has a cutoff.
