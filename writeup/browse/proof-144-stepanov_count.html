lemma stepanov_count

What it saysStepanov's count. Let W be a commutative local ring, R a commutative ring, \iota : W \to R a ring homomorphism, \Phi \in R[X] a nonzero polynomial (h_{0}) and E \in \mathbb{N} such that X^{E} divides the Taylor shift \Phi(X + \iota(x)) for every x \in W (h), i.e. (X - \iota(x))^{E} divides \Phi. Then E\cdotNat.card(residue field of W) \le \deg \Phi (natDegree). (Nat.card is 0 for an infinite residue field, where the claim is trivial.)
The argument in briefChoose a lift \tilde{x} of each of the q residue classes; the polynomials (X - \iota(\tilde{x}))^{E} are pairwise coprime (differences of distinct lifts are units), so their product, monic of degree E\cdot q, divides \Phi \ne 0.

  2. 2deductionlines 30–34

    Let \tilde{x} be a lift (surjInv of the residue map) of each residue class. For distinct classes the difference of the lifts is a unit (its residue is nonzero), so \iota of it is a unit and the polynomials (X - \iota(\tilde{x}))^{E} are pairwise coprime; each divides \Phi (h, via the Taylor shift); hence their product divides \Phi. It is monic of degree E\cdot |\kappa |, and a nonzero polynomial is not divisible by a monic polynomial of larger degree.

    UsesFintype.prod_dvd_of_coprime, Polynomial.isCoprime_X_sub_C_of_isUnit_sub, Polynomial.X_sub_C_pow_dvd_iff, Polynomial.Monic.not_dvd_of_natDegree_lt, Polynomial.monic_prod_X_sub_C
