lemma Parameters.exists_cyclotomic_different_factor

What it saysLet R be an integral domain of characteristic 0, N \in \mathbb{N}, \zeta \in R a primitive N-th root of unity, and l a prime factor of N. Then there are r \in \mathbb{N} and M \in \mathbb{N} with M \le N, N = l\cdot r, such that for every prime factor p of N: p \mid M \iff p \ne l; and there is V \in R with V \mid M in R and \Phi_N'(\zeta)\cdot(\zeta^r - 1)\cdot M = N\cdot V, where \Phi_N is the N-th cyclotomic polynomial over R and \Phi_N' its derivative.
The argument in briefWrite N = l\cdot r and r = l^a\cdot M with l \nmid M. Differentiating X^N - 1 = (X^r - 1)\cdot \Phi_N(X)\cdot q(X) at \zeta gives N = \Phi_N'(\zeta)(\zeta^r - 1)q(\zeta)\zeta; differentiating \Phi_N(X) = \Phi_{lM}(X^{l^a}) and \Phi_{lM}\cdot \Phi_M = \Phi_M(X^l) at \zeta shows that N divides \Phi_N'(\zeta)(\zeta^r - 1)M; the quotient V divides M.

    It suffices to show that N divides \Phi_N'(\zeta)(\zeta^r - 1)M in R: write the quotient as V. Then M \le N (M \mid r \mid N), N = l\cdot r, a prime factor of N divides M iff it is not l (r = l^a M, l \nmid M), and V \mid M: from (ii), N\cdot M = \Phi_N'(\zeta)(\zeta^r - 1)M\cdot q(\zeta)\zeta = N\cdot V\cdot q(\zeta)\zeta, and N is cancellable in characteristic 0, so M = V\cdot q(\zeta)\zeta.
