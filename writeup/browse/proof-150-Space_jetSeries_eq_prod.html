lemma Space.jetSeries_eq_prod

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, V a hermitian space over E of rank n with all signs +1, f \in \mathcal{W}_f, I a finite index type, G \colon I \to(\mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}) entire functions, and \mu \colon \mathbb{C} \to I \to(\Phi \times n \to \mathbb{C}) such that \Theta_f(c\cdot z) = \prod_i G_i(\mu_{c, i} \cdot z) for all c \in \mathbb{C} and all z (\cdot the bilinear dot product). Then \theta(f) = \prod_i G_i(0) (\theta = Space.θ the theta functional) and, for every c \in \mathbb{C}, T_c f = \prod_i (Taylor series of G_i at 0) substituted at the linear series z \mapsto \mu_{c, i}\cdot z.
The argument in brief\theta(f) is the case c = z = 0. For T_c f compare the coefficients at \alpha: two polynomials agree if they agree at every point v, and evaluating at v the homogeneous part of total degree |\alpha | is reading the coefficient of u^{|\alpha |} of the one-variable restriction z = v\cdot u; along a line both sides are Taylor series of the same function by taylor_line_eq_subst_jetSeries and the product formula.
