lemma amitsur_charpoly_mem_matrix

What it saysAmitsur's formula for matrices. For an R-algebra \Lambda, an R-algebra map \rho : \Lambda \to M_\sigma (L) such that every coefficient of the characteristic polynomial of every \rho (w) lies in the image of R (c), a matrix P \in M_N(\Lambda ) and k: the coefficient of X^k of the characteristic polynomial of the block matrix (\rho (P_{ij}))_{i,j}, flattened to an (N \times \sigma )-matrix over L, lies in the image of R.
The argument in briefApply Amitsur's span criterion to the flattened block map: the matrices with one nonzero entry span, and the characteristic polynomial of the block image of such a matrix is, up to a power of X, that of the image of one entry.
