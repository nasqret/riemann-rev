lemma IndexDvd.of_det_dvd

What it saysDeterminant criterion. Let O be a valuation domain (an integral domain whose ideals are totally ordered: ValuationRing O), \iota a finite type, z \in O, A an \iota\times\iota matrix over O with \det A dividing z (h), and N \subseteq O^{\iota} a submodule containing the image A(O^{\iota}) (e). Then IndexDvd O^{\iota} N z (the whole module \top on the left).
The argument in briefHermite form of the block matrix [[1, -\Phi], [0, A^{T}]] over the valuation domain O: it equals U\cdot D with U invertible over O and D block lower triangular. The upper-left block D_{11} is the required matrix: its determinant divides \det A (hence z), and comparing entries of U^{-1}Y = D shows that each combination \sum_k (D_{11})_{ik}\Phi_k is A applied to a vector of O^{\iota}, hence lies in N.
