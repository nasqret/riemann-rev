lemma AuxPair.descentsVanish_of_reflectionSum

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data, O an auxiliary pair over E (W(O) of rank r, V = I_n), y a form in FormV(O) and u a linear functional on coordinate vectors of weight \kappa(O). Assume (hQ) \psi(O, w) is nontrivial at every twist place w of O; (hM) for every chosen place i and every family v = (v_j), v_j in k_{w_j}^{r} for the chosen places j, with v_i nonzero, the element piTensorElem((reflectionSum(\psi(O, w_j), v_j))_{j}) of the group algebra of \prod_{j} \mathrm{GL}_r(k_{w_j}) kills y through formAction(O); and (h0) for every polynomial P, the descent Space.lift(swap of pairThetaKer(W(O), stdSpace(E, n), a(O), 1), v_P tensor auxDatum(O), \operatorname{conj}(algFormCoeff(1, u)(y)), g) is 0 for every point g, where v_P is the Fock vector of P. Then DescentsVanish(W(O), stdSpace(E, n), a(O), S, \operatorname{conj}(algFormCoeff(1, u)(y))) for S the set of finite places outside the places of O and their conjugates: every such descent with a datum that is the vacuum at the places of S and their conjugates is 0.
The argument in briefBy density it suffices to treat data v_P tensor (named coset indicator at the places of O) and the bilinear pairing C_z(y') of a coset indicator centred at z with a form y'. The transport lemma lv: moving the centre by an integral element 1 + A B^{T} changes y' into (\prod \psi)^{-1} chosenGLRep(s) y'. For each place an annihilator l of the stacked matrix (z, t) is chosen. If all \iota-column residues of all l vanish, z is moved to the inclusion matrix and the Upsilon-datum computation (h0) shows C_z(y) = 0: the residue datum \Psi is a sum over a finite set F of coset representatives q (with \Psi(q) \ne 0) of \Psi(q) times the coset indicator of q, every term \Psi(q) C_q(y) equals \Psi(z) C_z(y), so h0 gives |F| \Psi(z) C_z(y) = 0, and F is nonempty because \Psi(z) \ne 0. Otherwise some row c has nonzero residues m(c, j): at a chosen place, summing lv over the reflections 1 + x m^{T} gives the reflection sum, which kills y (hM); at a twist place, a single reflection of determinant t with \psi(t) not 1 multiplies C_z(y) by \psi(t)^{-1}, so C_z(y) = 0 (hQ).
