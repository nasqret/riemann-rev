lemma Filling.pairInvariant_mul_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S filling data, c,c' classes, s\in\mathsf{G} agreeing (hs), x real with FixesNormAtLeast(S,s,x) (hq), and \gamma,\gamma'\in G(E) both terms over (c,s,c') (h,h') of level j (hc, hd). Then there is i\in\mathbb{N} with k(\mathfrak{s})\le\dim commonLink(\gamma,\gamma')+i+(j-\varrho)_+, with \operatorname{link}(\gamma)=\operatorname{link}(\gamma') if i=0, and pairInvariant(S,c,c',\gamma,\gamma')\cdotpairInvariant(S,c,c',\gamma',\gamma)\cdot x'^{2}\le(2/g(S))^{2if}\cdotclassGauge(S,j,c,c',\gamma)\cdotclassGauge(S,j,c,c',\gamma'), where x'=x if \varrho<j and i=0, else 1. (Notation: T= ratMiddle(S) is the Gram matrix of the rational middle form X_0\oplus Ez with z of square h(z,z)=-N(S) (T= middleGram(N(S)): 1 on X_0 and -N(S) at z), J= ratGram(S) its split Gram matrix on BesselIndex(\mathfrak{s}); Z(\gamma)= frameProducts(S,\gamma); D_j(\gamma)= leadingMinor(Z(\gamma),j); \mathsf{g}(\gamma)= termDisc(S,\gamma); \operatorname{link}(\gamma)\subseteq(X_0\oplus Ez)^{2} is the image under linkMatrix(\gamma) of the J-orthogonal of the isotropic frame pair (f_a,\gamma f_a); zFormPair(T)=\operatorname{diag}(T_z,T_z) takes the z-pairing in both halves; linkMetric(S)=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\operatorname{diag}(T,T)+N(S)^{-1}\cdotzFormPair(T)^{*}zFormPair(T), the diagonal matrix with 1/2 at the X_0-coordinates and -N(S)/2+N(S)=N(S)/2 at the two z-coordinates; linkDiscSq(S,v,e)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(\det(v^{*}\cdot\mathtt{linkMetric}(S)\cdot v))|\cdot[\det e]^{2},[\cdot] the generalised index of a finite adele; minorNorm(S,j,\gamma)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(D_j(\gamma))|/N(S)^{2(j-\varrho)_+f}; termDiscNorm(S,\gamma)=\prod_v|\Phi_v(\mathsf{g}(\gamma))|; HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m): m= linkMatrix(\gamma)\cdot t\cdot w for a rational t with (isotropic frame pair of \gamma)*\cdot J\cdot t=0 and adelic w with classCoord(S,c)\cdot t\cdot w integral; f=f(E) the number of infinite places.)
The argument in briefApply exists_link_split_le_classGauge to (\gamma,\gamma') and to (\gamma',\gamma) (same L); the two complements y,y' have integral lifts; the pairing matrix (g^{-1}y)^{*}\cdotlinkForm\cdot y' between them is invertible (a kernel vector would be orthogonal to the link, hence in L) and integral (\gamma\gamma'^{-1}\in K(g)), so Liouville's inequality with Q=\operatorname{diag}(\mathtt{linkForm}/\mathtt{linkMetric}) gives 1\le(2/g)^{2if}\cdotlinkDiscSq(y,v)\cdotlinkDiscSq(y',v')\cdot\ldots; multiplying the two class-gauge bounds gives the claim.
