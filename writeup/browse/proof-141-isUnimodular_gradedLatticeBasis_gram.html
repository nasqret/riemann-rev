lemma isUnimodular_gradedLatticeBasis_gram

What it saysFor finite index types X, \iota, s, N \in \mathbb{N}, a bijection e : (X \sqcup \{\operatorname{pt}\}) \sqcup \{0, \ldots, s-1\} \simeq \iota, commutative rings S and R (R with an involution), a subring O of R, a ring homomorphism f : S \to R, units a, \pi of R with f(N) = \pi\cdot\overline{\pi} (hN) and a unitary u \in R: the Gram matrix G^{H}\cdot f(gradedGram(e, N))\cdot G of the graded lattice basis G = gradedLatticeBasis(e, a, \pi, u) is unimodular over O (IsUnimodular: hermitian and in \mathrm{GL}_{\iota}(O)).
The argument in briefThe Gram matrix is an explicit matrix H(N, 0) over O, whose inverse is H(0, N).
