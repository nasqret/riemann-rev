What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r, n, N, m and a finite types, O : AuxPair E r n, z \colon r \hookrightarrow m and V a divided splitting (DividedSplitting N m a O z), E with Weil data, L : DividedLattice O, s an a\times N matrix over O_E/M^2, b, b' characters with L glued for s (H: V.IsGlued s L) and b\cdot b' = O.b (hb), h, h' points of the finite unitary groups of I_N and I_m, y a coordinate vector of weight O.colsPlus, \eta a coordinate vector of weight O.colsMinus and g a point of the adelic group of W.
