abbrevLevelPlaces.TwistGL

Definition 2.2 of the note · unit 134 · New/Forms/AuxiliaryPair.lean, lines 15–16

For a CM field E, a system of places S over E (LevelPlaces: chosen places M and twist places Q) and a finite type n: TwistGL(S, n) is the product, over the twist places w of S (the family Q), of \mathrm{GL}_n(k_w), k_w the residue field at w.

defLevelPlaces.twistLift

Definition 2.2 of the note · unit 134 · New/Forms/AuxiliaryPair.lean, lines 24–25

For a system of places S over E and A in TwistGL(S, n): twistLift(S, A) = liftResidue of the family that is 1 at the chosen places M and A^{-1} at the twist places Q; that is, a chosen element of K_0 (integral unitary matrices of rank n) whose reduction at each place w of Q is A_w^{-1} and at each place of M is 1.

defAuxPair.levelIndicator

Definition 2.4 of the note · unit 134 · New/Forms/AuxiliaryPair.lean, lines 29–32

For an auxiliary pair O over E (W of rank n, V = I_{n'}) and a matrix y with rows indexed by n' and columns by n over the residue ring \mathcal{O}_E/MQ (M and Q the products of the chosen and twist places of O): levelIndicator(O, y) is 1 if, at every chosen place w of M, y reduced mod w is the inclusion matrix of incl(O), and at every twist place w of Q, the incl(O)-rows of y reduced mod w form the identity matrix; and 0 otherwise.

lemmaAuxPair.upsilon_eq_sum

Proposition 3.1 of the note · unit 134 · New/Forms/AuxiliaryPair.lean, lines 39–50

Let E be a CM field with Weil data and O an auxiliary pair over E with W of rank n and V = I_{n'}. Then auxDatum(O) equals the sum, over A in TwistGL(O, n), of \omega((1, twistLift(O, A)), 1) applied to modDatum(W(O), stdSpace(E, n'), m, levelIndicator(O, .), vacVector), m = levelModulus(O, 1) the modulus MQ. Here \omega = \omega_{a(O), b(O)} is the Weil representation of the pair (W, I_{n'}) (pairThetaKernel(O)); (1, twistLift(O, A)) is the element of the adelic group of W that is 1 at the infinite places and twistLift(O, A) at the finite places; modDatum(S, T, m, Psi, v) is the datum v tensor \Psi tensor vacuum on \operatorname{Hom}(S, T), \Psi a function on matrices modulo MQ named at the places dividing MQ, v = vacVector the Gaussian at the infinite places.

lemmaAuxPair.pairWeil_upsilon

Proposition 3.2 of the note · unit 134 · New/Forms/AuxiliaryPair.lean, lines 55–67

Let E be a CM field with Weil data, O an auxiliary pair over E with W of rank n and V = I_{n'}, k in K_0 of rank n (for W), k' in K_0 of rank n' (for V), and j in auxLevel(O) (families in \prod_w \mathrm{GL}_{n'}(k_w) over the places of O that fix the incl(O)-basis vectors at M and preserve the span of the others at Q). Assume the reductions of k' at the places of O equal firstBlockHom(O, incl(O)) of the reductions of k at the chosen places, times j. Then \omega(k, k')(auxDatum(O)) = \bigl(\bigl(\prod over the chosen places w_i of \psi(O, w_i)(\det(k \bmod w_i))\bigr)^{-1} auxLevelChar(O)(j)\bigr) auxDatum(O), with \omega the Weil representation of pairThetaKernel(O). Spelled out, auxLevelChar(O)(j) = \prod over the twist places w in Q of \psi(O, w)(\det j_{w,00})^{-1}, j_{w,00} the \mathrm{incl}(O) \times \mathrm{incl}(O) block of j_w: the chosen places and the twist places both contribute inverse character values.

defAuxPair.paramV

Definition 2.5 of the note · unit 134 · New/Forms/AuxiliaryPair.lean, line 74

For an auxiliary pair O over E (W of rank r, V = I_n), an infinite place w and an index i in n: paramV(O, w, i) = hcParameter(kappa(O), detExp(O), |n|, w, p), p = equivFin(n)(i) the position of i in a fixed numbering of n; that is, 2(\operatorname{detExp}(O, w) + \lambda_p) + |n| - 1 - 2p with \lambda_p = sum over p < h < |n| of \kappa(O)(w, h): twice the Harish-Chandra parameter \lambda + \rho of the weight (\kappa(O), \operatorname{detExp}(O)) of O on V at w.

lemmaAuxPair.isHCParam_paramV

structureAuxPair.FitsParams

Definition 2.7 of the note · unit 134 · New/Forms/AuxiliaryPair.lean, lines 85–90

For an auxiliary pair O over E (W of rank r with signs s = signs(O), V = I_n) and a parameter l on W (an integer for each infinite place and each index of r): FitsParams(O, l) holds iff (1) paramsCorrespond: at every infinite place w, paramV(O, w, .) and -l(w) match (IsMatch) for the signs 1 on V and s on W with the weights negCount(s) and -(\lfloor |n|/2 \rfloor + \operatorname{weight}(O, w)) (ParamsCorrespond); (2) ord_eq: for every w and c in r, ord(O, c) is the number of c' with s(c') = s(c) and s(c)\, l(w, c) < s(c)\, l(w, c'); (3) colsPlus_eq_zero: colsPlus(O, tau, i) = 0 whenever i exceeds the number of signs +1 of s; (4) colsMinus_eq_zero: colsMinus(O, tau, i) = 0 whenever i exceeds negCount(s).

lemmaAuxPair.FitsParams.exists_isJointHarmonic

Theorem 2 of the note · unit 134 · New/Forms/AuxiliaryPair.lean, lines 120–138

Let O be an auxiliary pair over E (W of rank r with signs s = signs(O), V = I_n) and l a parameter on W with FitsParams(O, l). Then there is a family \Phi of polynomials, one for each infinite place w, such that (1) for every w, \Phi(w) is a joint harmonic (IsJointHarmonic) for the pair std(1, s, negCount(s), -(floor(|n|/2) + weight(O, w))) with parameters paramV(O, w, .) and -l(w); (2) harmonicPoly(O)(highestVector(kappa(O), paramV(O, ., .))) is the product over w of \Phi(w) with its variables renamed into the variables of the place w; and (3) for every w and c in r, the lowest K-type of -l(w) for the signs s at c equals staircaseWeight(n, s, ord(O), k, -(floor(|n|/2) + weight(O, w)), c), where k(\eta) = colsPlus(O, w) if \eta = 1 and colsMinus(O, w) otherwise.

lemmaAuxPair.theta_eq_smul_star_lift_swap

Theorem 1 of the note · unit 134 · New/Forms/AuxiliaryPair.lean, lines 169–179

Let E be a CM field with Weil data and O an auxiliary pair over E (W of rank r, V = I_n). For every form \Xi in FormV(O) (algebraic forms on V of weight (\kappa(O), \operatorname{detExp}(O)), level levelGroup(O) and character auxLevelChar(O)) and every point h of the adelic group of W: theta(O)(Xi)(h) = c\,\operatorname{conj}(Space.lift(swap of pairThetaKernel(O), phi, conj(F_Xi), h)) = c\,\operatorname{conj}\bigl( integral over g in [U(I_n)] of \theta(\omega(g, h)(\phi))\, \operatorname{conj}(F_\Xi(g))\, dg\bigr), where c = |WeylIndex(n, kappa(O))| stdLevelIndex(levelGroup(O)) (stdLevelIndex(K) = [K_0 : K intersect K_0]); the integration variable g runs over the quotient [U(I_n)] of the definite source V with its quotient measure, and h is the point of the target W of the descent; \omega and \theta are the Weil representation and theta functional of the swap of pairThetaKernel(O) (the group of V first); \phi = fockDatum(O)(highestVector(kappa(O), paramV(O, ., .))), the Fock vector of P_{v+} tensor \Upsilon; and F_\Xi = ofAlgForm(paramV(O, ., .), 1)(Xi), F_\Xi(g) = \langle v+, \rho(g_\infty^{-1})\, \Xi(g_f) \rangle. Both conjugations matter: the lift is linear in its input, which is \operatorname{conj}(F_\Xi), and its output is conjugated again. theta(O) itself is defined (AuxPair.θ) as the finite class sum over the level of x \mapsto conjKernel(O, h, x)(Xi(x)); this integral description with the scalar c is the theorem.
