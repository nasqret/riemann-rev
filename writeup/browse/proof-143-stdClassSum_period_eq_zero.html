What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, r (nonempty), n and N finite types, O : AuxPair E r n, l_0 a parameter with O.FitsParams l_0 (hO), \psi_q \ne 1 at every twist place q (hQ), 2|r| + 2|N| + 3 < |n| (hn), w a split finite place (hw) outside the places of O and their conjugates (hS), and \Xi \in O.FormV killed by theta descent (h0: O.θ \Xi = 0), by the reflection sums (hM: for every chosen index i and every family v of rows with v_i \ne 0, \otimes_i \operatorname{reflectionSum}(\psi, v_i) acts on \Xi by 0) and by the Hecke polynomial at the test value (hH: O.heckePolyOnForms hw hS (O.testValue w) \Xi = 0). Let further l be a finite type, \varepsilon \colon N \oplus l \simeq n, \kappa a weight, Y the residue matrices at the chosen places with injective columns (hY), e' \colon r \hookrightarrow n with e' = \varepsilon \circ inl \circ u for some u (he), C a conjugate-linear map from coordinate vectors of weight \kappa to those of weight O.κ that is equivariant (hC: for g, y in the definite group GInf E 1 with g_\tau = \varepsilon-relabelled \operatorname{diag}(1, y_\tau), \operatorname{conjWeylRep}(O.\operatorname{detExp})(g)(C \zeta) = C(\operatorname{conjWeylRep}(\kappa, 0)(y) \zeta)), b a character, \mathfrak{m} a modulus with places among those of O (h\mathfrak{m}), \Psi a function on matrices modulo \mathfrak{m}, g, h₁ points, x \in G_f(I_n) supported on the chosen places (hx), K a level (IsLevel K, instance ho), and f the function h \mapsto \langle \operatorname{pointForm} (U.block \varepsilon (h₁, h)\cdot x) hY e' (C(\operatorname{kernelVector} O b \mathfrak{m} \Psi \kappa g h)), \Xi \rangle (hf). Suppose that every k \in K fixes \Psi(\Psi((k \text{ mod } \mathfrak{m})^{-1}\cdot T) = \Psi(T)) and that x^{-1}\cdot \operatorname{diag}(1, k)\cdot x lies in K_0 and fixes every Y_i (hk). Then the class sum of f at level K is 0.
The argument in briefTurn the class sum into a theta period of a sum of products of theta kernels with matrix coefficients of \Xi over U(I_l) \subset U(I_n); by period_eq_zero this period is a theta lift of the coefficients to W \oplus \mathbb{H}^N, which vanishes by descentsVanish_hypSum; a nonzero theta value at a real vector divides out.
