lemma Lift.KummerChart.exists_depth_step_zero

What it saysLet p, \Gamma, k be as in the setting and a \in \mathbb{N}. There are t_1, \ldots, t_N \in galMonoid(k), N = p^{\mathrm{cost}(a, 0)} = p^{d+3a}, such that for every g \in galMonoid(k) and every s \in S, p_a = \varphi^{a}(E) divides g\cdot\sum_l t_l\cdot s - \sum_l t_l\cdot s in A_{\mathrm{inf}}.
The argument in briefTake t_l = (0, \gamma_l) with \gamma_l z = [\varepsilon]^{l} z. Modulo p_a every s \in S is a polynomial \sum w_m z^{m}; \theta\varphi^{-a} sends \sum_l t_l\cdot(w_m z^{m}) to w_m \theta\varphi^{-a}(z)^{m} \sum_l \zeta^{lm} with \zeta a primitive p^{a}-th root of unity, which vanishes unless p^{a} | m, in which case the term is fixed by g.
