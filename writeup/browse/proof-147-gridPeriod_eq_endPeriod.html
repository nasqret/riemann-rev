lemma Data.gridPeriod_eq_endPeriod

What it saysLet P be parameters, D' admissible data, \Lambda a family of data (\Lambda(\vartheta) \in GridDatum(\vartheta)) and g a real point. There is \beta \in \mathbb{C} such that for every triple x =(\vartheta, \Xi, \varphi): gridPeriod(\Lambda, x, m \le m, g) = \beta \cdotendPeriod(\Lambda(\vartheta), (\Xi, \varphi)) (the pair of forms read as the function on the two grids).
The argument in briefAt the last stage the lifts along a last column are chains of theta kernels (C); the diagonal period of two chains is a multiple of their branching pairing (endBranching); collect the constants.
