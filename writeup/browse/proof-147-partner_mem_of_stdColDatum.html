lemma partner_mem_of_stdColDatum

What it saysLet F be a semilocal field (instance V) with compact open subring O and topologically nilpotent unit \varpi, a, b, n finite index types, \chi a tame multiplicative character of F (instance h\chi), e : b \sqcup a \simeq n, U a set of n \times b matrices, P a space of complex functions on n \times b matrices that is stable under \mathrm{GL}_n(F) (hP) and consists of Schwartz–Bruhat functions supported in U (hS), and \Phi, \Psi partners for \chi over O with respect to e (Q) with \Psi locally constant (h\Psi). Let p \in P (hp), x an integral n \times b matrix (hx), s an integral b \times n matrix (hs) with s\cdot x = 1 (e_{1}), and r a function from n \times b matrices to \mathbb{N} with p(y) = r(y)\cdotstdColDatum(\chi, s)(y) for all y (H) and r(x) \ne 0 (h_{1}). Then \Psi \in P.
The argument in briefAverage translates of p over the compact group \mathrm{GL}_n(O), weighted by values of \Psi: the average lies in P and equals a positive multiple of \Psi, because \Psi is invariant under small translations and transforms by \chi under \mathrm{GL}_b(O).
