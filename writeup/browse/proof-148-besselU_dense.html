lemma besselU_dense

What it saysStrong approximation for the unipotent group \mathsf{U}. Let E be a CM number field, \mathfrak{s} a shape, T a hermitian invertible Gram matrix on V_{\varrho} over E, and T' a Gram matrix over \mathbb{A}_f with T.splitGram mapped entrywise to \mathbb{A}_f equal to T'.splitGram (e). Then \mathsf{U}(\mathbb{A}_f) (the unipotent group of T') is contained in the closure of the image of \mathsf{U}(E) (of T) under the entrywise map E\to\mathbb{A}_f: every open set of matrices containing a finite adelic point of \mathsf{U} contains a rational one.
The argument in brief\mathsf{U} is generated floor by floor by Heisenberg elements \nu(v,\beta); these depend continuously on (v,\beta), and rational parameters are dense.
