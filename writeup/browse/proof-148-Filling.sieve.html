lemma Filling.sieve

What it saysLemma (the sieve). Let S be admissible, T a finite set of finite places stable under conjugation, E with Weil data, A a finite set of units of E with t,t^{-1} integral outside T,g^{-1} integral outside T, and U\subseteq A. Let J' be a sieve system of depth \varrho on \prod_{w\in T}E_w with unit map unitOrOne, map p\mapsto ratToPlaces(T)(g^{-1})\cdot p^{-1}, character charOnPlaces(S,T), bottom bound e, weights finHeadWeight(n^{6}) and frequency bound frequencyGain(S,T) (n=\varrho+1). For p : Fin \varrho\to A,t\in A and an index i put x_v(p,t,i)= unitOrOne(\mathtt{frameScaleSq}(S,v,i)\cdot\Phi_v(\mathtt{padTuple}(p,t,i))) and y(p,t)= the image of padTuple(p,t) in \prod_T E_w. Then \operatorname{Re}\Sigma_{t\in U}\Sigma_p\Sigma_q (\prod_v J_v(x_v(p,t),x_v(q,t)))\cdot J_n'(y(p,t),y(q,t))\le(\prod_{i<n}\operatorname{vol}(\hat{O})/\prod_v\mathtt{frameScaleSq}(S,v,i)^{2})\cdot((\prod_v(1+b^{-10})/\operatorname{deg}_{v})\cdot e+\mathtt{besselDegree}(S)^{-1}/b^{6\operatorname{kf}+10}), where J_v is the band-limit form of the Bessel system of S at v and \operatorname{deg}_{v} its degree.
The argument in briefApply one_sided_poisson to the product system (the band-limit systems of the Bessel systems at the infinite places times J' at T) with \Gamma a power of b: the frequency condition is sum_frequencies_le with c_v=\kappa_v^{2\varrho+1}/(\mathtt{gN}), and the weight condition (\Sigma_p\Lambda)^{2}\le\Gamma\prod c_a follows from the head weights via sum_frequencies_le applied to the partial products; then compare (1+b^{-10})/\deg and the error with prod_band_le_inv_deg.
