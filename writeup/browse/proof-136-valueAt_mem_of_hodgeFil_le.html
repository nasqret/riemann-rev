lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.valueAt_mem_of_hodgeFil_le

What it saysLet L be a lattice datum, a \in \mathbb{N}, i a letter, c an exponent function, u \in \mathbb{Z}, x \in withExponents(L, i, c) (hx), \mu \in formsOfType(L, \sigma^{-a}(i), u) (hμ), and W a B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{+}-submodule of B_{\mathrm{dR}} (an A_{\mathrm{inf}}-submodule) containing hodgeFil(c(a, v)) for every v \in U_{\sigma^{-a}(i)} with v \le u (H; default proof grind). Then valueAt(L, a, \mu, x) \in W; u itself need not be in U_{\sigma^{-a}(i)}.
The argument in briefSuppose not. Descending from u, a type that is not a jump of L can be lowered (formsOfType_subset_pred_of_notMem), and at jumps v \le u the exponent condition of x lands in W; so \mu(\varphi^{-a}x)\cdot(denominator) lies in every t^{-m}-step, forcing it to be 0.
