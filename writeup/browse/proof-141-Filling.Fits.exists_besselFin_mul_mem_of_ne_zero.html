lemma Filling.Fits.exists_besselFin_mul_mem_of_ne_zero

What it saysFor filling data S fitting V (h), coefficients \varphi and an adelic point g at which the Poincaré series \mathsf{W}_{\varphi} = \mathrm{poincare}_h(\varphi, Y.ratBessel) over the rational points of the Bessel group does not vanish (hg): some r in the finite Bessel group B_f (Grading.besselFin) has r\cdot g_f \in stdPrincipalLevel.
The argument in briefIf no element of B_f moved g_f into the level, every term of the Poincaré series would vanish at g.
