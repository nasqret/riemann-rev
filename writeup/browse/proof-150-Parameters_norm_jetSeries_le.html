lemma Parameters.norm_jetSeries_le

What it saysLet V be a standard hermitian space over E(P, s) of rank n, p a prime with a p-adic place \iota_p, \delta \in \mathcal{O}_K, f \in \operatorname{standardData}(V, \delta), c \in \mathbb{C}, L a \mathbb{C}-linear form on power series (in the variables \Phi \times n), and r \ge 0 (a non-negative real). Assume |\iota_p(L(\Theta))| \le r for every power series \Theta whose image under \iota_p lies in \operatorname{thetaProducts}(P, s, \delta, |\iota_p(c\cdot s_1)|, n) (s_1 = \operatorname{periodFactor}(P, s)). Then |\iota_p(L(T_{c\cdot per} f))| \le r, where T_{c\cdot per} = Space.jetSeries (c\cdot \operatorname{jetScale}(P, s)).
The argument in briefThe set of f with |\iota_p L(T f)| \le r is an O_\iota-submodule, so it suffices to treat the generators \operatorname{finHeisOp}(x)\theta^{\circ}. Write \theta^{\circ} through the vacuum expansion of the vector F of exists_finitePart_of_isTightFrame for a tight frame of length M^2, M = p(dN_1)^6 + 1 prime to p: the coefficients are p-adically integral, and each T(translate of F) is, by the product formula, a theta product of the required kind.
