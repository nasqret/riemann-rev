lemma IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel.eventually_sum_packets_le_energy

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma. For a standard level with units open and inversion continuous at 1,Z,R,\omega, a finite nonempty set i with o\in i,l_{\theta}\in U(V_{\varrho}) with l_{\theta}^{-1}z=e, and indices n_{\theta} such that the pairs (K(g)l_{\theta},n_{\theta}) are distinct: putting v_{\theta}(p)=\operatorname{correlation}(Z,\omega_p,l_{\theta})_{n_{\theta}}, for all x near e, all k\in\mathsf{H}\cap K(g) and y with k\cdot y=x: \mu_H(K(g))\cdot\Sigma_{\theta} (\operatorname{Re}v_{\theta}^{*}\mathtt{Rv}_{\theta})^{+}\le\operatorname{energy}(Z,R,\omega,y).
The argument in briefNear e, every relevant l is of the form l_o\cdot(Eichler element)\cdot\ldots; the energy integrand at h\cdot l dominates, on the \mathsf{H}-translates of K(g), the packets of the l_{\theta}, which are distinct; integrate over \mu_H.
