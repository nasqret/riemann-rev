lemma theta_pointForm

What it saysFor E, r, n, O, h, Y, hY and e as in pointForm, Weil data on E, a coordinate vector v of weight O.κ and a point g of the adelic group of W: O.θ (pointForm h hY e v) g = \operatorname{conj} modKernel O.W (stdSpace E n) O.a O.b (O.levelModulus 2) \Psi_Y (O.harmonicPoly \overline{v}) g h, where \Psi_Y(T) = \prod_j \psi_{q_j}(\det of the rows e of T mod q_j) if T mod w_i = Y_i at every chosen place w_i, and 0 otherwise, and \overline{v} is the conjugate vector.
The argument in briefThe class sum defining \theta, applied to a delta form, is the conjugate kernel at the point h\cdot k; moving k into the datum turns the residue datum into \Psi_Y.
