lemma Data.Pair.isIntegralMeasure_intAlgPart

What it saysOne group-ring element gives all twists of a modulus. For P, D, a pair p, n with H : HasLiftRank(p, n) and a tame character \vartheta: the normalised value \vartheta ' \mapsto intAlgPart(H, \vartheta ') is an integral measure for \vartheta (Data.TameChar.IsIntegralMeasure): intAlgPart(H, \vartheta ') = \sum_{a} c_a\cdot \vartheta '(u_a) for all \vartheta ' with the modulus Q of \vartheta, with points u_a and coefficients c_a not depending on \vartheta ' and integral at primes not dividing the norm of Q.
The argument in briefPoints \det((1, g_x)/(1, g_y)) over pairs of the class representatives g = u\cdottwistLift(A), coefficients the class sums of \operatorname{Gram} forms of the integral point kernels; the norm of the lift expands into this sum.
