lemma Data.isSplitLevel_nonInducedPlaces

What it saysFor admissible data D and a chosen \iota : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_N (PadicPlace N): D.nonInducedPlaces is a split level for N (IsSplitLevel): disjoint from its conjugate, of norm N with uniformiser N, and the places above N are those of S \cup \overline{S}.
The argument in briefThe places above N are the induced places and their conjugates, permuted simply by the Galois group.
