lemma IsApt.range_tupleProj_mem_invtSubmodule

What it saysFor an auxiliary pair O, labels c, a split finite place w of E (\overline{w} \ne w) not in the conjugate closure of the places of O, and X \in \mathbb{C}: the range of tupleProj O c is invariant under the Hecke operator H_w(X) = \sum _i (-X)^i q^{i(i-1)/2} T_{w,i} on the forms on V (heckePolyOnForms hw hS X).
The argument in briefThe range of the action of a group-algebra element is invariant under every operator that commutes with the group action; the Hecke operator at w commutes with translations at the chosen places.
