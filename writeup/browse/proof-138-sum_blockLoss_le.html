Then for all sufficiently large coefficient rings O (eventually along CoeffRing \ell): for every homomorphism T : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to M_r(O), every homomorphism T_0 : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to M_r(O), every family u = (u_x)_{x\in Y} of homomorphisms u_x : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to M_n(O) and every M : Option(\operatorname{Fin} t) \to \mathbb{N} such that (1) for all g, u_\pi (g) is the block-diagonal matrix with blocks blockRep T i (g) (T(g) on the block none, the 1\times 1 matrix cycInv(g)^e on the block some e) reindexed along \epsilon , (2) every u_x is a lattice in R'_x (IsLatticeIn (R'_x) (u_x)), (3) T_0 is a lattice in R (IsLatticeIn R T_0), and (4) T and T_0 agree on the decomposition group of the chosen place placeOver v of \Omega over every place v of E above \ell: \sum _{i \in \operatorname{Option}(\operatorname{Fin} t)} normLength_O(blockLoss (blockRep T i) u (M i)) \le 4\cdot |r|\cdotD.f\cdotloss D \ell. Here D.f is the number of infinite places of E, normLength_O the normalised length (O/\ell O has length 1), and blockLoss (blockRep T i) u (M i) the O-length of the module of cocycles \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to (O/\ell ^{M i})^{\text\{ block \} i} for blockRep T i that obey every relation among the u_x(g) - 1 (obeyingCocycles u (blockRep T i) (\ell ^{M i})) modulo those that obey the crystalline relations crysRels (blockRep T i) at the places above \ell. The crystalline losses above \ell are affordable.
The argument in briefFix O large enough for the local defect bound at every place v above \ell. The crystalline relations are the intersection over v of the local ones, so each block loss is at most the sum over v of local quotients; each is bounded by the length of Z_f of the dual Tate twist of the block modulo the cup-orthogonal of a class containing all lattices u_x and the block (relLength_obeyingCocycles_le), and that by the one-place defect [G_\ell : j_v]\cdotrank\cdotloss/x (block_defect_le_at_place). Summing over the blocks (rank |r| for the row, x = D.t for the t string blocks) and over v (\sum _v [G_\ell : j_v] \le [E : \mathbb{Q}] = 2f) gives 4\cdot |r|\cdotD.f\cdotloss.
