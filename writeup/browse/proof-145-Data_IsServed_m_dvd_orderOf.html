lemma Data.IsServed.m_dvd_orderOf

What it saysFor data D and a prime p (instance hq) that is served (Data.IsServed): m divides the order of the place named by - p in the group of infinite places (\cong ( \mathbb{Z} / N_{1} )^\times).
