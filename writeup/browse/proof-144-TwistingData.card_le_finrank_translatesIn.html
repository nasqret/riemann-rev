lemma TwistingData.card_le_finrank_translatesIn

What it saysLet N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, O a valuation domain (a commutative integral domain whose ideals are totally ordered), T : TwistingData N W O (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to O, w \in O with w^{2} = p), and write Fam for the families \mathbb{Z}/N \to Monomials (Fin k) n, \mathfrak{m} for the maximal ideal of O, \kappa_{O} = O/\mathfrak{m} and T mod \mathfrak{m} = map (residue O) T, k, n \in \mathbb{N}, X finite, E a type, Q \in Mat_{(X\times Fam)\times E}(O), R \in Mat_{E\times(X\times Fam)}(O), a finite set s of column indices j \in E, a labelling G of Fin k and a set B of families. If (R\cdot Q)_{\mathit{ij}} = \delta_{\mathit{ij}} for every j \in s and every i (h), and for every j \in s and x \in X the part of column j at the block x, Q_{(x,\cdot),j}, lies in translatesIn T n G \mathfrak{m} B (hs), then |s| \le \dim_{\kappa_{O}} (X \to translatesIn (T mod \mathfrak{m}) n G \perp B).
The argument in briefThe residues of the columns j \in s are |s| linearly independent elements of the space on the right: they lie there by \mathit{hs}, and R recovers each coefficient of a vanishing combination.
