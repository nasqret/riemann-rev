lemma Data.log_filling_besselDegree_mem_Icc

What it saysLemma. For data D, with \deg= besselDegree(\operatorname{filling}(D)) (the product over the infinite places of the degrees of the Bessel systems) and w(v)= nominalWeight(D,v): log \deg+f\cdot\Sigma_{k-1\le l<r}\log l! lies in the interval [\Sigma_v m(k-1)\cdot\log w(v),\Sigma_v m\cdot r\cdot(\log w(v)+1/x)],v over the f infinite places, m= D.m, r= D.r, x= D.x.
The argument in briefWeyl's dimension formula writes deg as exp of \Sigma_v\Sigma_{k-1\le l<r} (\Sigma_{i<l}\log(\rho_v(i)-\rho_v(l))-\log l!); each difference \rho_v(i)-\rho_v(l) lies in [1,w(l+2-k,v)] and is \ge w(v) for the relevant i, which gives the two bounds after summing (r-(k-1)=m).
