lemma Data.short_forms

What it saysTheorem A (short forms). For parameters P (N_{0} = P.N_{0}, d = P.d) and admissible data D for P (Lean's Data P): there are a rational number q > 0 and a family B with B = q\cdot P_D, i.e. B_s = q\cdot P_{D,s} for every s \in \mathsf{G}_D, such that (a) there is a set S of ramified columns with S \subseteq integralRamified(D) that spans the ramified columns over \mathbb{C}, such that for every \varphi \in S, every s \in \mathsf{G}_D and every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}, \lVert \Theta_D(1)(B_s(\varphi))\rVert^{2}_{\sigma} \le \exp(h_{\mathrm{main}}(D) - m_D\cdot\operatorname{rem}(D) + \log b_D\cdot\mathcal{B}(D)) (\lVert \cdot\rVert^{2}_{\sigma} the class-sum norm at \sigma, normSqAt); and (b) for every finite set t \subseteq \mathsf{G}_D, every natural number n and every family y : Fin n \to \operatorname{rows}(D) with each y_i of the form B_s(\varphi) for some s \in t and \varphi \in integralRamified(D): IndexLe(D, y, \#t\cdot\dim \operatorname{Column}(D)\cdot\mathcal{B}(D)).
The argument in briefq divides out the gains less the allowed losses; the seesaw identity turns lifts of the projected Poincaré series into the chains B_{\mu}, whose size (heightPerRow_le) and local divisibility (exists_indexDvd_Bmu_divisible) give (a) and (b).
