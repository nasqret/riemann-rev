Structured proof · Unit 138

lemma obeyingCocycles.exists_graph_subrepresentation

What it saysLet O be a commutative Noetherian ring, G a group, X a finite type, \sigma and d finite types, u = (u_x)_{x\in X} homomorphisms u_x : G \to M_\sigma (O), \rho : G \to M_d(O) a homomorphism, a \in O, and c \in obeyingCocycles u \rho a. Let G act O-linearly on M = (M_\sigma (O))^X by g\cdot (m_x)_x = (u_x(g)m_x)_x and on (O/aO)^d through \rho. Then there are k \in \mathbb{N} and a G-stable O-submodule Y of M \times (O/aO)^d (a subrepresentation of the product representation) such that the first projection Y \to M is injective and, for all g \in G and v \in M, ((u_x(g) - 1)_x + a^k\cdot v, c(g)) \in Y. So c is, on the elements (u(g) - 1) + a^k v, the value of an equivariant map defined on the projection of Y, and Y is its graph (Artin–Rees).
The argument in briefArtin–Rees for the image of \theta \mapsto \sum \theta (g)(u(g) - 1) in M = (M_\sigma (O))^X gives k; Y is the image of N(\theta , v) = (\sum \theta (g)(u(g) - 1), \sum \theta (g)c(g)) + a^{k+1}(v, 0). It is G-stable by the cocycle identity, its first projection is injective by Artin–Rees and the relations obeyed by c, and it contains N(\delta _g, v) = ((u(g) - 1) + a^{k+1}v, c(g)).
