There is a measure-preserving real-linear isomorphism e (of the parameter space with the real r \times r matrices) such that for every p = ( Z , \theta , q , \eta , r ) with Z \in \Omega: openCellChart is real differentiable at p, and | \det ( e^{- 1} \circ skewCoords \circ logDerivU(openCellChart, p ) ) | = \mathsf{g} ( Z )^{k - 1} \cdot cayleyDensity(1, skewColumnEquiv( k_{0} ) ( \theta , 0 ) ) \cdot cayleyDensity( 1 , \eta ), with k = | \kappa | and cayleyDensity( 1 , y ) = | \det ( 1 - \tfrac{1}{2} \cdotskewOfReal( y ) ) |^{- 2 k}.
The argument in briefConjugate the logarithmic derivative by the right factor and an explicit matrix K; the resulting map is block triangular in the chart coordinates, with blocks the Cayley Jacobians and the derivative of the section, whose determinants multiply to \mathsf{g} ( Z )^{k - 1} times the Cayley densities.
