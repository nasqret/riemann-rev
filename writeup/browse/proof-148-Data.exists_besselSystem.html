lemma Data.exists_besselSystem

What it saysLemma. For data D and an infinite place v there is a Bessel system B on G_1 (for the bound b= D.b and f= D.f) with IsBessel(B,\mathtt{paramW}(D,v),\kappa(D,v)): its degree is \deg(B)= weylDimensionPoly(\mathtt{paramW}(D,v)) (Weyl's dimension formula for the doubled parameter \varsigma(v); that it equals the formal degree of \varsigma(v) is Harish-Chandra's identity, supplied in the construction and not part of IsBessel); there are mutually inverse matrices A,A' with A\cdot W_{\mathrm{full}}(g\cdot y)=(\operatorname{schur}(\kappa(v))(g^{T}))^{T}\cdot A\cdot W_{\mathrm{full}}(y) for every g\in U(X_0) and every y (the transpose of the Schur action at the transposed element, transported by A); and W_{\mathrm{full}} is fixed by the reproducing kernel of the discrete series of \varsigma(v): \int dsKernel(\varsigma(v))(x)\cdot W_{\mathrm{full}}(y\cdot\varphi^{-1}(x))_i dx=W_{\mathrm{full}}(y)_i for every y and i (\varphi=\mathtt{frameEquiv}).
The argument in briefBuild the representation \operatorname{Sym}^{\kappa_v} of U(X_0) in an orthonormal basis, a Bessel function of that type for the doubled parameter (Laplace method), its cut (cut pair), Schur and band-limit bounds, and check the degree and type conditions.
