lemma Data.isPrimitiveMulChar_of_isTameFor

What it saysFor P, D, a tame character \vartheta, a character \nu, weights \gamma and a multiplicative character \chi of O_E/\mathfrak{m}(D, \vartheta, 1): if \nu is tame for the squarefree modulus \mathfrak{m}(D, \vartheta, 1) with character \chi (IsTameFor(\mathfrak{m}(D, \vartheta, 1), \nu, \gamma, \chi)), then \chi is primitive: for each a \ne 0 in O_E/\mathfrak{m} some \chi(1 + c\cdot a) is neither 0 nor 1.
The argument in briefAn element a \ne 0 of O_E/\mathfrak{m} has a non-zero residue at some place w of \mathfrak{m}; the tame part at w is non-trivial, so some x has \psi_{w}(x) \ne 1, and lifting (x - 1)/a gives the required c.
