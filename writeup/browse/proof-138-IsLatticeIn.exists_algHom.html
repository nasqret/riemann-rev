lemma IsLatticeIn.exists_algHom

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell, a coefficient ring O, a finite type n, R : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \operatorname{GL}_n(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell ) and u : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to M_n(O) a lattice in R (u(g) = P^{-1}R(g)P for some P \in \operatorname{GL}_n(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell )): there is an injective O-algebra map F : M_n(O) \to M_n(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell ) with F(u(g)) = R(g) for every g and \det F(A) = \det A, \operatorname{tr} F(A) = \operatorname{tr} A for every A \in M_n(O).
The argument in briefF is conjugation by P after the inclusion O \subseteq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell; conjugation preserves determinants and traces.
