lemma IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel.exists_gauge_depth_of_ne_zero

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma. For a standard level, Z,j\le\varrho,K\in\mathbb{N} and sequences of units p,q such that formMajorant(F,j,p,q,W_Z,W_Z) is not identically 0: there are \alpha,a,b such that, for \Gamma=\operatorname{gauge}(F,\alpha,a,b): p and q are heads of length j for \Gamma (IsHead), g^{-1} has size 1 if j\ne0,1/p_i-1/q_i has size 1 for i<j, and \Lambda_{\Gamma}^{E}\le(2/\operatorname{md}(g))^{K}\cdotfinHeadWeight(K,j,p)\cdotfinHeadWeight(K,j,q) whenever 4E\le K.
The argument in briefFrom a nonzero form majorant, near positions for p and for q (shifted by y and y\cdot x); the minors of the frame data give units \beta,\gamma controlling the partial products of p and q; the gauge of depth \alpha=g\overline{\beta}\overline{\gamma}\beta\gamma makes p,q heads and the head weights \sqrt{\beta}^{K},\sqrt{\gamma}^{K}.
