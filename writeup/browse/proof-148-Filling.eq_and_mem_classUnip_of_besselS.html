lemma Filling.eq_and_mem_classUnip_of_besselS

What it saysUnder the standing hypotheses (IsAdmissible S, an instance named ok), for filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), classes c,d and \gamma\in G(S,E) (implicit): if \gamma lies in the Bessel subgroup \mathsf{S} (h) and termPosition(S,c,d,\gamma)\in K(g) (o), then c=d and \gamma\in\mathsf{U}_c(g)= classUnip(S,c).
The argument in briefSplit \gamma=u\cdot\eta with u\in\mathsf{U} and \eta\in U(X_0)(E); the \mathsf{H}-part of the position lies in K(g), so freeness of the classes forces c=d and \eta=1.
