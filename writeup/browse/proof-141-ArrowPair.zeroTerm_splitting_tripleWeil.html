lemma ArrowPair.zeroTerm_splitting_tripleWeil

What it saysFor an arrow pair D, characters a, b of U(1)(\mathbb{A}), adelic points g \in U(X), g' \in U(X'), k \in U(X_{0}) and f \in \mathcal{S}(\operatorname{Hom}(V', V^{-} \oplus V'')): zeroTerm_{\mathrm{splitting} D}(tripleWeil(a, b)(\mathrm{inclX}_Y g, inclX_{Y'} g', inclX_{Y''} k)\cdot f) = tripleWeil(a, b)(g, g', \mathrm{embX}_D k)\cdotzeroTerm_{\mathrm{splitting} D}(f). tripleWeil(a, b)(g_{1}, g_{2}, g_{3}) is the Weil operator of (g_{2}, g_{1}^{-} \oplus g_{3}) on \operatorname{Hom}(V', V^{-} \oplus V'') times explicit characters of the determinants (tripleWeil). The zero term intertwines the two triple actions.
The argument in briefThe splitting is a composite of two splittings; for each, the zero term intertwines the Weil action up to a character (zeroTerm_omega_of_inclMatrix for the grade-zero part, homTarget_zeroTerm_pairWeil for the removed planes). The characters are matched by computing exponents, and the degree conditions entrywise.
