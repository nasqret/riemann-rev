lemma Parameters.norm_iota_periodFactor

What it saysFor parameters P, an exponent s, a prime p with a p-adic place \iota_p \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_p (instance) and an infinite place \tau of E: |\iota_p(s_1)|_p = |\operatorname{adeleExtension}(x \mapsto \iota_p(\Phi_\tau(\overline{x})))(\alpha)|_p^{-1}, where s_1 = \operatorname{periodFactor}(P, s), \alpha = \alpha(P, s) and x \mapsto \iota_p(\Phi_\tau(\overline{x})) = weightEmbedding \iota_p \tau.
The argument in briefCompare f-th powers (f = number of infinite places). |\iota_p(u_0)| = 1 and s_0^f = \chi_{\mathrm{fin}}(\alpha)^{-1}, so |\iota_p(s_1)|^f = |\iota_p(\chi_{\mathrm{fin}}(\alpha))|^{-1} = \prod_v |\operatorname{adeleExtension}(\operatorname{weightEmbedding} \iota_p v)(\alpha)|^{-1}, and every factor equals the one at \tau because the valuation of P'(\zeta) is invariant under \operatorname{Gal}(E/K).
