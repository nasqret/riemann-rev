lemma Lift.TameChart.aeval_linearFrob_extLattice_eq_zero

What it saysFor a tame chart d, a side S and q \in \mathbb{Z}_p[X] such that q (mapped to O) kills (s, 0) on Y (hK: IsKilledBy(Y, tameGen, 0, q)): (q\cdot(X - 1))(\beta_{s,0}) = 0 on B_{\mathrm{cris}} \otimes \widetilde{Y}, \widetilde{Y} the extension of C by Y.
The argument in brief(\beta - 1)y comes from B\otimes Y because \beta = 1 on the crystalline trivial lattice C; then q(\beta) kills it, so q(\beta)(\beta - 1) = 0, the value at \beta of the product q\cdot(X - 1).
