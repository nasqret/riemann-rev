lemma thetaLarge_move

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type, n, a finite index types, s, t signs, W, W_{1} : Space(E, n, s), V, V_{1} : Space(E, a, t), with Weil data on E; let c \in \mathrm{GL}_n(E) and k in the adelic unitary group G(E, s) with x_W = c^{-1}\cdot x_{W_{1}}\cdot k (\mathit{hk}; c read as an adelic matrix), \mu weights and \mathfrak{c} a split tame character of weight \mu. There is u in the unit circle such that for all real data v, local functions \Phi and points g: thetaLarge(W, V, \mathfrak{c}, v, \Phi)(g) = u\cdotthetaLarge(W_{1}, V_{1}, \mathfrak{c}, v, \Phi)(k\cdot g).
The argument in briefLandherr's theorem moves V_{1} to V (c', k'); the theta integrals are integrals over the same orbit, the theta functionals compared through the vacuum and products of line thetas; the constant is a character value of \det k/\det k'.
