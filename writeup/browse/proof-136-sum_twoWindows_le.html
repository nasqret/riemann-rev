lemma Lift.sum_twoWindows_le

What it saysFor n, p, d, m, H \in \mathbb{N} and c \in \mathbb{Z} with n \le p\cdot m (e) and |c| + n \le H (hH): \sum_{y\in\mathrm{twoWindows}(n, c)} (v_p(y) + d) \le (4m + 2)\cdot\lfloor \log_p H\rfloor + (4n + 2)\cdot d, where v_p(0) = 0.
The argument in briefEach nonzero valuation is at most \lfloor \log_p H\rfloor and comes from a multiple of p, of which there are at most 4m + 2; the windows have at most 4n + 2 elements.

    Write the sum as \sum_y v_p(y) + |twoWindows(n, c)|\cdot d; in the first sum only the y with v_p(y) \ne 0 count, each at most \lfloor \log_p H\rfloor (v_p(y) \le \log_p|y| and |y| \le |c| + n \le H); these y are multiples p\cdot k with k \in twoWindows(m, c div p) (shown next), so there are at most 4m + 2 of them; and |twoWindows(n, c)| \le 2(2n + 1).
