lemma Data.varsigma_add_w_gap

What it saysFor admissible data D over P, a natural number j, an infinite place \tau of E(D) and a natural number p: if p < \operatorname{posRank}(D, j) = k(D) + j - 1, then h_{2}(D) \le \operatorname{rowParameter}(D, \tau, p) + w(D, j, \tau); otherwise \operatorname{rowParameter}(D, \tau, p) + w(D, j, \tau) \le -h_{2}(D).
The argument in briefThe weights w_j grow in steps of at least h (w_add_gap_le), h \le w_j, and rowParameter is explicit on both sides of k - 1; the case analysis is arithmetic.
