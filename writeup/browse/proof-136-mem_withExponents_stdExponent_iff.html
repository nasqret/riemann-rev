lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.mem_withExponents_stdExponent_iff

What it saysFor a lattice datum L, x \in W(C^\flat)\otimes L, a letter i and l \in \mathbb{N}: x \in withExponents(L, i, stdExponent(l)) if and only if x \in frobKisinAinf(L, l) (\varphi^{-l}x \in kisinAinf(L)) and x \in letterPart(L, i).
The argument in briefWrite x = \varphi^{l} x'. Backward: x' \in kisinAinf at the letter \sigma^{-l}(i) has exponents (a, u) \mapsto -u, so \varphi^{l} x' has the standard exponents (phi_pow_mem_withExponents). Forward: \varphi^{l} x' (= x) lies in the \varphi^{l}(\tau)-saturation of the A_{\mathrm{inf}}-lattice (induction along kisinLocAinf; for a \ge l the standard exponents show divisibility of the coordinates by \varphi^{a} E); applying \varphi^{-l} puts a power of \tau times x' in the lattice; and the Frobenius values of x' are integral by the Hodge basis criterion.
