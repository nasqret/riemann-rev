lemma exists_frame_gradedGram

What it saysFor finite index types X, \iota, s \in \mathbb{N}, N > 0 (hN), a commutative ring R, a bijection e : (X \sqcup \{\operatorname{pt}\}) \sqcup \{0, \ldots, s-1\} \simeq \iota, a ring homomorphism \varphi : R \to \mathbb{C} and signs \varepsilon on \iota with exactly \lfloor (s + 1)/2\rfloor minus signs (h): there is a complex matrix y with y^{H}\cdot\varphi(gradedGram(e, N))\cdot y = \operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon), and y^{H}\cdot y = 1 if N = 1.
The argument in briefDiagonalise the string pairs by 2 \times 2 orthogonal changes of basis, after matching the signs by a bijection.
