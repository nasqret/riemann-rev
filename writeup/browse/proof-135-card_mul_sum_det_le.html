lemma card_mul_sum_det_le

What it saysSingular minors are rare (positive-type estimate). Let k be a finite field with q = |k| elements, n and m finite index types, G a finite group, \pi : G \to \mathrm{GL}_n(k) a group homomorphism, A : Bool \to Mat_{n\times m}(k) a pair of n\times m matrices A(true), A(false), \Theta : G \to \mathbb{R} with \Theta(s) \ge 0 for all s (h_{0}) and of positive type through a family u : G \to H in a complex inner product space H, \langle u(a), u(b)\rangle = \Theta(a^{-1}b) for all a, b (hu), and Y \ge 0 real (hY). Assume (h): for b \in \{true, false\} and every nonzero v \in k^{m}, \sum \Theta(s) over the s \in G with g_b(\pi(s))\cdot(A(b)v) = A(b)v is at most Y, where g_{\mathrm{true}}(\gamma) =(\gamma^{\mathsf{T}})^{-1} (invTransposeGL, the contragredient) and g_{\mathrm{false}}(\gamma) = \gamma, and \mathrm{GL}_n(k) acts on k^{n} by matrix–vector multiplication. Then q\cdot \sum_{s \in G : \det(A(\text{true})^{\mathsf{T}}\cdot \pi(s)\cdot A(\text{false})) = 0} \Theta(s) \le 2\cdot \sum_{s\in G} \Theta(s) + q^{|m|}\cdot \sqrt{q^{|n|}}\cdot Y. (The text reading of the signature also lists the section variables b (an orthonormal basis) and v : G \to H; the statement's b and v are bound variables of h and \mathit{hu}, so Lean is expected not to take them.)
The argument in briefFourier analysis on k^{m} \times k^{m}: with a primitive character \psi and M_s = A(true)^{\mathsf{T}}\pi(s)A(false), \sum_{y} E_v \psi(y\cdot M_s\cdot v) counts the y with y\cdot M_s = 0, which are at least q when \det M_s = 0. So the left side is at most \sum_{y} E_v \operatorname{Re} \sum_{s} \psi(\text{yM}_s v)\Theta(s). For y, v \ne 0 this inner sum is an inner product of two tensors whose squared norms are |G| times the \Theta-masses of the stabilisers in h (normalised characters for the contragredient side, the standard basis for the other), so it is \le \sqrt{q^{n}}\cdot Y; the terms with y = 0 or v = 0 contribute at most 2\sum \Theta.
