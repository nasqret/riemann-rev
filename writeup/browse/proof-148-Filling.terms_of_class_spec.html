What it saysLemma. Let j\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\}, classes c,c',s\in\mathsf{G} and \gamma\in G(E) with \gamma\in termsOver(S,c,s,c') (h_c^{-1}\gamma h_c'\in K with reduction s) and HasLevel(S,j,\gamma). Then every \delta in the double coset \mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g)\cdot\gamma\cdot\mathsf{U}_c'^{0}(g) (\mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g)=\mathtt{classUnip}{}_0(S,c)) satisfies: HasLevel(S,j,\delta); frameProducts(S,\delta)= frameProducts(S,\gamma) (the matrix Z(\cdot) of products h(f_a, \cdot f_b) with the frame f=(e_1,\ldots,e_{\varrho},z)); \delta\in termsOver(S,c,s,c'); and for every column index i the i-th column of (frameMiddle(k_{\delta})- frameMiddle(k_{\gamma})){}^{H} lies in gIdele(S)\cdotcolLattice(\hat{O}_E,\mathtt{xRows}(Z_{\mathrm{lat}}(k_{\gamma}))^{H}), where k_{\delta}= termPosition(S,c,c',\delta)=h_c^{-1}\delta h_c', frameMiddle(k) are the X_0\oplus Ez rows of k f,Z_{\mathrm{lat}}(k) is the frame Gram matrix of k for the lattice form latMiddle(S), xRows takes its first \varrho rows, colLattice(\hat{O}_E,M) is the \hat{O}_E-span of the columns of M and gIdele(S) is g=q_0q_1 as a finite idele.
The argument in briefWrite \delta=u\gamma x with u\in\mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g),x\in\mathsf{U}_c'^{0}(g). Multiplying by elements of \mathsf{U} that fix the frame changes neither the level nor Z, keeps the reduction, and changes the middle rows of the frame image by integral combinations of g times the e-rows.
