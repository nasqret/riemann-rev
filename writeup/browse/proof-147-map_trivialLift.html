lemma LValueData.map_trivialLift

What it saysGalois action on \Xi. Let P be parameters, s a natural number (the level of the tower; E(P, s) the field), n a finite index type, S level places of E(P, s) without twist places (h\psi: the twist index type of S is empty), and \sigma \in Gal(\mathbb{C}/K(P)). There are t, l, u with |t| = \Omega(P, s)^{-1/2} and l \ne 0 such that for all L-value data X on n with places S (X.1 = S) and all e, \mu, \psi, m with the weight relation is and the unramifiedness unr, writing Y = LValueData(S, jetDeg(X)\circ e^{-1}, \mu, \psi, is, \ldots, unr): if |n| = 2m, placePerm(s, \sigma) = e and \mu(u') = \sigma(\mu_{X}(u')) at every point u' with trivial infinite component, then for every finite point h and index i: \sigma((jetScale(P, s)^{\sum_{\tau} \mathit{dg}_{\tau}}\cdottrivialLift(X))(h)_{i}) = t^{\sum_{\tau} \mathit{dg}_{\tau}}\cdot(l\cdot b(auxPair(Y))(u))\cdotmultiplierRatio(\sigma, onHom(1, 1, h)_{2})\cdottrivialLift(Y)(h)_{i\circ e^{-1}}, \mathit{dg} = jetDeg(X).
The argument in briefA \sigma-semilinear conjugation L of data (Parameters.exists_semilinear_isJetConjugate) conjugates the jets of the kernel data place by place; applied term by term to the class sums defining \Xi_{X} and \Xi_{Y} (\Xi_{Y} translated by the scalar z^{-1}), it gives the formula.
