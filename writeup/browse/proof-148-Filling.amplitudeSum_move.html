lemma Filling.amplitudeSum_move

What it saysMoves. Under the standing hypotheses, for filling data S over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit), a stable set T (hT; T implicit), classes c,c_1,c',c_1',t,t'\in\mathsf{G} and \sigma,\sigma'\in G(S,E) (all implicit) with \sigma\in\mathsf{S} (h), \sigma\in termsOver(c,t,c_1) (o), \sigma'\in\mathsf{S} (h'),\sigma'\in termsOver(c',t',c_1') (o'),\gamma\in T\iff\sigma\gamma\sigma'^{-1}\in T for all \gamma (e), and a continuous linear map L with besselKernel(\sigma\gamma\sigma'^{-1},\mathtt{ratToInf}(\sigma)\cdot y)=L(\mathtt{besselKernel}(\gamma,y)) for all \gamma,y (hL), and s\in\mathsf{G}: A_T(c,t\cdot s\cdot t'^{-1},c')=L(A_T(c_1,s,c_1')).
The argument in briefChange the weight to \beta_c(\sigma y)\beta_{c'}(\sigma'\gamma^{-1}y), push through L, and reindex \gamma\mapsto\sigma\gamma\sigma'^{-1},y\mapsto\sigma y.
