lemma DividedLattice.exists_reducedInverse_eq

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r and n finite types and O : AuxPair E r n. If C and G are n\times n matrices over the integral finite adeles \hat{O}_E with C\cdot G = \varpi \cdot 1 (h; \varpi = O.genIntAdele), then there is a divided lattice L : DividedLattice O whose reducedInverse is G reduced modulo M^2 (entrywise).
The argument in briefCut the pair (C, \varpi^{-1}G) to the places of M^2 (cutGL), giving c \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_f); then \gamma = \overline{c}^{-1}c is unitary, supported on M and \overline{M}, and \varpi \gamma^{-1} \equiv G modulo M^2.
