lemma Gauge.floorMassLe_of_chart

What it saysLet A be a place (class Place: a locally compact Polish commutative ring with involution and Haar measure \,\mathrm{d}x, with an element \tau with \tau + \tau^{*} = 1, a skew unit \delta, a Haar measure \nu_{\mathrm{skew}} on the skew elements, a multiplicative modulus \operatorname{md} : A \to [ 0 , \infty ] such that v \mapsto Mv carries \,\mathrm{d}x to \operatorname{md} ( \det M )^{- 1} \,\mathrm{d}x whenever \det M is a unit, a unitary additive character \psi and a Plancherel constant c); the instances CommRing A, StarRing A, TopologicalSpace A, MeasureSpace A and P : Place A are arguments. Let \mathfrak{s} be a shape, T an \mathfrak{s}-form over A whose Gram matrix J_{T} = splitGram( T ) of V is hermitian and invertible (IsHermUnit), \iota a finite type and m \in \mathbb{N}; G_{T} = U ( J_{T} ), H_{l} = GT ( l ) (fixing e_{a} , e_{a}^{*} for a < l), Q_{l} = unitriGroup( l ), \mathcal{N}_{< l} = unipotentBelow( l ), f_{b} = besselFrame( b ) the frame (f_{b} = e_{b} for b < \varrho, f_{\varrho} = z). Let F be a level (Level \iota T m; unit g_{F}, box Bx_{F}, positions Cm_{F}, floor measures \theta_{l}^{F} = floorMeasure( F , l ), Heisenberg measures \eta_{l}) and \Gamma a gauge for F (sizes B_{j}^{\Gamma}, constant \Lambda_{\Gamma} , s_{F} = volumeExponent( F )); \delta_{F}^{b} = floorQuadric( F , b ) , O_{F} = orthonormalPart( F ), E_{b}^{F} = floorBasis( F , b ). Let b \le \varrho, f : G_{T} \to [ 0 , \infty ] measurable (hf, by default fun_prop) and left Q_{b + 1}-invariant (hi), and K \in [ 0 , \infty ]. If for every g \in Bx_{F} there is a measurable \Psi on V with \int_{\mathrm{vecsOfSize} ( \Gamma , 7 )} \Psi d \delta_{F}^{b} \le K and f ( \sigma g ) \le \Psi ( \sigma^{- 1} f_{b} ) for every \sigma \in H_{b} ( h ), then FloorMassLe( F , b , K , f ).
The argument in briefMove the box point into the variable with the right invariance of \theta_{b}^{F}, and transport to the quadric by \sigma \mapsto \sigma^{- 1} f_{b}.
