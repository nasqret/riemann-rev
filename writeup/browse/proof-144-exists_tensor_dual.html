lemma exists_tensor_dual

What it saysFor a finite type \iota, a type \sigma and a commutative ring R: there is an R-multilinear map S from \iota-tuples of R-linear forms on R[x_{\sigma}] to R-linear forms on R[x_{\iota \times \sigma}] with S(l)(\Pi_{j} f_j(x_{(j,\cdot)})) = \Pi_{j} l_j(f_j) for every tuple l of linear forms and every tuple f of polynomials (f_j renamed into the variables (j, v)). Declared in the root namespace.
The argument in briefS = (dual of the algebra map R[x_{\iota \times \sigma}] \to \otimes_{j} R[x_{\sigma}], x_{(i,v)} \mapsto x_v in the i-th factor) \circ dualDistrib \circ tprod.
