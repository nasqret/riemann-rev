lemma IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel.isCut_besselVector

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma. For a standard level and Z\in IntermediateGroup(F),y\mapsto besselVector(Z,y) is a cut Bessel vector for F (IsCut): it is strongly measurable and bounded by a uniform finite constant — \lVert W_Z(y)\rVert\le\sqrt{\#}(K(g)\setminus Z) for every y, each of its coset coordinates being a phase of modulus at most 1 (not a bound by 1 on the Euclidean norm) —, it transforms under \mathsf{U} by W_Z(uy)=\psi(g^{-1}\sigma(u))\cdot W_Z(y) (\sigma the superdiagonal sum of the frame block), and it is nonzero only at points y with u\cdot y in the box K for some u\in\mathsf{U}.
The argument in briefMeasurability and a uniform finite bound \sqrt{\#}(K(g)\setminus Z) on the norm (each coset coordinate is a phase of modulus \le1), the \mathsf{U}-transformation from phase_besselU_mul, and the support condition from exists_of_inner_besselVector_ne_zero.
