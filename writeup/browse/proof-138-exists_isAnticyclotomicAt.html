lemma exists_isAnticyclotomicAt

What it saysLet E_1 be a stably unramified CM field embedded in \Omega , \ell ' a prime, N \in \mathbb{N}, S a finite set of finite places of E_1, R a finite set of finite places, z \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E_1) an element of the normal closure of the inertia above the places of R that are not in S \cup \overline{S} and not above \ell ' (the smallest normal subgroup containing the union of those inertia groups: a weaker hypothesis than membership in the union itself, which is what the twist theorem's hypothesis hz asks for), and W a neighbourhood of z. Then there is a finite place P \notin R \cup \overline{R} of prime norm and there are auxiliary prime data l = (A, \gamma , \varphi , h) with h : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E_1) \to \mathbb{Z}/\ell '^N (into the multiplicative copy Multiplicative (ZMod (\ell '^N))) such that \varphi \in W, h(\varphi ) = 0 in \mathbb{Z}/\ell '^N (Lean's l.h l.\varphi = 1: the identity of the multiplicative copy, not the generator ofAdd 1 that IsAnticyclotomicAt prescribes at the tame generator \gamma; for N = 0 the target \mathbb{Z}/1 is trivial), and IsAnticyclotomicAt T P l for every T \subseteq S \cup (places above \ell ').
The argument in briefChoose a finite set T of places with ideles = principal \cdot T-units, generators s of the T-units and \ell '^N-th roots y_u of them. Chebotarev gives P of prime norm and a Frobenius \sigma fixing the \ell '^N-th roots of unity and the y_u, near z. Then q_P \equiv 1 \bmod \ell '^N, and the residue map at P followed by \mathbb{F}_P^\times \to \mathbb{Z}/\ell '^N extends from the T-unit ideles to an idele class character \theta; h = \theta (x/\overline{x})\circ \operatorname{Art}^{-1} by class field theory sends the tame generator to the generator 1 = ofAdd 1 of \mathbb{Z}/\ell '^N; \varphi = \sigma \gamma ^{a} with h(\varphi ) = 0. (h and f take values in Multiplicative (ZMod (\ell '^N)), the multiplicative copy of \mathbb{Z}/\ell '^N: where a step writes f(t) = 1, f(u_P) = 1 or h(\varphi ) = 1, it means the identity, the additive 0; h(\gamma ) = ofAdd 1 is the additive 1.)
