lemma explicit_matching_of_sum

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, n and b finite types, s and t sign vectors, V a space of rank |b| with signs t, \mathfrak{m} a modulus, U a space of rank |n| with signs s, a, \beta, \beta' characters, e a weight and \chi a character of O_E/\mathfrak{m} with \beta \beta' tame for (\mathfrak{m}, e, \chi) (hχ). Let \Xi and F be complex functions and \kappa \in \mathbb{C} with \kappa \cdot \sum_h \Xi(h)\cdot \theta^{V, I_b}_{a, \beta }[\chi \circ \det, 1](x, h) = F(x) for all x (hF; class sum over U(I_b) at level K_0). Let \mathfrak{n} be a modulus with \mathfrak{m} \subseteq \mathfrak{n} as ideals (\varphi: \mathfrak{n} divides \mathfrak{m}), f a function on matrices modulo \mathfrak{n}, P a polynomial, g a point, K a function of a datum \Phi and a point h with values functions, and \kappa' \in \mathbb{C} such that for all \chi^{-1}-partners \Phi, \Psi (IsPartner \top (refl) \chi^{-1} \Phi \Psi) and all h: \kappa'\cdot \sum_u K(\Phi, h)(u) = \int_{[U(V)]} \theta^{V, I_b \oplus U}_{a, \beta \beta'}[\Psi, P'](x, ((1, h), g)) dx (hK; class sum over U(I_n) at level K_0, P' = P renamed into the second block). Then for \Phi(x) = \chi(\det x'')\cdot \sum_{k \in \mathrm{GL}_b(O/\mathfrak{n})} f(((x'')^{-1}x' \text{ mod } \mathfrak{n})\cdot k), x = (x' | x''): \kappa'\cdot(\kappa /|\mathrm{GL}_b(O_E/\mathfrak{n})|)\cdot \sum_u \sum_h \Xi(h)\cdot K(\Phi, h)(u) = \int_{[U(V)]} F(x)\cdot \theta^{V, U}_{a, \beta'}[f, P](x, g) dx.
The argument in briefRewrite the kernel modulo \mathfrak{n} as one modulo \mathfrak{m} and swap the class sums; apply hK to the partner pair given by partner_block (averaged over \mathrm{GL}_b(O/\mathfrak{n})); then hF and linearity of the kernel in its datum give the identity.
