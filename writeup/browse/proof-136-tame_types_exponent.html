lemma Lift.tame_types_exponent

What it saysTame types. Let j : \Gamma \to G_p be an open embedding (ho) onto G_p (hj surjective), O a p-adic integer ring, r, N \in \mathbb{N}, L a lattice over O, U, U' jump sets, F a filter on \Gamma and K a predicate on pairs (s, polynomial over O) such that L \in InClass(F, (s, Q) \mapsto K(s^{-1}, Q), U') (hL) and IsTameType(p, r, N, K(s)) for every s with j(s) in inertia (hK). Let \eta, W, b \in \mathbb{N} and a sign \varepsilon with U(g) \subseteq [-\eta, \eta] \cup (-\varepsilon)[b, b + W] and U'(g) \subseteq [-\eta, \eta] \cup \varepsilon[b, b + W] for every embedding g (hU), and b + 6(W + \eta) + r^{2}(N + 1)^{2} + 2 < p (hp). Then for every a \in Z_f(L): p^{3r^{2}(N+1)^{2} + 150(\eta + W + 1)}\cdot a \in cupOrthogonal(InClass(F, K, U), L).
The argument in briefChoose a tame chart with index e = \operatorname{lcm}(p^{j} - 1) \le p^{r^{2}}; lattices of tame type are crystalline over \Gamma': on inertia s \mapsto \beta_{s,0} is a character with open kernel and order dividing e (X^{M} - 1 kills, M = e), so by the stabilizer clause of tame_inertia it is trivial on the stabilizer of \varpi, which contains the inertia elements of \Gamma'; the Tate dual of Y \otimes L is killed by a monic polynomial of degree \le N^{2}, so killsCokernel_of_isKilledBy gives p^{\kappa} cokernel killers for the three sides and scaledCupBounds_of_killsCokernel applies with a = 0. The chart's d = differentExp is 0 if e = 1 and v_p(e) + 1 = 1 otherwise (p \nmid e), not 0 in general; 3(\kappa + (48(\eta + W) + 4)d) is within the exponent 3r^{2}(N + 1)^{2} + 150(\eta + W + 1) for d \le 1.
