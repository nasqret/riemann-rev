lemma exists_isBaseAdapted

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, r, p finite index types, s signs on n, W : Space(E, n, s), and t signs on r with |r| = |n| + 2|p| (h) and negCount(t) = |p| + negCount(s) (h'). There are a space W' : Space(E, r, t) and a base-adapted splitting A of W' over W along p (W' is W plus |p| hyperbolic planes).
The argument in briefReindex the hyperbolic sum hypSum(p, W) by an equivalence matching the signs; the standard splitting is base-adapted.
