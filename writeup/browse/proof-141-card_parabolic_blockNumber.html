lemma card_parabolic_blockNumber

What it saysFor finite index types N, T and j \in \mathbb{N}: there is one k \in \mathbb{N} such that for every commutative ring R (finite or not), Nat.card of the subgroup of \mathrm{GL}(BlockIndex(N, T, j), R) of the matrices that are block upper triangular for blockNumber(j) equals Nat.card(\mathrm{GL}_N(R))\cdotNat.card(\mathrm{GL}_T(R))^{j}\cdotNat.card(R)^{k} (Nat.card is 0 on an infinite type). For a finite ring R this counts those matrices; that reading is a special case.
The argument in briefInduction on j: a parabolic matrix for j + 1 is fromBlocks(A, B, 0, D) with A parabolic for j and D \in \mathrm{GL}_T.
