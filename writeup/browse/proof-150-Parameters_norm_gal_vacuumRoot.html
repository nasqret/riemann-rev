lemma Parameters.norm_gal_vacuumRoot

What it saysFor parameters P, every exponent s and every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C} that fixes K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d}) pointwise: |\sigma(\operatorname{vacuumRoot}(P, s))|^2 = rd_E/\sqrt{d}, where rd_E is the root discriminant of E = E(P, s).
The argument in briefRaise both sides to the power f (the number of infinite places of E). Then \sigma(s_0)^f = \sigma(\chi_{\mathrm{fin}}(\alpha))^{-1}, and a power \chi_{\mathrm{fin}}(\alpha)^k is a norm from E to K, so \sigma fixes it and |\sigma(\chi_{\mathrm{fin}}(\alpha))| = |\chi_{\mathrm{fin}}(\alpha)|. The absolute value of \chi_{\mathrm{fin}}(\alpha) is the inverse square root of the idele norm of \alpha, which is computed from \alpha \cdot \overline{\alpha}\cdot P'(\zeta), a unit at every finite place; the product of |v(\varpi_E)| over the infinite places v is rd_E^f.
