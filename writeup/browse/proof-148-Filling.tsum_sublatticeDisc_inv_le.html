lemma Filling.tsum_sublatticeDisc_inv_le

What it saysFor a stably unramified CM field E (f infinite places, root discriminant \operatorname{rd}_{E}), a finite set \kappa (k=|\kappa|),u\in G_f(\kappa) and a natural number s: \sum over all subspaces T of E^{\kappa} with \dim T+s=k of d(T)^{-(k+1)} is at most (200\cdot f\cdot k\cdot\operatorname{rd}_{E})^{f(k+1)s} (as a sum in [0,\infty]), d(T)= sublatticeDisc(u,T) the discriminant of T\cap u\hat{O}^{\kappa}.
The argument in briefEach T of codimension s is cut out by s nonzero lattice vectors with product of lengths bounded by Siegel's lemma; summing over the s-tuples of lattice vectors and using that lattice vectors are 1-separated gives a convergent bound.
