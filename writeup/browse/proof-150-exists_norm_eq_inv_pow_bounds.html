lemma exists_norm_eq_inv_pow_bounds

What it saysLet p be a prime and e, Q natural numbers with p^e = Q + 1. For natural numbers x, t, \varepsilon, J, k with e \ne 0, \varepsilon \le 1 and x + 1 + Q\cdot J = p\cdot t + \varepsilon + Q\cdot k (the last hypothesis H has a default proof by omega), there is a \in \mathbb{N} with |x + 1|_p = p^{-a} in \mathbb{C}_p, (a > 1 \Longrightarrow p < x + 1), (a = 0 \text{ or } k \equiv \varepsilon + J \text{ mod } p), and n\cdot(a - e) \le k + t for every n \le p (a - e truncated at 0).
The argument in briefTake a = v_p(x + 1).
