lemma IsSplitLevel.translateSum_eq_sum_name

What it saysFor a field E with a CM type and Weil data, finite index types n, n', m, a prime N and two split levels P (instance h) and S (h') for N — so P \cup \overline{P} = S \cup \overline{S} — and finite data v, v': \tau_N(P^{S} v \otimes P^{S} v') = \sum_{z} \mathrm{name}_P(\mathbf{b}_z(\cdot D))(v) \otimes \mathrm{name}_P(\mathbf{c}_z(D'\cdot))(v'), the sum over z \in ((\mathbb{Z}/N)^{P})^{n'}, where \tau_N = translateSum(n, n', S, N, m), P^{S} = projOnFinPart(S), D = diagN(n, S), D' = diagN(m, P \setminus S), \mathbf{b}_z = lastColDatum(z) and \mathbf{c}_z = charDatum(z).
The argument in briefPass to functions: the projections P^{S} v are names of box indicators, \tau_N translates them, and the resulting tensor of functions is compared with \sum_{z} \mathbf{b}_z(\cdot D) \otimes \mathbf{c}_z(D'\cdot) pointwise; only one z contributes at each point, and the two additive characters agree.
