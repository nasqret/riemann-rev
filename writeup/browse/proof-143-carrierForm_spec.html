lemma carrierForm_spec

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, m \ne 0 even (he), \mathfrak{m} a modulus, c a character and \chi a multiplicative character of O_E/\mathfrak{m} with c tame for (\mathfrak{m}, 0, \chi) (hc; such data exist for the argument). Then carrierForm E m lies in \operatorname{rapidOfLevel}(\operatorname{signedSpace}, N) for every N (it is rapidly decreasing and of every principal level N), and there is an order o, positive-first for the signs (+1 \text{ on } \operatorname{Fin} (2m), -1 \text{ on } \operatorname{Fin} m), such that carrierForm E m is adelic holomorphic of order o and weight (0 on the positive block, 3m on the negative block) at every infinite place.
The argument in briefBy the carrier identity the carrier is a finite combination of theta kernels \theta(\cdot, h) with a tame datum; each is rapidly decreasing, invariant under K_0 (the tame character on determinants), and holomorphic by the holomorphic polynomial \prod \det(y^-)^{2m}.
