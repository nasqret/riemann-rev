Structured proof · Unit 152

lemma exists_mul_principal

What it saysPigeonhole in the class group. For a number field K with class number h_K and families I, J of h_K + 1 finite places of K (indexed by \operatorname{Fin}(h_K + 1)) such that H : every product I_i\cdot J_i of prime ideals is principal: there are indices i \ne j with I_i\cdot J_j principal.
The argument in briefClasses of the h_K + 1 ideals I_i in a group of order h_K: two coincide, c(I_i) = c(I_j) with i \ne j, and then c(I_i)\cdot c(J_j) = c(I_j)\cdot c(J_j) = 1.
