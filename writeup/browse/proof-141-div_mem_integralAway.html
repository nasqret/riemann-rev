lemma div_mem_integralAway

What it saysFor natural numbers q, c, d, k with c \mid d\cdot q^{k} (hypothesis h): the complex number d/c lies in integralAway(q), i.e. |\iota(d/c)|_{l} \le 1 for every prime l coprime to q and every ring homomorphism \iota : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_l.
The argument in briefWrite d\cdot q^{k} = c\cdot m; for l coprime to q, \iota(q) is an l-adic unit, so |\iota(d/c)| = |\iota(m)|\cdot|\iota(q)|^{-k} = |\iota(m)| \le 1.

    By h, d\cdot q^{k} = c\cdot m for a natural number m. Since l does not divide q, |\iota(q)|_{l} = 1, so taking norms gives |\iota(d)| = |\iota(c)|\cdot|\iota(m)|; hence |\iota(d/c)| = |\iota(m)| \le 1, because the image of a natural number lies in the valuation ring of \mathbb{C}_l.
