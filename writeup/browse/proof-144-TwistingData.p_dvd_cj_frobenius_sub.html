What it saysLet N be a natural number, W a commutative local ring, O a commutative ring and T : TwistingData N W O (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to O, parity isEven, w \in O with w^{2} = p), k \in \mathbb{N}, h \in \mathrm{GL}_k(W), a letter s, d \in \mathbb{N}, a matrix Y \in Mat_k(O) and b, c \in Fin k: p divides (\iota_{s}(h)\cdot(\iota_{s-d}(h^{-1})\cdot Y\cdot \iota_{s-d}(h))^{(P^{d})}\cdot \iota_{s}(h^{-1}))_{\mathit{bc}} -(Y_{\mathit{bc}})^{P^{d}} in O, where M^{(P^{d})} is the entrywise P^{d}-th power; i.e. letterConj T h s ((letterConj T h^{-1} (s - d) Y)^{(P^{d})}) \equiv Y^{(P^{d})} mod p entrywise.
The argument in briefModulo p, x \mapsto x^{P^{d}} is a ring homomorphism f, and \pi \circ \iota_{s} = f\circ \iota_{s-d} (Frobenius congruence); so modulo p the conjugation by \iota_{s}(h) and the P^{d}-th power of the conjugation by \iota_{s-d}(h^{-1}) cancel.
