lemma Filling.norm_sq_middle_part_eq

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), \gamma\in G(S,E),y\in E^{\varrho+1} (implicit), D\in E and a vector t (implicit): if Z(\gamma)\cdot y=D\cdot e_{\varrho} (h) and t=(\gamma f)\cdot y (e), then (1) every e^{*}-coordinate of t vanishes and -N\cdot t_z=D, and (2) for every infinite place v, with s_v=N\cdot\sum_{i\in\kappa}|\Phi_v(t_{x_i})|^{2}: s_v=|\Phi_v(D)|^{2}-(N|\Phi_v(y_{\varrho})|)^{2}, and if s_v\le0 then t=f\cdot x for some x (t lies in the span of the frame).
The argument in briefCompare h(t,t)=h(fy,fy)=-N|y_{\varrho}|^{2} with the coordinates of t, which Z(\gamma)y determines.
