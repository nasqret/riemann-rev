lemma HyperbolicBasis.eventually_mass_near_quadric_le

What it saysLemma (slabs near the quadric h(e,e)=-N). Let A be a commutative topological ring with involution, Haar measure and place structure P,\iota finite, m\in\mathbb{N},\pi a unit with \pi\cdot\overline{\pi}=N, the whole Gram matrix of latMiddleGram(\mathfrak{s},N) hermitian invertible, F a hyperbolic basis of latMiddleGram(\mathfrak{s},N) (m+1 hyperbolic pairs and an orthonormal part \iota), \Phi\ge0 continuous with compact support, and c>\int\Phi d(\mathtt{floorQuadric}(F, top)). Then for every sufficiently small set S near 0 (eventually in the small sets of 0) that is measurable: \int d\tau\int_{y:\overline{y}y-((\pi\overline{\pi})^{-1}\tau\overline{\tau}-N)\in S}\Phi(\mathtt{tailVector}(\pi,\tau,y))dy\le\operatorname{md}(\pi)\cdot\nu(\{\Gamma:\mathtt{skewUnit}{}^{-1}\cdot\Gamma\in S\})\cdot c, with md the modulus and \nu= skewMeasure of P.
The argument in briefUpper semicontinuity of the fibre integrals of \Phi against the disintegration of the floor volume along the quadric gives, for S small, fibre integrals <c over the points of S; the left side is the floor volume of \{\text{quadric value}+N\in S\} weighted by \Phi, which by the disintegration and the change of variables to (\tau,y) is at most \operatorname{md}(\pi)\cdot\nu(\mathtt{skewUnit}-scaled S)\cdot c.
