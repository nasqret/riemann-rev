lemma exists_isLaw

What it saysFor a prime \ell and a stably unramified CM field E embedded in \Omega with at least 4 infinite places (hf); natural numbers r and e with r > 0 (hr); a finite index type I and a natural number k with k \le |I| (hI); a family x : I \to FrobeniusDatum E of Frobenius data x_i = (w_i, A_i, s_i) (w_i a finite place of E of prime norm, A_i a place of \Omega over w_i, s_i \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) with s_i^{-1} an arithmetic Frobenius at A_i) such that IsSplitPlace r x_i holds for every i (hx: w_i does not lie above \ell and cycCharMod \Omega (\ell ^r) s_i = 1) and the places primeBelowPlus w_i of E^{+} are pairwise distinct (i); and elements y_0, \ldots , y_{k-1} \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) (y): there are a function z : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \mathbb{Z}/\ell ^r and a finite set T of finite places of E^{+} such that IsLaw r e \{s_i : i \in I\} T z holds (\Phi the set of the Frobenius elements s_i), z(y_j) = 0 for every j < k, and there is g \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) with z(g) a unit of \mathbb{Z}/\ell ^r and cycCharMod \Omega (\ell ^r) g = 1.
The argument in briefPass to E^{+}: \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) is an open subgroup of index 2 of \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E^{+}) containing no complex conjugation, a map p : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E^{+}) \to \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) lifting \sigma on the index-two subgroup \operatorname{res}(\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E)) and \sigma \cdot c_0 on its complement (so p \circ \operatorname{res} = \operatorname{id} and p(c_0) = 1; p is not a section of \operatorname{res}) gives the character \kappa = cycChar \Omega \ell \circ p of \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E^{+}), trivial on complex conjugations, and M = \mathbb{Z}/\ell ^r with the action \kappa ^{-e} \bmod \ell ^r. The Greenberg–Wiles relations at split primes (exists_split_primes_relations) for M, unramified outside S = (places above \ell) \cup (places below the w_i), with local conditions trivial off \ell and on \ker \kappa above \ell, give split places v_i with tame generators \gamma _i and an equivariant extension of every relation. A count of the \ell-torsion of the Selmer group (the Greenberg–Wiles bound for M[\ell ] with two extra Chebotarev primes, a procyclic generator of \kappa , k \le \#(S off \ell) and 4 \le \#\infty) gives \#Y[\ell ]\cdot \ell ^{k+1} \le \ell ^{|\iota |}, so a primitive relation n with a unit entry and zeros at the y_j exists (exists_primitive_relation). Then z(g) = n_{\pi (\operatorname{res} g^{-1})} is a law: local constancy, the conjugation rule and oddness come from the equivariance of the extended relation; the reciprocity comes from the relation at the new split places applied to the cocycle 2\cdot f\circ c\circ p made from an even class c. Notation of the proof: E^{+} the maximal real subfield, \operatorname{res} : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E^{+}) the restriction, c_0 the chosen complex conjugation, p the lift of step 5 (\operatorname{res}(p(\sigma )) = \sigma on the image of \operatorname{res}, \sigma \cdot c_0 off it), \kappa = cycChar \Omega \ell \circ p, \rho = \kappa \bmod \ell ^r, V = \operatorname{res}(\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E)) \cap \ker cycCharMod \Omega (\ell ^r), M = \mathbb{Z}/\ell ^r with g acting by \rho (g)^{-e}; in the count B: W = M[\ell ], K_\psi = \ker \psi (O in the source), \gamma _0 a procyclic generator (\gamma in the source).
