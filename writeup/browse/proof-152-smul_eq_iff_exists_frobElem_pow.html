lemma Parameters.smul_eq_iff_exists_frobElem_pow

What it saysDecomposition group. For parameters P, an exponent s, a prime p (instance hp), an infinite place v and a finite place w of E(P, s), a ring automorphism c of \mathcal{O}_E and an integer e with he : (c = \operatorname{id} and e = 1) or (c = \operatorname{conj} and e = -1), hw : p \in w and hc : p \nmid N_1(P, s)\cdot d(P): v \bullet w = c^{-1}(w) (that is w for c = \operatorname{id}, the conjugate place \overline{w} for c = \operatorname{conj}) if and only if there is a natural number n with (v, e) = frobElem(P, s, p)^n.
The argument in briefv \bullet w = c^{-1}(w) means that galOfPlace(P, s)(v)\circ c^{-1} preserves w and so induces an automorphism of the finite residue field, a power of Frobenius; conversely a Frobenius congruence galOfPlace(P, s)(v)(x) \equiv c(x)^{p^n} \bmod w gives the equality of places.
