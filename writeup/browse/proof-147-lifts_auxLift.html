lemma Data.lifts_auxLift

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data for P, \vartheta a tame character of D and j a lift index (1 \le j \le m). Let p be an eigensystem and y a form in eigenPart(D, spaceW(D, j), p) (principal level g on W_j, each heckeOperator(D, x) acting by p(x)). Then Lifts(auxPair(D, \vartheta, j), auxLift(D, \vartheta, j)(y), (w, i) \mapsto p(w, i)): at every finite place w with \overline{w} \ne w and w off the places of the auxiliary pair (and their conjugates), \theta_{j}^{\ast}y is spherical with Hecke polynomial T \mapsto trivialHecke(q_w, n - r, \nu(w)T)\cdotheckePolyEval(r, w, p(w, \cdot), q_w^{n-r}\nu(w)T/\nu '(w)), where r, n are the ranks of W_j, V_j and \nu, \nu ' the Frobenius values (AutChar.frobValue) of the characters a, b of the pair; equivalently \prod_{l<n-r}(1 - q^{l+r/2}\chi_{W}(w)X)\cdot P(q^{(n-r)/2}\chi_{W}\chi_{V}^{-1}(w)X) for the Hecke polynomial P of y at w.
The argument in briefThe adjoint lift is the algebraic form of a theta lift; transfer the sphericity of y through it.
