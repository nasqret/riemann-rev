lemma Filling.amplitudeAbs_lt_top_and_cut_le

What it saysUnder the standing hypotheses, for filling data S over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit), the side \sigma, a set T\subseteq G(S,E) and c,s (a class and an element of \mathsf{G}), with X(d)=T\cap termsOver(S,c,s,d): (1) for every class c', (a) there is M\ge0 with \sum_{\gamma\in X(c')}\beta_{c'}(\gamma^{-1}y)\cdot\prod_v\lVert W_{\sigma,v}((\gamma^{-1}y)_v)\rVert\le M for every y, and (b) \sum_{\gamma\in X(c')} amplitudeAbs(S,c,c',\gamma)<\infty; and (2) \sum_d\sum_{\gamma\in X(d)}\int^{-}\beta_c(y)\beta_d(\gamma^{-1}y)\cdot\lVert\mathtt{besselKernel}_{\sigma}(\gamma,y)-\mathtt{besselKernel\_cut}(\gamma,y)\rVert dy\le\nu\cdot b^{-k^{3}f}/2 (\gamma^{-1}y= ratToInf(\gamma)^{-1}\cdot y,\nu= unipCovolume, k=|\kappa|, f the number of infinite places).
The argument in briefDominate the Bessel functions by the \mathsf{U}-invariant majorants A_v of the cut pairs; the frame count bounds the sum over terms at boxed points; Tonelli and the covolume evaluate the integrals; where full and cut differ the majorant carries the factor b^{3r^{3}f}, which gives the cut estimate.
