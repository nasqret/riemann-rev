Chapter V · Unit 149

A crude bound for the class number of the standard definite hermitian genus, and divisibility of indices of submodules

note and structured proofs · 11 declarations · 5 structured proofs · 1 note

Two independent pieces of bookkeeping. (1) Space.card_classes_le: for a CM field E with a nonzero natural number N in its different, the number of classes \Gamma_f\backslash G_f/K_f of the standard definite hermitian space of rank n is at most (1 + 2C^3)^{4fn^2}, C = 6N(fn)^2, by mapping each class injectively to the integral, archimedean-bounded entries of two matrices built from a short basis. (2) IndexDvd M N z, a divisibility form of 'the index of N in M divides z' (every finite family in M is moved into N by a square matrix whose determinant divides z), with its calculus: pull-back, push-forward, transitivity, spans, composition, products of operators of a given type (HasType: y(M) \subseteq M and y(M) + p^n M has index dividing p^l with l \le c), and the determinant criterion over a valuation domain (IndexDvd.of_det_dvd).

    Let E be a CM field with a CM type (CMType E: a CM number field with a chosen complex embedding \Phi_w inducing each infinite place w), N a nonzero natural number (NeZero N) whose image in the ring of integers lies in the different ideal of E (h), and n a finite index type.   • defIndexDvdline 20

    For a commutative ring O, an O-module V, submodules M, N \subseteq V and an element z \in O: IndexDvd M N z holds when for every natural number n and every family \Phi_1, \ldots, \Phi_n of elements of M there is an n\times n matrix m over O whose determinant divides z such that every combination \sum_k m_{ik}\cdot\Phi_k (i = 1, \ldots, n) lies in N. (A divisibility form of 'the index of N in M divides z'; when M \subseteq N, m = 1 works.) The definition takes M, N, z as explicit arguments (variable (M N z) in).
