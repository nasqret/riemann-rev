lemma partner_block

What it saysLet n, a, b be finite index types, R a commutative ring, e : b \sqcup a \simeq n, \chi a multiplicative character of R and f any complex function on a \times b matrices over R. Cut an a \times n matrix x by e into column blocks x =(x_{1} | x_{2}) with x_{2} square (a \times a), and an n \times b matrix y into row blocks y =(y_{1} ; y_{2}) with y_{1} square (b \times b). Then the functions \Phi(x) = \chi^{-1}(\det x_{2})\cdot f(x_{2}^{-1}x_{1}) and \Psi(y) = \chi^{-1}(\det y_{1})\cdot f(-y_{2}y_{1}^{-1}) are partners for \chi over R with respect to e and the whole ring as O (IsPartner ⊤ e χ Φ Ψ): \Phi vanishes off the matrices invLastRows(e, k), \Psi off firstCols(e, k), k \in \mathrm{GL}_n(R), and \Phi(invLastRows(e, k)) = \chi(\det k)\cdot \Psi(firstCols(e, k)) for all k.
The argument in briefWitnesses: x = invLastRows(e, k) for k the block matrix (1 0; x_{1} x_{2})^{-1} and y = firstCols(e, k) for k =(y_{1} 0; y_{2} 1); the identity is Jacobi's complementary minors: \det x_{2}\cdot \det k = \det of the first block of k.
