lemma Filling.inclHFin_mem_latStabilizer

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit) and k in the finite adelic unitary group of X_0 (implicit): inclHFin(S)(k)\in K iff k lies in the congruence group of the row k_0 modulo \overline{\pi}\hat{O} (k integral with row k_0 congruent to that of 1 modulo \overline{\pi}\hat{O}).
The argument in briefinclHFin(k)\in K iff inclHFin(k)-1 is integral (the lattice form is unimodular); translate this by forall_inclHFin_sub_one_iff into a condition on k and use the integrality criteria for middleBaseChange.
