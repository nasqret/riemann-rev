Chapter VII · Unit 146

Vanishing of theta descents: reflection sums at the level, and climbing the theta tower

note and structured proofs · 8 declarations · 7 structured proofs · 1 note

Two vanishing results for theta descents (DescentsVanish: every descent of Y from U(V) to U(W), with a datum that is the vacuum at a given set of places, is 0). For an auxiliary pair O: if \psi is nontrivial at Q, reflection sums of nonzero rows at the chosen places kill y, and the descents with data P tensor \Upsilon vanish, then all descents of \operatorname{conj}\bigl(algFormCoeff(u, y)\bigr) with data vacuum off the places of O vanish (descentsVanish_of_reflectionSum, via lv: moving coset indicators by integral elements changes y by chosenGLRep). And adding hyperbolic planes: if descents to W vanish and Y is killed by a Hecke polynomial at a split place with a root that is too small, the descents to W + H^p vanish (descentsVanish_add_planes), by induction on p, a Hecke transfer, a maximum principle for bounded functions killed by a Hecke polynomial (eq_zero_of_heckePolyOp_eq_zero), and the Iwasawa decomposition for the zero term (integral_sourceTerm_eq_zero).
