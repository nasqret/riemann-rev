Structured proof · Unit 134

lemma AuxPair.upsilon_eq_sum

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data and O an auxiliary pair over E with W of rank n and V = I_{n'}. Then auxDatum(O) equals the sum, over A in TwistGL(O, n), of \omega((1, twistLift(O, A)), 1) applied to modDatum(W(O), stdSpace(E, n'), m, levelIndicator(O, .), vacVector), m = levelModulus(O, 1) the modulus MQ. Here \omega = \omega_{a(O), b(O)} is the Weil representation of the pair (W, I_{n'}) (pairThetaKernel(O)); (1, twistLift(O, A)) is the element of the adelic group of W that is 1 at the infinite places and twistLift(O, A) at the finite places; modDatum(S, T, m, Psi, v) is the datum v tensor \Psi tensor vacuum on \operatorname{Hom}(S, T), \Psi a function on matrices modulo MQ named at the places dividing MQ, v = vacVector the Gaussian at the infinite places.
The argument in briefBoth sides are data named from residue functions on matrices modulo MQ; the translates by the lifts of A^{-1} move the level indicator so that its incl-rows at Q become A. Comparing the functions at a matrix x: if the incl-rows of x at every place of Q are invertible, exactly one A contributes and gives \prod \psi(\det); otherwise both sides vanish.

  3. 3setupline 48

    Case split on whether, at every place of Q, the incl-rows of x form an invertible matrix.

  4. 4caseline 49

    If they do, call them A_w: exactly the term A of the sum survives, and it equals \prod_w \psi_w(\det A_w), the left side.

  5. 5caseline 50

    Otherwise some incl-row block has determinant 0, so \psi of it is 0 and the left side vanishes; and no A makes the indicator 1, so the right side vanishes.
