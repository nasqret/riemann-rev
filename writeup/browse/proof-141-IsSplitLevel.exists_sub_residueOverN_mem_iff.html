lemma IsSplitLevel.exists_sub_residueOverN_mem_iff

What it saysFor a split level P for the prime N (instance h) and a finite adele z: there is b \in (\mathbb{Z}/N)^{P} such that, for every y \in (\mathbb{Z}/N)^{P}, \operatorname{idem}(P)\cdot(z - residueOverN(y)) \in \hat{O} iff \operatorname{idem}(P)\cdot(N\cdot z) \in \hat{O} and b = y: z - y/N is integral at P exactly when Nz is integral at P and y is the reduction b of Nz. When Nz is integral at P the witness b is its reduction; when it is not, both sides are false for every y, and the equivalence holds whatever b is.
The argument in briefb is the reduction of the components of Nz at P.
