If HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m), then with k= termPosition(S,c,c',\gamma) and T_f= latMiddle(S,\mathbb{A}_f) there is an integral matrix n\in M_{\mathtt{BesselIndex}\times\tau}(\hat{O}) with zFormPair(T_f)\cdotlinkMatrix(k)\cdot n= zFormPair(T)\cdot m, (isotropicFramePair k)*\cdot J_f\cdot n=0 (J_f the split Gram matrix of T_f), and, for every p, if the first row block of m is (p; 0) then the X_0-rows of n are classCoordX{}_0(S,c)\cdot p.
