lemma Filling.energy_compl_besselS_le

What it saysLemma (second moment off the Bessel subgroup). Let E be a stably unramified number field with a CM structure, \mathfrak{s} a shape, S admissible filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s} and i\in\{0,1\}. Then \operatorname{energy}(\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(S,\mathsf{S}(E)^{c}, \cdot, \cdot, \cdot),\Omega_i)\le b(S)^{-12}\cdot\mathsf{d}_1(S), where \mathsf{S}(E)^{c} is the set of rational points outside the Bessel subgroup and \Omega_i=\{s\in\mathsf{G}:s_i=t_i for some t\in reducedImage(S,\mathsf{S})} (the i-th component of s is that of an element of the reduced image \overline{S}). (Notation: \mathsf{G}= GRes(S)=\prod_{i\in\{0,1\}}\mathsf{G}_i,\mathsf{G}_i=\prod_{w\in\mathtt{chosenPlaces}(S,i)}\operatorname{GL}_{\mathtt{BesselIndex}(\mathfrak{s})}(\mathbb{F}_w); \iota_i(s)= Pi.mulSingle(i,s)\in\mathsf{G} for s\in\mathsf{G}_i; amplitudeSum(S,T,c,s,c')\in\ell^{2}(\mathtt{BesselCoord}\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}) is the sum of \operatorname{amplitude}(S,c,c',\gamma) over the rational \gamma\in T that are terms over (c,s,c'), with the full Bessel functions; \mathsf{S}(E)= besselS of the rational middle form; \nu(S)= unipCovolume(S); \operatorname{energy}(A,\Omega)=\sum_{c,c'}\sum_{s\in\Omega}\lVert A(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu(S)^{2}; \mathsf{d}(S)= mainEnergy(S)= columnDim(S)^{2}/(\mathtt{besselDegree}(S)\cdot\operatorname{mass}(E,r(\mathfrak{s}))); \mathsf{d}_1(S)= besselEnergy(S)= \#reducedImage(S,\mathsf{S})\cdotcolumnDim(S); columnDim(S)= \#Classes(S)\cdot\#BesselCoord(S).)
The argument in briefCrude estimate with T=\mathsf{S}^{c} (stable), X=q_i^{f} (s\in\Omega_i fixes f_0 at every chosen place above q_i), C=\mathsf{d}_1,w=0 and R=\emptyset (no link energy). The hypothesis h_1 holds vacuously: a term \gamma\notin\mathsf{S} over s\in\Omega_i in normal form of index 0 would, by freeness at the places above q_i, have scalar 1 on the first frame vector and then fix all frame vectors, so \gamma\in\mathsf{S}. The constant condition \tau^{12}/X^{k}\cdot\mathsf{d}\le\mathsf{d}_1 follows from mainEnergy_le.
