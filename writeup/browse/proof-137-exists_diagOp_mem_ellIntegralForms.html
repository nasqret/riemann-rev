lemma BaseLevel.exists_diagOp_mem_ellIntegralForms

What it saysIn the setting (\ell prime, \iota_\ell \colon \mathbb{C} \cong \overline{Q}_\ell, O the \ell-adic integers of \overline{Q}_\ell, the CM field E \subset \Omega, the nonempty rank type n with N = |n|, V standard of rank n, the weight \kappa, Weil data for E, J finite, \nu, c, the base datum D, the Taylor–Wiles places Q with places w_j, norms q_j \equiv 1 mod \ell^\nu, tame generators \tau_j and Frobenius elements s_j, every w_j off level for D at base level (hQ), a character a \colon \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to O and representations N_x with N_x(g) = a(g)\cdot \rho_x(g) (hN) and a(\tau_j) = 1 for every j (ha)); w_Q denotes the family j \mapsto w_j, and \operatorname{traceElem}(h_{N}, \cdot, \cdot, \cdot), \operatorname{heckeAction}(D, w_Q) are taken at the level w_Q. Let b \colon \operatorname{Eigensystem}(D, w_Q) \to \overline{Q}_\ell, p \in O[X], \mathcal{X} a set of eigensystems of level w_Q, \pi \in \mathcal{X}, e \colon J \to O[X], t \in \mathbb{N} with t \ne 0 (ht), m \in \overline{Q}_\ell and \gamma \in [0, \infty]. Suppose (he): for every j \in J and every eigensystem x of level w_Q that is an eigensystem of base level, with p(b(x)) \ne 0 and such that every u \in \operatorname{heckeAlgebra}(D, w_Q) vanishing at x has |u(\pi)| < 1, \operatorname{HasRootString}(P_{x, w_j}, e_j, q_j, a(s_j)^{-1}, t) holds; (hν): 2\cdot N!\cdot(p_0\cdot c^2)^{|J|+1} divides \ell^\nu in O, p_0 the constant coefficient of p; (rib): for every O-subalgebra S of the functions from \operatorname{Eigensystem}(D, w_Q) to \overline{Q}_\ell [X_j \colon j \in J] and every S-submodule \mathcal{J} with \operatorname{IsTraceIdeal}(\mathcal{J}, (x, g) \mapsto N_x(g), \ell^\nu, j \mapsto \tau_j, j \mapsto s_j, \operatorname{id}, e), there is d with \operatorname{IsRibetElement}(\mathcal{J}, b, \pi, d) and \operatorname{ellVal}(m\cdot d) \le \gamma. Then there is s \colon \operatorname{Eigensystem}(D, w_Q) \to \overline{Q}_\ell with \operatorname{ellVal}(m\cdot s(\pi)) \le \gamma such that for every base-level form l, every d \in \mathbb{N} with d\cdot l in ellIntegralForms, and every base-level form y with \operatorname{diagOp}(s')(y) = 0 for every s' vanishing on \mathcal{X} and \operatorname{diagOp}(s')(l) = \operatorname{diagOp}(s')(y) for every s' vanishing at all x with p(b(x)) = 0: d\cdot c\cdot \operatorname{diagOp}(s)(y) lies in ellIntegralForms. (Here ellVal is the truncated \ell-adic valuation on all of \overline{Q}_\ell, applied to the unrestricted scalars m\cdot d and m\cdot s(\pi): \operatorname{ellVal}(0) = \infty and \operatorname{ellVal}(z) = \max(0, -\log_\ell |z|) for z \ne 0 (Lean: ENNReal.ofReal of -\log_\ell |z|), normalised by \operatorname{ellVal}(\ell) = 1, so \operatorname{ellVal}(z) = 0 whenever |z| \ge 1, e.g. \operatorname{ellVal}(1/\ell) = 0; \operatorname{diagOp}(s') multiplies the forms of each eigensystem x by \iota_\ell^{-1}(s'(x)), and a base-level form is one in \operatorname{formsOfLevel}(D, ![]).)
The argument in briefFeed rib with S = the evaluations of polynomials over the Hecke algebra and \mathcal{J} = the ideal generated by \ell^\nu \cdot \operatorname{tr} and the tie relations; this gives a Ribet element: b in the Hecke algebra and f with C(b)\cdot f in the ideal, f without monomials properly dividing \prod X_j on \mathcal{X}. Take s = A^k\cdot f_o (A = \prod u_x separates \pi, o = \prod X_j). Pass to the module B that l generates over the integral operators modulo \ell^\nu; by exists_tie at each place, the operators \Theta_d = \prod_j (M_j \text{ or } F_j) kill the image of the ideal (the family \prod(M_j + X_j F_j)), the lower terms vanish because the lifts do not move (F2), and the top term gives \ell^\nu \cdot V(l) = c\cdot \operatorname{heckeAction}(A^k f_o)(y) times the unit factors; divide by \ell^\nu.
