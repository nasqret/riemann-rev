lemma AuxPair.heckePolyOnForms_comm

What it saysFor a field E with a CM type, finite types r, n, an auxiliary pair O over E with index types r \subset n, finite places w and u of E with \overline{w} \ne w (hw), \overline{u} \ne u (h), both outside the places of O and their conjugates (hS, s), X, Y \in \mathbb{C} and y \in O.FormV: O.heckePolyOnForms hw hS X (O.heckePolyOnForms h s Y y) = O.heckePolyOnForms h s Y (O.heckePolyOnForms hw hS X y); the Hecke polynomial operators H_w(X) and H_u(Y) commute on O.FormV.
The argument in briefThe Hecke operators at two split places commute on spherical vectors; so do the polynomials in them.
