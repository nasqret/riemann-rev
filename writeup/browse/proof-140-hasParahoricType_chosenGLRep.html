lemma Data.hasParahoricType_chosenGLRep

What it saysLet D be admissible data over P; n, r finite types; O an auxiliary pair over E(D) with rank types r and n; S a complex subspace of the functions from G_f to the functions on \operatorname{WeylIndex}(n, \kappa(O)); \pi a representation of G_f on S; u a vector of S of level \operatorname{levelGroup}(O) and character \operatorname{levelChar}(O); i an index of the chosen places of O (the places of M), w = \operatorname{chosenPlace}(O, i), and v a proof that w is split (w differs from its conjugate); f a function from G_f to the functions on \operatorname{WeylIndex}(n, \kappa(O)). Assume that for every k in \mathrm{GL}_n(E_w), \pi(\operatorname{atSplit}(v, k)) u is the right translate of f by \operatorname{atSplit}(v, k). Then \operatorname{HasParahoricType}(f, v, \operatorname{incl}(O), b, \psi(O, w)^{-1}, 1, F) holds, where b(c) = 0 for c in the image of \operatorname{incl}(O) and 1 otherwise, 1 is the trivial character of the one further block, and F(g) is the function \operatorname{chosenGLRep}(O, \pi)(g \text{ at } i, 1 elsewhere)(u). That is: the 0-block of b is the image of \operatorname{incl}(O), and for every k in \mathrm{GL}_n(O_w) whose reduction mod w is block triangular for b with 0-block x, the right translate of f by \operatorname{atSplit}(v, k) equals \psi(O, w)(\det x)^{-1} F(x).
The argument in briefThe block condition is the definition of b. For k integral at w, block triangular mod w with block x, the hypothesis turns the right translate of f into \pi(\operatorname{atSplit}(v, k)) u, and smul_chosenGLRep_eq computes this as \psi_w(\det x)^{-1} \operatorname{chosenGLRep}(x \text{ at } i)(u); its hypothesis on the reductions holds at i by the block structure and trivially elsewhere.
