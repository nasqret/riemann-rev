lemma finrank_sub_le_re_inner_dyadSum

What it saysHilbert–Schmidt inequality. Let H be a finite-dimensional complex inner product space with an orthonormal basis b indexed by a finite type m, n a finite type, \mathbb{C}^{n} = EuclideanSpace \mathbb{C} n with standard basis (e_x), P : \mathbb{C}^{n} \to H and R : H \to H linear, U \subseteq \mathbb{C}^{n} and A \subseteq H subspaces, and \varepsilon a real number. If \sum_{x\in n} \lVert(P^{*}P - 1)e_x\rVert \le \varepsilon (k) and P v - R(P v) \in A for every v \in U (h), then \dim U - \varepsilon - \sum_{x\in n} \lVert R(P e_x)\rVert^{2} \le \operatorname{Re} \langledyadSum b (x \mapsto P e_x), dyadSum b (a_j)_{j}\rangle, where (a_j) is Mathlib's standard orthonormal basis of A (stdOrthonormalBasis \mathbb{C} A). By inner_dyadSum the right side is \sum_{x} \sum_{j} |\langle a_j, P e_x\rangle |^{2} = \sum_{x} \lVert p_A(P e_x)\rVert^{2}, the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of p_A\circ P, p_A the orthogonal projection onto A. (The text reading of the signature also lists G, [Group G], [Fintype G] and the section variable v : G \to H; the statement's v is bound by \forall v \in U, so Lean is expected not to take them.)
The argument in briefTake an orthonormal basis u of U. Computing the trace of p_U(1 - P^{*}P) in u and in the standard basis gives \dim U - \sum_{j} \lVert Pu_j\rVert^{2} \le \varepsilon. For u_j \in U, Pu_j - RPu_j \in A, so \lVert Pu_j\rVert^{2} = \lVert p_A Pu_j\rVert^{2} + \lVert p_{A^{\perp}}RPu_j\rVert^{2} \le \lVert p_A Pu_j\rVert^{2} + \lVert RPu_j\rVert^{2}; summing, and bounding sums over u by the Hilbert–Schmidt sums over the standard basis (Bessel), gives the inequality.
