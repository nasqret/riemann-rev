lemma IsPencil.length_le_of_unseen_class

What it saysMirrors. For a pencil x (hp), O the integers of a finite extension of \mathbb{Q}_\ell (PadicIntegerRing), \rho balanced up to \beta (IsBalanced \ell S \rho L \gamma \beta M) (r), V_i = (\rho (\cdot ) \equiv 1 \bmod \ell ^M) (hV), S \subseteq S_0 (hS) and a \ne 0 dividing \ell ^M (ha): with X(T) the Selmer module of \rho modulo a for the places S \cup T \cup \overline{T} and the relations L, the ties tieRelations J x i and vanishing at \gamma: if some y \in X(J) has a unit entry, and for every place P \notin J and auxiliary data l such that x extended by (l, slope e_i\cdot t) is a pencil for every t, every element of X(J) is, at \varphi _P and \varphi _P^{-c}, the value at \gamma _P and \gamma _P^{-c} of an element of X(J \cup \{P\}), the Selmer module that may also ramify at P and \overline{P} but keeps the relations L \cup tieRelations J x i \cup \{c(\gamma ) = 0\} of the original J (X(J) \subseteq evalConjPair(\varphi _l)^{-1}(evalConjPair(\gamma _l)(X(J \cup \{P\})))), then length_O X(J) \le \beta.
The argument in briefApply the Greenberg–Wiles relations at split primes to the discrete module X = B^n modulo a, with the subgroup U = \ker (\rho \bmod \ell ^M) \cap \ker (\operatorname{cyc} \bmod \ell ^{M+1}); bound the number of relation families by the mirror lemma card_le_torsion_of_isotropic (isotropy coming from the hypothesis on new primes, realised by an anticyclotomic character at a Chebotarev prime), and convert cardinalities into lengths; the local terms and the scalar defect give \le \beta by the balanced inequality.
