lemma isRapid_modKernel

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, n and b finite types, \mathfrak{m} a modulus of E with at least one place (h\mathfrak{m}), s and t sign vectors, S a space of rank |n| with signs s and V a space of rank |b| with signs t (Space), a and \beta characters of U(1)(\mathbb{A}_E)/U(1)(E), \Psi a complex function on the matrices modulo \mathfrak{m} that vanishes off the left-invertible matrices (hΨ: \Psi(y) \ne 0 implies that y has a left inverse), P a polynomial and h a point of the adelic group of V. Then the function g \mapsto modKernel S V a \beta \mathfrak{m} \Psi P g h = \theta^{S, V}_{a, \beta }[\Psi, P](g, h) on the adelic group of S is rapidly decreasing (S.IsRapid: for every B, |\theta(g, h)|\cdot h_{t}(g)^B is bounded in g).
The argument in briefDefined by cases on the exponent B: the decay bound of theta kernels of modular data, with the finite moment of a polynomial Fock vector.
