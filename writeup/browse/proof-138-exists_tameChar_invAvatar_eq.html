lemma exists_tameChar_invAvatar_eq

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, a finite set I of places auxiliary for badPlaces D \ell and a character \psi : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell from U(1) for the places above \ell with modulus I (IsFromU1): there is a tame character \tau of D with τ.Q = I and \psi = τ.invAvatar.
The argument in briefThe idele character of \psi induces a character of the class group of U(1) of tame level I, i.e. an element \mu of tameChars I; \tau = tameCharOfAuxiliary I \mu ^{-1} has invAvatar = \psi on Artin images, hence everywhere.
