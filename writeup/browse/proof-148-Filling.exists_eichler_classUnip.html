lemma Filling.exists_eichler_classUnip

What it saysLet S be filling data over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit), c a class, a<\varrho,l a row vector over E (implicit) supported on the free entries of row a (l_j=0 unless IsFreeEntry(a,j); h) with l\cdotclassBasis(c) integral (k), and s an integral skew element of \mathcal{O}_E (\overline{s}=-s). Then there is \gamma\in\mathsf{U}_c(g) whose rows e_b (b<\varrho) are, at every column j with j\ne e_a^{*} or l=0: \gamma(e_b,j)=\delta(e_b,j)+g\cdot[b=a]\cdot(l_j+[j=e_a^{*}]\cdot s).
The argument in briefWrite l=h(u,\cdot) and apply exists_mem_classUnip with one column.
