Unit 135Heights of families indexed by ℓ-adic embeddings, the product formula, and the height of one Gram determinant

note and structured proofs · 18 declarations · 7 structured proofs

A family y of complex numbers indexed by the pairs (ℓ, ι) of a prime ℓ and a ring isomorphism ι : ℂ → ℚ̄_ℓ has height at most c (HeightLe c y) when one nonzero H ∈ ℂ, a real α and reals e(ℓ) bound |σH| ≤ e^α for all σ ∈ Aut(ℂ), ‖ι(H·y(ℓ,ι))‖_ℓ ≤ exp e(ℓ), and α + Σ_{ℓ<M} e(ℓ) ≤ c for all large M. Height bounds are monotone, add under pointwise products and powers, and the product formula for ℚ (applied to the norm of H) gives HeightLe.nonneg: a bound for the constant family 1 is ≥ 0. The second file proves the linear algebra behind the lower bound: a Fourier/positive-type estimate for singular minors (card_mul_sum_det_le), a Hilbert–Schmidt inequality (finrank_sub_le_re_inner_dyadSum), the greedy 'few translates' lemma (exists_few_translates), basis extension, and one_determinant, which bounds the height of the ratio of squared volumes normDet(u_θ)²/normDet(u_o)² by α + 2δ + k·g from integral recombinations at every ℓ-adic place.
