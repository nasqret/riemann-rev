What it saysDivisibility of the chain at each prime. For data D, a prime p with \iota_{p} and an integer \beta with \beta < g ( p ) - \operatorname{err} ( p ) + 1, where g ( p ) = \operatorname{gain} ( p ) if p \in nonsplitPrimes and 0 otherwise, and err = allowedLoss: there are one exponent l \in \mathbb{N} with l \le \operatorname{err} ( p ) \cdot \dim_{\mathbb{C}} Column( D ) and one O_{\iota}-submodule S of Column( D ) ( O_{\iota} = \{ z \in \mathbb{C} : | \iota_{p} ( z ) | \le 1 \} ) such that (1) IndexDvd(integralColumns, S , p^{l} ): for every finite family v_{1} , \ldots , v_{n} of integral columns there is a square n \times n matrix A over O_{\iota} with \det A \mid p^{l} (in O_{\iota}) such that every row combination \sum_{k} A_{ik} \cdot v_{k} lies in S (this is the definition of IndexDvd, New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean:17–20; it is not a statement about the cardinality of a quotient module, which would need a separate comparison over O_{\iota}); and (2) for every twist \vartheta, every \Psi with IsGoodDatum( \vartheta , \Psi ) and p prime to levelNorm( \vartheta ), and every \varphi \in S that is a ramified column: p^{- \beta} \cdot B_{\mu} ( \vartheta , \Psi ) ( \varphi ) \in integralFormsAt( \top , 1 ), i.e. at every point g its Weyl gauge at \iota_{p} is \le 1 (pointwise integrality in the weight lattice). The same l and S serve all twists \vartheta of conductor prime to p, all good \Psi for them, and all ramified columns in S.
The argument in briefThree cases. p = N: the submodule from the Fourier operator (exists_indexDvd_fourierOp_pow) and gaugeLe_Bmu_at_N. p non-split below x_{1} and served: S = Y^{m} M + p^{j_{p}} M with hasType_prod_markOperator_pow, and the two gauge bounds. Otherwise S = M (IndexDvd with l = 0) with gaugeLe_collisionSum (non-split, not served) or gaugeLe_one.
