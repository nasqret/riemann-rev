lemma Parameters.mem_raises_of_jet_bound

What it saysLet k = \operatorname{thetaKernel}(h\kappa, c, ha, hb, K) be the theta kernel of the definite pair I_n, I_{n'} at scale c (data in the finite part of \mathcal{S}(\operatorname{Hom}(I_n, I_{n'})), jet matrices along the Schur matrix S_\kappa), p a prime with a place \iota_p, \delta \in \mathcal{O}_K and \Psi a datum with \Psi \in \operatorname{standardData}(\operatorname{Hom}(I_n, I_{n'}), \delta). Let x, y be finite points integral above p (x, y \in integralAbove p), d a finite point and u_\tau such that at every infinite place \tau the image of d under \operatorname{adeleExtension}(x \mapsto \iota_p(\Phi_\tau(\overline{x}))) is the diagonal matrix \operatorname{diag}(u_\tau), and B \in \mathbb{R} such that for every f \in \operatorname{standardData}(\operatorname{Hom}, \delta) and all indices i', i: (\prod_\tau(\prod_b |u_\tau(b)|)^{ea_\tau }\cdot \prod_h\prod_j\prod_t |u_\tau((i'_\tau)_{h, j, t})|)\cdot |\iota_p(\operatorname{schurJet}_\kappa(T_c f)_{i', i})| \le B. Then \omega(1, x\cdot d\cdot y)\Psi \in \operatorname{raises}(k, 1, 1, B).
The argument in briefMove g and g' by elements of the rational points so that they become integral above p, use the equivariance of the kernel, and bound the gauge of the jet matrix of a datum in Std_\delta directly.
