lemma Filling.Fits.isGeneric_poincare

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data, S admissible filling data fitting V (h), \psi a column, and assume \jmath\cdot c(Y) = -(g\varpi)^{-1} (hjc). Then the Poincaré series of h.columnCoords(\psi) (the coordinate map of the arbitrary fitting data h, Filling.Fits.columnCoords, not D.columnCoords) over the rational Bessel points satisfies Grading.IsGeneric for Y, which comprises three conditions: it is continuous, left invariant under the rational points of the Bessel group, and P(n\cdot g) = \psi_{\mathrm{std}}((\jmath/2)\cdot\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{shift}(Y)\cdotratCoord(n)))\cdot P(g) for every g and every unipotent n with ratCoord(n) - 1 raising grades by at least 2.
The argument in briefLocally P(n\cdot x) = \chi(n)\cdot F_g(x) with F_g continuous (from the normal form, or F = 0 where P vanishes); this gives continuity, and the unipotent law with \chi(n) = \psi_{\mathrm{std}}((\jmath/2)\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{shift}\cdot n)) because shift = c\cdotbesselCharMatrix and \jmath c = -(g\varpi)^{-1}.
