lemma Filling.sum_pairWeight_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S admissible and j\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\}. Then \Sigma_c\Sigma_{c'}\Sigma_{\gamma}\Sigma_{\gamma'} pairWeight(S,j,c,c',\gamma,\gamma')\le2\tau^{2},\tau= tailScale(S)=b^{\operatorname{kf}/4}, where pairWeight(S,j,c,c',\gamma,\gamma') is termWeight(S,j,i,c,(c',\gamma),(c',\gamma'),T^{\circ}) for congruent terms \gamma,\gamma' over (c, \cdot, c') that are both near representatives of level j, with T^{\circ}= commonLink(\gamma,\gamma') and i=k-\dim T^{\circ}, and 0 otherwise (sums over classes and over G(E), in [0,\infty]).
The argument in briefRewrite the sum over congruent near pairs as a sum over tuples (codimension i, label t, class c, subspace T= commonLink, terms q,p); for codimension m bound the p-sum and the q-sum by tsum_invPowWeight_le_of_codim_le (codimensions \le m+\varrho+1 and m), the divisibility hypotheses coming from lattice inclusions given by the links, and the sum over classes and subspaces by sum_latticeDisc_inv_le; against countWeight(m) this gives 2^{-m}\tau^{2}, summing to 2\tau^{2}.
