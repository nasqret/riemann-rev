lemma real_matching

What it saysLet E be a number field, n, m, a, b, p and l finite types, f \colon p \oplus l \simeq b, j \colon m \oplus l \simeq n and e \colon p \oplus a \simeq m bijections, \iota a family of finite types indexed by the infinite places of E, and \gamma the weights with \gamma_\tau = |l| - |\iota \tau | at every infinite place \tau (hypothesis hγ). Then there is a polynomial Q_0 such that for every vector v of the Fock space of posFockVector j there is \kappa \in \mathbb{C} with the following property. Let \ell be a family of continuous linear functionals \ell_g on that Fock space, indexed by g in the archimedean group GInf E (blockSigns j) of signature (|m|, |l|), with \ell_g(\omega(x)w) = \ell_{g\cdot x_1}(w) for all g, all pairs x = (x_1, x_2) and all w (\omega = realPairWeil \gamma 0), and let \ell' be a linear functional on polynomials such that \ell_x(v) = \ell'(Q) whenever the real Weil representation for the signs blockSigns f (realPairWeil \gamma 0 at (1, x)) maps v_{Q_0} to v_Q. Then \kappa \cdot \ell_1(\operatorname{posFockVector} j (stackedDetProd e \eta)) = \ell'(\operatorname{matchedPoly} f j \eta) for every family \eta of complex p\times m matrices \eta \tau k (k \in \iota \tau).
The argument in briefQ_0 is matchedPoly for the special family \eta_0 (rows e(p) of the identity). The constant \kappa for a vector v is found by density of polynomial Fock vectors and closedness: for v = v_P, averaging the torus action over the circle and Fock polarisation give \kappa. Then the identity for all \eta follows by evaluating the polynomials at points, writing each stacked determinant as c\cdot \det(Z\cdot \eta^{\mathsf{T}}) (exists_det_fromCols), and polarising in \eta with the equivariance of \ell and the hypothesis on \ell'.
