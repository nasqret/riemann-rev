abbrevFixesColsOff

Definition 2.2 of the note · unit 143 · New/Rows/PartnerCut.lean, lines 16–17

For a commutative ring R, finite types r and N, an embedding e \colon r \hookrightarrow N and an N\times N matrix z over R, FixesColsOff e z is the proposition z\cdot(1 - I_e\cdot I_e^{\mathsf{T}}) = 1 - I_e\cdot I_e^{\mathsf{T}}, where I_e = inclMatrix R e is the N\times r matrix whose j-th column is the standard basis vector of index e(j). Since 1 - I_e\cdot I_e^{\mathsf{T}} is the diagonal projection onto the coordinates outside e(r), this says: every column of z whose index is not in the image of e is the corresponding column of the identity matrix.

defrowStabilizer

Definition 2.2 of the note · unit 143 · New/Rows/PartnerCut.lean, lines 21–23

For commutative rings R and S, finite types r and N, a type c, a ring homomorphism \varphi \colon R \to S, a c\times N matrix w over S and an embedding e \colon r \hookrightarrow N, rowStabilizer \varphi w e is the subgroup of \mathrm{GL}_N(R) whose elements are the invertible matrices A with FixesColsOff e A (the columns of A outside the image of e are those of the identity) and w\cdot \varphi(A) = w, where \varphi(A) applies \varphi to each entry. Lean builds it as an intersection of a stabiliser and the preimage under \mathrm{GL}_N(\varphi) of the stabiliser of w for right multiplication, with its carrier replaced by (definitionally) this set.

For commutative rings R and S, finite types r and N, a type c, a multiplicative character \chi \colon R \to \mathbb{C}, a ring homomorphism \varphi \colon R \to S, a c\times N matrix w over S, an embedding e \colon r \hookrightarrow N and an N\times r matrix \sigma over R: columnDatum \chi \varphi w e \sigma = \chi(\det \sigma_e) if w\cdot \varphi(\sigma) = w\cdot I_e, and 0 otherwise. lemmacolumnDatum_mul_ite_eq

Lemma 2.3 of the note · unit 143 · New/Rows/PartnerCut.lean, lines 33–43

Let R and S be commutative rings, r and N finite types, a and c types, \chi \colon R \to \mathbb{C} a multiplicative character, \varphi \colon R \to S a ring homomorphism, w a c\times N matrix over S and e \colon r \hookrightarrow N an embedding. Let F be a complex function on the N\times a matrices over R that is invariant under left multiplication by H = rowStabilizer \varphi w e (hF: F(A\cdot y) = F(y) for every A \in H and every y). Then for every N\times a matrix y over R and every N\times N matrix z over R: columnDatum \chi \varphi w e (z_e) \cdot (F(y) if FixesColsOff e z, else 0) = \chi(\det z)\cdot F(z^{-1}\cdot y) if z is the matrix of an element of H, and 0 otherwise. Here z_e is the N\times r matrix of the columns e(r) of z and z^{-1} the inverse matrix.

defHasWeightNormLe

Definition 2.4 of the note · unit 143 · New/Rows/SemiInvariantNorm.lean, line 11

For finite types \Phi and n, a proposition p, a weight \kappa (for each place \tau \in \Phi a function \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} of column multiplicities), a polynomial F and a real number b: HasWeightNormLe p \kappa F b means that there is a complex coordinate vector \xi, indexed by the standard fillings of weight \kappa, with toPoly n \kappa \xi = F (that is, F = \sum_i (G\xi)_i b_i, where b_i are the basis products of minors of weight \kappa and G = weylGram n \kappa is the integer Gram matrix of the reproducing kernel), and such that if p holds then weylNormSq \xi = \operatorname{Re}(\xi *\cdot G\cdot \xi) \le b.

lemmaHasWeightNormLe.prod

Lemma 2.5 of the note · unit 143 · New/Rows/SemiInvariantNorm.lean, lines 13–20

Let \Phi and n be finite types, p a proposition, \iota a type, s a finite subset of \iota, and for each i \in \iota a weight \kappa i, a polynomial F i and a real number b i. If HasWeightNormLe p (\kappa i) (F i) (b i) holds for every i \in s, then HasWeightNormLe p (\sum_{i\in s} \kappa i) (\prod_{i\in s} F i) (\prod_{i\in s} b i) holds: the product of the polynomials is the polynomial of a coordinate vector of the summed weight, whose squared norm is, when p holds, at most the product of the bounds.

For a CM number field E, level places S, finite types r and n and an embedding u \colon r \hookrightarrow n, auxLevel S u is the subgroup of \prod_p \mathrm{GL}_n(k_p) (p over the places of S) of the families g such that: at every chosen place w_i, g_{w_i}\cdot I_u = I_u, i.e. g_{w_i} fixes each basis vector e_{u(j)} (I_u = inclMatrix k_{w_i} u, the n\times r matrix with columns e_{u(j)}); and at every twist place q, g_q lies in rangeParabolic u, i.e. the entries of g_q in the rows u(r) and the columns outside u(r) vanish, so g_q maps the span of the basis vectors with index outside u(r) into itself.

Then there is C such that for every modulus \mathfrak{m}, character c and multiplicative character \chi of O_E/\mathfrak{m} with c tame for (\mathfrak{m}, 0, \chi) (weight 0), there is \gamma with |\gamma |^2 = C / (\operatorname{lValueProd}(\operatorname{places} \text{ of } \mathfrak{m}, c^{-1}\cdot \upsilon^m, m)\cdot \operatorname{lValueProd}(\operatorname{places} \text{ of } \mathfrak{m}, c\cdot \upsilon^{m/2}, m/2)) and, for every point x, \gamma \cdot \sum_h carrierXi m c h\cdot \theta^{S, I_{3m}}_{\upsilon^m, c}[\chi \circ \det, 1](x, h) = carrierForm E m x, where S = signedSpace with signs (+1 \text{ on } \operatorname{Fin} (2m), -1 \text{ on } \operatorname{Fin} m), of signature (2m, m); the outer kernel pairs S with the definite space I_{3m} on the same index set \operatorname{Fin} (2m) \oplus \operatorname{Fin} m (the determinant datum \chi \circ \det needs square matrices), and the outer sum is the class sum over U(I_{3m}) at level K_0, h running over its classes; carrierXi m c h is itself a class sum over U(I_m) (of the kernel of the pair (I_m, I_{3m})). The constant C comes first; \gamma depends on (\mathfrak{m}, c, \chi).
