lemma Filling.amplitudeSum_besselS_one

What it saysThe identity term. Under the standing hypotheses, for filling data S over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit), classes c,c' and tuples i,j of Bessel coordinates: with the full Bessel functions, the (i,j) entry of A_{\mathsf{S}(E)}(c,1,c') (the sum of the amplitudes over the rational points of the Bessel subgroup that are terms over (c,1,c')) is \nu= unipCovolume(S) if c=c' and i=j, and 0 otherwise.
The argument in briefFor c\ne c' there are no terms; for c=c' the change of weight keeps only \gamma=1, and the integral is \nu times the orthonormality of the Bessel functions.
