lemma stdExchange_of_periods

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, a, b, m finite index types; signs s, t, r; X : Space(E, m, r); W_{1} : Space(E, n, s) of even rank (\mathit{hn}: 2 divides |n|); D a base-adapted splitting of X over W_{1} along b; W : Space(E, a, t); weights \mu; signs t' and V' : Space(E, b, t'); D_{1} a base-adapted splitting of W_{1} over W along a line; \sigma ' an identification for standard row data; v, v' rapid real data (\mathit{hv}, \mathit{hv}') for (X, V' \oplus W_{1}) and (X, V'); l a complex function on split tame characters of weight \mu; \rho a real function on U(V' \oplus W_{1})(\mathbb{R}) with \rho \ge 0 (h_{0}) and \rho \ne 0 near 1 (h_{1}). Assume the period condition (hc): for every split tame character \mathfrak{c} of weight \mu, with \gamma = rankTwist(\nu_{\mathfrak{c}}^{-1}, n), and every h there is p such that for all B \in \mathrm{GL}_b and z with \det B at the infinite places equal to z, and all \beta, f, x, g, y: the integral over l' \in [U(W_{1})] of thetaOnRealData(W_{1}, V' \oplus W_{1}, \gamma, \gamma \beta, boxProd(f, namedVector(W_{1}, W_{1}, stdColDatum(\chi_{\mathfrak{c}}, 1), readsAt(\mathfrak{c}))), (x; 0), (g, sum(y, l')))(realPairWeil(i \mapsto |n|/2 - \mu(i), 0)(1, sum(p)\cdot h)(realSlice(D, V' \oplus W_{1}, mat(sum(p)\cdot h, 1)^{-1}\cdot(B; 0), v))) equals \rho(h)\cdot l(\mathfrak{c})/(BC(\nu_{\mathfrak{c}})(z)\cdot(\sqrt{|}z|_{\mathbb{A}})^{|n|}) \cdot thetaOnRealData(W_{1}, V', \gamma, \beta, f, x, (g, y))(realSlice(D, V', B, v')) (slices of v integrate over [U(W_{1})] to \rho \cdot l(\mathfrak{c}) times those of v', up to the Levi factor; here z is read as the idele with infinite components z and finite components 1, BC(\nu_{\mathfrak{c}}) = \mathfrak{c}.\nu.baseChange is the base-change character x \mapsto \nu_{\mathfrak{c}}(x/\overline{x}) of the ideles of E (\nu_{\mathfrak{c}} itself lives on the unitary line U(1)(\mathbb{A})), and |z|_{\mathbb{A}} = ideleAbs(z)); and assume the support or sign condition (hz): either every non-zero real slice realSlice(D, V' \oplus W_{1}, x, v) has \operatorname{Gram} matrix x^{*}\cdotstdForm(t' \oplus s)\cdot x represented by t', or t' = -1 and W_{1} has a positive sign i (s(i) = 1) with 2\cdotnegCount(s) \le |n|. Then StdExchange(X, V' \oplus W_{1}, V', \mu, \sigma ', v, v', \mathfrak{c} \mapsto |l(\mathfrak{c})|^{2}/lValueProdInt(\mathfrak{c}, |n|/2)).
The argument in briefUse the equivariant lift \mathcal{D} of the difference thetaLarge - c\cdotthetaSmall with c = R\kappa \varepsilon \cdot \nu(a)\cdot l(\mathfrak{c}); it kills every standard datum (IsEquivariantLift.apply_stdColDatum_eq_zero) once its coefficients vanish at the two kinds of indices of h_{0}. At the unimodular index J_{V'}, unfold the big coefficient in stages over U(V') \times U(W_{1}) (R from exists_integral_stages_eq_mul), insert the explicit theta terms and the period condition \mathit{hc}; at non-represented indices both coefficients vanish (\mathit{hz}, centreCoeff_eq_zero_of_not_negative).
