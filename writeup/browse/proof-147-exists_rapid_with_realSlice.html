lemma exists_rapid_with_realSlice

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, a, b, m finite index types; s, r signs; X : Space(E, m, r), W_{1} : Space(E, n, s), D a base-adapted splitting of X over W_{1} along b; t' signs and V' : Space(E, a, t'). Let \tau be a real function (on the real coordinates of the slices) which is smooth (c: C^{\infty}), compactly supported (k), non-negative (h) and non-zero at some point y (o), and let v be a rapid real datum (hv). Then there are \kappa \in \mathbb{C} and a rapid real datum \Lambda for (X, V') such that the slice of \Lambda at 0 for the opposite splitting is v, realSlice(D.symm, V', 0, \Lambda) = v, and at every x the slice of \Lambda in the mixed model of D is (\tau(x)\cdot \kappa)\cdotrealWeil(1, v).
The argument in briefTake \kappa =(\int \tau)^{-1} and the section with slices \tau(x)\kappa \cdotrealWeil(1, v) (exists_section); its slice at 0 for the opposite splitting is the integral of the slices, which is (\int \tau)\kappa \cdot v = v, checked on zero terms.
