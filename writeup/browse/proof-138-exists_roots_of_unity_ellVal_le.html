lemma exists_roots_of_unity_ellVal_le

What it saysFor a prime \ell, a finite type \kappa , \delta > 0 (hδ), natural numbers m, L, a, q and bounds B : \kappa \to \mathbb{R}\ge 0: for all sufficiently large N, for all sufficiently large coefficient rings O: for all finite types \sigma (with |\sigma | \le q) and T, monic polynomials D_n \in O[X] (n \in T) of total degree \sum \deg D_n \le L, multivariate polynomials F_{k,d} over O in the variables \sigma (k \in \kappa , 1 \le d \le m) and points u_{k,d} \in O^\sigma with \ell dividing (u_{k,d,j} - 1)^a for every coordinate j and v_\ell (F_{k,d}(u_{k,d})) \le B_k: there is \zeta \in O^\sigma with v_\ell (F_{k,d}(\zeta ^d)) \le \delta + B_k for all (k, d) (\zeta ^d taken coordinatewise), and, for every j, \zeta _j^{\ell ^N} = 1 and v_\ell (D_n(\zeta _j)) \le \delta for every n (v_\ell = ellVal).
The argument in briefChoose r with \lVert \ell \rVert \vee \ell ^{-\delta } < r^{q+L+\ell +\text\{ ma \}} < 1. Then build \zeta coordinate by coordinate: replacing the j-th coordinate of u by a root of unity z keeps every F_{k,d} large (Gauss norm argument, exists_root_of_unity_eval_large applied to one-variable restrictions) and makes \prod D_n(z) large; induction over the coordinates gives \zeta.
