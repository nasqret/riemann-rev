Structured proof · Unit 143

lemma exists_det_fromCols

What it saysFor finite types a, L and N, a bijection e \colon L \simeq a \oplus N and a complex L\times N matrix Z, there are a complex a\times L matrix Y and a complex number \gamma such that, for every complex L\times a matrix X, \det([X | Z]_e) = \gamma \cdot \det((Y\cdot X)^{\mathsf{T}}), and |\gamma |^2\cdot |\det(Y\cdot Y^{\mathsf{H}})| \le |\det(Z^{\mathsf{H}}\cdot Z)|. Here [X | Z]_e is the L\times L matrix whose columns are those of X and Z, in the order given by e.
The argument in briefClear the columns of Z^{\mathsf{H}} by an invertible V (Hermite form over \mathbb{C}); then multiplicativity of determinants for [U_1 | W]^{\mathsf{H}}\cdot [X | Z] gives \det [X | Z] = \gamma \cdot \det(U_1^{\mathsf{H}}X) with Y = U_1^{\mathsf{H}}, and the case W = Z, X = U_1 gives the bound.
