lemma Size.adjoint_apply_mem_ofSize

What it saysLet A be a commutative ring with involution (CommRing A, StarRing A ) , \mathfrak{s} a shape and T an \mathfrak{s}-form over A; V is the free A-module on the basis e_{0} , \ldots , e_{\varrho - 1} , ( x_{i} )_{i \in \kappa} , z , e_{\varrho - 1}^{*} , \ldots , e_{0}^{*} with Gram matrix J_{T} = splitGram( T ) and U ( J_{T} ) its unitary group; assume J_{T} is hermitian and invertible, and let S be a size for T. Let X , Y be square matrices indexed by the basis indices with J_{T} \cdot X = Y^{H} \cdot J_{T} (hypothesis e), and let a , b be basis indices. If every row Y_{n} with J_{nb} \ne 0 lies in vecsOfSize( S , i ) (hypothesis h), then X_{ab} \in B_{i + 4}^{S}.
The argument in briefX = J_{T}^{- 1} ( Y^{H} J_{T} ); count sizes in the two products.
