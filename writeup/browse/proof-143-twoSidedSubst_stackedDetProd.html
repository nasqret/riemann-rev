lemma twoSidedSubst_stackedDetProd

What it saysLet \Phi, a, N and L be finite types, e \colon a \oplus N \simeq L a bijection, \iota a family of finite types indexed by \Phi, M \tau k a complex a\times L matrix for each \tau \in \Phi and k \in \iota \tau, c a family of complex N\times N and g a family of complex L\times L matrices indexed by \Phi. Then twoSidedSubst c g (x_\tau \mapsto c_\tau^{\mathsf{T}} x_\tau g_\tau^{\mathsf{T}}) maps stackedDetProd e (\tau, k \mapsto M \tau k\cdot(g \tau)^{\mathsf{T}}) to (\prod_\tau(\det c_\tau \cdot \det g_\tau)^{|\iota \tau |})\cdot \operatorname{stackedDetProd} e M.
The argument in briefPush the substitution through the products; for each factor, the substituted stacked determinant is the determinant of a product of the stacked matrix [M ; x_\tau] with block matrices built from c_\tau and g_\tau, which multiplicativity of \det turns into \det c_\tau \cdot \det g_\tau times the original.
