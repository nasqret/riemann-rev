lemma Filling.enorm_amplitudeSum_le_classSize

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S admissible, and use the cut Bessel functions. Let T be stable (IsStable(S,T)) and j\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\} with HasLevel(S,j,\gamma) for all \gamma\in T. Then for all classes c,c' and s\in\mathsf{G}: \lVert\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(S,T,c,s,c')\rVert\le\Sigma\nu\cdotclassSize(S,j,\mathtt{classGauge}(S,j,c,c',\gamma)), the sum over the \gamma\in T\cap termsOver(S,c,s,c') such that Agrees(S,s) and IsNearRep(S,c,c',\gamma); in particular the block is 0 unless s agrees (Agrees(S,s): integral u,u'\in\mathsf{U} with \overline{u}s=s\overline{u}' have the same value of unipotentChar). \nu= unipCovolume(S).
The argument in briefIf s does not agree the block is 0. Otherwise split T at K=\{N^{f}\cdot\mathtt{minorNorm}<\mathtt{termDiscNorm}\}: on T\cap K (\mathsf{g}\ne0, level \varrho+1) use enorm_amplitudeSum_le_classSize_top; on T\setminus K bound the block by the sum of absolute amplitudes over the double cosets of near representatives and use tsum_amplitudeAbs_class_le.
