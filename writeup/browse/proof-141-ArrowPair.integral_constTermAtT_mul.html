lemma ArrowPair.integral_constTermAtT_mul

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data, gradings Y of V (X, s), Y' of V' (X', s'), Y'' of V'' (X_{0}, s'') of norm N, and D an arrow pair for Y \to Y' \to Y''. There is z \in \mathbb{C} such that for all characters a, b of U(1)(\mathbb{A}), data f, f', points k \in U(X_{0})(\mathbb{A}) and t, and every P generic for Y (Grading.IsGeneric): with F(g, r) = P(g\cdot t)\cdotconstTermAtT_{Y,Y'}(rankTwist a, f)(g, r)\cdotconstTermAtT_{Y',Y''}(rankTwist b, f')(r, inclX_{Y''} k), \int_{[B']} \int_{[B]} F(g, r) dg dr = \int_{[U(X)]} P(\mathrm{inclX}_Y(g)\cdot t)\cdot(z\cdot\int_{[U(X')]} \theta(tripleWeil(a, b)(g, r, \mathrm{embX}_D k)(zeroTerm_{\mathrm{splitting} D}(homHeisOp(stackedT)(f \boxtimes f')))) dr) dg, where [B], [B'] are the quotients of the Bessel groups of Y, Y' by their rational points: the two unfolding steps give a double theta integral of the zero term of the translated arrow product.
The argument in briefUnfold [B] for Y (integral_besselMeasure_eq_of_mean), swap the integrals, unfold [B'] for Y'; the means over the unipotent radicals turn the product of the two constant terms into the zero term of the arrow product (the identity hz), and the zero term intertwines the triple Weil action.
