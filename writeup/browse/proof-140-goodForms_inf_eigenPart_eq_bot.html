lemma Data.goodForms_inf_eigenPart_eq_bot

What it saysLet D, s, s', V, V' be as in trace_heckeOnGood_eq: \operatorname{paramW}(D, v, .) has gap h(D) for s and for s' at every infinite place v, and \operatorname{HasRigidLabels}(D, V) and \operatorname{HasRigidLabels}(D, V') hold. Let q be an eigensystem (a function from pairs (w, i), w a finite place of E(D) and i a natural number, to C). If \operatorname{goodForms}(D, V') meets \operatorname{eigenPart}(D, V', q) only in 0, then \operatorname{goodForms}(D, V) meets \operatorname{eigenPart}(D, V, q) only in 0.
The argument in briefTake \pi projecting to the q-part and killing the other eigenparts of V'. On \operatorname{goodForms}(V') \pi is 0 (its image lies in the q-part of the good forms of V', which is 0), so 1 - \pi has determinant 1 there, hence (equal determinants) nonzero determinant on \operatorname{goodForms}(V); but 1 - \pi kills the q-part of \operatorname{goodForms}(V).
