lemma tilting

What it saysTilting. For a discrete valuation ring O, a finite type m, an O-module B, an ideal I with O/I Artinian, O-linear maps \Phi _0, \Phi _1 : B \to (O/I)^m and a submodule Y \subseteq B: there is a list T of residues of O with |T| \le |m| such that for every s \in O whose residue is not in T, length(Y \cap \ker (s\cdot \Phi _0 - \Phi _1)) \le length(Y \cap \ker \Phi _0 \cap \Phi _1^{-1}(\Phi _0(Y))).
The argument in briefLet Z be the sum over s of Y \cap \ker (s\Phi _0 - \Phi _1); \Phi _1 maps Z into \Phi _0(Z)'s preimage, so \Phi _1 = \psi \circ \Phi _0 on Z for an endomorphism \psi of a free module (projectivity); the excluded residues are the roots of the characteristic polynomial of \psi, and for other s, s\Phi _0 - \Phi _1 = (s - \psi )\Phi _0 with s - \psi invertible.
