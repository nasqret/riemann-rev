lemma avatar_conjGal_mul

What it saysLet D be admissible data, \ell a prime with \iota \ell fixed, a a character of U(1)(\mathbb{A}_E) trivial on E with weight e and tame part \psi (h : a.HasWeight e \psi), k, j \in \mathbb{Z}, and \mu the \ell-adic avatar of the algebraic Hecke character h.toAlgebraic k j (the character bc(a)\cdot \upsilon ^{-k}\cdot |\cdot |^{j-k/2}). Then for every g \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E): \mu (g^c)\cdot \mu (g) = \operatorname{cyc}(g)^{2j-k}, with g^c = conjGal g = cgc and \operatorname{cyc} the \ell-adic cyclotomic character (cycCharIn).
The argument in briefBoth sides are continuous characters of \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E); at most geometric Frobenii they agree by the values of the avatar at Frobenius elements, so they agree on a dense set and hence everywhere.
