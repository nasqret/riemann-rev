lemma TwistingData.sum_deepIndicator

What it saysLet N be a natural number, W a commutative local ring, K a field and T twisting data over them (T : TwistingData N W K, with prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to K for s \in \mathbb{Z}/N, parity isEven and w \in K), N \ne 0, k \in \mathbb{N}, r \in \mathbb{N} and an index b \in Fin k: \sum_{s\in \mathbb{Z}/N} deepIndicator T r (b, s) = N/2 (natural division): the variable (b, s) is deep for exactly half of the letters, as the parity alternates.
The argument in briefdeepIndicator(b, s) + deepIndicator(b, s - 1) = 1 since parities alternate; summing over s gives 2\cdot \sum = N.
