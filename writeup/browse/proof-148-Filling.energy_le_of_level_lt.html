lemma Filling.energy_le_of_level_lt

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified and S admissible; cut Bessel functions. Let 0\le C,\Omega\subseteq\mathsf{G},T stable, X\ne0 with FixesNormAtLeast(S,s,X) for all s\in\Omega,\mathsf{d}_1\le C, and \tau^{20}\mathsf{d}_1\le C if some \gamma\in T over some (c,s,c') with s\in\Omega is in normal form of level 0. Let n\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\} be such that every \gamma\in T with CharsAgree(S,\gamma) has a level j<n. Then \operatorname{energy}(\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(S,T),\Omega)\le C/b^{30}/4^{\varrho+1-n}.
The argument in briefInduction on n. For n=0 no term satisfies CharsAgree (it would have a level <0), so all blocks vanish. For n+1, split T into the terms of level n and the rest; the first part is bounded by energy_le_of_level_pairs with R=\emptyset (link energy 0), the second by the induction hypothesis, and energy_add_le with \varepsilon=1 combines them.
