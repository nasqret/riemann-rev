lemma Filling.lintegral_classCutoff_eq_unipCovolume

What it saysUnder the standing hypotheses, for a stably unramified CM field E, a shape \mathfrak{s}, filling data S, a class c and an archimedean point y=(y_v)_v: \int^{-}\beta_c((u_v\cdot y_v)_v)d(\prod_v\mathtt{besselHaar})(u)=\nu, the integral over u in the product over the infinite places of the unipotent groups \mathsf{U} of G_1 with the product Haar measure, \nu= unipCovolume(S); in particular it does not depend on c or y.
The argument in briefGive \prod_v\mathsf{U} graded coordinates (free entries of the rows e_a, and Im of the entries (e_a,e_a^{*})); \mathsf{U}_c(g) acts by translations by lattices in each grade, whose fundamental domains have explicit volumes; a box of fundamental domains is a fundamental domain for \mathsf{U}_c(g), and unfolding the cutoff sum (which is 1) gives the volume of the box, which equals \nu.
