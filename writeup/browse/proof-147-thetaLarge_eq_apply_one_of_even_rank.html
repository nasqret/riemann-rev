lemma thetaLarge_eq_apply_one_of_even_rank

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; a, m finite index types, t, r signs, X : Space(E, m, r) of even rank (\mathit{hn}: 2 divides |m|), W : Space(E, a, t), D_{1} a base-adapted splitting of X over W along an isotropic line (IsBaseAdapted D₁), \mathfrak{c} a split tame character of weight \mu and v a rapid real datum (hv). Then the theta integral for (X, X) with the characters of \mathfrak{c} and the finite datum \chi_{\mathfrak{c}}^{-1}\circ \det is constant: for every g, thetaLarge(X, X, \mathfrak{c}, v, stdColDatum(\chi_{\mathfrak{c}}, 1))(g) = thetaLarge(X, X, \mathfrak{c}, v, stdColDatum(\chi_{\mathfrak{c}}, 1))(1).
The argument in briefThe function is \mathcal{D}\Psi for the equivariant lift of exists_isEquivariantLift with c = 0; it is automorphic, of level supported at S and K_f off S, and its constant term along the line is given by the tower property; the constancy criterion IsAutFunction.apply_eq_apply_one (strong approximation for the special unitary group with the torus hypothesis) makes it constant.
