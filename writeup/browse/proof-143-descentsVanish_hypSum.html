lemma descentsVanish_hypSum

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, r (nonempty), n and N finite types, O : AuxPair E r n, l_0 a parameter with O.FitsParams l_0 (hO), \psi_q \ne 1 at every twist place q (hQ), 2|r| + 2|N| + 3 < |n| (hn), w a split finite place (hw) outside the places of O and their conjugates (hS), and \Xi \in O.FormV killed by theta descent (h0: O.θ \Xi = 0), by the reflection sums (hM: for every chosen index i and every family v of rows with v_i \ne 0, \otimes_i \operatorname{reflectionSum}(\psi, v_i) acts on \Xi by 0) and by the Hecke polynomial at the test value (hH: O.heckePolyOnForms hw hS (O.testValue w) \Xi = 0). Then for every coordinate vector u of weight O.κ, all descents of the conjugate matrix coefficient \operatorname{conj}(\operatorname{algFormCoeff} 1 (gramForm u) \Xi) vanish: DescentsVanish (hypSum N O.W) (stdSpace E n) (O.a\cdot \upsilon^{|N|}) S, where S is the complement of the conjugate closure of the places of O (the chosen and the twist places): for every datum f with \operatorname{projOn}(S)(f) = f, that is, f is the vacuum outside the places of O and their conjugates (fixed by the mean over the standard lattice at every finite place not among them: DescentsVanishDef.lean 9–13 with S \cup \overline{S} = S), the theta lift g \mapsto \int \theta(\omega_{O.a\cdot \upsilon^{|N|}, 1}(g, h)f)\cdot Y(h) dh of that coefficient Y from U(I_n) to the unitary group of W \oplus \mathbb{H}^N (hypSum: W plus |N| hyperbolic planes) is 0.
The argument in briefAdding hyperbolic planes keeps the vanishing of descents (descentsVanish_add_planes) once the descents to W itself vanish, which follows from the reflection sums (descentsVanish_of_reflectionSum) and from \theta \Xi = 0 through the edge property of the pair.
