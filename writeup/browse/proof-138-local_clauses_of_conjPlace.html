lemma local_clauses_of_conjPlace

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, a finite type r, j, a representation R : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \operatorname{GL}_r(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell ) and a place A of \Omega: if R is conjugate self-dual for j (IsConjSelfDual j R) and of finite type at the conjugate place conjPlace A (IsFiniteTypeAt R (conjPlace A)), then (1) R is of finite type at A; (2) for every weight \kappa, if R has the jumps jumps \kappa j at conjPlace A, it has the jumps jumps \kappa j at A; and (3) for every s in the inertia group of conjPlace A and every polynomial Q, if Q kills s on R at conjPlace A (PolyKillsAt R (conjPlace A) s Q), then Q kills conjGal(s^{-1}) = (s^{-1})^c on R at A.
The argument in briefThe dual of R twisted by \operatorname{cyc}^{1-j} has the traces of R \circ \operatorname{conj}, hence is conjugate to it (Brauer–Nesbitt); the local clauses pass along this conjugation from the conjugate place to A (local_clauses_of_retract), and jumps pass through the duality pairing with a shift.
