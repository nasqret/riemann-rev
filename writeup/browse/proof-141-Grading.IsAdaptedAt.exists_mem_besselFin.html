lemma Grading.IsAdaptedAt.exists_mem_besselFin

What it saysLet E be a stably unramified field with a CM type, V a hermitian space with a grading Y (X plus a string of length s, norm N), x, x' \in G_f(\iota) with adapted bases b at x (string diagonal \beta, h) and b' at x' (\beta', h'), such that \beta_i and \beta'_{i} have the same valuation at every finite place for every string index i of the first half (i \le s - 1 - i) (l). Then there is k \in K_{0} = \operatorname{Kf}(E, \iota) with x'\cdot k\cdot x^{-1} \in B_f, the finite Bessel group of Y (Grading.besselFin).
The argument in briefMake the string diagonals of the two adapted bases equal on the first half; then Witt's theorem over \hat{O} moves the first-half string columns of one lattice basis onto those of the other, and the resulting x'kx^{-1} fixes the string vectors of grade \ge 0 up to higher grades.
