defFilling.energy

Definition 2.3 of the note · unit 148 · New/Filling/SchurTest.lean, line 20

Definition. For a normed additive group V, a family A : Classes(S)\times GRes(S)\times Classes(S)\to V (written A(c,s,c'), with c,c' double-coset classes of S and s in the finite group \mathsf{G}= GRes(S)=\prod_{i\in\{0,1\}}\prod_{w\text{ chosen above }q_i} GL_r(\mathbb{F}_w)) and a set \Omega\subseteq\mathsf{G}, the energy is \operatorname{energy}(A,\Omega)=\Sigma_c\Sigma_{c'}\Sigma_{s\in\Omega}\lVert A(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu^{2}, where \nu= unipCovolume(S) is the covolume of \mathsf{U}_c(g) in \mathsf{U}_{\infty}.
