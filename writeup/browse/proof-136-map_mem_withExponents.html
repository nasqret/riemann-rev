lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.Hom.map_mem_withExponents

What it saysLet F : L \to L' be an equivariant map of lattice data with U'_{j} \subseteq U_j for every letter j (e). For a letter i, an exponent function c and x \in W(C^\flat)\otimes L: if x \in withExponents(L, i, c) then F.wittMap(x) \in withExponents(L', i, c).
The argument in briefThe three conditions are transported by 1 \otimes F: the Kisin module, the letter part, and the valuation conditions through forms composed with F.
