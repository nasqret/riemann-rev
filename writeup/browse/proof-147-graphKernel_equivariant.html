lemma graphKernel_equivariant

What it saysFor a finite commutative ring R, finite a, b, n, \chi, e : b \sqcup a \simeq n, g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(R), f \in \mathrm{GL}_a(R), q \in \mathrm{GL}_b(R) and matrices x, y: graphKernel(\chi, e, x, y) = \chi(\det g)/\chi(\det f)/\chi(\det q)\cdotgraphKernel(\chi, e, f^{-1}\cdot x\cdot g, g^{-1}\cdot y\cdot q).
The argument in briefWithout loss of generality the kernel is non-zero at (x, y); then (x, y) are the blocks of some k, and (f^{-1}\mathit{xg}, g^{-1}\mathit{yq}) are the blocks of g^{-1}\cdot k\cdot \operatorname{diag}(q, f).
