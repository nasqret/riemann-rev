What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data, l a prime with an l-adic place (PadicPlace l), p, q, q_2, q_3 pairs with q flat for \operatorname{Fin} (2z_p - 20) (H), q_2 and q_3 flat for \operatorname{Fin} 4 (H_2, H_3), p of rank one for a lift index j (hE), \vartheta a twist with l coprime to N(Q_\vartheta) (hl), x_0 a matrix modulo the level, \alpha an exponent and b a character. Write X = lValueData hE.1 \vartheta and u = the trivial twist. If \sum_i \alpha(\tau, i, ()) = \operatorname{jetDeg}_X(\tau) for every \tau and b\cdot(b of lValueData H_2.2 \vartheta)\cdot(b of lValueData H_3.2 u)\cdot(b of lValueData H.2 u) = X.auxPair.b, then there is a form \Xi on the space V of X such that for every point g: \langle \Xi, X.kernelForm g\rangle \cdot(\operatorname{stdClassSum} X.chosenLevel 1)^3\cdot \kappa_0^{2z_p-20+8} (\kappa_0 = P.vacuumScale D.s) = (\prod_\tau \operatorname{jetDeg}(\tau)!/\prod_k \alpha_k!)\cdot \operatorname{intAlgPart}(q, u)\cdot \operatorname{intAlgPart}(q_3, u)\cdot \operatorname{intAlgPart}(q_2, \vartheta)\cdot \theta^{I_1, I_{12}}_{1, b}[\mathbb{1}_{x_0}, X^\alpha]((1, g), (1, 1)) (modKernel modulo X.levelModulus 1 false), and \Xi is a test form at l (X.auxPair.IsTestForm l \Xi: integral at every l-adic place and tested).
