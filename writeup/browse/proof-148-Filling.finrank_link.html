lemma Filling.finrank_link

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit) and \gamma\in G(S,E): \operatorname{dim}_{E}\operatorname{link}(\gamma)=k+1 (k=|\kappa|): the link is Lagrangian in a space of dimension 2(k+1).
The argument in briefRank–nullity for p\mapsto v^{H}\cdot\operatorname{diag}(T,-T)\cdot p,v a basis of the link: the map is onto and its kernel is the link.
