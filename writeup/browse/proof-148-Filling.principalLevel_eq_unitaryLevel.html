lemma Filling.principalLevel_eq_unitaryLevel

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit): K(g)= principalLevel(S) equals unitaryLevel(\hat{O},g,J), the k\in GFin(S)\cap GL(\hat{O}) with g^{-1}(k-1) integral.
The argument in briefAn integral k reduces to 1 modulo all chosen w iff every entry of k-1 and of its conjugate vanishes there, iff g divides k-1.
