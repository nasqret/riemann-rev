lemma integral_theta_eq_lift₂

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data, X, X', X_{0} finite index types, \kappa, e, e', e'' weights, O a type, a, b : O \to characters of U(1)(\mathbb{A}), and for every o linear maps k(o), k'(o) that are theta lifts X \to X' for rankTwist(a(o)) (hk) and X' \to X_{0} for rankTwist(b(o)) (hk') in the sense of Space.IsThetaLift. Let u be a real datum and t a functional on weight-(\kappa, e) vectors, and put L(o)(y) = the composite k'(o)(\Phi') \circ k(o)(\Phi) for y = \Phi \otimes \Phi' (extended linearly). Then there is a functional t'' such that for all o, all y in \mathcal{W}_f(\operatorname{Hom}(X, X')) \otimes \mathcal{W}_f(\operatorname{Hom}(X', X_{0})), all forms \varphi on X and all points g: \int_{[U(X)]} algFormCoeff(1, t, \varphi)(x)\cdot\int_{[U(X')]} \theta(tripleWeil(a(o), b(o))(x, r, g)(u \otimes arrowTensor(y))) dr dx = algFormCoeff(1, t'', L(o)(y)(\varphi))(g): integrating the coefficient t of \varphi against the theta kernel of u \otimes arrowTensor(y) over [U(X)] \times [U(X')] gives the coefficient t'' of the double lift.
The argument in briefReduce by continuity and density to polynomial real data v_P, split P into a sum of products of polynomials in the two blocks of variables, apply the theta-lift property twice (hk then hk'), and match the integrals with \theta(f \boxtimes f') = z\cdot\theta(f)\theta(f').
