lemma coeff_subst_tangent

What it saysFor a prime p, a power series Z over \mathbb{C}_p with X^{m+1} \mid Z - X, every k \le m + 1 and every power series g over \mathbb{C}_p: the k-th coefficient of g(Z) is g_k + g_1\cdot(Z - X)_k (first-order Taylor formula for a substitution tangent to the identity).
The argument in briefWrite Z = X + X^{m+1}W and induct on k, writing g = X\cdot \widetilde{g} + g(0).
