Unit 138The choice of the twists: Galois representations of lifts, Selmer groups made short by one anticyclotomic twist, and congruence bounds by Ribet's method

note and structured proofs · 205 declarations · 96 structured proofs

For admissible data D and every prime \ell with a fixed isomorphism \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell, the unit chooses weight-zero anticyclotomic twists \vartheta for which the congruence bounds of a fit family hold (exists_isFitFamily): the \ell-adic Galois representations attached to the forms of the auxiliary pairs are read as columns and rows (conjugate self-dual, with prescribed Hodge–Tate jumps, labelled by the tame characters at the chosen places), one anticyclotomic twist with tied auxiliary primes makes their Selmer groups short, and Ribet's method with congruent blocks gives a Hecke element whose valuation at the lift is within the budget, the crystalline losses above \ell being affordable. Chunk 138-a holds the definitions (columns, rows, apt label tuples, the Selmer length bound, families of interpolated L-values, pencils of tied primes, balanced representations, even classes, blocks and the cocycles that obey relations), the existence of anticyclotomic characters at new primes, the Galois representations of labelled columns, and the crystalline defect at one place above \ell.

Unit 137A Taylor–Wiles prime at a time: the deformed trace at one auxiliary place clears the denominator

note and structured proofs · 10 declarations · 6 structured proofs

At one auxiliary (Taylor–Wiles) place w_j with q_j \equiv 1 \bmod \ell^\nu, the stabiliser of a line in \mathrm{GL}_n(k_{w_j}) carries a character to \mathbb{Z}/\ell^\nu whose transfer factors through the determinant; an abstract first-order lemma about weighted sums over a finite group then produces an operator F at w_j that ties the trace of tame inertia to that of Frobenius on forms, modulo \ell^\nu (exists_tie). Taking one such tie at each auxiliary place, the top coefficient of Ribet's element (a polynomial in one variable per place) clears the denominator of the projection to the eigensystems \mathcal{X} up to the factor d \cdot c (exists_diagOp_mem_ellIntegralForms).

Unit 136Lifting crystalline extension classes in large weight: Kisin modules with exponents, cokernel killers and cup products that vanish up to a power of p

note and structured proofs · 141 declarations · 69 structured proofs

For crystalline lattices over a Kummer chart of p-adic Hodge theory, the unit measures, at each Frobenius translate \varphi^a(E) of the Eisenstein element, the exponents of elements of Kisin modules (withExponents), deepens them by Galois operators (g - 1)/(z\tau) at an explicit cost in p, and finds annihilators p^\kappa E^s of the cokernels of Kisin modules of crystalline extensions (KillsCokernel). A walk along Frobenius orbits then makes the cup product of two crystalline classes a coboundary after multiplication by an explicit power of p independent of the rank (ScaledCupBounds), for weights in a low and a far window and through a long-gap lemma (chunk a); chunk b (FewLowJumps, SparseThickLetters, ExponentTheorems) turns this into the length bound for classes with few low weights and the exponent for tame types used in the Selmer bound.
