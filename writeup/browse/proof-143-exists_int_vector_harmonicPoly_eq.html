lemma exists_int_vector_harmonicPoly_eq

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data over P, n a finite type, X an L-value datum (LValueData D.E n), r \in \mathbb{N}, e \colon \operatorname{Fin} r \hookrightarrow n, and \alpha an exponent (finitely supported on triples (\tau, i, ())) with \sum_i \alpha(\tau, i, ()) = X.jetDeg \tau for every infinite place \tau (hα). Then there is an integer vector v (indexed by the standard fillings) such that X.\operatorname{auxPair}.\operatorname{harmonicPoly}(\operatorname{conj} v) = (\prod_\tau (X.jetDeg \tau)!/\prod_k (\alpha_k)!)\cdot(the monomial X^\alpha with its rows renamed by e), and for every field R of characteristic 0, every family g of matrices in \mathrm{GL}_n(R) whose columns e(a) are those of the identity (g_i(b, e(a)) = 1 \text{ if } b = e(a), else 0), and w = v cast to R: \operatorname{weylRep}(X.\operatorname{auxPair}.\operatorname{detExp})(g)\cdot w = w.
The argument in briefv is the vector of coefficients at the monomial \delta of the integral basis polynomials; Fock duality identifies its harmonic polynomial, and the invariance follows because the monomial is fixed by the row substitution.
