lemma exchange_theorem

What it saysExchange theorem. Let E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; n, a, b finite index types with signs s, t, t'; W : Space(E, n, s), V : Space(E, a, t), V' : Space(E, b, t'); \sigma : b \sqcup a \simeq n (so \mathit{rk} W = \mathit{rk} V + \mathit{rk} V'); suppose negCount(s) = negCount(t) + negCount(t') (hs), |a| = |b| + 2e for a natural number e (he), 2\cdotnegCount(s) + 2 \le |n| (hn); let \mu be weights with |n| \le 2\mu(v) + 2\cdotnegCount(s) at every infinite place v (h\mu), and v a rapid real datum (hv). There are a real C and a rapid real datum x such that for every modulus \mathfrak{m}, character \nu of U(1)(\mathbb{A}), and multiplicative character \chi of O_E/\mathfrak{m} with IsTameFor(\mathfrak{m}, \nu, \mu, \chi) (\mathfrak{m} \ne (1); \nu of weight \mu with tame part non-trivial exactly at the places of \mathfrak{m}; \chi the product of its local tame parts), writing \gamma = rankTwist(\nu^{-1}, n) = \nu^{-1}\upsilon^{\lfloor |n|/2\rfloor}: there is c \ne 0 with |c|^{2} = C\cdotlValueProd(places(\mathfrak{m}), \nu, e) = C\cdot(\prod_{w\in S} Nw)^{e^{2}}\cdot \prod_{j=1}^{e} |L^{S\cup \overline{S}}(j, BC \nu)|^{2}, S = places(\mathfrak{m}), where L^{S\cup \overline{S}}(\cdot, BC \nu) = \nu.L(S, \cdot) is the Hecke L-function of the base change x \mapsto \nu(x/\overline{x}) without the Euler factors at S and at the conjugate places (both products are 1 when e = 0), such that for all partners \Phi, \Psi for \chi over O_E/\mathfrak{m} with respect to \sigma (IsPartner ⊤ σ χ Φ Ψ) and every point g: thetaIntegral(W, V, \gamma, 1, modDatum(W, V, \mathfrak{m}, \Phi, x))(g) = c\cdot \int_{[U(V')]} pairTheta(V', W, \gamma, \gamma, modDatum(V', W, \mathfrak{m}, \Psi, v), (h, g)) \mathit{dh}. C and x are chosen once, before the triple (\mathfrak{m}, \nu, \chi); c depends on the triple only, and serves all partners and all g; only |c|^{2} is specified. Here |n| = 2|b| + 2e.
The argument in briefThe equal-rank constant (exists_equalRankThetaRatio_value) and the exchange with implicit constant (exchange_of_partners) give the constant C_{0}/C; for tame (\mathfrak{m}, \nu, \chi), make the split tame character \mathfrak{c} with places those of \mathfrak{m}, read the identity for it, and transport partners and data from O_E/\mathfrak{m} to the local product at S by pulling them back along the surjective ring map \pi : O_S \to O_E/\mathfrak{m} through which the reduction factors (entry by entry on integral matrices, 0 elsewhere).
