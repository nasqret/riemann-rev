lemma Data.occurringLabels_subsingleton

What it saysLet D be admissible data over P, s signs on RowIndex(D), V a hermitian space over E(D) with signs s, n a nonempty finite type, v : RowIndex(D) \to n an embedding, and L a linear map from principalForms(D, V) to the algebraic forms LevelForm(S, J, chi', kappa, nu) on I_n (S a system of places, J a level, \chi' a level character, \kappa a weight and \nu a determinant exponent). Let p be an eigensystem, F a family indexed by the finite places, x a chosen place of D, m a natural number, b an assignment of blocks, \chi = (\chi_l) for l < m characters of the residue field k_x and \psi a character of k_x. Here F assigns to every finite place w of E(D) a function F(w) : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C} (one scalar F(w)(X) for every complex number X, not a list of eigenvalues). Assume: (hL) L is injective; (hF) for every y in eigenPart(D, V, p) and every finite place w split off the level, IsSphericalWith(rightRegular, w, F(w), smooth form of L(y)): the smooth form of L(y) is fixed by the part of K_0 above w and \operatorname{conj}(w), and for every complex number X the Hecke polynomial operator heckePolyOp(w, X) = \sum_{i=0}^{|n|} (-X)^i q_w^{\binom{i}{2}}\, T_{w,i} acts on it by the scalar F(w)(X); (hk) for every y in principalForms(D, V), the smooth form of L(y) has parahoric type at x (HasParahoricType) with embedding v, blocks b, characters \psi and \chi, and the function g \mapsto L(principalRep(D, V)(g at x)(y)) of g in \mathrm{GL}_{\text{RowIndex}(D)}(k_x), the general linear group of the residue field at x; (hchi) \chi is injective; (hpsi) \psi^{M_\omega} = 1; (hM) \chi_l^{M_\omega} = 1 for every l, where M_\omega = Mω(P, s(D)). Then occurringLabels(D, V, p, x) has at most one element. (No existence is asserted: a label occurs only through some y in eigenPart(D, V, p) that its idempotent does not kill, so if eigenPart(D, V, p) = 0 no label occurs.)
The argument in briefFix l = 2 and an isomorphism \mathbb{C} = algebraic closure of \mathbb{Q}_2. The scalars of the Hecke operators at w are chosen from F(w) alone, so one family Q of Hecke polynomials serves every form and every label. For an occurring label c with a form y of the p-part not killed by e_c at x, the twisted label c' = c.scaleRoots(x_psi) is primitive, L(y) has the product label c', \chi at x (Frobenius reciprocity), L(y) is an eigenform for Q, so a Galois representation attached to Q exists, and every representation attached to Q is labelled at x by c' \prod (X - x_{\chi_l})^{t_l} (Attached.isLabelledAt, the authors' local-global compatibility at tame level). For two occurring labels c_1, c_2, the representation r attached to Q found for c_1 is therefore labelled by both (no two independently chosen representations need to be compared). If c_1' \ne c_2', at a tame inertia element s of exponent 1 every eigenvalue of r(s) is a common root of the two labelling polynomials; distinct irreducible labels have no common root, so each eigenvalue comes from some x_{\chi_l} and is an M_\omega-th root of unity; then \det r(s), which is the product of the images of the roots of c_1' times M_\omega-torsion, shows that the product of the roots of c_1' has M_\omega-th power 1. That product is a primitive (q-1)-th root of unity (an individual root can have order q^r - 1) and 0 < M_\omega < q - 1: a contradiction. Hence c_1' = c_2' and c_1 = c_2.
