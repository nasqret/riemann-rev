lemma card_le_of_subset_placesAboveG

What it saysFor admissible data D and a finite set T of finite places of E contained in placesAboveG D (places above g_0 or g_1): |T| \le 4f, f = D.f the number of infinite places of E.
The argument in briefEvery place above g_0 or g_1 is a chosen place or the conjugate of one; there are f chosen places above each g_i.
