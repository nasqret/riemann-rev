lemma Data.GaugeLe.restrictToY_of_diag

What it saysLet p be a prime with a chosen \iota_{p} : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_p, d \in G_f(X_m) whose image at each \tau \in \Phi (adeleExtension along weightEmbedding(\iota_{p}, \tau)) is \operatorname{diag}(1, \ldots, 1, N) if the conjugate of the induced place of \tau divides \pi and the identity otherwise (hs), and \varphi an algebraic form on X_m with GaugeLe(d, c, \varphi) (h: the gauge of \varphi(g) at g\cdot d is \le c for all g). Then GaugeLe(1, c, D.restrictToY(\varphi)): the restriction to Y_m has gauge \le c.
The argument in briefThe restriction is a scalar times the branching matrix applied to \varphi(D.borderU g); the branching matrix turns d into a scalar, whose p-adic size matches the scalar of D.restrictToY, and integral matrices do not increase p-adic norms.
