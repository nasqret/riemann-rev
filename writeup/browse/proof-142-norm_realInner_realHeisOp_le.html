lemma norm_realInner_realHeisOp_le

What it saysLet \iota be a nonempty finite type, \mathit{eps} signs on \iota , \sigma in U(\mathit{eps}) (the unitary group of \operatorname{diag}(\mathit{eps})), P a complex polynomial in the variables \iota , v a vector of the Fock space with v = polyVector(\mathit{eps}, P) (v_P(x) = P(\zeta (x)) \exp (-|x|^2/2)), d, A, n natural numbers and y in C^{\iota }. Assume P is homogeneous of degree d (P(c x) = c^d P(x) for every real c and every x), d + |\iota | \le A, A is nonzero, and |y| > 0 (Euclidean norm). Then |realInner(v, realHeisOp(y)(realWeil(\sigma )(v)))| \le |\sigma |^{(|\iota | + 2n)} \operatorname{Re}(realInner(v, v)) (4(A + n))^n |y|^{(-2n)}, where |\sigma | is the l2 operator norm, realInner the inner product of the Fock space, realHeisOp(y) the Heisenberg translation by y and realWeil the Weil representation of U(\mathit{eps}) on the Fock space. Remark: the hypothesis A \ne 0 follows from the others (\iota is nonempty, so |\iota | \ge 1 and d + |\iota | \le A gives A \ge 1); Lean takes it as an argument all the same (h0).
The argument in briefWrite the coefficient as an integral over C^{\iota }; bound the multiplier by |\sigma |^{|\iota |} and the integrand pointwise by (|v(x/c)|^2 + |v((\sigma x + y)/c)|^2) times (4(A+n)|\sigma |^2/|y|^2)^n / c^{(2|\iota |)} / 2 with c = \operatorname{sqrt}(n/A + 1). Both summands integrate to c^{(2|\iota |)} \operatorname{Re}\langle v, v\rangle (the second is the first composed with the measure-preserving map x \to \sigma x + y), so the factor 2 and c^{(2|\iota |)} cancel. The pointwise bound: |v(z)| = c^d |v(z/c)| \exp (-n|z|^2/(2(A+n))) at z = x and z = \sigma x + y (homogeneity and the Gaussian), 2\text\{ ab \} \le a^2 + b^2, then with S = |x|^2 + |\sigma x + y|^2 the scalar c^{(2(d+|\iota |))} \exp (-nS/(2(A+n))) is at most e^{(n(1-t))} \le t^{(-n)}, t = S/(2(A+n)) (by 1 + u \le e^u, d + |\iota | \le A and t \le e^{(t-1)}), and |y|^2 \le 2|\sigma |^2 S (triangle inequality, |\sigma | \ge 1).
