lemma DescentsVanish.integral_sourceTerm_eq_zero

What it saysLet W' = W + H^{p} be split by an adapted splitting D over W (p hyperbolic planes), V a definite hermitian space (signs 1) of rank n, a a character, S a set of places, Y a function on the group of V, and assume DescentsVanish(W, V, a, S, Y). Then for every datum f on \operatorname{Hom}(W', V) with projOn(S) f = f (the vacuum at the places of S and their conjugates) and every g in the adelic group of W': the integral over [U(V)] of \theta(sourceTerm(D, V, 0)(\omega_{a \upsilon^{|p|}, 1}(g, h) f)) Y(h) dh is 0, where sourceTerm(D, V, 0) is the zero term along the maps killing the orthogonal of the frame k.
The argument in briefWrite g by the Iwasawa decomposition as a radical element times a Levi element times \kappa^{-1}, \kappa integral; the radical and Levi elements act on the source term explicitly, turning the integral into a descent from V to W of a datum still vacuum on S, which vanishes by h0.
