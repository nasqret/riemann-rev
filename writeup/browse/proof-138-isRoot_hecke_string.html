lemma isRoot_hecke_string

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, an auxiliary pair O with n nonempty, an isotypic space x of forms on the standard definite space of rank |n| of the weight of O, a nonzero form f \in x (hf, h_0), and every g having eigenvalue 1 on normalisedRep O x.galRep (h): for a split finite place w (hw) not above \ell (h\ell ) at which f has Hecke polynomial S (hS: HasHeckePolyAt f w hw S) and every i < |n| - |r|, \nu _w\cdot q_w^i is a root of S, \nu _w = AutChar.frobValue (O.a) r w.
The argument in briefAt a geometric Frobenius at w, the attached representation and the Hecke polynomial match (sphericalCompat_std); eigenvalue 1 of the normalised representation gives the root \nu _w of S, and unitarity of the local representation (Satake parameters) gives the whole string \nu _w q_w^i.
