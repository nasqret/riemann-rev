lemma tableTheta_adjoint_eq_zero

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D over P and a lift index j (1 \le j \le m) and a twist \vartheta, a finite place w that is split (hw) and not a place of the pair nor the conjugate of one (hS), and a form \Xi on V_j that lies in the range of the action of e_0 (good), in the kernel of the Hecke polynomial at w evaluated at the test value (D.auxPair \vartheta j).\operatorname{testValue} w, and in the orthogonal complement of the range of D.goodLift \vartheta j (h). Then for every choice i and every point g: tableTheta j ((comparisonForm j \vartheta)^*(\Xi)(i, \cdot)) g = 0.
The argument in briefWrite tableTheta of the adjoint as an inner product of \Xi with the comparison form of a table of kernel vectors; each class sum of the product forms against \Xi vanishes by stdClassSum_inner_productForm_eq_zero, whose hypotheses hold for good forms orthogonal to the lifts.
