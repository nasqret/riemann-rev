lemma carrierForm_ne_zero

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data and m \ne 0. Then carrierForm E m \ne 0: the theta lift of the constant 1 from U(0, m) to U(2m, m) is not identically zero.
The argument in briefIf the lift vanished, the integral of the constant 1 against a matrix coefficient of the sharp datum would vanish; that coefficient is a product of holomorphic minors, not zero, and the quotient has nonzero volume.
