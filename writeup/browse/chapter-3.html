The data: parameters, fields and scales

Unit 152The data of the proof: the parameters N₀ and d, the CM field E = K(ζ) and its places, the scales x, x₁, b, the ranks m, k, r, t, the order a, the comparison of sizes on the logarithmic scale, and the existence of admissible data at every large x

note and structured proofs · 237 declarations · 39 structured proofs

Parameters are a multiple N_0 of 18480 and a prime d \equiv N_0/2 - 1 \bmod N_0; they give K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d}) and, for every exponent s, the CM field E = K(\zeta), \zeta a primitive root of unity of order N_1 = N_0 \cdot 3^c \cdot 2^s, whose infinite places form a group identified with \operatorname{Gal}(E/K) and with (\mathbb{Z}/N_1)^\times; the unit proves the dictionary between places, Galois elements, residue classes and Frobenius elements, the different of E (generated by a purely imaginary \varpi_E whose product over the places is a power of the root discriminant) and the Weil data of E. Admissible data over the parameters are three scales \log b \ll x \ll x_1 \ll b stored by their logarithms, ranks m \approx \sqrt{x} (a power of 2) and r \approx x^5 (a power of 3), a set of thick places free of three-term relations, two primes g_0 \ne g_1 \equiv 1 with an integer of K of norm g_0 g_1, and primes \equiv 1 \bmod M_\omega^2 in every short window; from them the unit names k = r + 1 - m, t = 5r, the order a, the weights, the prime sum, the budget and the cost of one direction, and builds the class HasLogBound by which instance search compares monomials in the scales (le_of_hasLogBound). The last result, exists_data, says that for 0 < \varepsilon < 1/100 admissible data with scale x, \log x_1 = x^{\varepsilon/6} and \log\log b = \varepsilon \log x exist for every large x.
