lemma Data.exists_factorisation_le

What it saysMultiplicative pigeonhole: let G be a finite group, F a normed field, \iota \colon \mathbb{C} \to F a ring homomorphism, a, b \colon G \to \mathbb{C} and V \in \mathbb{C} with V^{|G|} = \prod_\chi a(\chi)\cdot b(\chi) (e). Then for every \psi \in G there are \varphi, \chi \in G with \varphi \cdot \chi = \psi and |\iota(V)| \le |\iota(a(\varphi)\cdot b(\chi))|.
The argument in briefTake \chi maximising |\iota(a(\psi \chi^{-1})b(\chi))|.
