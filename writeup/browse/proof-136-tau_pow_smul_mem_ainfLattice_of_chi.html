lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.tau_pow_smul_mem_ainfLattice_of_chi

What it saysLet L be a lattice datum over O on which \Gamma acts through the c-th power of the cyclotomic character: g\cdot x = \chi(g)^{c}\cdot x for all g, x (hc; default proof simp), c \in \mathbb{N}. If x \in withExponents(L, i, stdExponent(0)) for a letter i (hx), then \tau^{c}\cdot x \in ainfLattice(L).
The argument in briefFrame theorem: \tau^{h} M \subseteq A_{\mathrm{inf}} \otimes L with h the least bound of the jumps; here the only jump is -c, so h \le c.
