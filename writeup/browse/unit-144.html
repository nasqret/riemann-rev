Chapter V · Unit 144

Elementary divisors of a Frobenius-twisted Hecke operator: twisting data, Frobenius inequalities for least terms, interlacing-cone volumes, flag counts and an approximate projector

note and structured proofs · 78 declarations · 32 structured proofs · 1 note

Twisting data T: letters \iota_s : W \to R (s \in \mathbb{Z}/N) of a local ring W with residue field of q = P^N elements, consecutive letters congruent up to the P-power Frobenius mod p, alternating parity, and w with w^2 = p. \mathrm{GL}_k(W) acts on forms of degree n in each letter through g_s = \iota_s(g) or \iota_s((g^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}}) (letterRep, polyAction). The unit proves hasType_twisted_hecke: a Frobenius-twisted Hecke operator has small elementary divisors (HasType at p^m with an explicit bound), from (i) the root lemma (a term least in all Levi frames obeys Frobenius inequalities, via Stepanov's count of roots and Gauss filtrations), (ii) dimension bounds mod p: forms whose exponents nearly obey those inequalities fill about one part in the Hecke degree (flag count against the volume of a cyclic interlacing cone), and forms deep in every frame are rare, (iii) an approximate projector from Levi-invariant means on polynomials in a generic matrix, and (iv) Smith/Hermite normal forms over the valuation ring.
