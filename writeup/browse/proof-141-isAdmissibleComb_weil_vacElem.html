lemma isAdmissibleComb_weil_vacElem

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, n a finite index type, s signs on n, V a hermitian space over E with basis indices n and signs s; let q and d be natural numbers, g \in G_f(n) a finite adelic unitary element, x \in (\mathbb{A}_f)^{n}, A a set of finite adelic vectors, p a proposition, and C, c \in \mathbb{C}. Assume e: c\cdot C = \Delta_f(g) (the finite multiplier finMultiplier g of the Weil representation); I: g\cdot(x + y) \in A for every y \in \hat{O}^{n}; hc: d\cdot(c/|\Delta_f(g)|^{2}) \in integralAway(q); hp: if p, then |\sigma(c)| \le 1 for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}. Then \omega(1, g)(\varrho_f(x)\cdot\operatorname{vac}) satisfies IsAdmissibleComb(z \mapsto C\cdot\varrho_f(z)\cdot\operatorname{vac}, A, q, d, p, 1, \omega(1, g)(\varrho_f(x)\cdot\operatorname{vac})), where \varrho_f = Space.finHeisOp is the finite Heisenberg operator, \operatorname{vac} the vacuum of \mathcal{S}(V) and \omega the Weil representation: the moved translate of the vacuum is an admissible combination of the vacuum translates C\cdot\varrho_f(z)\operatorname{vac}, z \in A, with denominator d and size 1.
The argument in briefExpand \omega(1, g)\varrho_f(x)\operatorname{vac} by the finite formula for the Weil action on the vacuum: a sum over the |\Delta_f(g)|^{2} cosets of \hat{O}^{n} \cap g^{-1}\hat{O}^{n} in \hat{O}^{n} of roots of unity times \varrho_f(g(x + y))\operatorname{vac}, with the factor \Delta_f(g)/|\Delta_f(g)|^{2} = C\cdot c/|\Delta_f(g)|^{2}.
