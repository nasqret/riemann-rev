Structured proof · Unit 141

lemma zeroTermFin_spec

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, n, n_{0}, p finite index types, V, V_{0} hermitian spaces (signs s, s_{0}) and D a splitting of V over V_{0} along p. Then (i) zeroTermFin(D) maps the finite part \mathcal{W}_f(V) (the span of the translates \varrho_f(x)\cdot\operatorname{vac} of the vacuum) into \mathcal{W}_f(V_{0}); and (ii) for every adelic vector y of V and every real datum v (a vector of the Fock model of V) there is a real datum v_{0} of V_{0} such that, for all f \in \mathcal{S}(V), \mathrm{zeroTerm}_D(\varrho(y)(v \otimes f)) = v_{0} \otimes zeroTermFin(D)(\varrho_f(y_f)f), where \varrho is the Heisenberg action, \otimes = Space.integrated and y_f = finCoord(y) the finite coordinates of y.
The argument in briefBoth parts come from the adapted real point g that defines zeroTermFin: (i) zeroTerm \circ \omega(g) maps the spanning translates of the vacuum into the finite part of V_{0}; (ii) the Heisenberg action moves to the finite datum, and the real zero-term law for g gives the real datum v_{0}.
