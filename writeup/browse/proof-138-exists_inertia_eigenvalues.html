lemma exists_inertia_eigenvalues

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, an auxiliary pair O, labels c apt for a twist \vartheta (IsApt O c \vartheta), a finite type m, an index l, a finite place v above g_l, a place A of \Omega over v and t \in \mathbb{N}: there is \gamma \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E), equal to the chosen tame generator at A when v is chosen, such that for every s in the inertia group of A there is a multiset \mu \subseteq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell with |\mu | = |r| + t, every y \in \mu satisfying (t = 0 and s = \gamma \Rightarrow y \ne 1), y^{g_l^{|r|!} - 1} = 1 and y^{g_l^d - 1} = 1 for some 1 \le d \le D.r, and such that for every R, k, J, S with IsLabelledColumn O k J (i \mapsto twistedLabel O c i\cdot (X - 1)^t) S R (R of dimension |m|), the polynomial P = \prod _{y\in \mu }(X - y) kills s on R at A and kills \operatorname{conj}(s^{-1}) = (s^{-1})^c on R at the conjugate place of A (PolyKillsAt).
The argument in briefReduce to a chosen place v = w_i. Take \gamma the tame generator; for s in inertia with tame exponent y, \mu is the multiset of \iota \ell T(x)^y over the roots x of the twisted label times (X - 1)^t; labelled columns are killed by \prod (X - y) at A by definition and at the conjugate place by conjugate self-duality; the roots are roots of unity of the stated orders.
