lemma descentsVanish_add_planes

What it saysLet W' = W + H^{p} be split by an adapted splitting D over W, V a definite hermitian space of rank n, r the rank of W, a a character, S a set of places and Y a function on the group of V with DescentsVanish(W, V, a, S, Y). Assume: Y is automorphic; w is a split place with \operatorname{conj}(w) in S; the base change of a is unramified at \operatorname{conj}(w) (finite part); Y is spherical at w; the Hecke polynomial operator heckePolyOp(finRightRep, w, X) kills Y, where |X| = (\sqrt{q_w}^{r} q_w^{n - r - 1})^{-1}; and 2r + 2|p| + 3 < n. Then DescentsVanish(W', V, a \upsilon^{|p|}, S, Y), \upsilon = upsilonU1 the splitting character on U(1).
The argument in briefInduction on |p|. For p empty the descent is the integral of the source term (integral_sourceTerm_eq_zero). For |p| = l + 1: by density it suffices to treat polynomial data; the descent F to W' is spherical at w and, by Hecke transfer and adjointness, killed by a Hecke polynomial with a small root (the transfer factor is a unit); so F = 0 by the maximum principle (eq_zero_of_heckePolyOp_eq_zero) once F is bounded. Boundedness: F(g) = F(\gamma g) for a rational \gamma; split \theta into \ell = \theta - \theta \circ zeroTerm (the zero term along one plane) and the zero term. \ell(\omega(\gamma g, h) f) is bounded uniformly in g and h by the decay estimate exists_norm_name_le with N = 0 (the definite side V has height 1 at every h; moderate growth and averaging over the translates along the frame k give its norm hypotheses; when the height of W' at g exceeds 2, a short isotropic vector \xi at g and \gamma with \gamma \xi = k (Witt) supply the short isotropic frame it requires), Y is bounded on the definite quotient, and the integral of the zero term vanishes by the induction hypothesis for l planes. No fundamental domain is used.
