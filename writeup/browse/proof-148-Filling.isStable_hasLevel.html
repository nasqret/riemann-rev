lemma Filling.isStable_hasLevel

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit) and a level j: the set \{\gamma\in G(S,E):\mathtt{HasLevel}(S,j,\gamma)\} is stable (IsStable): it is invariant under \gamma\mapsto u^{-1}\gamma u' for rational u,u'\in\mathsf{U}.
The argument in briefNormal form transfers by besselU_mul and inv; leading minors and \mathsf{g} are unchanged.
