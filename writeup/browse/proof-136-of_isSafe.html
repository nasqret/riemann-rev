lemma Lift.Pairing.ScaledCupBounds.of_isSafe

What it saysLet p, \Gamma, the Kummer chart k (with subgroup \Gamma' and Frobenius \varphi \in \mathbb{Z} \times \Gamma) and O be as in the setting, S, S' sides, P a pairing of Y and Y' modulo p^{m}, e, e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3} \in \mathbb{N} with e_{1} + e_{2} + e_{3} \le e (he), c \in C and c' \in C'. Let x, w \in W(C^\flat)\otimes\widetilde{Y} lie over p^{e_{1}}c: projTriv(S)(x) = projTriv(S)(w) = p^{e_{1}}\cdot(1 \otimes c) (hx, hw), and x' \in W(C^\flat)\otimes\widetilde{Y}' over p^{e_{2}}c': projTriv(S')(x') = p^{e_{2}}\cdot(1 \otimes c') (hx'). Suppose (H) that for every g in galMonoid(k) = \{(0, g) : g \in \Gamma'\}: IsSafe(P, e_{3}, gx - x, g(hx' - x')) for every h \in galMonoid(k); IsSafe(P, e_{3}, w - x, gx' - x'); IsSafe(P, e_{3}, gw - w, g(\varphi x' - x')); and IsSafe(P, e_{3}, \varphi x - w, g\varphi x' - \varphi x'). Then ScaledCupBounds(P, e, c, c') holds.
The argument in briefFix a topology making j an open embedding. With B(y) = p^{e_{3}}⟨wittFst y, \cdot⟩ and the Artin–Schreier–Witt description of the small part S_m of W(C^\flat)\otimes\mathbb{Z}/p^{m}(1), the safety conditions give for every g \in \Gamma' an element \kappa(g) \in \mathbb{Z}/p^{m}(1) such that the cup product (g, h) \mapsto p^{e}\langle \alpha(c)(g), g\alpha'(c')(h)\rangle equals r times the coboundary of \kappa (their difference lies in \mathbb{Z}/p^{m}(1) \cap S_m = 0). So the restriction of the cup-product class to the open subgroup \Gamma' vanishes; as [\Gamma : \Gamma'] is prime to p and the class is p^{m}-torsion, the class vanishes.
