defweightedSum

Definition 4.1 of the note · unit 137 · New/Patching/OnePrime.lean, line 61

For a finite group \Gamma, a ring R, a natural number m, a homomorphism \rho \colon \Gamma \to R into the multiplicative monoid of R and a homomorphism c \colon \Gamma \to \mathbb{Z}/m (\mathbb{Z}/m written multiplicatively), \operatorname{weightedSum}(\rho, c) is the additive map from integer-valued functions on \mathbb{Z}/m to R given by \operatorname{weightedSum}(\rho, c)(f) = \sum_{g\in \Gamma } f(c(g))\cdot \rho(g).

lemmamul_weightedSum

Lemma 4.2 of the note · unit 137 · New/Patching/OnePrime.lean, lines 64–65

For a finite group \Gamma, a ring R, a natural number m, a homomorphism \rho \colon \Gamma \to R into the multiplicative monoid of R, a homomorphism c \colon \Gamma \to \mathbb{Z}/m, every f \colon \mathbb{Z}/m \to \mathbb{Z} and every g \in \Gamma: \rho(g)\cdot \operatorname{weightedSum}(\rho, c)(f) = \operatorname{weightedSum}(\rho, c)(x \mapsto f(x - c(g))).
