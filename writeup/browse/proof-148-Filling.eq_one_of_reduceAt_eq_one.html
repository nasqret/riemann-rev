lemma Filling.eq_one_of_reduceAt_eq_one

What it saysFreeness. For filling data S over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit), a finite type n with |n|\le k (hn), i\in\{0,1\}, a rational \gamma in the standard unitary group U_n(E) and g\in G_f(n) (both implicit) such that g^{-1}\gamma g is integral (hk) and reduces to 1 modulo every chosen place above q_i (h): \gamma=1.
The argument in briefNeatness at the f chosen places above q_i, which have norm q_i\ge b^{10}.
