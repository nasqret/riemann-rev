What it saysElementary divisors of a Frobenius-twisted Hecke operator. Let N \ne 0, W a commutative local ring, O a valuation domain (a commutative integral domain whose ideals are totally ordered), T : TwistingData N W O (prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to O, w \in O with w^{2} = p), and write Fam for the families \mathbb{Z}/N \to Monomials (Fin k) n, \mathfrak{m} for the maximal ideal of O, \kappa_{O} = O/\mathfrak{m} and T mod \mathfrak{m} = map (residue O) T, O of characteristic 0, p not a unit in O (hp), k, n \in \mathbb{N}, r, \ell \in \mathbb{N} and \kappa, y, z \in \mathbb{R} with HeckeSizes P o_{0} N k n r \ell \kappa y z (big); X, H, I finite types, \mathrm{GL}_k(W)/\mathcal{P} finite for \mathcal{P} = parahoric W (twoBlock k r); M an O-module; f : (X \to I \to O) \to M O-linear; Y an O-linear endomorphism of M; src : X \times \mathrm{GL}_k(W)/\mathcal{P} \to X; g : X \times \mathrm{GL}_k(W)/\mathcal{P} \to \mathrm{GL}_k(W); u : X \times \mathrm{GL}_k(W)/\mathcal{P} \to \mathrm{GL}_H(O); J : I \simeq Fam \times H; such that e \mapsto (src(e), (g(e)^{\mathsf{T}})^{-1}\cdot \mathcal{P}) is injective (hs) and, for every F, Y(f(F)) = f(F') with F'(c, i) = \sum_{b \in \mathrm{GL}_k(W)/\mathcal{P}} \sum_{j\in I} u(c, b)_{J(i).2, J(j).2}\cdot(\rho(g(c, b))\cdot \operatorname{diag}(p^{\text{depth} T k n r \cdot})\cdot \rho(\tilde{b}^{-1}))_{J(j).1, J(i).1}\cdot F(src(c, b), j), \tilde{b} the chosen representative of b (hY). Then for every m \in \mathbb{N}: HasType p (range f) m Y ((z + \kappa \cdot m + y)\cdot(Nat.card X\cdotNat.card I)), i.e., with L = range f: Y(L) \subseteq L, and there is a natural number l \le(z + \kappa m + y)\cdot |X|\cdot |I| with IndexDvd L (Y(L) + p^{m}\cdot L) (p^{l}): every finite family \Phi_{1}, \ldots, \Phi_{n} of elements of L admits a square n\times n matrix A over O with \det A dividing p^{l} and \sum_{k} A_{\mathit{ik}}\cdot \Phi_{k} \in Y(L) + p^{m}\cdot L for every i (New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean:20, 55–56). This is a determinant condition on finite families, not a statement about the cardinality of a quotient; it is meaningful also when L has torsion or L/(Y(L) + p^{m}\cdot L) is infinite. (The text reading also lists the section variable c, which hY binds in fun c i ↦.)
The argument in briefFactor the operator twice through exists_depth_factorization (once for the map e \mapsto (src e, g e), once for the coset representatives), pair the factorisations with the projector of exists_projector, and apply of_pairing: this gives IndexDvd with exponent controlled by dimensions of excess and deep spaces over the residue field \kappa_{O} of O, which index_mul_finrank_excessCond_le and sum_finrank_deepForms_le bound (for S = T mod \mathfrak{m}, twisting data over \kappa_{O}; \kappa_{O} can differ from the residue field of W, which has q = P^{N} elements, and it has characteristic p because p is not a unit in O). Push the index statement through f\circ \varepsilon to the image of f.
