lemma Filling.exists_besselU_approx

What it saysStrong approximation for \mathsf{U} on the filling data. For filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), x\in GFin(S) with x\in\mathsf{U} (x implicit; hx) and y\in GFin(S): there is a rational u\in\mathsf{U}(E) with y^{-1}\cdot(x^{-1}\cdot\mathtt{ratToFin}(u))\cdot y\in K(g)= principalLevel(S).
The argument in briefK(g) is open; its preimage under z\mapsto y^{-1}x^{-1}(\mathtt{toLatBasis}z)y is an open neighbourhood of toLatBasis{}^{-1}(x) in \mathsf{U}(\mathbb{A}_f), which meets the dense set of rational points.

By strong approximation (besselU_dense for ratMiddle, map_splitGram_ratMiddle) this point is in the closure of \mathsf{U}(E), so the open set contains the image of some rational u\in\mathsf{U}, i.e. y^{-1}x^{-1}ratToFin(u)y\in K(g).
