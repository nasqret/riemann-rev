lemma Filling.minorNorm_sq_le_linkDiscSq_mul

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S filling data, c,c' classes, s\in\mathsf{G} agreeing (hs), \gamma\in G(E) a term over (c,s,c') (h) of level j (hc), and x real with FixesNormAtLeast(S,s,x) (hq). Let r,i\in\mathbb{N},y a rational matrix with r columns, v\in GL_r(\mathbb{A}_f) and w an adelic matrix with r columns, such that (hp) if i=0 then \dim(\operatorname{link}(\gamma)\cap\ker\mathtt{zFormPair}(T))+r=\dim\operatorname{link}(\gamma); (hy) the columns of y lie in \operatorname{link}(\gamma); (hi) zFormPair(T)\cdot y has a left inverse (the columns of y are independent modulo the vectors with zero z-pairing in both halves); (hl) HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,w); and (o) zFormPair(T)\cdot w= zFormPair(T)\cdot y\cdot v. Then r\le1+(j-\varrho)_+ and x'^{2}\cdotminorNorm(S,j,\gamma)^{2}\le linkDiscSq(S,y,v)\cdot\max(\mathtt{termDiscNorm}(S,\gamma),N(S)^{f}\cdot\mathtt{minorNorm}(S,j,\gamma))^{2}, where x'=x if \varrho<j and i=0, and x'=1 otherwise. (Notation: T= ratMiddle(S) is the Gram matrix of the rational middle form X_0\oplus Ez with z of square h(z,z)=-N(S) (T= middleGram(N(S)): 1 on X_0 and -N(S) at z), J= ratGram(S) its split Gram matrix on BesselIndex(\mathfrak{s}); Z(\gamma)= frameProducts(S,\gamma); D_j(\gamma)= leadingMinor(Z(\gamma),j); \mathsf{g}(\gamma)= termDisc(S,\gamma); \operatorname{link}(\gamma)\subseteq(X_0\oplus Ez)^{2} is the image under linkMatrix(\gamma) of the J-orthogonal of the isotropic frame pair (f_a,\gamma f_a); zFormPair(T)=\operatorname{diag}(T_z,T_z) takes the z-pairing in both halves; linkMetric(S)=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\operatorname{diag}(T,T)+N(S)^{-1}\cdotzFormPair(T)^{*}zFormPair(T), the diagonal matrix with 1/2 at the X_0-coordinates and -N(S)/2+N(S)=N(S)/2 at the two z-coordinates; linkDiscSq(S,v,e)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(\det(v^{*}\cdot\mathtt{linkMetric}(S)\cdot v))|\cdot[\det e]^{2},[\cdot] the generalised index of a finite adele; minorNorm(S,j,\gamma)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(D_j(\gamma))|/N(S)^{2(j-\varrho)_+f}; termDiscNorm(S,\gamma)=\prod_v|\Phi_v(\mathsf{g}(\gamma))|; HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m): m= linkMatrix(\gamma)\cdot t\cdot w for a rational t with (isotropic frame pair of \gamma)*\cdot J\cdot t=0 and adelic w with classCoord(S,c)\cdot t\cdot w integral; f=f(E) the number of infinite places.)
The argument in briefUse the integral lifts n of w at k (exists_isIntMatrix), a nonzero r\times r minor \rho of zFormPair\cdot y, and the two link vectors \varepsilon=((a,-1),(0,u)) of \gamma and ((0,u'),(a',-1)) from \gamma^{-1} (exists_mem_link_termDisc_eq). Liouville's inequality for the Gram determinant of (\varepsilon restricted to \rho,y) bounds x'^{2}\cdot(\ldots) by |N\det(\varepsilon_{\rho}^{*} LM \varepsilon_{\rho})|\cdot\mathtt{linkDiscSq}(y,v); r\le #rows of \varepsilon\le2, and r=2 forces j=\varrho+1. The determinant of the 1\times1 or 2\times2 Gram block is N^{f}-sized or \mathsf{g}-sized, which gives the max; the lower bound x' comes from FixesNormAtLeast when \varrho<j and i=0 (the minor is a unit of index \ge x), else from integrality.
