lemma DividedSplitting.theta_stdClassSum_productForm

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r, n, N, m and a finite types, O : AuxPair E r n, z \colon r \hookrightarrow m and V a divided splitting (DividedSplitting N m a O z), E with Weil data, L, s, b, b' with H: V.IsGlued s L and hb: b\cdot b' = O.b, h' a point of the finite unitary group of I_m, c \in \mathbb{C}, y a function from points of the finite unitary group of I_N to coordinate vectors of weight O.colsPlus, \eta a coordinate vector of weight O.colsMinus and g a point. Then the theta lift at g of the class sum, at level rowFixingLevel O.1 (V.stackOverGenScalar s) V.fixedEmb, of h \mapsto V.productForm L (h, h') (\operatorname{conj}(c\cdot y(h))) \eta equals \operatorname{conj}(L.\operatorname{weilScalar}\cdot c)\cdot \sum_{Y \in V.\operatorname{congruenceGroup}} \operatorname{conj}(\operatorname{cosetKernelSum} O b (V.stackOverGenScalar s) V.fixedEmb y g_Y \cdot \theta^{W, I_m}_{O.a, b'}[\mathbb{1}_{I_z}, \operatorname{letterPoly}(-1)(\overline{\eta})](g_Y, h')), with g_Y = g\cdot \operatorname{liftMod}(Y) and the kernel modulo M^2.
The argument in brief\theta commutes with the class sum (it is linear, and evaluation at g is linear); apply theta_productForm to each summand and collect the class sums into cosetKernelSum.
