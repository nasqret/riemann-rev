lemma TwistingData.p_dvd_letterMatrix_of_mem_parahoric

What it saysLet N be a natural number, W a commutative local ring, R a commutative ring and T twisting data over them (T : TwistingData N W R, with prime p, P = p^{o_{0}}, q = P^{N}, letters \iota_{s} : W \to R for s \in \mathbb{Z}/N, parity isEven and w \in R), N \ne 0, k \in \mathbb{N}, a labelling \tau of Fin k, g \in \mathrm{GL}_k(W) in the parahoric subgroup of \tau (\mathit{hg}: the reduction of g modulo the maximal ideal has (i, j) entry 0 whenever \tau(i) < \tau(j)), a letter s and indices i, j \in Fin k with \tau(i) < \tau(j) if s is even and \tau(j) < \tau(i) if s is odd (h): p divides the (i, j) entry of g_s = letterMatrix T s g in R.
The argument in briefThe entry e of g (or of (g^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}}) lies in the maximal ideal, so 1 - e^{q-1} is a unit; p divides \iota_{s}(e) - \iota_{s}(e)^{q} = \iota_{s}(e)\cdot \iota_{s}(1 - e^{q-1}) by the Frobenius congruence over the N letters (s + N = s), hence p divides \iota_{s}(e).
