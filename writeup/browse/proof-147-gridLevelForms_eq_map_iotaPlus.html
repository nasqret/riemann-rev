lemma Data.gridLevelForms_eq_map_iotaPlus

What it saysFor parameters P and admissible data D: gridLevelForms(D, 0) = the image of gridLevelForms(D, m) under iotaPlus(1, true, 0); the untwisted descent from Z_{m,0} to Z_{0,0} (nodes (0, m) and (0, 0) of the grid Z) maps the forms of principal level gridLevelGen onto those of the same level.
The argument in briefThe image is contained in the target (level invariance from Data.iotaPlus_adjoint_weightedChain); its orthogonal complement in the target is zero because a form orthogonal to the image has a weighted lift chain orthogonal to itself (adjointness), and the lift chain is injective (Data.exists_isTriangle).
