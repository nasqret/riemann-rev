lemma AuxPair.exists_atPlace_eq

What it saysLet O be an auxiliary pair over a CM field E (W of rank r, V = I_n), m a finite type, and g = (g_i) a family with g_i in \mathrm{GL}_m(E_{w_i}) for every place w_i of O (chosen or twist). Then g is the family of components at the places of O of some element of G_f (unitary group of rank m) supported on the places of O and their conjugates (Gf.supportedOn).
The argument in briefIt suffices to treat families with one nontrivial coordinate; for g_i at the place w_i take atSplit(w_i, g_i), which is supported on w_i and its conjugate and is 1 at the other places of O.
