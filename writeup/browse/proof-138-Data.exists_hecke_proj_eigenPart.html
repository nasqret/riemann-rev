lemma Data.exists_hecke_proj_eigenPart

What it saysFor admissible data D, a system q : FinPlace E(D) \times \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C} and a finite set F of such systems: there is an element b of the Hecke algebra away from g (D.heckeAlgebra) such that for every d \in F \cup \{q\}, all signs s and hermitian spaces V over E(D) with index set RowIndex(D) and signs s, and every y \in D.eigenPart V d: b_V(y) = y if d = q and b_V(y) = 0 otherwise.
The argument in briefInduction on F. Adding a system e \ne q: choose x with e(x) \ne q(x) and replace b by b - (e(x) - q(x))^{-1}(b\cdot T_x - q(x)b), which kills the e-part and keeps the others.
