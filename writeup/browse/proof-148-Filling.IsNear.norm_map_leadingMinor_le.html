lemma Filling.IsNear.norm_map_leadingMinor_le

What it saysUnder the standing hypotheses, for filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), \gamma\in G(S,E) (implicit), an order l\le\varrho+1 and an infinite place w: if \gamma is near (h), then |\Phi_w(D_l(Z(\gamma)))|^{2}\le b^{11n^{3}},D_l the leading minor of order l.
The argument in briefA determinant of size l with entries \le B is at most l!\cdot B^{l}.
