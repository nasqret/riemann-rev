lemma Filling.reduction_inclHFin_eq_one_iff

What it saysFor filling data S (over a CM number field E with its CM type, for a shape \mathfrak{s}; E and \mathfrak{s} implicit), k in the finite adelic unitary group of X_0 (implicit) with inclHFin(k)\in K (h), i\in\{0,1\} and a chosen place w above q_i: the reduction of inclHFin(k) at w is 1 iff the reduction of k modulo w (as an element of K_0(\kappa)=G_f(\kappa)\cap GL(\hat{O}), integral by inclHFin_mem_latStabilizer) is 1.
The argument in briefBoth sides say that every entry of the matrix minus 1 has valuation <1 at w; \overline{\pi} is a unit at w.
