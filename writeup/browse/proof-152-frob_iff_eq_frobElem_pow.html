lemma Parameters.frob_iff_eq_frobElem_pow

What it saysFor parameters P, an exponent s, a prime p (instance hp), a commutative ring R of characteristic p which is a domain (instance IsDomain R), an infinite place v, a ring homomorphism \varphi : \mathcal{O}_E \to R, a natural number n, a ring automorphism c of \mathcal{O}_E and an integer e with he : (c = \operatorname{id} and e = 1) or (c = \operatorname{conj} and e = -1), and hc : p \nmid N_1(P, s)\cdot d(P): [\varphi(galOfPlaceInt(v)(x)) = \varphi (c x)^{p^n} for every x \in \mathcal{O}_E] if and only if (v, e) = frobElem(P, s, p)^n in \Phi (P, s) \times \mathbb{Z}.
The argument in briefBy frob_ext, the condition is: v is the place of e\cdot p^n (powers of the primitive root \varphi (\zeta (P, s)) are distinct in the domain R) and \chi _K(p)^n\cdot e = 1 in R; as -1 \ne 1 in R this means e = \chi _K(p)^n, i.e. (v, e) = \operatorname{frobElem}^n.
