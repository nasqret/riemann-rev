lemma exists_hasLargeSplit_of_pairs

What it saysLet p be a prime with a p-adic place \iota_p and \chi_K(p) \ne 1, P parameters, s an exponent, N a weight on exponents (exponent \mapsto non-negative real), \Theta a power series over \mathbb{C}_p in the variables (places) \times Unit, i, y : places \to \mathbb{N} and n an exponent (finitely supported places \times Unit \to \mathbb{N}) such that \operatorname{IsFrobPacking}(p, \sigma \mapsto n(\sigma, ()), i, y), \Theta is carried by one copy (IsCarriedBy N 1 \Theta: every exponent \alpha has a split into one exponent with N \ge |\Theta_\alpha |), \Theta(0) = 1, \Theta^{-1} is carried by five copies (IsCarriedBy N 5 \Theta^{-1}), and \Theta \in \operatorname{thetaProducts}(P, s, 1, 1, Unit). Then there are exponents b \le n and \tau \le n with |b| = \sum_\sigma y_\sigma(p^{i_\sigma } + 1), |\tau | = \sum_\sigma y_\sigma, and \operatorname{HasLargeSplit}(N, 6, b, p^{(1 - 1/(p-1))|\tau | + \rho_p|b| + \sum_x v_p(\tau_x!)}): b is a sum of six exponents \alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_6 with \prod_k N(\alpha_k) \ge that bound. (At p = d condition (1) of the packing forces y = 0, so b = \tau = 0 and the bound is 1. In general the statement gives b, \tau \le n and the product bound on six summands of b; it asserts no optimal coefficient at n.)
The argument in briefWrite F = \Theta(\varpi z) = \exp(\kappa \Psi)\cdot V (normal form at inert p). Differentiating once along the partner variable (-p^{i_\sigma })\sigma for each of the y_\sigma pairs multiplies by |\kappa | = p^{1-1/(p-1)} and produces an integral series Q whose reduction has lowest term u\cdot z^{\sum y_\sigma p^{i_\sigma }e_\sigma } (u \ne 0), because \partial \Psi mod \mathfrak{m} has its unit coefficient at z_\sigma^{p^{i_\sigma }} and the packing makes that term minimal. Since Q = Q\cdot F\cdot F^{-1} has a unit coefficient there, some coefficient of F^{-1} times one of Q\cdot F is \ge 1; the carrying hypotheses on \Theta^{-1} (5 copies) and \Theta (1 copy) give the six-fold split.
