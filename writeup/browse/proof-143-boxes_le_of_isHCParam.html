lemma boxes_le_of_isHCParam

What it saysLet L and N be finite types with L nonempty, e \colon N \to \mathbb{Z}, l \colon N \to L \to \mathbb{Z} and \kappa a weight with IsHCParam \kappa e l (h: for every w \in N, l_w is injective and each l_w(i) is 2(e_w + \lambda(w, p)) + |L| - 1 - 2p for some position p < |L|, twice the Harish-Chandra parameter of (\kappa, e)), and \beta \in \mathbb{N} with |l(w, i)| \le \beta for all w, i (hβ). Then boxes \kappa |L| \le |N|\cdot |L|\cdot \beta and |e(w)| \le \beta for every w \in N.
The argument in briefboxes is the sum of the diagram weights over the |N|\cdot |L| positions; each is at most \beta, read off the HC parameter at two positions.
