lemma Filling.isIntMatrix_scaleAt_sub_one

What it saysLet E be a CM field, \kappa a finite type (implicit), p a finite idele such that at every finite place w,p is a unit at w or at \overline{w} (hp), o\in\kappa an index, and k in the finite adelic unitary group G_f(\kappa) (implicit) lying in the congruence group of the rows \{o\} modulo p\hat{O} (k integral with row o congruent to that of 1 modulo p\hat{O}; h). Then \operatorname{diag}(p^{-1} at o)\cdot(k-1)\cdot\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{conj}(p^{-1}) at o) is integral: k-1 with row o divided by p and column o by \overline{p} is integral.
The argument in briefRow o of k-1 lies in p\hat{O}; by unitarity (k^{-1}=k^{H}) column o of k-1 lies in \overline{p}\hat{O}; at each place one of p,\overline{p} is a unit.
