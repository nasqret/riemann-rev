Definition (structure). For admissible data D, a tame character consists of a character toAutChar of U(1)(\mathbb{A}) trivial on U(1)(E), a finite set Q of finite places and a family \psi of residue characters such that the character has weight 0 and tame part \psi (hasWeight), \psi_w = 1 for w \notin Q (ψ_eq_one), \overline{w} \notin Q for w \in Q (star_notMem), and g_{0}g_{1}N is coprime to \prod_{w\in Q} N(w) (coprime, the seventh field). lemmaexists_branching_pairing

Theorem C of the note · unit 141 · New/Seesaw/PairTensorData.lean, lines 22–58

Let E be a CM field, n a non-empty and n' a finite index type, \kappa, \kappa' weights, e, e' determinant exponents, l' and l doubled Harish-Chandra parameters on n' and n with IsHCParam(\kappa, e, l') (h) and IsHCParam(\kappa', e', -l) (h'), interlacing: \#\{j : x < l'_{w}(j)\} - \#\{i : x < l_w(i)\} \in \{0, 1\} for every place w and x (hi); and let \sigma : n \sqcup \{\operatorname{pt}\} \simeq n' with signs s on n, r on \operatorname{pt}, t on n' such that (s \sqcup r)\circ\sigma^{-1} = t (hσ) and t = 1 (ht). Write v' \cdot (B\cdot v) = (v')^{T}\cdot B\cdot v for the bilinear pairing given by B (the dot product \cdot, no complex conjugation). Then there is an integer matrix B such that (a) there are integers N_{\tau} with (\rho_{\kappa',e'}(g)v') \cdot (B\cdot\rho_{\kappa,e}(\operatorname{diag}_{\sigma}(g, u))v) = \prod_{\tau} \det(u_{\tau})^{N_{\tau}}\cdot(v' \cdot (B\cdot v)) for all commutative rings S with involution, fields R of characteristic 0, ring maps f_{\tau} : S \to R and g, u, v', v (\rho = weylRep, entries mapped by f); |N_{\tau}| \le \operatorname{boxes}(\kappa, |n'|) + |e_{\tau}|; and |v' \cdot (B\cdot v)| \le (M^{M})^{3}\cdot\sqrt{\texttt{weylNormSq}(v')}\cdot\sqrt{\texttt{weylNormSq}(v)} (the norms of the positive definite Weyl Gram form) for every M \ge \operatorname{boxes}(\kappa, |n'|); and (b) for every g there is c = c_g (chosen after g and before V, \eta, \varphi, u and K; c_g = 0 is not excluded) such that for every standard space V (indices n, signs s), all algebraic forms \eta of weight (\kappa', e') and \varphi of weight (\kappa, e), every u and every level K: \int_{[U(V)]} F_{\eta}^{(-l, s)}(x)\cdot F_{\varphi}^{(l', t)}(\sigma-\operatorname{block}(x, 1, u)\cdot(g, 1)) dx = c_g/[K_{0} : K]\cdot(the class integral at level K of h \mapsto \eta(h) \cdot (B\cdot\varphi(\operatorname{diag}_{\sigma}(h, u)))), F = ofAlgForm. So B pairs the two weights bilinearly and invariantly up to a power of \det u, with explicit bounds, and periods over definite U(n) are constants times means of this pairing.
