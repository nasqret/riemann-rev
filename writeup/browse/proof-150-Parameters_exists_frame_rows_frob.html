lemma Parameters.exists_frame_rows_frob

What it saysLet p be a prime with \chi_K(p) \ne 1 and a p-adic place \iota_p, P parameters, s an exponent, J a finite type, w \colon J \to E and M with (w, 1) a tight frame of length M, and \beta \in \mathfrak{o}. There is z : (infinite places) \to J \to \mathfrak{o} with z_{v, i} = \beta \cdot \iota_p(\operatorname{conj} \Phi_v(w_i)) for all v, i, such that for every commutative ring Q of characteristic p and ring map g \colon \mathfrak{o} \to Q killing every x with |x| \le p^{(\chi_K(p)-1)/2}, every j with j odd or p = d, and all places v, v': \sum_i g(z_{v, i})^{p^j}\cdot g(z_{v', i}) = g(\beta)^{p^j}\cdot g(\beta)\cdot M if (-p^j)\cdot v = v' (product with the place named by -p^j), and 0 otherwise.
The argument in briefPut x_i = N_1w_i \in \mathcal{O}_E and \beta = N_1\alpha (N_1 is a p-adic unit). Modulo the prime above p, the Frobenius power p^j acts on images of integers of E as \sigma_{-p^j} composed with complex conjugation (frob_ext), so the pairing becomes N_1^2\cdot g(\alpha)^{p^j}g(\alpha)\cdot \sum_i \Phi_{(-p^j)v}(w_i)\operatorname{conj} \Phi_{v'}(w_i), given by the tight frame.
