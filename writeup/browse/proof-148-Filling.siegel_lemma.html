lemma Filling.siegel_lemma

What it saysSiegel's lemma (Bombieri–Vaaler) for hermitian lattices. Let E be a stably unramified CM field with f infinite places and root discriminant \operatorname{rd}_{E},\kappa a finite set, u\in G_f(\kappa) (the finite adelic unitary group of the standard form), T a subspace of E^{\kappa} and s a natural number with \dim T+s=|\kappa| (hs). Then there is an s\times\kappa matrix X over E with rows X_1,\ldots,X_s such that T is the orthogonal complement of the rows (T=\ker(x\mapsto(\sum_j\operatorname{conj}(X_{\mathrm{ij}})x_j)_i)), every row is a nonzero vector of the lattice E^{\kappa}\cap u\hat{O}^{\kappa} (the base lattice of the standard space moved by (1,u)), and (\prod_i\lVert X_i\rVert)^{2f}\le(8fs\cdot\operatorname{rd}_{E})^{fs}\cdot d(T), where \lVert X_i\rVert is the Euclidean norm of (\Phi_v(X_{\mathrm{ij}}))_{v,j} over all infinite places and d(T)= sublatticeDisc(u,T) is the discriminant of T\cap u\hat{O}^{\kappa}.
The argument in briefChoose bases of T\cap u\hat{O}^{\kappa} and of its orthogonal complement; Jacobi's identity makes their discriminants equal; Minkowski's second theorem for the adelic Gram–Schmidt matrix of the complement gives short vectors X_i spanning it, and their product of lengths is controlled by the discriminant.
