lemma Parameters.exists_finitePart_of_isTightFrame

What it saysLet P be parameters, s an exponent, V a standard hermitian space over E = E(P, s) of rank n (all signs 1, adelic point 1; instance [V.IsStd]). Let M, N be natural numbers with N \ne 0, I a finite type, w \colon I \to E and m \colon I \to \mathbb{N} with (w, m) a tight frame of length M (IsTightFrame w m M: \alpha^{-1}w_i \in \hat{O} for all i and \sum_i m_i\cdot \Phi_\sigma(w_i)\cdot \operatorname{conj} \Phi_\tau(w_i) = M\cdot [\sigma = \tau] for all infinite places \sigma, \tau; the length M is this scalar, not the number of indices or \sum_i m_i), \delta \in \mathcal{O}_K with \delta \cdot \overline{\delta} = N, and x \colon I \times n \to \mathbb{N}^3 with \operatorname{monomialDeg}(x_i) = m_{i.1} (\operatorname{monomialDeg}(a, b, c) = a + 2b + 3c). Let C be a CM datum for d and a \in \Lambda(C) with \operatorname{area}(C) = |a|^2/N\cdot M\cdot \sqrt{d}/2. Then there is F \in \mathcal{W}_f (V.finitePart) such that: (1) for every \gamma \colon n \to E, writing G_i = U^{m_{i.1}}_{y_i}(\theta^{x}) — the twisted translate (CMLattice.twistedTranslate) of the monomial section \theta^a \sigma^{2b} G^c of C at x_i by y_i = (a/\overline{\delta})\cdot(-\sum_\tau \Phi_\tau(\gamma_{i.2})\cdot \operatorname{conj} \Phi_\tau(w_{i.1})) — one has \theta(\varrho(\operatorname{finVec}(\alpha^{-1}\gamma))F) = \prod_i G_i(0), and for every c \in \mathbb{C} the jet series T_c(\varrho(\operatorname{finVec}(\alpha^{-1}\gamma))F) equals \prod_i \operatorname{taylor}(G_i) substituted at the linear form z \mapsto \sum_{\tau } (a/\overline{\delta})/\operatorname{areaScale}\cdot c\cdot \operatorname{conj} \Phi_\tau(w_{i.1})\cdot z_{(\tau, i.2)}; (2) for every prime p, every p-adic place \iota_p and every finite adelic vector x: |\iota_p(F(\operatorname{finVec} x))| \le 1; (3) F has a vacuum expansion for \delta (in E) and N (Space.VacuumExpansion: a nonempty finite index set of N^{2f|n|} elements (f the number of infinite places of E), finite adelic shift vectors x_j (shift j) with \delta x_j integral, and a natural m' with \sum_j m'\cdot \ell(\varrho(\operatorname{finVec} x_j)vac)\cdot \varrho(\operatorname{finVec}(-x_j))F = N^{2f|n|}\cdot \ell(F)\cdot vac for all linear forms \ell; the translates are by \pm x_j themselves, with no factor \alpha, unlike the argument \operatorname{finiteImage}(\gamma) = \alpha^{-1}\gamma of (1); ConjugateJetDefs.lean 30–36).
The argument in briefPut b = a/\overline{\delta} and A_i(z) = \sum_\tau(b/\operatorname{areaScale})\cdot \operatorname{conj} \Phi_\tau(w_{i.1})\cdot z_{(\tau, i.2)}. The tight-frame identity and the area hypothesis make \sum_i m_{i.1}\cdot(pull-back of the hermitian form of C along A_i) the standard form. The entire function S = \prod_i (monomial section of C at x_i)\circ A_i then has the transformation law of a theta function under the translations by the lattice, so it is \Theta_F for some F \in \mathcal{W}_f with a vacuum expansion (Space.exists_entireTheta_eq). Translates give the product formulas by jetSeries_eq_prod, and integrality follows from the naive height bound for translates of monomial sections.
