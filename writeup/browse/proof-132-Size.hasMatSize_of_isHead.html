What it saysLet A be a commutative ring with involution (CommRing A, StarRing A ) , \mathfrak{s} a shape and T an \mathfrak{s}-form over A; V is the free A-module on the basis e_{0} , \ldots , e_{\varrho - 1} , ( x_{i} )_{i \in \kappa} , z , e_{\varrho - 1}^{*} , \ldots , e_{0}^{*} with Gram matrix J_{T} = splitGram( T ) and U ( J_{T} ) its unitary group; assume J_{T} is hermitian and invertible and let S be a size for T. Let K be a set of elements of U ( J_{T} ) all of whose matrices have size 1 for S (hypothesis o), let j \le \varrho (hj), let some \eta \in Q_{j} satisfy \eta \cdot ( w_{j} ( p )^{- 1} \cdot g ) \in K ( h' ), and let s \in Q_{j} with s \cdot g \in K ( h ); here g \in U ( J_{T} ), p : \mathbb{N} \to A^{x}, w_{j} ( p ) = weylElem( j , p ) is the Weyl element of length j and Q_{j} = unitriGroup( j ).
