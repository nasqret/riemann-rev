lemma IsNewPlaces.mem_selmerModule_of_tame_eq_zero

What it saysLet \ell be a prime, E a stably unramified CM field, \Lambda a commutative ring, e, \nu \in \mathbb{N}, (w, \tau , \varphi ) new places (IsNewPlaces \nu w \tau \varphi), n a finite type, \rho : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to M_n(\Lambda ), c : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to (\Lambda /\ell ^e)^n, S and T sets of finite places and L a set of relations, such that c \in selmerModule \rho T L (\Lambda /\ell ^e) (k), c(\tau _j) = 0 and c((\tau _j^c)^{-1}) = 0 for every j (h), T \subseteq S \cup (range w)\cup(its conjugate) (l), and \rho (g) = 1 for every g in the inertia above the places off S with \operatorname{cyc}(g) = 1 (hu). Then c \in selmerModule \rho S L (\Lambda /\ell ^e): c is unramified outside S.
The argument in briefOnly the places of T \setminus S matter; they are new places w_j or their conjugates, where vanishing at the tame generator on the right side forces vanishing on inertia.
