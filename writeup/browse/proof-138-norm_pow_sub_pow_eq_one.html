lemma norm_pow_sub_pow_eq_one

What it saysFor a prime \ell, a root of unity x \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell (IsOfFinOrder x) and a, b \in \mathbb{N} with (x^a)^{\ell ^u} \ne (x^b)^{\ell ^u} for every u \in \mathbb{N}: \lVert x^a - x^b\rVert = 1, i.e. x^a - x^b is a unit.
The argument in briefWrite the order of x as \ell ^u\cdot k with \ell \nmid k; (x^a)^{\ell ^u} and (x^b)^{\ell ^u} are distinct k-th roots of unity, so their difference has norm \ge \lVert k\rVert = 1, and it is a multiple of x^a - x^b.
