lemma Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.SidePairing.scaledCupBounds_of_killsCokernel

What it saysRank-free exponents for crystalline classes at large weights. Let S, S' be sides with a pairing h, a, \kappa, \eta, W, H \in \mathbb{N}, and for every letter j an integer b(j) \in \mathbb{N} and a sign \varepsilon(j). Suppose: for every letter j, p^{\kappa}E^{a} kills the cokernels of thirdSide(h), S and S' at j (hK); NoMultipleNear(p, 6(a + \eta + W), b(j)) (hs); the jump sets of S at j lie in [-\eta, \eta] \cup (-\varepsilon(j))\cdot[b(j), b(j) + W] and those of S' in [-\eta, \eta] \cup \varepsilon(j)\cdot[b(j), b(j) + W] (hU); either no jump of S or S' lies in [-a, 0), or the chart is unramified, p \ge 5, \eta \ge 1 and all jumps of S' lie in [-\eta, 0] (h₀); and 3(\kappa + (48(a + \eta + W) + 4)\cdot d) \le H, d = differentExp (hH). Then ScaledCupBounds(h, H, c, c') for all c \in C, c' \in C'.
The argument in briefEvery letter is a cut. Points come from the cokernel killers, deepened through the windows (noMultipleNear) to depth D = raisedAtE(2a + \eta); in the unramified case the points of S' are first moved by \varphi. Then the walk (scaledCupBounds_of_points_at_cuts) applies, and all inequalities follow from the window conditions.
