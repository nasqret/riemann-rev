What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma. For a standard level and Z\in IntermediateGroup(F): \int_{x\in K(g)}|\int_Z\operatorname{phase}(\mathtt{F.g},y x y^{-1})d\mathsf{M}(y)|^{2}d\mathsf{M}(x)=\mathsf{M}(Z)^{2}\cdot\mathsf{M}(K(g)).
The argument in briefOn K(g) the integrand is constant: for y\in Z and x\in K(g),yxy^{-1}\in K(g), so \operatorname{phase}(g,yxy^{-1})=\psi(0)=1 and the inner integral is \mathsf{M}(Z).
