lemma Size.det_mem_ofSize

What it saysLet A be a commutative ring with involution (CommRing A, StarRing A ) , \mathfrak{s} a shape and T an \mathfrak{s}-form over A; V is the free A-module on the basis e_{0} , \ldots , e_{\varrho - 1} , ( x_{i} )_{i \in \kappa} , z , e_{\varrho - 1}^{*} , \ldots , e_{0}^{*} with Gram matrix J_{T} = splitGram( T ) and U ( J_{T} ) its unitary group; let S be a size for T and j \in \mathbb{N}. For every j \times j matrix Z over A with j \le \varrho and HasMatSize( S , 7 , Z ) , \det Z \in B_{8 j}^{S}.
The argument in briefInduction on j with Laplace expansion along the first row.

    Induction on the size j of the square matrix Z (with j \le \varrho and all entries in B_{7}^{S}): \det Z \in B_{8 j}^{S}.

    1. 1.1caseline 86

      j = 0: the determinant of the empty matrix is 1, and 1 \in B_{0}^{S} = B_{8 \cdot 0}^{S}.

      UsesSize unit 132

    2. 1.2caselines 86–88

      j + 1 (with j + 1 \le \varrho, Z of size 7; k is the induction hypothesis for j): Laplace expansion along row 0 writes \det Z = \sum_{c} ( - 1 )^{c} Z_{0 c} \det Z^{( 0 , c )} as a sum of j + 1 \le r + 1 terms. Each ( - 1 )^{c} Z_{0 c} lies in B_{7}^{S} (B_{7}^{S} is closed under negation), each minor \det Z^{( 0 , c )} lies in B_{8 j}^{S} by k (it is a j \times j matrix of size 7 and j \le \varrho), so each term lies in B_{7 + 8 j}^{S}; by the sum axiom the sum lies in B_{8 j + 8}^{S} = B_{8 ( j + 1 )}^{S} (monotonicity for the bookkeeping).
