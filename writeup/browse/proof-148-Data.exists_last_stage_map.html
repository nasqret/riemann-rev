lemma Data.exists_last_stage_map

What it saysLemma (last step of the seesaw). For data D of parameters P and M with e : D.m =M (m the number of lifts), there is a \mathbb{C}-linear map R from Stage(D,M) (smooth algebraic forms on X_M of weight (\kappa,e_M)) to the algebraic forms on Y_M such that (1) for every twist \vartheta, finitely supported coefficients \Psi on translations, form y of principal level on Y_0 and column \varphi: if liftChainY(\vartheta,M,y)=R(\mathtt{chainComb}(\vartheta,\Psi,\mathtt{translateDatum},M,\varphi)) then \iota(\vartheta,y)=B\mu(\vartheta,\Psi,\varphi); and (2) for every linear functional t on Weyl coordinates there is C\in\mathbb{C} such that for every form x on Y_M, every \psi\in Stage(D,M) and every function F on the adelic group of Y_M with F(\mathtt{inclX}(k))= algFormCoeff(1,t,\psi)(\mathtt{blockEmb}(k,1)) for all k (lastGrading(D,e).inclX), the integral of F\cdot\overline{x} over the quotient (ratBessel(\mathtt{lastGrading}(D,e)).qms) equals C\cdot\langle x,R\psi\rangle.
The argument in briefTake R= toAlgForm{}^{-1}\circ restrictToY: (1) holds by the definitions of \iota and B\mu through the equivalence toAlgForm; (2) is the branching integral formula for algebraic-form coefficients along the block embedding, with the integral over the quotient identified with the class integral.
