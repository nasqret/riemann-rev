lemma exists_cut_coverMultiplicity_dvd

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, \iota a finite index type, S, A, J, B subgroups of G_f(\iota) with A, J levels (k, o), J \le A (l) and A \le B (b), D \subseteq G_f(\iota) compact (cD), q a natural number and W a property of elements. Assume hD: every x \in D lies in B and has n with (x u x^{-1})^{q^{n}} \in A for all u \in J; and hW: for every x with W(x) some r \in S has r\cdot x \in D. Then there is a finite set T of cosets of J such that (i) T is stable under S \cap A (for p \in S with p \in A: t \in T \iff p\cdot t \in T); (ii) every x with xJ \in T lies in B; (iii) for every x with xJ \in T or W(x), coverMultiplicity(S, A, T, xJ) divides q^{j} for some j.
The argument in briefPick representatives C of the S-orbits of the image of D in G_f/J; T = (S \cap A)\cdot C is finite. The multiplicity at xJ is the index of \mathrm{Stab}_S(xJ) \cap A in \mathrm{Stab}_S(xJ), which divides a power of q because conjugates of J by x are q-power torsion modulo A.
