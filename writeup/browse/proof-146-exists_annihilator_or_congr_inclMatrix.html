lemma AuxPair.exists_annihilator_or_congr_inclMatrix

What it saysLet E be a CM field, r and n finite types, \iota : r \to n an embedding, w a finite place of E, and x an n \times r matrix over the completion E_w with a left inverse over E_w. Then there is an n \times n matrix l over the integers O_w with l x = 0 such that: if, for every row c of l, the residues of the entries of l in row c at the columns \iota(r) vanish, then there is a in \mathrm{GL}_r(E_w) with |(x a)_{ij} - inclMatrix(O_w, \iota)_{ij}|_{w} < 1 for all i, j.
The argument in briefMake x primitive by a right factor b, write x b = P with an integral left inverse L, and take l = P L - 1, which kills x. If the \iota-columns of l vanish mod w, then the \iota-rows of P times the \iota-columns of L are 1 mod w, so T = (\iota-columns of L) is invertible over O_w and a = b T makes x a congruent to the inclusion matrix.
