lemma exists_rat_log_eq_and_padic_norm_eq

What it saysFor a finite set T of natural numbers that are all prime (hT) and a function g : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Z} with g(l) = 0 for every l \notin T (h): there is a rational number q with 0 < q, \log q = \sum_{l\in T} g(l)\cdot\log l, and, for every prime l (instance Fact (Nat.Prime l)), \lVert q\rVert = \lVert l\rVert^{g(l)} in \mathbb{C}_l (the completion of an algebraic closure of \mathbb{Q}_l), i.e. |q|_{l} = l^{-g(l)}.
The argument in briefq = \prod_{l\in T} l^{g(l)}.
