lemma Parameters.norm_dataInner_le

What it saysFor a standard hermitian space V over E(P, s) of rank n, f \in \mathcal{S}(V) and M \in \mathbb{R} with |f(\operatorname{finVec} x)| \le M for every finite adelic vector x: |\langle f, f\rangle | \le M^2, where \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle = Space.dataInner is the inner product \int \overline{f} f' over \mathbb{A}^n.
The argument in brief\langle f, f\rangle is a mean of |f|^2 over a compact quotient; bound |f|^2 by M^2 on a dense set of points and pass to the closure.
