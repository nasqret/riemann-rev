What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, an auxiliary pair O with n nonempty, labels c apt for a twist \vartheta, the set Q of \vartheta auxiliary for badPlaces D \ell, a form f on the standard definite space of rank |n| of the weight of O, a Hecke system H with f an eigenform for H (he), an \ell-adic representation R attached to H (h), and f of parahoric type (1, \psi _w^{-1}) at each twist place w of O (p): for every g in the inertia above Q \cup \overline{Q}, (u(g) - a(g)\cdot 1)\cdot (u(g) - 1) = 0, where u = normalisedRep O R and a = ϑ.invAvatar.
The argument in briefReduce to the twist places w \in Q (conjugate places by conjugate self-duality); there f has parahoric type, hence a product label, so R is labelled by (X - 1)(X - x_{\psi _w^{-1}}) and inertia is killed by (X - 1)(X - a(s)); chiOneInv is trivial on that inertia.
