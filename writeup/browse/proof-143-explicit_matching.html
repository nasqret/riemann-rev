lemma explicit_matching

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data; n, m, a, b, p, l finite types; s a sign vector on n and W a space of rank |n| with signs s; f \colon p \oplus l \simeq b a bijection and V' a space of rank |b| with signs blockSigns f; \sigma an identification of index types used for the partners; e \colon p \oplus a \simeq m and j \colon m \oplus l \simeq n bijections with s = blockSigns j (hj); d a natural number with |a| = |b| + 2d (hd); 2|l| + 2 \le |n| (hn); and \iota a family of finite types indexed by the infinite places. Then there is C such that for every modulus \mathfrak{m}, character \alpha, weight \gamma and multiplicative character \chi of O_E/\mathfrak{m} with \alpha tame for (\mathfrak{m}, \gamma, \chi) and \gamma_\tau = |l| - |\iota \tau | at every infinite place, there is c with |c|^2 = C / \operatorname{lValueProd}(\operatorname{places} \text{ of } \mathfrak{m}, \alpha^{-1}\cdot \upsilon^{|b|+d}, d) such that for every family \eta of complex p\times m matrices \eta \tau k (k \in \iota \tau), all \chi^{-1}-partners \Phi, \Psi (IsPartner \top \sigma \chi^{-1} \Phi \Psi), every character \beta and every point g: c\cdot \sum_h \theta^{W, I_a}_{\alpha, \beta }[\Phi, P_\eta](g, h) = \int_{[U(V')]} \theta^{V', W}_{\alpha /\beta, \alpha }[\Psi, matchedPoly f j \eta](x, g) dx, where P_\eta = stackedDetProd e \eta with its columns renamed into the positive block of n (renameRowsCols \operatorname{id} (j \circ inl)), and the sum is the class sum over U(I_a) at level K_0.
The argument in briefCombine the archimedean matching (real_matching) with the exchange theorem for theta integrals of complementary rank: the exchange identity, read through the functionals \ell (theta lift of the constant 1) and \ell' (integral against the kernel of \Psi), satisfies the hypotheses of real_matching, whose \kappa gives c.
