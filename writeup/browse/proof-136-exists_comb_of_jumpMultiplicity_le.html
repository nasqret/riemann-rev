lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.exists_comb_of_jumpMultiplicity_le

What it saysLet the Kummer chart be unramified (hk), L a lattice datum, o an exponent function, i a letter, u \in \mathbb{Z} and n \in \mathbb{N} with jumpMultiplicity(L, i, u) \le n (hn), b, l with \sigma^{-b}(l) = i (f), and suppose o(b, u) < o(b, v) for every jump v < u in U_i (ho). Then any f\cdot n + 1 elements x_j of kisinLoc(L) with exponents o at the letter l (X) have a \mathbb{Z}_p-linear combination \sum_j c_j x_j with some c_j = 1 lying in locKisinPart(withExponents(L, l, (a, v) \mapsto o(a, v) + [a = 0])) + locKisinPart(withExponents(L, l, raiseExponent(o, b, u))), f the residue degree of the chart.
The argument in briefThe leading terms t_{jm} (values against forms \nu_m of type u at the leading exponent) span a space U of dimension \le n; an Auerbach basis expresses all leading terms integrally through at most n of them. The \Lambda-parts of the coefficients, f\cdot n + 1 vectors in a \mathbb{Z}_p-module of rank \le f\cdot n, are dependent; dividing by the coefficient of least valuation gives the combination, whose E-part gains one at E and whose rest gains one at (b, u).
