lemma AuxPair.lift_auxDatum_eq_lift_modDatum

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data; let r, n be finite index types and O : AuxPair E r n an auxiliary pair with space W = W(O). Let v be a real datum (a vector of the Fock model at the infinite places) and y a function on U(W)(\mathbb{A}) which is rapidly decreasing and automorphic on W (\mathit{hy} : y \in rapidFunctions(W)) and fixed by the principal level at the chosen places M (h : HasLevel(y, levelOf(chosenPlace(O), \perp)), i.e. by the elements of K_{0} that reduce to 1 at every place of M). Then the theta lift of y by pairThetaKernel(O) with the datum integrated(v, auxDatum(O)) = v \otimes \Upsilon equals its theta lift with the datum modDatum(\cdot, \cdot, \cdot, \#TwistGL(O, r)\cdotlevelIndicator(O), v), whose real part is v and whose finite part is \#TwistGL(O, r) = \prod_{w\in Q} \#\mathrm{GL}_r(k_w) times the indicator AuxPair.levelIndicator of O.
The argument in briefWrite \Upsilon as the sum over A \in \prod_{w\in Q} \mathrm{GL}_r(k_w) of translates of the level-indicator datum; each translate fixes y, so by equivariance every summand gives the same lift.
