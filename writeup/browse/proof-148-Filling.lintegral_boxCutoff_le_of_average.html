lemma Filling.lintegral_boxCutoff_le_of_average

What it saysLemma (averaging). Let S be admissible, c a class, (X,m) a measurable space with an s-finite measure m,s:X\to (infinite places \to G_1) measurable with s(x)_v\in\mathsf{U}^{0} for all x,v; F:G_{\infty}\to[0,\infty] measurable and left invariant under \mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g) (F(u_{\infty}\cdot y)=F(y) for u\in classUnip{}_0(S,c)); B\subseteq X with 1\le m(B); C\in[0,\infty] such that for every u\in G_{\infty} with all u_v\in\mathsf{U}^{0} and every y\in boxedPoints(S): \int_B F(u\cdot s(x)\cdot y)dm(x)\le C. Then \int boxCutoff(S,c,y)\cdot\prod_v\lVert W_v^{\mathrm{cut}}(y_v)\rVert^{2}\cdot F(y)dy\le C\cdot(\nu\cdot\#\mathtt{BesselCoord}(S)), with \nu= unipCovolume(S) and \#BesselCoord(S)=\prod_v\#I_v.
The argument in briefUnfold the integral over G_{\infty} along the chosen cutoff of \mathsf{U}_c^{0}(g): \int\beta_c^{0}\prod\lVert W_v\rVert^{2}F becomes an integral over \mathsf{U}^{0}\setminus G_{\infty} of \prod\lVert W_v\rVert^{2} times an integral over \mathsf{U}_{\infty}^{0} of F; insert 1\le m(B), average over x\in B with the translates s(x) (right invariance), and use the hypothesis C together with \int_{\mathsf{U}\setminus G_1}\lVert W_v^{\mathrm{cut}}\rVert^{2}\le\#I_v and the covolume identity.
