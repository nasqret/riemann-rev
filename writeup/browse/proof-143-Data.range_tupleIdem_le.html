lemma Data.range_tupleIdem_le

What it saysLet U be a complex inner product space, P parameters, D admissible data, f a \mathbb{C}-algebra homomorphism from the group algebra (of \mathsf{G}) to \operatorname{End}_\mathbb{C}(U) and c a good tuple of labels (h: c \in D.goodTupleFamilies). Then the range of f(\operatorname{tupleIdem} c) is contained in the range of f(D.e_0).
The argument in briefe_0 is the sum of the orthogonal idempotents of the good tuples and is central, so f(\operatorname{idem} c) = f(e_0)\cdot f(\operatorname{idem} c).
