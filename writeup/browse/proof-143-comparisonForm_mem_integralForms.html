lemma comparisonForm_mem_integralForms

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D over P and a lift index j (1 \le j \le m) and a twist \vartheta, a table \zeta, a prime p with a p-adic place \varphi, p coprime to the norm N(Q) of the modulus of \vartheta (q: p.Coprime ϑ.levelNorm), and \operatorname{conj} \zeta(a) integral at the representative of its class for the conjugate embedding \varphi \circ \operatorname{conj} (hζ: weylGauge \le 1 for every index a). The argument in briefTermwise: the delta forms do not increase the gauge, the Cartan product loses at most |g|_p^{-\operatorname{loss}}, which g^{\mathrm{loss}} repays, and the line multipliers and the table vectors are integral.
