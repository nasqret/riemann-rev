lemma Parameters.exists_zeta_pow_eq

What it saysFor parameters P, an exponent s and an element y of E(P, s) (all implicit) with h : y has finite order (a root of unity): there is a natural number k < N_1(P, s) with \zeta (P, s)^k = y.
The argument in briefIt suffices that y^{N_1(P, s)} = 1. Otherwise y^{N_1(P, s)} would be a primitive d(P)-th root of unity in E (the order of y divides N_1(P, s)\cdot d(P), the order of the torsion of \mathbb{Q}(\zeta _{N_1(P, s)\cdot d(P)}) ⊃ E), and \varphi (d(P))\cdot \varphi (N_1(P, s)) \le [E:\mathbb{Q}] = 2\varphi (N_1(P, s)) would give d(P) \le 3.
