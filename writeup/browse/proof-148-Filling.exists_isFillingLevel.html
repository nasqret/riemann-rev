lemma Filling.exists_isFillingLevel

What it saysFor the filling data S over E and a finite set T of finite places of E (stable under conjugation where stated). Lemma. If E carries Weil data, then for every conjugation-stable finite set T of finite places, \prod_{w\in T}E_w carries an integral place structure P (character charOnPlaces(S,T)) and a level F with IsFillingLevel(S,T,F).
The argument in briefBuild the integral place on \prod_T E_w: Haar measure normalised on the skew-adjoint integers, the modulus, the character charOnPlaces(S,T), with self-duality — the annihilator of O is O: (\forall r\in O,\mathtt{charOnPlaces}(S,T)(r\cdot a)=1)\iff a\in O — and Plancherel (integrating the characters over O gives the indicator of O); then a lattice level of modulus g, and the standard-level conditions (\zeta bound, unimodular Haar measure from the adelic one).
