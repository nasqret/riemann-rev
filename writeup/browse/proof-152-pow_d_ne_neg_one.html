lemma Parameters.pow_d_ne_neg_one

What it saysFor parameters P (implicit) and a natural number n: (d(P) \bmod N_0(P))^n \ne -1 in \mathbb{Z}/N_0(P).
The argument in briefIn \mathbb{Z}/N_0(P), d(P)^{2} = 1 (as 2(d(P) + 1) = 0 and d(P) is odd) and d(P) \ne -1; so the powers of d(P) are 1 and d(P), never -1.
