lemma Data.v_N_eq_exp_neg_one

What it saysFor parameters P and admissible data D over P (both implicit), a finite place w of E(D) and h : the level N(D) lies in w (N(D) \in w as an ideal of the ring of integers): the valuation of N(D) in the completion LocField(w) = E(D)_w is \exp (-1) (multiplicative notation: Mathlib's .exp (-1) in the value group \mathbb{Z}^{m0} of the discrete valuation, additive order 1, not the real number e^{-1}), i.e. N(D) is a uniformiser at w.
The argument in briefThe valuation of N(D) in E(D)_w is its w-adic valuation, which is \exp (-1) because N(D) is prime, lies in w and is prime to N_1(D)\cdot d(P).
