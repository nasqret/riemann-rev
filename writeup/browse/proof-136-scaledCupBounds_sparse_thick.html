lemma Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.SidePairing.scaledCupBounds_sparse_thick

What it saysSparse thick letters. Let S, S' be sides with a pairing h whose jump sets lie in opposite windows of width V (hU: for every letter j the jumps of S lie in [-\eta, \eta] \cup (-\varepsilon(j))\cdot[\beta(j), \beta(j) + V] and those of S' in [-\eta, \eta] \cup \varepsilon(j)\cdot[\beta(j), \beta(j) + V]) with \beta(i) = b if T(i) and G otherwise (hβ), V + 4\eta \le W (hW), G + V \le \eta (hG), \eta \ge 1 (hη), 11(p^{J} + 1)\cdot W \le b (hb), e_k\cdot2b \le p^{J+1}X (hδ), 8e_k \le p^{J+1} (h₈), 4(J + 1 + d + \lfloor \log_p(2b)\rfloor)\cdot W \le X (he), no three thick letters at gaps in [1, J]: T(i) and T(\sigma^{s} i) imply \neg T(\sigma^{s+t} i) for s, t \in [1, J] (hth), and either the chart is unramified with p \ge 7 or the signs \varepsilon are all equal (hε). Then ScaledCupBounds(h, 10^{4}\cdot X\cdot(J + 1), c, c') for all c \in C, c' \in C'.
The argument in briefCuts are the letters with no thick letter in the next J steps; by sparseness every letter has a cut within 2J + 1 steps before it; a thick letter at offset a < n before i (n the distance to the nearest previous cut) has a = 0 or a + J + 1 = n, and both offsets can occur; either every offset passes the sign/width test against t = 0 (the sign alternative) or a failing offset t gives the long-gap alternative of scaledCupBounds_of_cut_windows.
