lemma IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel.lintegral_shells_le

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma (shells). For a standard level, Z,j\le\varrho,t with t+10^{8}n^{4}\le k (n=\varrho+1), the gauge \Gamma=\operatorname{gauge}(F,\alpha,a,s\alpha) for which p,q are heads of length j and g^{-1} has size 1 if j\ne0, a function f\le formMajorant(F,j,p,q,W_Z,W_Z), and e such that, if e<1,f(x)^{2}\le(e^{s})^{2}\cdotselfNormProduct(\Gamma,j,p,q,W_Z,W_Z,x) on positions (s=\mathtt{volumeExponent}(F)): headDensity(F,j,p,q)\cdot\int f d\mathsf{M}\le\min(1,e)^{t}\cdotmassRatio(Z)\cdot\Lambda_{\Gamma}^{10^{9}n^{4}}.
The argument in briefRestrict to the positions, decompose by the content ideal of g^{-1}(x-1) (ideal zeta bound), and on each content class use a finer gauge \Gamma' (depth \overline{c}c) to bound \int f by the pairing norm; the pairing norm times unipotentMass is \le massRatio(Z); the shell estimate uses the hypothesis he when e<1 and a contradiction argument with floor values otherwise.
