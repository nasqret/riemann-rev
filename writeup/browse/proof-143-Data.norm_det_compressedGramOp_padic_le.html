lemma Data.norm_det_compressedGramOp_padic_le

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data, j a lift index, \vartheta a twist, gl a \mathbb{C}-linear map from tables (Tables j) to forms on V_j ((D.auxPair \vartheta j).\operatorname{FormV}), L a subspace of the forms on V_j and \beta \in \mathbb{R}, such that gl* is injective on L (sep) and gl(gl\cdot(z)) is tested for every z \in L (tst: AuxPair.IsTested e_0 L^\perp — orthogonal to every form of L^\perp that is good and killed by the Hecke polynomial at the test value of some split place off the level). Let \iota be a prime \ell with an isomorphism \mathbb{C} \cong \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell (PrimePadicIso), suppose that gl \zeta is integral at every \ell-adic place \varphi (LevelForm.integralForms \varphi) whenever \operatorname{conj} \zeta(a) is integral at its class representative for \varphi \circ \operatorname{conj} for every index a (int), and that L has denominators at most \beta at \iota (h: DenominatorLe \iota.2 _ e_0 L \beta). Then |\iota(\det(\operatorname{compressedGramOp} \mathsf{h} L gl))| \le \exp(\dim L\cdot r\cdot \beta), \mathsf{h} = smallHookNumber j, r = D.r.
The argument in briefChoose test tables f_a from the integral Fock pairing matrix, an Auerbach basis z of L for the forms \langle f_a, gl*\cdot \rangle, and apply DenominatorLe to the family y_a = gl(f_{c(a)}) (integral at every \ell-adic place) and the family y'_b = gl(gl\cdot(\mathsf{h}\cdot z_b)) in the ambient space of forms (tested by tst, integral at \iota): the matrix \langle y_a, pr_L y'_b\rangle is the matrix of compressedGramOp in the basis z.
