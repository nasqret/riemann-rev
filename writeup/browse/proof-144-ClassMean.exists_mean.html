lemma ClassMean.exists_mean

What it saysAn approximate projector from Levi-invariant means. Let W be a commutative local ring, O a valuation domain of characteristic zero, k, D \in \mathbb{N}, \tau : Fin k \to \mathbb{N} a labelling, \iota : W \to O a ring homomorphism, p a prime number whose image in O is not a unit (\mathit{pp}, \mathit{hp}), f \in \mathbb{N} with D < p^{f} (hD) and D + k < Nat.card of the residue field of W (hW), x : Fin k \to \mathbb{R} (weights of the indices) and P a property of real numbers. For an exponent \alpha on the entries (i, j) of a generic k\times k matrix, let its row weight be \sum_{(i,j)} \alpha_{\mathit{ij}}x(i) and its column weight \sum_{(i,j)} \alpha_{\mathit{ij}}x(j). Then there is an O-linear form \kappa : O[x_{\mathit{ij}}] \to O such that (a) \kappa(conjSubst \iota a g) = \kappa(g) for every a in the Levi subgroup levi W \tau and every polynomial g; (b) \kappa(x^{\alpha}) = 0 whenever the row weight of \alpha differs from its column weight; (c) for \alpha whose row weight satisfies P: \kappa(x^{\alpha}) = p^{k^{2}f}\cdot(x^{\alpha} evaluated at the identity matrix) if \deg \alpha \le D, and \kappa(x^{\alpha}) = 0 otherwise; (d) \kappa lies in the O-span of the forms l \circ conjSubst \iota u with u \in levi W \tau and l an O-linear form vanishing on every x^{\alpha} whose row weight does not satisfy P.
The argument in briefInduction on the degree d \le D + 1, adding at each step the correction \operatorname{tr}_N(\rho(K f)) in degree d. Here g is the d-fold Kronecker power of the generic Levi matrix Y, S sends coefficient vectors on degree-d monomials to matrices (S of the values of a form l is l applied to g), N is the module spanned by the columns of the S(c) whose index weight satisfies P, \rho(c) is S(c) restricted to N (N is stable, by Zariski density), and K multiplies coefficients by the invariant diagonal form q. Invariance follows from the equivariance of S\circ K and the cyclicity of the trace; the weight condition from nilpotence; the values from q\cdotmultinomial = p^{k^{2}f}; membership in the span from writing the trace in a basis of the free module N.
