lemma Filling.exists_hasSum_cosetAmplitude

What it saysFor the filling data S over E and a finite set T of finite places of E (stable under conjugation where stated). Lemma. Let E carry Weil data, S be admissible and \sigma a side. If amplitudeSum(S,\mathtt{termsOfLink}(S,\Lambda),c,s,c')\ne0 and all terms over (c,s,c') have nonzero leading minors, then there are a unit t,\sigma'\in G(E) in GT \varrho with Z(\sigma')_{\mathrm{top},\mathrm{top}}=t,N^{2}<|\Phi_v(t)|^{2} at every v,k\in K with reduction s and \Lambda=\operatorname{link}(w_1\sigma'), and a family L(p) with HasSum L= amplitudeSum(S,\mathtt{termsOfLink}(S,\Lambda),c,s,c') and, for every p, cosetAmplitude(S,T,c,s,c',w_{(p,t)}\sigma')\to L(p) as T grows.
The argument in briefTake a term \gamma_0 in the block; its big-cell decomposition \gamma_0=v\cdot w_m\cdot\sigma\cdot v' gives \sigma (\operatorname{floor} group) and t=Z(\sigma)_{\mathrm{top},\mathrm{top}}\ne0; the parameterization \delta(p,u,u')=u\cdot w_{(p,t)}\sigma\cdot u'^{-1} is injective, maps every parameter into termsOfLink(\Lambda) and has every term of the block in its image, while membership in termsOver(c,s,c') is still tested by an indicator (a parameter need not have the prescribed reduction); summing over (p,u,u') and grouping by p gives L(p), and dominated convergence gives the limits of the coset amplitudes.
