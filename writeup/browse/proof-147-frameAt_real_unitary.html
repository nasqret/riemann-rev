lemma frameAt_real_unitary

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type (no Weil data); n, n', r finite index types, signs s on n and s' on n', W : Space(E, n, s), W' : Space(E, n', s') and D a splitting of W over W' along r. Let t be signs and suppose D is adapted at 1 with t (h : IsAdapted(D, 1, t)). For every infinite place w, the real frame matrix P = (frameAt(D, 1, t)) at w is unitary (P^{*}P = 1 and PP^{*} = 1) and \operatorname{diag}(s)\cdot P = P\cdot B, where B is the block matrix with blocks (0 1; 1 0) on r \sqcup r and \operatorname{diag}(s') on n'.
The argument in briefAdaptedness gives P^{*}P = 1; square, so PP^{*} = 1; the \operatorname{Gram} matrix of the frame is the block form.
