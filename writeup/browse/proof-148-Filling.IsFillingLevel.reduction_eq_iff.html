lemma Filling.IsFillingLevel.reduction_eq_iff

What it saysFor the filling data S over E and a finite set T of finite places of E (stable under conjugation where stated). Let \prod_{w\in T}E_w carry an integral place structure P and F be a filling level (IsFillingLevel(S,T,F)), and assume g^{-1} is integral outside T. Lemma. For k,l\in K: \operatorname{reduction}(S)(k)=\operatorname{reduction}(S)(l) iff toPlaces(k)^{-1}\cdottoPlaces(l)\in congGroup(F).
The argument in briefWrite l=k\cdot l' with l'\in K; then the reductions agree iff l'\in K(g), iff toPlaces(l')\in congGroup(F).
