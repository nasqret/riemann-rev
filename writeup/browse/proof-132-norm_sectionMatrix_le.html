lemma norm_sectionMatrix_le

What it saysLet \mathfrak{s} be a shape and n = \varrho + 1; FrameMat( \mathfrak{s} ) is the space of complex n \times n matrices (indices 0 , \ldots , \varrho, the last one being \varrho); for Z \in FrameMat( \mathfrak{s} ), \xi ( Z ) = invCorner( Z ) = ( Z^{- 1} )_{\varrho \varrho} (Z^{- 1} the matrix inverse, 0 if Z is singular), \Omega = openCell( \mathfrak{s} ), \delta ( Z ) = cornerDefect( Z ) = 1 - | \xi ( Z ) |^{2}, diagLast( x ) = \operatorname{diag} ( 1 , \ldots , 1 , x ); F_{\mathfrak{s}} = frameCols( \mathfrak{s} ) (the columns e_{0} , \ldots , e_{\varrho - 1} , z of the frame in \mathbb{C}^{r}) and F'_{\mathfrak{s}} = dualFrameCols( \mathfrak{s} ) (the columns e_{0}^{*} , \ldots , e_{\varrho - 1}^{*} , x_{k_{0}}); \mathsf{g} ( Z ) = gramDiscReal( Z ) = | \det Z |^{2} - | D_{\varrho} ( Z ) |^{2}, D_{\varrho} the leading principal minor of order \varrho; \lVert \cdot \rVert is the operator norm. Let Z \in \Omega (unnamed hypothesis), r > 0 (hr), P \ge 8 (hP), and assume IsBoundedInCell( y , P ) for every y in the open ball of radius r about Z ( H ). Then \lVertsectionMatrix( Z ) \lVert \le P^{12} and the real derivative of Z' \mapsto sectionMatrix( Z' ) at Z has operator norm at most 2 P^{12} / r (sectionMatrix with its default arguments).
The argument in briefBound sectionMatrix on a ball around ( Z , Z^{H} ) in the product space, then the Schwarz-type Lipschitz bound for holomorphic maps gives the derivative bound.
