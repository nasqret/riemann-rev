lemma Data.heightLe_inv_ratio

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D, a family \theta of m-fold twists indexed by PrimePadicIso that is fit (o: D.IsFitFamily \theta), a lift index j and a pair p of rank one for j (hE: p.IsRankOne j): the family \iota \mapsto(p.\operatorname{ratio}((\theta \iota)^j))^{-1} of inverse L-value ratios has height (HeightLe) at most 6\cdot B_0 - \mathsf{U} (B_0 = D.B₀, \mathsf{U} = D.dirCost), where p.ratio \vartheta = |L(z, (\nu \vartheta)^{-1})|/|L(z, \nu^{-1})| without the Euler factors at the chosen places (and at Q in the numerator).
The argument in briefCombine the height of the squared norm of the trivial lift (negative, against parallel weight) with the fit family's bound for the auxiliary pair q_2 and a family y' whose size is controlled: at each \iota a test form pairs with the trivial lift to a multiple of the jet of depth_indicator_jet, so its \iota-adic size is at least p^{-F(p)} times |\iota V|, V a fixed root of a product of norms; the archimedean size of V is at most \exp(B_0).
