lemma Level.IsPairingKernel.pivotModulus_sq_le

What it saysLet R be a place (instances CommRing R, StarRing R, TopologicalSpace R, MeasureSpace R, Place R), \mathfrak{s} a shape, T an \mathfrak{s}-form over R with J_{T} = splitGram( T ) hermitian and invertible, \iota a finite type, m \in \mathbb{N} and F a level (Level \iota T m) with floor measures \theta_{l}^{F} = floorMeasure( F , l ); k : G_{T} \to G_{T} \to \mathbb{C} is a kernel, and upperPairing, pivotModulus, weightedSquare, weightedSquareSum, selfNorm, floorWeight and IsNear are those of LevelGauge; hk : IsPairingKernel( F , j , k , d ) is the standing hypothesis of the namespace (include hk): k is a pairing kernel for F from floor j with twist d. Let u , v : G_{T} \to [ 0 , \infty ] be measurable (hu, hv), with FloorMassLe( F , l , K , u ) (hK) and |upperPairing( F , l + 1 , k , \sigma g , \sigma y ) |^{2} \le u ( \sigma g ) \cdot v ( \sigma ) for every \sigma \in H_{l} (h; by default proved when u = 1 and v is the squared modulus). Then pivotModulus( F , l , k , g , y )^{2} \le K \cdot \int v d \theta_{l}^{F} (Cauchy–Schwarz on floor l).
