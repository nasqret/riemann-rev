lemma name_vacElem_of_isEmpty

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data, n an empty finite index type (IsEmpty n), s signs, W : Space(E, n, s), \mu weights, \mathfrak{c} a split tame character of weight \mu and \Phi a complex function on (empty) matrices. Then the vector named by \Phi at the places of \mathfrak{c} is \Phi(0) times the vacuum: name(W, S, \Phi, vac) = \Phi(0)\cdot \text{vac}.
The argument in briefOn empty matrices \Phi is the constant \Phi(0) times the box indicator, and naming by the box indicator is the projection, which fixes the vacuum.
