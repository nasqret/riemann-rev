lemma Data.det_heckeOnGood_eq

What it saysLet D, s, s', V, V' be as in trace_heckeOnGood_eq: \operatorname{paramW}(D, v, .) has gap h(D) for s and for s' at every infinite place v, and \operatorname{HasRigidLabels}(D, V) and \operatorname{HasRigidLabels}(D, V') hold. Then for every p in \operatorname{heckeAlgebra}(D) the determinant of \operatorname{heckeOnGood}(D, V)(p) on \operatorname{goodForms}(D, V) equals the determinant of \operatorname{heckeOnGood}(D, V')(p) on \operatorname{goodForms}(D, V').
The argument in briefEqual traces of all powers give equal characteristic polynomials (S empty in charpoly_eq_mul_of_trace_pow_add), hence equal determinants.
