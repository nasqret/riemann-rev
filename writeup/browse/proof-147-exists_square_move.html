Let P, D, an m-fold twist \vartheta, a grid z, indices a, b, n, a finite set w of ordered pairs of nodes and \Lambda \in GridDatum(\vartheta); nodes p, v, Q, U, R, r; a finite type T, a type J; bijections \tau : GridIndex(z, a, b) \simeq NodeIndex(U), \sigma : NodeIndex(p) \sqcup T \simeq NodeIndex(Q), \rho : NodeIndex(r) \sqcup T \simeq NodeIndex(R); an additive character \psi of the residue ring; a multiplicative character \chi, a bijection \varepsilon and a constant C; and families L(i), M(i), i \in J, with SquareLaw(\chi, \varepsilon, C, L(i), M(i)) for all i (q). Assume (hQ) every admissible family g has g(R) = blockInlGL(\rho, g(r)) and, for every k, the family u = g updated at p by k and at Q by blockInlGL(\sigma, k) is admissible with u(p) = k; (h) U \ne v, (Q, U) is a \mu-edge and (U, R) is not, or (p, v) is and (v, r) is not, the nodes U, R, v, r have index pairs at most (m, m) and are different from p and Q, and every edge of w has source outside \{p, Q\} and target outside \{p, Q, R, r\}; (H) \Lambda is tidy of order n on w \cup \{(p, v), (Q, U)\}; and (X) \chi = (edgeChar(\vartheta, (p, v))\cdotedgeChar(\vartheta, (Q, U)))^{-1}, edgeChar(\vartheta, (U, R)) = edgeChar(\vartheta, (p, v)) and edgeChar(\vartheta, (v, r)) = edgeChar(\vartheta, (Q, U)).
