    Let c (with c\overline{c}=1) witness IsNormalForm(j,\gamma). Take \overline{c} for \gamma^{-1} (\overline{c}\cdot\operatorname{conj}(\overline{c})=1). For a\ge j, from f x=(\gamma f)y with x_a=c,y_a=1 and x,y vanishing above a, the vectors \overline{c}y and \overline{c}x satisfy f(\overline{c}y)=(\gamma^{-1}f)(\overline{c}x),(\overline{c}y)_a=\overline{c},(\overline{c}x)_a=\overline{c}c=1, and vanish above a.
