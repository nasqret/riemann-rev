lemma Space.eq_zero_of_jetSeries_eq_zero

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, V a hermitian space over E of rank n with all signs +1, f \in \mathcal{W}_f, and a \in \mathbb{C} with a \ne 0. If the jet series T_a f (Space.jetSeries a f) is 0, then f = 0.
The argument in briefA vector of \mathcal{S}(V) vanishes if its values at all real vectors do; there f(y) = \Theta_f(y)\cdot \operatorname{realVac}(y), and the entire function u \mapsto \Theta_f(u\cdot a\cdot y) has zero Taylor series (previous lemma), hence vanishes, also at u = a^{-1}.

    For each real vector y: by taylor_line_eq_subst_jetSeries, the Taylor series at 0 of the entire function u \mapsto \Theta_f(u\cdot a\cdot y) is the substitution of T_a f = 0, i.e. 0; by the identity theorem it is the zero function; at u = a^{-1} this gives \Theta_f(y) = 0, so f(\operatorname{realVec} y) = \Theta_f(y)\cdot \operatorname{realVac}(y) = 0; and a vector of \mathcal{S}(V) vanishing at all real vectors is 0 (eq_zero_of_realVec).
