lemma AuxPair.FitsParams.exists_isJointHarmonic

What it saysLet O be an auxiliary pair over E (W of rank r with signs s = signs(O), V = I_n) and l a parameter on W with FitsParams(O, l). Then there is a family \Phi of polynomials, one for each infinite place w, such that (1) for every w, \Phi(w) is a joint harmonic (IsJointHarmonic) for the pair std(1, s, negCount(s), -(floor(|n|/2) + weight(O, w))) with parameters paramV(O, w, .) and -l(w); (2) harmonicPoly(O)(highestVector(kappa(O), paramV(O, ., .))) is the product over w of \Phi(w) with its variables renamed into the variables of the place w; and (3) for every w and c in r, the lowest K-type of -l(w) for the signs s at c equals staircaseWeight(n, s, ord(O), k, -(floor(|n|/2) + weight(O, w)), c), where k(\eta) = colsPlus(O, w) if \eta = 1 and colsMinus(O, w) otherwise.
The argument in briefAt each place the staircase polynomial built from the column numbers is a highest joint harmonic with staircase weights (S); joint harmonics for matched parameters exist and are unique up to scalar, which identifies the lowest K-type of -l with the staircase weight (J); and harmonicPoly at the highest vector is, after unfolding, the product over the places of these staircases.
