lemma zeroTermFin_support

What it saysLet E, n, n_{0}, p, V, V_{0} (signs s, s_{0}) and D be as in zeroTermFin; let M, M' be n \times n matrices over \mathbb{A}_f with M\cdot M'^{H} = 1 (m), f \in \mathcal{S}(V) with \varrho_f(Mx)f = f for every x \in \hat{O}^{n} (hf), and y a finite adelic vector of V with zeroTermFin(D)(\varrho_f(y)f) \ne 0 (h). Then there is l \in \hat{O}^{n} with k_f^{H}\cdot(y - M'l) = 0, where k_f is the finite part of the adelic isotropic frame k of D (Splitting.kAdelic): y lies in M'\hat{O}^{n} + k^\bot.
The argument in briefChoose a primitive basis t of the frame M'^{H}k_f with an integral left inverse L; take l = L^{H}\cdot u for the frame coordinates u = t^{H}k_f^{H}y of y. Then u is integral by the character criterion, because zeroTermFin(\varrho_f(y)f) \ne 0 is invariant under translation along k, and k_f^{H}(y - M'l) = 0 by the left inverse.
