classIsSplitLevel

Definition 2.1 of the note · unit 141 · New/Seesaw/NamesAtSplitLevel.lean, lines 25–29

Definition (class). For a field E with a CM type, a finite set P of finite places of E and a natural number N, IsSplitLevel(P, N) holds when (star_notMem) \overline{w} \notin P for every w \in P; (q_eq) every w \in P has absolute norm N; (v_natCast) N has valuation \exp(-1) in E_w, i.e. N is a uniformiser at w, for every w \in P; and (natCast_mem_iff) a finite place w divides N exactly when w \in P or \overline{w} \in P. So the places above N are those of P \cup \overline{P}, each of norm N with uniformiser N.

defresidueOverN

Definition 2.1 of the note · unit 141 · New/Seesaw/NamesAtSplitLevel.lean, lines 33–34

Definition. For a number field E, a finite set P of finite places, a natural number N and y \in (\mathbb{Z}/N)^{P}: residueOverN(y) is the finite adele with component \widetilde{y}_w/N at w \in P, \widetilde{y}_w \in \{0, \ldots, N-1\} the least non-negative representative of y_w, and 0 at the places outside P (extension by zero). The definition accepts every natural number N; the description by representatives in \{0, \ldots, N-1\} is for N > 0. For N = 0, ZMod 0 = \mathbb{Z} and division by 0 in the local field gives 0, so residueOverN(y) = 0.
