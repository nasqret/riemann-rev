lemma exists_contDiff_witt

What it saysWitt's theorem, smooth local form. Let b, m be finite index types, J an m \times m complex matrix with J^{*} = J (hJ), and x an m \times b complex matrix whose \operatorname{Gram} matrix x^{*}Jx is invertible (d: \det(x^{*}Jx) \ne 0). There is a map k from m \times b matrices to the unitary group U(J) such that y \mapsto k(y) (as a matrix) is smooth (C^{\infty} over \mathbb{R}) and, for every y near x, k(y)\cdot x\cdot c = y for some b \times b matrix c: elements of U(J) depending smoothly on y carry the span of x to that of y near x.
The argument in briefWith w =(x^{*}Jx)^{-1}x^{*} and the J-skew L(a) = (a x w - x w a^{*})J, the map a \mapsto \exp(L(a))(a - L(a)) has derivative 1 at a = 1; its smooth local inverse A gives k(y) = \exp(L(A(p(y)))) with p(y) = 1 -(x - y)wJ, since p(y)x = y.
