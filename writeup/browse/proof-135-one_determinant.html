lemma one_determinant

What it saysOne determinant. Let W be a finite-dimensional complex inner product space, \Theta a type (of twists), \theta : PrimePadicIso \to \Theta, and for each \vartheta \in \Theta a complex inner product space H(\vartheta) and a linear map u_{\vartheta} : W \to H(\vartheta); let o \in \Theta with u_o injective (hu). Let S be finite and x : S \to W linearly independent (i) with \dim W = |S| (hk), so x is a basis of W. Let \alpha, \delta, g be real numbers with |\sigma(\det \operatorname{Gram}(u_o\circ x))| \le \exp(\alpha) for every ring automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C} (h\alpha), \operatorname{Gram}(v) =(\langle v_s, v_t\rangle)_{s,t\in S}. Let \gamma : PrimePadicIso \to \mathbb{C} with \gamma(\iota) \ne 0 for all \iota (\gamma 0) and HeightLe g (\iota \mapsto |\gamma(\iota)|^{-2}) (h\gamma); let e : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R} with \sum_{l\in U} e(l) \le \delta for every finite set U of natural numbers (he); let \Lambda assign to every (\ell, \iota) \in PrimePadicIso and every ring homomorphism j : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_{\ell} a subset \Lambda(\iota, j) \subseteq H(\theta(\iota)); and let k \in \mathbb{N}. Assume (w): for all \iota, j, all x' \in \Lambda(\iota, j\circ \operatorname{conj}) and y' \in \Lambda(\iota, j), \lVert j(\langle x', y'\rangle)\rVert_{\ell} \le 1; and (l): for all \iota =(\ell, \iota) and j there is a complex S\times S matrix m with \exp(-e(\ell))\cdot \lVert j(\gamma(\iota))\rVert^{k} \le \lVert j(\det m)\rVert and \sum_{l\in S} m_{\mathit{il}}\cdot u_{\theta(\iota)}(x_l) \in \Lambda(\iota, j) for every i \in S. Then HeightLe (\alpha + 2\delta + k\cdot g) (\iota \mapsto normDet(u_{\theta(\iota)})^{2} / normDet(u_o)^{2}), where normDet(u) = \sqrt{\det}(u^{*}u) \ge 0 is the volume factor of a linear map (Mathlib's LinearMap.normDet), the quotient taken in \mathbb{C}. (The text reading of the signature also lists the section variables m and [Fintype m]; the statement's m is bound by \exists m in l, so Lean is expected not to take them.)
The argument in briefWrite the ratio as (ratio\cdot |\gamma |^{2k})\cdot |\gamma |^{-2k}. The second factor has height k\cdot g (h\gamma to the k-th power). The first has height \alpha + 2\delta with witness H = \det \operatorname{Gram}(u_o\circ x): at every \ell-adic j, the \operatorname{Gram} determinant of u_{\theta(\iota)}\circ x times the two determinants of the recombination matrices for j and j\circ \operatorname{conj} is the determinant of a matrix of pairings of integral vectors, hence of norm \le 1, and the two lower bounds on those determinants cost \exp(e(\ell)) each.
