lemma Level.exists_of_inner_besselVector_ne_zero

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma. If \langle\mathtt{besselVector}(Z,x),\mathtt{besselVector}(Z,y)\rangle\ne0, then there are u,v\in\mathsf{U} with u\cdot x\in K and (u\cdot x)^{-1}(v\cdot y)\in K(g).
The argument in briefA nonzero inner product has a coset c with both coordinates nonzero; the two nonzero phases give u,v\in\mathsf{U} with x a_c^{-1}\in u^{-1}K(g) and y a_c^{-1}\in v^{-1}K(g), whence the claims.
