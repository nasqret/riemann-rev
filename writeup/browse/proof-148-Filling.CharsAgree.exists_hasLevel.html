lemma Filling.CharsAgree.exists_hasLevel

What it saysLemma (agreeing characters force shared vectors up the flag). If \gamma\in G(E) satisfies CharsAgree(S,\gamma) and termDisc(S,\gamma)=0 (\mathsf{g}(\gamma)=|D_{\varrho+1}|^{2}-N^{2}|D_{\varrho}|^{2}=0 for the leading minors D_l of Z(\gamma)), then HasLevel(S,j,\gamma) for some j\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\} (normal form of level j,D_j\ne0, and \varrho<j iff \mathsf{g}(\gamma)\ne0; so in fact j\le\varrho).
The argument in briefTake j minimal with IsNormalForm(j,\gamma). Using hasCommonFlagVector_of_le one shows that a common vector of f and \gamma f supported below j vanishes; hence D_j\ne0 (a kernel vector of paddedLeading(j,Z) would give such a vector, with norm 0 by the middle-part identity), and \varrho<j is impossible since \mathsf{g}(\gamma)=0 would force \det Z(\gamma)=0.
