lemma chiOneInv_spec

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, an auxiliary pair O, a finite place w of E, a place A of \Omega over w (a), w not above \ell (l), k \in \mathbb{N} and s \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) such that s^{-1} acts on the residue field of A as the (q_w)^k-th power (IsFrob A (q_w^k) s^{-1}): chiOneInv O (s) = \iota \ell (\nu _w^{-1})^k, where \nu _w = AutChar.frobValue (O.a) r w = \chi (\varpi _w)\cdot q_w^{|r|/2} for \chi = bc(O.a)\cdot \upsilon ^{-|r|} (O.a = \upsilon ^{r_-}).
The argument in briefs is the local Artin image of an idele x at w of order -k; the avatar at s is \iota \ell of the character at x, which, the character being unramified at w, is its value at the uniformiser to the power k.
