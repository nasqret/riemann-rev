lemma Filling.sum_norm_amplitudeSum_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be a stably unramified number field with a CM structure, \mathfrak{s} a shape, S admissible filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s},i\in\{0,1\} and x\in\mathbb{C}[\mathsf{G}_i] a central idempotent. Then \sum_{a\in\mathtt{Classes}(S)\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}(S)}\lVert(\sum_{t\in\mathsf{G}_i}x(t)\cdot\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(S,\mathsf{S}(E)^{c},a_1,\iota_i(t)^{-1},b_1)(a_2,b_2)/\nu(S))_{b\in\mathtt{Classes}(S)\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}(S)}\rVert_{\ell^{2}}\le b(S)^{-4}\cdotcolumnDim(S): the \ell^{2} norms of the rows of the matrix \sum_t x(t)\cdot(off-\mathsf{S} amplitude sums at \iota_i(t)^{-1})/\nu sum to at most b^{-4}\cdotcolumnDim. (Notation: \mathsf{G}= GRes(S)=\prod_{i\in\{0,1\}}\mathsf{G}_i,\mathsf{G}_i=\prod_{w\in\mathtt{chosenPlaces}(S,i)}\operatorname{GL}_{\mathtt{BesselIndex}(\mathfrak{s})}(\mathbb{F}_w); \iota_i(s)= Pi.mulSingle(i,s)\in\mathsf{G} for s\in\mathsf{G}_i; amplitudeSum(S,T,c,s,c')\in\ell^{2}(\mathtt{BesselCoord}\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}) is the sum of \operatorname{amplitude}(S,c,c',\gamma) over the rational \gamma\in T that are terms over (c,s,c'), with the full Bessel functions; \mathsf{S}(E)= besselS of the rational middle form; \nu(S)= unipCovolume(S); \operatorname{energy}(A,\Omega)=\sum_{c,c'}\sum_{s\in\Omega}\lVert A(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu(S)^{2}; \mathsf{d}(S)= mainEnergy(S)= columnDim(S)^{2}/(\mathtt{besselDegree}(S)\cdot\operatorname{mass}(E,r(\mathfrak{s}))); \mathsf{d}_1(S)= besselEnergy(S)= \#reducedImage(S,\mathsf{S})\cdotcolumnDim(S); columnDim(S)= \#Classes(S)\cdot\#BesselCoord(S).)
The argument in briefLet Y(\sigma)=\sum_{c,c'}\lVert\sum_t x(t)\cdot\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(\mathsf{S}^{c},c,\iota_i(t)^{-1}\sigma,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu^{2}. Moving by an element of \mathsf{S} over (c,\sigma,p(c)) is a unitary change on the rows (a character times \otimes_v A_v), so Y(\sigma)=Y(1) for every \sigma in the reduced Bessel image \overline{S}; and \sum_{\sigma\in\overline{S}}Y(\sigma)\le\operatorname{energy}(\mathsf{S}^{c},\Omega_{i+1}) (convolution by the central idempotent x is a contraction) \le b^{-12}\mathsf{d}_1=b^{-12}\#\overline{S}\cdotcolumnDim. Hence Y(1)\le b^{-12}columnDim, and Cauchy–Schwarz over the columnDim rows gives \sum\lVert\text{row}\rVert\le\sqrt{\mathtt{columnDim}\cdot Y(1)}\le b^{-6}columnDim \le b^{-4}columnDim.
