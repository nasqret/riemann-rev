lemma exists_sharp_fitting_poly

What it saysFor \ell \ne 0, M, q \in \mathbb{N}, a number field E embedded in \Omega, finite types n and \sigma, commutative rings O_0 and O with O a discrete valuation ring of characteristic 0, \rho : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to M_n(O_0), a ring map e_0 : O_0 \to O, a finite set S of finite places, homomorphisms h_j : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \mathbb{Z}/q (j \in \sigma ) that are locally constant (hh), \rho modulo \ell ^M locally constant (hρ), a set L of relations and y_0 \in O^\sigma: there is a polynomial F in the variables \sigma over O with v_O(F(y_0)) \le length_O of the Selmer module of twistAtPoint h y_0 e_0 \rho with values in O/\ell ^M (unramified outside S, obeying the image of L under e_0), and such that for every discrete valuation ring O', ring map e : O \to O' and y \in O'^\sigma, the O'-length of the Selmer module of twistAtPoint h y (e\circ e_0) \rho modulo \ell ^M is at most v_{O'}(e(F)(y)). Taking O' = O, e the identity and y = y_0 gives equality at y_0: v_O(F(y_0)) is the O-length of the Selmer module of twistAtPoint h y_0 e_0 \rho modulo \ell ^M.
The argument in briefCorepresent the Selmer modules over the polynomial ring by finitely many coordinates and a module of relations R; a square matrix with entries in R evaluated at y_0 has determinant of valuation at most the length at y_0; its determinant F bounds the length at every specialisation.
