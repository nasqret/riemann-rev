lemma isTameType_of_roots

What it saysFor a prime \ell, a coefficient ring O, N, r \in \mathbb{N}, a property K of polynomials over O and a finite multiset M of integral elements of \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell with |M| \le N (b), each y \in M satisfying y^{\ell ^a - 1} = 1 for some 1 \le a \le r (k), and K holding for every Q \in O[X] vanishing on M (h): K is of tame type, IsTameType \ell r (N\cdot r) K (K holds for X^L - 1, L = \operatorname{lcm}(\ell ^a - 1 : 1 \le a \le r), and for some monic polynomial over \mathbb{Z}_\ell of degree \le N\cdot r).
The argument in briefEach y has a monic minimal polynomial over \mathbb{Z}_\ell of degree \le r (a root of unity of order \ell ^a - 1 lies in the unramified extension of degree a); X^L - 1 vanishes on M, and so does the product of the minimal polynomials, monic of degree \le N\cdot r.
