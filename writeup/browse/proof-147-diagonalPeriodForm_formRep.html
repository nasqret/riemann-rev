lemma Data.diagonalPeriodForm_formRep

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data, \vartheta an m-fold twist, j a natural number with h : j \le m and g a real point. For every finite point u of U(V_{j,j}), every form y at node (j, j) of the grid V and every form \varphi at node (j, j) of the grid Z: diagonalPeriodForm(\vartheta, h, g)(formRep(u)(y), formRep(U.block(gridDiagEquiv(D, j))(u, 1))(\varphi)) = diagonalPeriodForm(\vartheta, h, g)(y, \varphi): the period is unchanged when y is translated by u and \varphi by u \oplus 1.
The argument in briefSubstitute x \mapsto x\cdot u in the integral over [U(V_{j,j})] (right invariance of the quotient measure); the block point moves accordingly, and rationality of the forms absorbs the rest.
