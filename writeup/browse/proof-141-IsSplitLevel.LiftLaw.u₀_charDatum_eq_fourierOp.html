lemma IsSplitLevel.LiftLaw.u₀_charDatum_eq_fourierOp

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, P a split level for the prime N (instance taken by Lean), L a lift law for \pi, \pi' with constant \gamma (H) and \varphi \in \operatorname{ramified}(\pi) (h). Put C = \pi'(u_{0})(L[\mathbf{c}_e]\varphi). Then C = F'(L[\mathbf{b}_e]\varphi) and C = \gamma\cdot L[\mathbf{b}_e](F\varphi), where F = fourierOp(\pi), F' = fourierOp(\pi'), u_{0} = diagNInv, \mathbf{c}_e = charDatum and \mathbf{b}_e = lastColDatum at the last basis vector e.
The argument in briefExpand \mathbf{c}_e and \mathbf{b}_e as sums over residues; ramification kills the non-unit terms; each remaining term is matched by the equivariance of the law at \kappa_y.
