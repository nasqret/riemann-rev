lemma CMLattice.katz_divisibility_limit

What it saysSharpness of Katz's divisibility. Let d be a prime \equiv 7 mod 8, C a CM datum for d, p a prime with a p-adic place \iota_p, and n \in \mathbb{N}. There is f \in \mathfrak{o}[[\varphi]] (CMLattice.phiSeriesRing) with (p^{\rho_p - 1/(p-1)})^n \le |n!\cdot f_n|_p, where f_n is the n-th coefficient and \rho_p = nonsplitWeight d p. The witness f may depend on p, \iota_p and n; the statement is a lower bound for each n, not one series serving every n and not an equality.
The argument in briefSuppose not. Let c(k) be the normalized maximal k-th Taylor coefficient of the powers of \varphi; it is submultiplicative (addition law of \varphi), and c(n) < 1, so c(k) \to 0. Then the coefficients of the formal parameter S of exists_formal_parameter of degree \ge l are in the maximal ideal, so \overline{S} = S mod \mathfrak{m} is a polynomial P; but \overline{S} - \overline{S}^{p^e} = X, i.e. (X - X^{p^e})\circ P = P - P^{p^e} = X, is impossible for a polynomial (its degree is p^e\cdot \deg P \ne 1).
