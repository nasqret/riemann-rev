lemma exists_row_for_all_twists

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data for P, r and n finite types, O an auxiliary pair over E(D) with index types r \subset n, and \ell a prime with a fixed ring isomorphism \iota \ell : \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell (\iota : PadicIso \ell). Let c be labels at the chosen places of O that are apt for the trivial twist (IsApt O c 1, hO), b a family of eigenvalues (for every finite place w of E a sequence b(w) of complex numbers), J jumps (a multiset J(\sigma ) of integers for every complex embedding \sigma of E), and R : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to \operatorname{GL}_r(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell ) an \ell-adic representation of rank |r| with: (h) R is a labelled column IsLabelledColumn O |r| J c \varnothing R (R is a conjugate self-dual column of jumps J, empty extra level, and at each chosen place of O inertia is killed through the tame characters given by the roots of c); (s) for every g \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E), \iota \ell(b(x.w)(1)) tends to \operatorname{tr} R(g) along the Frobenius data x = (w, A, s) with s approaching g (mostFrobeniiAt); (hJ) the shifted jumps \sigma \mapsto {u - ((|r| - O.rankDiff)/2 - cmSign(\sigma )\cdot \text\{ weight \}_\mu (\sigma )) : u \in J(\sigma )} (\text\{ weight \}_\mu the weight of the character \mu of O at the place of \sigma; the half-difference (|r| - O.rankDiff)/2 is taken in \mathbb{Z} — |r| - O.rankDiff is a difference of integers, possibly negative, not a truncated subtraction of natural numbers, and it is even, |r| and O.rankDiff being odd) satisfy IsRowType D |r| O.rankDiff. Then there is an \ell-adic representation T of rank |r| with HasRow O b T: T is a row (IsRow D O.rankDiff \tau T for some jumps \tau), and for every tame character \vartheta of D, every auxiliary pair V over E(D) with index types r \subset n and character V.\mu = O.\mu \cdot \vartheta, every form y on V lifting b (V.Lifts y b), every Hecke system H for which y is an eigenform (IsEigenform y H), every representation S attached to H (Attached H S) and every g \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E): \operatorname{tr}(normalisedRep V S (g)) = ϑ.invAvatar(g)\cdot \operatorname{tr} T(g) + Σ_{0≤e<V.rankDiff} \operatorname{cyc}^{-1}(g)^e, where normalisedRep V S is S twisted by the character chiOneInv V and \operatorname{cyc}^{-1} = cycInv is the inverse \ell-adic cyclotomic character. (The statement uses \iota \ell, so Lean also takes the section variables \{\ell \}, [ThePrime \ell] and (\iota : PadicIso \ell), which the line census does not list: a prime \ell and a fixed ring isomorphism \iota \ell : \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell; to be confirmed with \#check.)
The argument in briefT is the twist of R by the avatar of \mu ^{-1}, a labelled column with the twisted labels, hence a row; the trace identity is proved at Frobenius elements, where both sides are read off the Hecke polynomials of the lift, and extended by continuity (limits along Frobenii).
