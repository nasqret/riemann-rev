lemma DividedLattice.reduceAt_conj_eq_one

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r and n finite types, O : AuxPair E r n, L : DividedLattice O with point \gamma, and j \in K_0(n) whose reduction modulo M^2 is 1 (t: O.chosenModulusSq.reductionK₀ j = 1). Then \gamma^{-1}\cdot j\cdot \gamma lies in K_0 (the witness h), and its reduction at every chosen place of O is 1.
The argument in briefEntrywise: j - 1 is divisible by \varpi^2, so the entries of \varpi^{-1}(\gamma^{-1}j\gamma - 1) are sums of products of entries of \varpi \gamma^{-1}, of \overline{\varpi}^{-1}\varpi^{-2}(j - 1) and of conjugates of entries of \varpi \gamma^{-1}, all integral, hence it is integral; hence \gamma^{-1}j\gamma = 1 + \varpi \cdot(integral) is in K_0 and reduces to 1 at the chosen places.
