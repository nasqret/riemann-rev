lemma fixes_emb_of_fixes_rootsOfUnity

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell, a ring map e : E \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell and g \in G_\ell that fixes every \zeta \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell with \zeta ^{N_1d} = 1 (H): g(e(x)) = e(x) for every x \in E.
The argument in briefE lies in \mathbb{Q}(\zeta _{N_1d}); e extends to it, and g fixes its generator, hence all of it.
