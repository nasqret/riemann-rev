lemma dvd_one_add_mul_pow_sub_one

What it saysFor a prime \ell, a commutative ring O, t, u \in O with u^{\ell ^k} = 1 (h), and M, N with k + M \le N (a): \ell divides (1 + t(u - 1) - 1)^{\ell ^k}, and \ell ^M divides (1 + t(u - 1))^{\ell ^N} - 1.
The argument in briefBinomial expansion modulo \ell of p-th powers, then lifting the exponent from k to N.
