Structured proof · Unit 147

lemma matchingPairs.translate_mem

What it saysLet F be a semilocal field, a, b, n finite index types, \chi a multiplicative character of F and e : b \sqcup a \simeq n. If p =(\Phi, \Psi) lies in matchingPairs(\chi, e) (h), then for every g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(F) the pair (\chi(\det g)\cdot \Phi(\cdot g), \Psi(g^{-1}\cdot)) also lies in matchingPairs(\chi, e): matched pairs are stable under the action of \mathrm{GL}_n(F).
The argument in briefBoth functions stay Schwartz–Bruhat with the right supports, and the matching identity at k follows from the one at g^{-1}k.
