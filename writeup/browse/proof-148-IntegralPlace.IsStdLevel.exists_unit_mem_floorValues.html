What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma.
