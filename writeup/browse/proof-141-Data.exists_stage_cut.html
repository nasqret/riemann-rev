lemma Data.exists_stage_cut

What it saysFor admissible data D and k \le 2\varrho + 1 (hk), let Y = gradingY(k) and B_f its finite Bessel group (the statement's let). There is a level A (an open subgroup of K_{0}, IsLevel) with D.columnLevel(k) \le \mathrm{inclXFin}_Y^{-1}(A), i.e. the image \mathrm{inclXFin}_Y(b) of every b \in D.columnLevel(k) lies in A, such that for every tame character \vartheta (A comes before \vartheta; each \vartheta has its own cut), with n = levelNorm(\vartheta) = \prod_{v\in Q} N(v) and \mathcal{J}_k = levelY(\vartheta, k), there is a finite set T of cosets of \mathcal{J}_k with: (i) T is stable under the elements of B_f that lie in A; (ii) every x with x\mathcal{J}_k \in T is integral at every place not dividing n (localLevel) and, if k = 0, lies in chosenLevel; (iii) for every x such that x\mathcal{J}_k \in T, or such that unfoldStage(\vartheta, \varphi, \xi, y, k)(g, x) \ne 0 on Y for some ramified column \varphi, real points \xi, finite points y with y_{0} \in chosenLevel and a real point g, the multiplicity coverMultiplicity(B_f, A, T, x\mathcal{J}_k) divides a power of n (as n \ge 1, such a multiplicity is non-zero).
The argument in briefFor k = 0 take A = chosenLevel; for k > 0 take A = K_{0}. In both cases apply exists_cut_coverMultiplicity_dvd; for k > 0 the compact set D consists of finitely many local corrections at Q (valuation profiles in [-k, k]) times K_{0}, and the points where stage k does not vanish are moved into D by B_f because their lattices have near lines (hasNearLines_of_unfoldStage_ne_zero) and then a Witt-type orbit argument (exists_mem_besselFin). (Lines 39–43 are the rest of the statement after its let Y :=; the line census reads them as part of the value, so they stand in the introduction.)
