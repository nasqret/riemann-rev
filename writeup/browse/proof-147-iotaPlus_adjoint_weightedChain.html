lemma Data.iotaPlus_adjoint_weightedChain

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data, z a grid, \vartheta an m-fold twist, i \le m (h) and y a form at node (0, 0) of grid z (of the triangle space). Put U = weightedChain(triangleOrder(z), triLift(D, z), \vartheta, 0, i)(y), the lift of y from node (0, 0) to (0, i) with each step weighted by triangleOrder, and \iota(b) = iotaPlus(\vartheta, z, b). Then: if i \ne 0, every \iota(i - 1)(\varphi) and U are fixed by the elements u of K_{0} whose reduction modulo triangleModulus(z, \vartheta) is 1; and for every form \varphi at node (0, m): \int \iota(i)(\varphi)(g)\cdot U(g) \mathit{dg} = \int \iota(0)(\varphi)(g)\cdot y(g) \mathit{dg} over the quotients of the spaces at (0, i) and (0, 0).
The argument in briefInduction on i: the descent and the lift through an edge family are adjoint (IsArrow.integral_mul_formLift_swap), and both transform under level elements by the grid character, which is 1 on the elements reducing to 1.
