lemma exists_labelIdem_eq_off_irreducible

What it saysLet k be a finite field, n a finite type and a a natural number with |n| = 3^{a+1} and 9 | |k| - 1; let c in k[X], and let S, T be sets of polynomials over k with at most one element each. Then there is P in k[X] such that (1) if \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(|n|, c), then \operatorname{IsPrimitiveLabel}(|n|, P) and P is not in the union of S and T; and (2) for every g in \mathrm{GL}_n(k) whose characteristic polynomial is not irreducible, the coefficients at g of \operatorname{labelIdem}(n, c) and of \operatorname{labelIdem}(n, P) in the group algebra C[\mathrm{GL}_n(k)] are equal.
The argument in briefIf c is not primitive, P = c. If c is primitive with root \xi of order q^r - 1 (r = |n| = 3^{a+1}), the three cubic twists c.\operatorname{scaleRoots}(x), x^3 = 1, are pairwise distinct (Frobenius orbits of \xi^3 have length r), so one of them avoids S and T; call its character \chi(\chi^3 = 1) and P = c.\operatorname{scaleRoots}(\operatorname{charName} \chi). By labelChar_mul_det, \Xi_P(g) = \Xi_c(g) \chi(\det g). If the coefficients differ at g, then \Xi_c(g) is nonzero, so the characteristic polynomial of g is a power Q^j of an irreducible Q (cuspidality); j divides 3^{a+1}, and j = 1 is excluded (\operatorname{charpoly} reducible) while 3 | j makes \det g a cube, so \chi(\det g) = 1, a contradiction.
