lemma Filling.tsum_integral_apply

What it saysFor a measurable space X with a measure \mu, a countable type J, a finite type I and measurable g_j:X\to\mathbb{C}^{I} (m) with \sum_j\int^{-}\lVert g_j\rVert d\mu<\infty (h), and a coordinate i\in I: (\sum_j\int g_j d\mu)_i=\int\sum_j g_j(x)_i d\mu(x).
The argument in briefEach integral commutes with the coordinate; sum and integral commute under absolute summability.
