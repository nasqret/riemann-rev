lemma coeffEmb_surjective

What it saysFor a prime \ell and a coefficient ring O: every \mathbb{Z}_\ell-algebra map O \to \mathbb{C}_\ell is coeffEmb O g for some g \in G_\ell (the map g \mapsto coeffEmb O g is surjective).
The argument in briefAn algebra map O \to \mathbb{C}_\ell extends to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell (O is algebraic over \mathbb{Z}_\ell , \mathbb{C}_\ell algebraically closed and torsion-free over O), and every such extension is an element of G_\ell.
