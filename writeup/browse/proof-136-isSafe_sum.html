lemma Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.isSafe_sum

What it saysFor sides S, S', a pairing P, \kappa \in \mathbb{N} and families u, v indexed by the letters with u_j \in letterPart(extLattice(S), j) and v_j \in letterPart(extLattice(S'), j) for every letter j (h): if IsSafe(P, \kappa, u_j, v_j) for every j (f), then IsSafe(P, \kappa, \sum_j u_j, \sum_j v_j).
The argument in briefComponents at different letters pair to zero (\zeta acts on them by different Teichmüller lifts, whose difference is a unit), so the pairing of the sums is the sum of the diagonal pairings.
