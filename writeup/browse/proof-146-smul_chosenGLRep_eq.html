lemma AuxPair.smul_chosenGLRep_eq

What it saysLet O be an auxiliary pair over E (W of rank r, V = I_n), \pi a representation of G_f (rank n) on a complex vector space, y a vector of \pi of level levelGroup(O) and character levelChar(O), k in K_0 (rank n) with reductions \tau = (\tau_j) at the places w_j of O, and s = (s_j) with s_j in \mathrm{GL}_r(k_{w_j}). Assume: at every chosen place j, \tau_j inclMatrix(\operatorname{incl}(O)) = inclMatrix(\operatorname{incl}(O)) s_j, and at every twist place j, \tau_j^{T} inclMatrix(\operatorname{incl}(O)) = inclMatrix(\operatorname{incl}(O)) s_j^{T}. Then (prod over all places j of O of \psi(O, w_j)(\det s_j))^{-1} chosenGLRep(O, \pi)((s_j) at the chosen places)(y) = \pi(k)(y).
The argument in briefWith S = \operatorname{diag}(s, 1) at M and 1 at Q, the element liftResidue(S)^{-1} k lies in the level of O (its reductions S^{-1} \tau fix the \operatorname{incl}-columns at M and lie in the parabolic at Q, by the block conditions), so it acts on y by the level character; applying \pi(liftResidue(S)) gives chosenGLRep(s) y times the character values.
