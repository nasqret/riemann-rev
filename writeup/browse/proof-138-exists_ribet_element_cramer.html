lemma exists_ribet_element_cramer

What it saysRibet's construction by Cramer's rule twice. Let O be a commutative ring, G a group, \sigma a nonempty finite type, N a finite type, R a commutative ring and L a commutative R- and O-algebra, S an R-subalgebra of the functions Y \to L, u_x : O[G] \to M_\sigma (O) (x \in Y) O-algebra maps, \kappa : O \to S a ring map compatible with the algebra maps into Y \to L (h_1), the functions x \mapsto (characteristic polynomial of u_x(g) read in L)_k in S for all g, k (h_2), \mathcal{J} an S-submodule of Y \to L, w \in O[G]^N, V : O[G]^N \to O[G] an O[G]-linear map, and \beta with \beta (x) = \det u_x(V(w)) (hβ). Then \beta \in S, and for every h \in O[G], M \in M_N(O[G]) and left ideal \mathfrak{a} of O[G] such that each V(M_p) lies in span{r : (x \mapsto \operatorname{tr}(u_x(r)\cdot u_x(g))) \in \mathcal{J} for all g} + \mathfrak{a}\cdot V(w): there is f \in S with f(x) = \det(u_x(M) flattened)\cdot \operatorname{tr} u_x(h) for every x with u_x(m\cdot h) = 0 for all m \in \mathfrak{a}, and f\cdot \beta \in \mathcal{J}.
The argument in briefRepresent S[G] on matrices over Y \to L; Amitsur puts all characteristic polynomial coefficients in S. The adjugate of \rho (V w) gives \beta \in S; the adjugate W of the block matrix of M gives a = \det \cdot 1 - w^{T} W m with V(M)\cdot W = \det; f = \operatorname{tr} \rho (a\cdot h); f\cdot \beta is a sum of traces of elements of the trace span, hence in \mathcal{J}.
