lemma Data.trace_heckeOnGood_eq

What it saysLet D be admissible data over P; s, s' signs on \operatorname{RowIndex}(D); V, V' hermitian spaces over E(D) with signs s, s'. Assume that for every infinite place v the doubled parameter \operatorname{paramW}(D, v, .) has gap h(D) for the signs s and for the signs s' (so \operatorname{principalForms}(D, V) and \operatorname{principalForms}(D, V') are finite-dimensional), and assume \operatorname{HasRigidLabels}(D, V) and \operatorname{HasRigidLabels}(D, V'). Then for every p in \operatorname{heckeAlgebra}(D) the trace of \operatorname{heckeOnGood}(D, V)(p) on \operatorname{goodForms}(D, V) equals the trace of \operatorname{heckeOnGood}(D, V')(p) on \operatorname{goodForms}(D, V').
The argument in briefRigid labels let one twist every primitive label at each chosen place to a primitive label that occurs neither for V nor for V', agreeing with it off irreducible characteristic polynomials. On good forms p acts as p composed with e_0; subtracting the twisted idempotents (which kill every eigenpart) and splitting by eigenparts, the trace becomes a sum of coefficients times sums \langle b_i, R(h) b_i\rangle over orthonormal bases, where only group elements h with an irreducible characteristic polynomial at a chosen place survive; for these the comparison of simple trace formulas gives the same value on V and V'.
