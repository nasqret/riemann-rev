Columns and the chain of theta lifts: levels and branching on the spaces X_j, short ramified columns, divisibility of the chain at each prime (marked primes and scaled Hecke operators), and size bounds

note and structured proofs · 105 declarations · 46 structured proofs · 1 note

The folder New/Columns. Columns are algebraic forms on the definite space X_0 of weight (κ, e_0) at the level Data.columnLevel 0 (principal at the chosen places, last row ≡ (0,…,0,1) mod π, ππ̄ = N); the ramified columns are the image of ∏_{w|π}(1 − z_w), and they are spanned by short columns (integral everywhere, all conjugates of size ≤ exp(fa(log b)³)). For each prime p and each integer β < g(p) − err(p) + 1 there are one exponent l ≤ err(p)·dim Column and one O_ι-submodule S of columns, of small index in the integral columns in the sense of IndexDvd (a determinant condition: every finite family of integral columns has a square matrix over O_ι with determinant dividing p^l whose row combinations lie in S), such that for every twist ϑ of conductor prime to p and every good Ψ for ϑ the chain of theta lifts B_μ(ϑ, Ψ) maps the ramified columns of S to forms divisible by p^β (Data.exists_indexDvd_Bmu_divisible), using jet exponents along the chain, marked sets of primes at inert p and scaled Hecke operators of small elementary divisors; and the untwisted chain enlarges squared norms of all conjugates by at most an explicit factor (Data.weylNormSq_Bmu_le). The size bookkeeping (row lengths, factorials, class numbers, sum of allowed losses ≤ budget) is also here.
