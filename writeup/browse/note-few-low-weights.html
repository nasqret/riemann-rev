For a prime p, r, N \in \mathbb{N} and a predicate K on O[X]: IsTameType(p, r, N, K) means that K holds for X^{M} - 1 with M = \operatorname{lcm}\{p^{j} - 1 : 1 \le j \le r\}, and for the image in O[X] of some monic q \in \mathbb{Z}_p[X] of degree \le N.

For a Kummer chart k, \zeta \in O with \zeta^{p^{f} - 1} = 1, a filter F on \Gamma every member of which contains \Gamma' (hF: the principal filter of \Gamma' is \le F; \Gamma' itself need not belong to F — F = \top is allowed), and a lattice L with InClass(F, \bot, U, L) (h: L admissible for the periods fixed near 1 and F-almost everywhere, with jumps in U): universalSide(k, \zeta, hF, h) is the side whose lattice datum is L with the letter sets letterJumps(k, \zeta, U, \cdot) and embedding jump sets U, whose lattice C is Z_f(L) (the crystalline cocycles, finitely generated) with the trivial action, and whose cocycle map \alpha is the identity.

lemmaLift.KummerChart.few_low_jumps

Theorem A of the note · unit 136 · New/Lift/ExponentTheorems.lean, lines 69–99

Length bound for crystalline classes with few low weights. Let j : \Gamma \to G_p be an open embedding (ho), O a p-adic integer ring, L a lattice over O, U, U' jump sets indexed by the embeddings O \to \mathbb{C}_p, k an unramified Kummer chart (hk) with residue degree f, \zeta \in O with \zeta^{p^{f} - 1} = 1, p \ge 5 (hp), a, \eta, W \in \mathbb{N}, b : letters \to \mathbb{N}, \varepsilon : letters \to \mathbb{Z}^\times, with b(i) + W + \eta \le (p - 3)a (ha), \eta \ge 1 (hη), 6(a + \eta + W) < b(i) (hb) and NoMultipleNear(p, 6(a + \eta + W), b(i)) (hs) for every letter i, L in InClass(\top, \bot, U') (hL), and letterJumps(k, \zeta, U, i) in [-\eta, \eta] \cup (-\varepsilon(i))[b(i), b(i) + W], letterJumps(k, \zeta, U', i) in [-\eta, \eta] \cup \varepsilon(i)[b(i), b(i) + W] for every i (hV). Then: (1) if no u \in U(g) \cup U'(g) lies in [-a, 0) for any g, or U'(g) \subseteq [-\eta, 0] for every g, then Z_f(L) \subseteq cupOrthogonal(InClass(\top, \bot, U), L); (2) for every m with \sum_{u\in[-\eta, \eta]} \operatorname{jump}(L, g, u) \le m for every g: relLength(Z_f(L), cupOrthogonal(InClass(\top, \bot, U), L)) \le 11\cdot f\cdot(m(a + 1) + \mathrm{rank}_O(L)\cdot(W + 1))\cdot\mathrm{length}_O(O/pO), relLength(S, T) being the O-length of S/(S \cap T). Conclusion (2) does not assume the alternative of (1).

lemmaLift.tame_types_exponent

Theorem B of the note · unit 136 · New/Lift/ExponentTheorems.lean, lines 107–139

Tame types. Let j : \Gamma \to G_p be an open embedding (ho) onto G_p (hj surjective), O a p-adic integer ring, r, N \in \mathbb{N}, L a lattice over O, U, U' jump sets, F a filter on \Gamma and K a predicate on pairs (s, polynomial over O) such that L \in InClass(F, (s, Q) \mapsto K(s^{-1}, Q), U') (hL) and IsTameType(p, r, N, K(s)) for every s with j(s) in inertia (hK). Let \eta, W, b \in \mathbb{N} and a sign \varepsilon with U(g) \subseteq [-\eta, \eta] \cup (-\varepsilon)[b, b + W] and U'(g) \subseteq [-\eta, \eta] \cup \varepsilon[b, b + W] for every embedding g (hU), and b + 6(W + \eta) + r^{2}(N + 1)^{2} + 2 < p (hp). Then for every a \in Z_f(L): p^{3r^{2}(N+1)^{2} + 150(\eta + W + 1)}\cdot a \in cupOrthogonal(InClass(F, K, U), L).

lemmaLift.KummerChart.PairingSide.relLength_deepConstants_le

Lemma 3.1 of the note · unit 136 · New/Lift/FewLowJumps.lean, lines 35–94

Let the chart be unramified (hk), S a side, i a letter, c, c' exponent functions, \eta \in \mathbb{N}, and n, r : letters \to \mathbb{N} such that, at every letter j, f_k\cdot\sum_{u\in[-\eta, \eta]} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le n(j) (hn) and f_k\cdot\sum_{u\in U_j} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le r(j) (hr) (f_k the residue degree of the chart, Y the lattice of S), and a, A, B \in \mathbb{N}, i' = \sigma^{-a}(i) (f). Suppose: no integer in the open interval (c(0, v), c(0, v) + p^{a}) is divisible by p, for every integer v (ha); for every b' and u \in U_{\sigma^{-b'}(i)}: -u \le c(b', u), and c'(b', u) \le c(b', u) if b' \ne a (hc); c'(a, \cdot) is antitone on U_{i'} (ho); and \sum_{n'\in[c(a,u), c'(a,u))} \operatorname{cost}(a, n') \le A + (B if u \in [-\eta, \eta], else 0) for every u \in U_{i'} (hA). Then the relative length relLength(deepConstants(S, i, 0, c), deepConstants(S, i, 0, c')) (the length of the first modulo its intersection with the second) is at most A\cdot r(i') + B\cdot n(i').

lemmaLift.KummerChart.PairingSide.exists_relLength_deepConstants_le

Lemma 3.2 of the note · unit 136 · New/Lift/FewLowJumps.lean, lines 101–143

Let the chart be unramified (hk), p \ge 5 (hp), S a side, a, \eta, W \in \mathbb{N}, b, n, r : letters \to \mathbb{N} and \varepsilon : letters \to \mathbb{Z}^\times such that for every letter j: b(j) + W + \eta \le (p - 3)\cdot a (ha); 6(a + \eta + W) < b(j) (hb); NoMultipleNear(p, 6(a + \eta + W), b(j)) (hs: p divides no nonzero integer within 6(a + \eta + W) of 0, b(j) or -b(j)); the jump set U_j of S lies in [-\eta, \eta] \cup \varepsilon(j)\cdot[b(j), b(j) + W] (hU: IsInJumpWindow(\eta, \varepsilon(j), b(j), W, U_j)); f\cdot\sum_{u\in[-\eta, \eta]} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le n(j) (hn); and f\cdot\sum_{u\in U_j} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le r(j) (hr) (f the residue degree of the chart, Y the lattice of S). (\eta \ge 1 is not assumed.) Then for every letter i: relLength(range(constantAt(S, i)), deepConstants(S, i, 0, c_i)) \le (2a + 1)\cdot n(i) + 3(W + 1)\cdot r(\sigma^{-1}(i)) + 6a\cdot n(\sigma^{-1}(i)), where c_i = raisedAtE(2a + \eta) if \varepsilon(i) = 1, and otherwise c_i(1, u) = W + 1 - u + (2a if |u| \le \eta, else 0), c_i(0, u) = b(i) + W + 2a, c_i(l, u) = -u for l \ge 2.

lemmaLift.KummerChart.PairingSide.exists_scaledCupBounds_few_low_jumps

Proposition 3.3 of the note · unit 136 · New/Lift/FewLowJumps.lean, lines 148–163

Few low jumps. Let the chart be unramified (hk), p \ge 5 (hp), S a side, a, \eta, W \in \mathbb{N} with \eta \ge 1 (h\eta; only this lemma of the file assumes it), b, n, r : letters \to \mathbb{N} and \varepsilon : letters \to \mathbb{Z}^\times such that for every letter j: b(j) + W + \eta \le (p - 3)\cdot a (ha); 6(a + \eta + W) < b(j) (hb); NoMultipleNear(p, 6(a + \eta + W), b(j)) (hs); the jump set U_j of S lies in [-\eta, \eta] \cup \varepsilon(j)\cdot[b(j), b(j) + W] (hU); f\cdot\sum_{u\in[-\eta, \eta]} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le n(j) (hn); and f\cdot\sum_{u\in U_j} jumpMultiplicity(Y, j, u) \le r(j) (hr) (f the residue degree of the chart, Y the lattice of S). Then there is a \mathbb{Z}_p-submodule V of C with relLength(C, V) \le 11\cdot\sum_j (n(j)(a + 1) + r(j)(W + 1)) such that for every side T whose jump sets lie in the opposite windows (IsInJumpWindow(\eta, -\varepsilon(j), b(j), W, U^{T}_{j}) for every j), every pairing P of T and S, every c in the lattice of T and every d \in V: ScaledCupBounds(P, 0, c, d).

classLift.TameChart

Definition 2.3 of the note · unit 136 · New/Lift/SparseThickLetters.lean, lines 27–33

For a prime p and a group \Gamma over G_p, a tame chart d for \Gamma is a Kummer chart for \Gamma together with: \zeta_E \in \Lambda = W(F_{p^{f}}) whose zeroth Witt coordinate is a primitive e-th root of unity (e the index of the chart); an element s = tameGen of \Gamma with s^{k}\cdot z = \zeta_E^{k}\cdot z for every k, (j(s), 0) a Weil pair (j(s) in inertia), and j(s^{k}) fixing \Lambda for every k; an element \varpi fixed by j(g) for every g \in \Gamma', with \varpi^{e} = p; p \nmid e; and \Gamma' normal in \Gamma.
