lemma Parameters.nonempty_infinitePlace_equiv_finPlace

What it saysFor parameters P, an exponent s, a prime p (instance hp), g : p \equiv 1 \bmod N_1(P, s)\cdot d(P), an integer a of E(P, s) and ha : a\cdot \operatorname{conj}(a) = p\cdot c for a natural number c coprime to p: there is a bijection between the infinite places \Phi (P, s) and the finite places w of E(P, s) with a \in w and p \in w.
The argument in briefFix w above p. The map v \mapsto (v \bullet w or its conjugate, whichever contains a) is a bijection: the action of \Phi on the places above p is simply transitive up to conjugation, and exactly one of a place and its conjugate contains a.
