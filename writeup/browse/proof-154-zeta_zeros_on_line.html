lemma New.zeta_zeros_on_line

What it saysIf DirichletLNonvanishingRightOfHalf holds (hypothesis h), then RiemannZetaZerosOnLine holds: every complex number s with \zeta (s) = 0, s not of the form -2(n + 1) for a natural number n, and s \ne 1, has \operatorname{Re} s = 1/2 (\zeta is Mathlib's riemannZeta).
The argument in briefModulo 1 the L-function is \zeta; after s = 0 and the negative even integers are disposed of, a nontrivial zero left of 1/2 is reflected by the functional equation to a zero right of 1/2 (the qualifier 'nontrivial' added after the second reading, a second-reader message).
