lemma IntegralPlace.exists_isLatticeLevel

What it saysLemma. At a finite place with integers R (A semilocal with involution and integral place structure P), let N\in R with \overline{N}=N (the whole Gram matrix of latMiddleGram(\mathfrak{s},N) hermitian invertible), g a unit with g\in R and \overline{g}=g, and k=|\iota|+2m+1. Then there is a level F' of latMiddleGram(\mathfrak{s},N) with m pairs which is a lattice level (IsLatticeLevel(P,F')) and has F'.g=g.
The argument in briefBuild an explicit integral hyperbolic basis E of latMiddleGram(\mathfrak{s},N) (from an element of norm -1 and the reindexing of \kappa\setminus\{k_0\}), take \theta the image of the flat chart measure under the Eichler orbit map and \mu H the normalised Haar measure of the integral points of \mathsf{H} (inverted), and verify the level axioms and the lattice-level properties.
