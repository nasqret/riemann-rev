lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.exists_coordRetract

What it saysFor a lattice datum L there is an S-linear map r : kisinLocAinf(L) \to kisinLoc(L) with r(a\cdot x) = \lambda_{0}(a)\cdot x for every a \in A_{\mathrm{inf}} and x \in kisinLoc(L) (with a\cdot x \in kisinLocAinf), \lambda_{0} = \operatorname{coord}_{0} : A_{\mathrm{inf}} \to S the chosen S-linear coordinate that is the identity on S.
The argument in briefDefine r on A_{\mathrm{inf}} \otimes_{S} kisinLoc by a \otimes x \mapsto \lambda_{0}(a)x and descend along the surjection onto the span kisinLocAinf; well defined because \lambda_{0} preserves relations (frame theorem).
