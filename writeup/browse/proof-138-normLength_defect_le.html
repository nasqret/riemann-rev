lemma normLength_defect_le

What it saysFor a prime \ell, a coefficient ring O, a group \Gamma with j : \Gamma \to G_\ell of open image, a potentially crystalline Galois lattice L over O for \Gamma (hT), a submodule S of the cocycles \Gamma \to L, k \in \mathbb{N} and y \ge 0 with \operatorname{rk}_O L\cdot (2k) \le y (hk) and \ell ^k\cdot c \in S for every c \in Z_f(L) (h): the normalised O-length of Z_f(L)/(Z_f(L) \cap S) is at most [G_\ell : j(\Gamma )]\cdot y.
The argument in briefZ_f(L)/(Z_f \cap S) is a quotient of Z_f/\ell ^k Z_f \cong (O/\ell ^k)^{\operatorname{rk} Z_f}, of normalised length k\cdot \operatorname{rk} Z_f, and \operatorname{rk} Z_f \le (1 + [G_\ell : j(\Gamma )])\cdot \operatorname{rk} L \le 2[G_\ell : j(\Gamma )]\cdot \operatorname{rk} L.
