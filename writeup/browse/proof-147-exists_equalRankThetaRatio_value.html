lemma exists_equalRankThetaRatio_value

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type and carries Weil data, n a finite index type, s signs, W : Space(E, n, s), \mu weights and k a natural number with |n| = 2k + 2\cdotnegCount(s) (hk) and k \le \mu(v) at every infinite place v (hm). There are a rapid real datum v and a constant C \ne 0 such that equalRankThetaRatio(hypSum(Unit, W), \mathfrak{c}, v) = C for every split tame character \mathfrak{c} of weight \mu.
The argument in briefCompare W with J = I_{2k} plus planes. If all ratios vanished, the descended exchange identity at the standard datum would give a contradiction; otherwise the two exchange identities between J and the zero space, multiplied, show that the square of the ratio is B\cdot C\cdot |\langle \text{vac} at 0\rangle |^{2}, the same for every \mathfrak{c}.
