lemma Parameters.exists_isJetConjugate_generator

What it saysFor parameters P there is a natural number C such that for every exponent s there is a family B \colon \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{C}/K) \to \mathbb{A}_f(E) (E = E(P, s)) with the following property for every finite index type n, signs \varepsilon and standard hermitian space V over E of rank n (instance [V.IsStd]): (i) there is c \in \mathbb{C}, c \ne 0, such that |\iota_p(c\cdot vac(\operatorname{finVec} x))| \le 1 for every prime p, every p-adic place \iota_p and every finite adelic vector x, and |\sigma(c\cdot vac(\operatorname{finVec} x))| \le \exp(C\cdot N_1^2)^{|n|} for every \sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{C}/K) and every x (vac = Space.vacElem V); and (ii) there is F \in \mathcal{W}_f (V.finitePart) with \theta(F) = 1 (\theta the theta functional), \mathcal{W}_f \subseteq the \mathbb{C}-span of the translates \varrho(\operatorname{finVec} x)F, and for every \sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{C}/K) there are t \in \mathbb{C} and F' \in \mathcal{W}_f with |t|^2 = \Omega^{-1} (\Omega = \operatorname{bigOmega}(P, s)) such that for every finite adelic vector x: \sigma applied to the coefficients of T_{\mathrm{per}}(\varrho(\operatorname{finVec} x)F) equals T_t(\varrho(\operatorname{finVec}(B_\sigma \cdot x))F') with every variable (\tau, k) renamed to (\pi^{-1}(\tau), k), \pi = \operatorname{placePerm}(s, \sigma) (Space.IsJetConjugate \sigma (placePerm s \sigma) per t (\varrho(\operatorname{finVec} x)F) (\varrho(\operatorname{finVec}(B_\sigma \cdot x))F'): map \sigma(T_{\mathrm{per}} f) = rename (\pi^{-1} \times \operatorname{id}) (T_t f')), per = \operatorname{jetScale}(P, s).
The argument in briefTake for F the vector of exists_finitePart_of_isTightFrame attached to the frame (\zeta^i) and the curve \mathcal{E}; its vacuum expansion writes c\cdot vac as a sum of translates of F. The constant C comes from a bound M for |U_y\theta | on the finitely many Galois conjugates of \mathcal{E} (a_1 is algebraic). For \sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{C}/K), complex_multiplication_theta (the authors' soft form of the main theorem of complex multiplication) gives \Omega_\sigma, approximants a_S \in K whose diagonal images cluster at a finite adele \beta_\sigma, and the conjugate theta series; the vector F' is the analogous vector for \sigma \mathcal{E}, t = \sigma(s_1)/\Omega_\sigma \cdot \sqrt{2\pi /\sqrt{d}}, and the jet identity is checked on the dense set of x = \alpha^{-1}\gamma by comparing the product formulas factor by factor (the linear forms have coefficients s_1/N\cdot \operatorname{conj} \Phi_\tau(w) and \sigma(s_1)/N\cdot \operatorname{conj} \Phi_\tau(w)).
