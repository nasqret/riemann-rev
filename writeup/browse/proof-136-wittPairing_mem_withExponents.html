lemma Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.wittPairing_mem_withExponents

What it saysExponents add. Let A, L, C be lattice data, b : A \times L \to C an O-bilinear map with b(gy, gx) = g\cdot b(y, x) for all g \in \Gamma (hb), i a letter, c, c', c'' exponent functions, y \in withExponents(A, i, c') (hy), x \in withExponents(L, i, c) (hx), and suppose (H) that c''(a, v) \le c(a, u') + c'(a, u) for every a, u \in U^{A}_{\sigma^{-a}(i)}, u' \in U^{L}_{\sigma^{-a}(i)} and v \in U^{C}_{\sigma^{-a}(i)} with u + u' \le v. Then wittPairing(b)(y, x) \in withExponents(C, i, c'').
The argument in briefA form \mu of C applied to b(y, x) is expanded by the Hodge basis criterion on L into products \sigma(y)\nu(x) with \sigma a form of A of the complementary type; the Kisin, letter and filtration conditions follow from those of y and x.
