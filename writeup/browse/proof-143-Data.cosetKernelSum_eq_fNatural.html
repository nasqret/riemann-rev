lemma Data.cosetKernelSum_eq_fNatural

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D, a finite type a and a lift index j: there are C (not shown to be nonzero) and c : Twist \to \mathbb{C} such that for every twist \vartheta: |c(\vartheta)|^2 = C/\operatorname{heckeValueProd} j \vartheta, and for every CarrierIndex \times a matrix u and a \times BigIndex j matrix w over O_E/\overline{\varpi}^2 and every choice i \in MemberIndex j with u\cdot w = i.1, and every point g: fNatural j i g = c(\vartheta)\cdot \operatorname{cosetKernelSum} (D.auxPair \vartheta j) (bigChar \vartheta j) w (inl \circ inl) (lineMultiplier \vartheta j i) g.
