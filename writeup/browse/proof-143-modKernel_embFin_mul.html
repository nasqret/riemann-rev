lemma modKernel_embFin_mul

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, L and N finite types, a and b characters with weights e and e' and tame parts \psi, \psi' (ha, hb), \mathfrak{m} a modulus, \Psi a function on matrices modulo \mathfrak{m}, Q a polynomial, \gamma \in U(I_L)(E), \gamma' \in U(I_N)(E) rational points, and g, h finite adelic points of U(I_L), U(I_N). Then modKernelI (stdSpace E L) a b \mathfrak{m} \Psi Q (1, \gamma \cdot g) (\gamma'\cdot h) = (\prod_\tau \Phi_\tau(\det \gamma')^{-e_\tau }\cdot \Phi_\tau(\det \gamma)^{e'_\tau })\cdot \operatorname{modKernelI} (stdSpace E L) a b \mathfrak{m} \Psi (twoSidedSubst (\Phi(\gamma'^{-1})) (\Phi(\gamma)) Q) (1, g) h, where \Phi_\tau is the embedding of the CM type at \tau (applied entrywise) and \gamma, \gamma' act on the finite arguments through their finite images.
The argument in briefMove the rational points to the infinite places by the invariance of the theta kernel, where they act by the real Weil representation on the Fock vector.
