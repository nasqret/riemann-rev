lemma AuxPair.pairWeil_upsilon

What it saysLet E be a CM field with Weil data, O an auxiliary pair over E with W of rank n and V = I_{n'}, k in K_0 of rank n (for W), k' in K_0 of rank n' (for V), and j in auxLevel(O) (families in \prod_w \mathrm{GL}_{n'}(k_w) over the places of O that fix the incl(O)-basis vectors at M and preserve the span of the others at Q). Assume the reductions of k' at the places of O equal firstBlockHom(O, incl(O)) of the reductions of k at the chosen places, times j. Then \omega(k, k')(auxDatum(O)) = \bigl(\bigl(\prod over the chosen places w_i of \psi(O, w_i)(\det(k \bmod w_i))\bigr)^{-1} auxLevelChar(O)(j)\bigr) auxDatum(O), with \omega the Weil representation of pairThetaKernel(O). Spelled out, auxLevelChar(O)(j) = \prod over the twist places w in Q of \psi(O, w)(\det j_{w,00})^{-1}, j_{w,00} the \mathrm{incl}(O) \times \mathrm{incl}(O) block of j_w: the chosen places and the twist places both contribute inverse character values.
The argument in brief(k, k') acts on the datum by translating its residue function by k'^{-1} and multiplying by character values. At the places of Q the reduction of k'^{-1} is the parabolic j^{-1}, which multiplies the incl-row determinant by \det(j_{00})^{-1}, giving \psi_w(\det j_{00})^{-1} at each w in Q, that is auxLevelChar(O)(j); at the places of M it is (\operatorname{diag}(k, 1)\, j)^{-1}, j fixing the incl-columns, which moves the inclusion matrix by k^{-1} (giving the factors \psi(\det k)^{-1}). Both kinds of places contribute inverse character values.
