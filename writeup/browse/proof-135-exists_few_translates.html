Structured proof · Unit 135

lemma exists_few_translates

What it saysFew translates. Let \Theta be an index type, V a nonzero finite-dimensional complex vector space, W a finite-dimensional complex inner product space, B =(B_s)_{s\in \Theta} a family of linear maps V \to W and \eta > 0 real (h\eta) with IsSpread B \eta (h). Then there is a finite set t \subseteq \Theta with |t|\cdot \dim V \le 5\cdot \dim W\cdot \log(1 + 1/\eta) and (1 - 4\eta)\cdot \dim W \le \dim(\sum_{s\in t} B_s(V)), the dimension of the span of the images of the B_s with s \in t (both inequalities in \mathbb{R}).
The argument in briefGreedy covering with a logarithmic potential \psi(F) = 5\cdot \dim W\cdot \log(1 + codim F/(\dim W\cdot \eta)). Among finite t with |t|\cdot \dim V + \psi(span of the images) \le 5\cdot \dim W\cdot \log(1 + 1/\eta) (\varnothing qualifies) take one whose span T(t) is maximal. If \dim T(t) <(1 - 4\eta)\cdot \dim W, the spread index s for F = T(t) lowers \psi by at least \dim V when added (\log x \le x - 1), so t \cup \{s\} still qualifies and maximality forces B_s(V) \subseteq T(t), contradicting the spread bound.
