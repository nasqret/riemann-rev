lemma integral_integral_sum_eq

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type; n, n', n_{1}, n_{2} finite; A : Space(E, n', s'), U_{1} : Space(E, n_{1}, s_{1}), U_{2} : Space(E, n_{2}, s_{2}); \mu the product of the quotient measures of U_{1} and U_{2}. For families c, G of complex numbers and F of functions on (U(U_{1}) \times U(U_{2})) \times U(A) indexed by n: if every F(i) with c(i) \ne 0 is integrable for \mu \times (quotient measure of A) with integral G(i), then \int_{x} \int_{y} \sum_{i} c(i)\cdot F(i)(x, y) \mathit{dy} d\mu(x) = \sum_{i} c(i)\cdot G(i).
The argument in briefFubini and linearity over the finite sum.
