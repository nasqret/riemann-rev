lemma eq_zero_of_heckePolyOp_eq_zero

What it saysLet E be a CM field, m a finite type, t signs on m, F a complex function on the adelic unitary group G(E, t) of rank m, w a split finite place (w differs from its conjugate) and X a complex number. Assume F is spherical at w (fixed by right translation by the elements of K_0 that are 1 away from w and its conjugate), the Hecke polynomial operator heckePolyOp(finRightRep(E, t), w, X) (sum over i of (-X)^{i} q^{i(i-1)/2} T_{w,i}, q the norm of w) kills F, q^{|m|+1} |X| \le 1, and |F| is bounded. Then F = 0.
The argument in briefThe Hecke relation writes F(g) as minus the sum over nonzero subspaces U of heckePolyCoeff(X, U) times translates of F; by the q-binomial theorem the sum of the absolute values of these coefficients is at most \prod(1 + q^{l} |X|) - 1 < 1 when q^{m+1}|X| \le 1, so the maximum principle gives |F| \le 0.
