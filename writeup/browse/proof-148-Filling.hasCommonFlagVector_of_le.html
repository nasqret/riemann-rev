lemma Filling.hasCommonFlagVector_of_le

What it saysLemma. Let \gamma\in G(E) satisfy CharsAgree(S,\gamma), let a\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho\} and c,c'\in E, and suppose HasCommonFlagVector(\gamma,c,c',a): there are coefficient vectors x,y with f\cdot x=\gamma f\cdot y (f the frame (e_1,\ldots,e_{\varrho},z)), x_a=c,y_a=c' and x_b=y_b=0 for b>a. Then c\cdot\overline{c}=c'\cdot\overline{c}', and HasCommonFlagVector(\gamma,c,c',b) holds for every b\ge a.
The argument in briefDownward induction on a. At the top index the z-coordinates of a common vector have equal norms. Below, given a common vector at a, the agreement of the characters on the Eichler elements built from it shows that the coefficients at a can be cleared, so that a common vector at a+1 exists, and the induction hypothesis applies.
