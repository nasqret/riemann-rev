lemma eq_zero_of_mem_evenClasses

What it saysFor a prime \ell, a stably unramified CM field E embedded in \Omega, a coefficient ring O (the ring of integers of a finite Galois extension of \mathbb{Q}_\ell inside \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell), a set S of finite places of E, natural numbers r and e, a function c : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to (O/\ell ^r O)^1 (functions from a one-point set to O/\ell ^r O) and an element b \in (O/\ell ^r O)^1: suppose (k) c \in evenClasses O S r e, i.e. c is a locally constant cocycle for the character g \mapsto cycChar \Omega \ell (g)^{-e} with values in O/\ell ^r O (the Tate twist tateTwist \ell E O e), unramified outside the places above \ell and S \cup \overline{S}, with c(g^c) = c(g) for all g and c = 0 on shaLocalSet \ell E 1; and suppose (h) u(b) = 0 for every additive map u : (O/\ell ^r O)^1 \to \mathbb{Z}/\ell ^r such that g \mapsto u(c(g)) is a continuous 1-cocycle of \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) with values in \mathbb{Z}/\ell ^r on which g acts by the scalar (cycCharMod \Omega (\ell ^r) g)^{-e}, i.e. u\circ c \in Z1(charRep (\mathbb{Z}/\ell ^r) ((cycCharMod \Omega (\ell ^r))^e)^{-1}). Then b = 0. (cycCharMod \Omega (\ell ^r) : \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to (\mathbb{Z}/\ell ^r)^\times is the action on the \ell ^r-th roots of unity.)
The argument in brief\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}-valued characters separate the points of (O/\ell ^r O)^1; as this module is killed by \ell ^r, every such character comes from an additive map u to \mathbb{Z}/\ell ^r, and u\circ c is automatically a continuous cocycle for (cycCharMod \Omega (\ell ^r))^{-e}, so the hypothesis h kills b.
