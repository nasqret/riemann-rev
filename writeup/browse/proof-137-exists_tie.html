lemma BaseLevel.exists_tie

What it saysIn the setting (\ell prime, \iota_\ell \colon \mathbb{C} \cong \overline{Q}_\ell, O the \ell-adic integers of \overline{Q}_\ell, the CM field E \subset \Omega, the nonempty rank type n with N = |n|, V standard of rank n, the weight \kappa, Weil data for E, J finite, \nu, c, the base datum D, the Taylor–Wiles places Q with places w_j, norms q_j \equiv 1 mod \ell^\nu, tame generators \tau_j and Frobenius elements s_j, every w_j off level for D at base level (hQ), a character a \colon \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E) \to O and representations N_x with N_x(g) = a(g)\cdot \rho_x(g) (hN) and a(\tau_j) = 1 for every j (ha)); w_Q denotes the family j \mapsto w_j, and \operatorname{traceElem}(h_{N}, \cdot, \cdot, \cdot), \operatorname{heckeAction}(D, w_Q) are taken at the level w_Q. Let j \in J, e \in O[X], t \in \mathbb{N} with t \ne 0 (ht) and T \in \operatorname{heckeAlgebra}(D, w_Q), and suppose (H): for every eigensystem x of level w_Q whose isotypic space is an eigensystem of base level (in \operatorname{Eigensystem}(D, ![])) and with T(x) \ne 0, \operatorname{HasRootString}(P_{x, w_j}, e, q_j, a(s_j)^{-1}, t) holds, P_{x, w_j} the Hecke polynomial of x at w_j (e \text{ and } a(s_j) mapped to \mathbb{C}): P_{x, w_j}(a(s_j)^{-1}X) = (X - 1)(X - q_j)\cdots(X - q_j^{t-1})\cdot S for some S dividing e. Let R be a ring and \sigma \colon \operatorname{integralEnd}(D, w_Q) \to R a ring homomorphism with \ell^\nu = 0 in R (hm). Let \rho = \operatorname{residueRep}(D, Q) \circ (\mathrm{GL}_N(k_{w_j}) \to \prod_i \mathrm{GL}_N(k_{w_i}), g \mapsto g at the place j) and \mathcal{N} = \sum_{g\in \mathrm{GL}_N(k_{w_j})} \rho(g). Then there is F \in \operatorname{localOps}(D, Q, j) such that (i) for every g \in \mathrm{GL}_N(k_{w_j}) there is Z \in \operatorname{integralEnd}(D, w_Q) with \sigma(c\cdot F\cdot(\rho(g) - 1)) = \sigma(\mathcal{N}\cdot Z) and \operatorname{heckeAction}(D, w_Q)(T)\cdot(\sum_{g'\in \prod_i \mathrm{GL}_N(k_{w_i})} \operatorname{residueRep}(D, Q)(g'))\cdot Z = 0; and (ii) for every g \in \operatorname{Gal}(\Omega /E), writing r = \sigma(\operatorname{heckeAction}(D, w_Q)(\operatorname{traceElem}(h_{N}, X - 1, \tau_j, g))): \sigma(\mathcal{N})\cdot r = 0 and (2\cdot N!)\cdot \sigma(F)\cdot r = (2\cdot N!)\cdot \sigma(\mathcal{N})\cdot \sigma(\operatorname{heckeAction}(D, w_Q)(\operatorname{traceElem}(h_{N}, (X - 1)\cdot e, s_j, g))).
The argument in briefApply exists_line_stabilizer_log at w_j to a unit \varepsilon whose Artin image is \tau_j, and build from the Hecke operator U of \operatorname{diag}(\varpi, 1, \ldots, 1) (\varpi with Artin image s_j) the operators B(f) = G(U)\circ \operatorname{weightedSum}(\rho', b)(f), G(X) = p(a(s_j)X), p = (X - q_j^{t-1})e. Then one_prime_first_order gives F; its hypothesis L\cdot B(1) = 0 follows from the chain lemma (base-level eigenforms with T \ne 0 are orthogonal to the image of G(U)), and its property (ii) is turned into the tie: \mathrm{GL}_N acts through \det, so \tau_j acts as the diamond operator, and on parahoric eigenvectors the trace with \tau_j - 1 against G(U) is the trace against G(s_j).
