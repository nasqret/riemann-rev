lemma primeError_eq_sum

What it saysFor a prime d, a natural number M \ne 0, a natural number A and a real number z with 0 < z (u) and z \le A (h): R_{d,M}(z) = \sum_{\nu=1}^{A} \lambda_{d,M}(\nu)\cdot(A/\nu - A/z)^{+}/A - \log z/\varphi(M) - \operatorname{Re} G_{d,M}(1), where (t)^{+} = \max(t, 0).
The argument in briefThe truncated sum up to z equals the positive-part sum up to A \ge z.
