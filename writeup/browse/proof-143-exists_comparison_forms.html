What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D over P and a lift index j (1 \le j \le m): there are \gamma : Twist \to \mathbb{C}, a \mathbb{C}-linear map A from tables (Tables j) to the good forms on U(W_j), and for every twist \vartheta a \mathbb{C}-linear map gl_\vartheta from tables to forms on V_j, such that: (i) for every \vartheta, \gamma(\vartheta) \ne 0 and |\gamma(\vartheta)/\gamma(1)|^2 = heckeValueProd j \vartheta / heckeValueProd j 1; (ii) for every \vartheta, (D.goodLift \vartheta j)^* \circ gl_\vartheta = \gamma(\vartheta)\cdot A; (iii) A is surjective; (iv) for every \vartheta and every good form y, gl_\vartheta(A^* y) is tested (AuxPair.IsTested _ e_0 (range of goodLift \vartheta j)^\perp: orthogonal to every form that is good, orthogonal to the lifts and killed by the Hecke polynomial at the test value at some split place w off the level); (v) for every \vartheta, table \zeta, prime l with an l-adic place \varphi, l coprime to N(Q_\vartheta), if every \operatorname{conj} \zeta(a) is integral at \varphi \circ \operatorname{conj} then gl_\vartheta \zeta is integral at \varphi; (vi) for every table \zeta and automorphism \sigma: smallHookNumber j\cdot(gl_1 \zeta).\operatorname{normSqAt} \sigma \le \exp(2rB_0)\cdot \sum_a \operatorname{weylNormSq}(\sigma \circ \zeta_a).
