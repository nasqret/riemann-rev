lemma exists_line_stabilizer_log

What it saysLet E be a number field, w a finite place of E with completion E_w, local integers O_w, maximal ideal \mathfrak{p}_w and residue field k_w with q_w elements, N \ge 1 an integer (the instance NeZero N), and m a natural number with m \mid q_w - 1 (hm). Let u be a unit of E_w lying in the unit group of O_w (the proof h), and suppose (hu) that every z \in k_w^\times lies in the cyclic subgroup generated by the reduction \overline{u} \in k_w^\times of u. Then there exist a subgroup H of \mathrm{GL}_N(k_w) (indices 0, \ldots, N-1), a homomorphism a \colon H \to \mathbb{Z}/m (\mathbb{Z}/m written multiplicatively), functions lg, d \colon E_w \to \mathbb{Z}/m and an element \gamma \in \mathrm{GL}_N(k_w) such that, writing \operatorname{Tr}_a \colon \mathrm{GL}_N(k_w) \to \mathbb{Z}/m for the transfer of a from H to \mathrm{GL}_N(k_w) and \overline{g} \in \mathrm{GL}_N(k_w) for the reduction \operatorname{reduceGL}(g) of g: (1) for every g in Kw(\operatorname{Fin} N, w) = \mathrm{GL}_N(O_w): (a) \overline{g} \in H if and only if g lies in \operatorname{blockParahoric}(i \mapsto \min(1, i)), i.e. g_{i_0} \in \mathfrak{p}_w for all i \ge 1; (b) a(\overline{g}) = lg(g_{00}) whenever \overline{g} \in H; (c) \operatorname{Tr}_a(\overline{g}) = d(\det g); (d) if g lies in the pro-p Iwahori subgroup proIwahori of \mathrm{GL}_N(O_w), then \operatorname{Tr}_a(\overline{g}) = 1 and a(\overline{g}) = 1 (whenever \overline{g} \in H); (2) lg(u) is the generator 1 of \mathbb{Z}/m; (3) \operatorname{Tr}_a(\gamma) = d(u); (4) N!\cdot \operatorname{Tr}_a(\gamma) = N! in \mathbb{Z}/m (written additively).
The argument in briefTake for H the stabiliser of the first coordinate line and a(h) = l(h_{00}), l \colon k_w^\times \to \mathbb{Z}/m sending \overline{u} to 1. The transfer of a agrees with d\circ \det, d(x) = \operatorname{Tr}_a(\operatorname{diag}(x, 1, \ldots, 1)), on diagonal matrices (conjugate the entries to the first place) and on transvections (killed since q \equiv 1 mod m), hence everywhere. For \gamma = \operatorname{diag}(\overline{u}, 1, \ldots, 1), comparing the transfer of the central element \overline{u}\cdot 1 (a(\overline{u})^{[\mathrm{GL}_N:H]}, index \equiv N mod m) with d(\overline{u}^N) gives N\cdot d(\overline{u}) = N, so N!\cdot d(\overline{u}) = N!.
