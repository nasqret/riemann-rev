Chapter V · Unit 150

Theta functions of the CM curve: periods and scales, tight frames, the integral vacuum, Galois conjugates of jets, and divisibility of jets at non-split primes

note and structured proofs · 89 declarations · 36 structured proofs · 1 note

The folder New/Curve. It fixes a CM elliptic curve \mathcal{E} = \mathbb{C}/\Omega_0\mathcal{O}_K for K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d}) and the scales s_0, s_1 and \operatorname{per} at which Taylor series of theta functions are taken; builds tight frames (substitutes for orthonormal bases) of every large length in E = K(\zeta_{N_1}); shows that the scaled vacuum \kappa_0^n \cdot \operatorname{vac} of a standard hermitian space has p-adically integral values with bounded archimedean conjugates; and describes how \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{C}/K) acts on the jets (Taylor coefficients) of theta functions. At primes p inert or ramified in K it proves a normal form of theta series (an integral series times one exponential), the resulting lower bound for the p-adic valuation of Schur jets (divisibility, norm_schurJet_le) and its sharpness (exists_hasLargeSplit_of_pairs, CMLattice.katz_divisibility_limit), and it defines the asked divisibility (Data.localAsk, Data.gain), the allowed loss and the column counts \kappa used by the chain of theta lifts.
