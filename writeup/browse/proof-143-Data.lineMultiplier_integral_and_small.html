lemma Data.lineMultiplier_integral_and_small

What it saysFor parameters P, admissible data D over P and a lift index j: there is c \ne 0, one for all i, \vartheta and h (it depends on j only), such that for every choice i \in MemberIndex j, every tame character \vartheta and every point h, the vector y = \operatorname{conj}(c\cdot \operatorname{lineMultiplier} \vartheta j i h) satisfies (i) for every prime p with a chosen p-adic place (PadicPlace p) and p coprime to N(Q_\vartheta) (ϑ.levelNorm): \operatorname{weylGauge}(k^+, 0, h, y) \le 1 (y is integral at h); and (ii) if Q_\vartheta = \emptyset, then \operatorname{weylNormSq}(\varphi \circ y) \le \exp(r\cdot B_0) for every automorphism \varphi of \mathbb{C} (r = D.r, B_0 = D.B₀). Here \vartheta is a tame character (Data.TameChar: weight 0, tame part trivial off its finite set Q_\vartheta); Q_\vartheta = \emptyset makes every finite tame component of \vartheta trivial, but \vartheta itself need not be the trivial character, so (ii) is not restricted to the trivial twist.
The argument in briefWrite y as a double class sum of conjugated products of three theta kernels (carrier class sum, the negative definite constant, the L^+ kernel of the cleared coordinates H\cdot \operatorname{linePolyCoords}); exists_modKernel_integral_bound for the three pairs gives the constant c, the p-integrality of each factor (with the points made integral above p) and, when Q_\vartheta = \emptyset (then N(Q_\vartheta) = 1, as the conjugate bounds of exists_modKernel_integral_bound need q = 1), the conjugate bounds, which the scales absorb into \exp(rB_0).
