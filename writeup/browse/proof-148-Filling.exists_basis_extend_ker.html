lemma Filling.exists_basis_extend_ker

What it saysFor a field k, finite P and \sigma, a \sigma\times P matrix A over k (implicit), a P\times\sigma matrix x (implicit) with \det(A\cdot x)\ne0 (h) and a subspace L of k^{P} (implicit) containing the columns of x (hL): there are s\in\mathbb{N} and a matrix y with s columns such that s=\dim(L\cap\ker A), the block matrix (x|y) has a left inverse, its columns \operatorname{span}L, the columns of y lie in L,A\cdot y=0, and y\cdot t\in colSpan(x) only for t=0. (The census also lists the section variable y and the variables P,\tau it depends on; the statement binds its own y.)
The argument in briefTake a basis y of L\cap\ker A; with C=(Ax)^{-1}A, the matrix (x|y) has left inverse (C; l- lxC) and spans L.

    Let y be a basis matrix of L\cap\ker A with left inverse l (exists_basis_matrix), s its number of columns =\dim(L\cap\ker A), and C=(Ax)^{-1}A, so that Cx=1 and Cy=0 (Ay=0 by mul_eq_zero_of_range_le_ker). Then (C; l- lxC) is a left inverse of (x|y); every v\in L is x(Cv)+(v-\mathtt{xCv}) with v- xCv \in L\cap\ker A, so the columns \operatorname{span}L; the columns of y lie in L,Ay=0; and if yt=xu then 0= Cyt =u, so yt=0 and t=l(yt)=0.
