lemma exists_hasRow_auxPair

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data for P, \ell a prime with a fixed ring isomorphism \iota \ell : \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell (\iota : PadicIso \ell), and p : FinPlace E(D) \times \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C} a system of Hecke eigenvalues that occurs in a nonzero good form on W_m: goodForms(D, spaceW(D, m)) \cap eigenPart(D, spaceW(D, m), p) \ne 0 (H). Then for every j with 1 \le j \le m there is an \ell-adic representation T with HasRow (auxPair D 1 j) (w \mapsto (i \mapsto p(w, i))) T: a row for the untwisted j-th auxiliary pair and the system p that serves every twist (T is a row, and for every tame character \vartheta and every pair V with character \mu _j\cdot \vartheta, the normalised representation of every eigenform on V lifting p has trace ϑ.invAvatar\cdot \operatorname{tr} T + \sum _{e<\operatorname{rankDiff}} \operatorname{cyc}^{-e}). (The statement uses \iota \ell, so Lean also takes the section variables \{\ell \}, [ThePrime \ell] and (\iota : PadicIso \ell), which the line census does not list: a prime \ell and a fixed ring isomorphism \iota \ell : \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell; to be confirmed with \#check.)
The argument in briefA nonzero good form z in the p-part gives an algebraic form L z of some weight, with a good label c at the chosen places; its attached \ell-adic representation is a labelled column for the untwisted j-th pair with the apt labels c, its traces are the Hecke values, and its jumps are those of the rows; exists_row_for_all_twists gives the row.
