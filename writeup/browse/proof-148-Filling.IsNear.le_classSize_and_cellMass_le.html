lemma Filling.IsNear.le_classSize_and_cellMass_le

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified and S admissible. Let \gamma\in G(E) be near (IsNear(S,\gamma): some archimedean point y with y and ratToInf(S)(\gamma)^{-1}\cdot y in boxedPoints(S)) and let x= termDiscNorm(S,\gamma)=\prod_v|\Phi_v(\mathsf{g}(\gamma))|\ge1. Then (1) b^{-k^{3}f}\le classSize(S,\top,x), and (2) \prod_v cellMass(\mathtt{ratToInf}(S)(\gamma)_v,\operatorname{deg}_{v},\mathtt{boxSize}(S), \#I_v)\le(\nu\cdot\mathtt{classSize}(S,\top,x)/2){}^{2}, where \top=\varrho+1,\operatorname{deg}_{v} and I_v are the degree and the index set of the Bessel system of S at v, boxSize(S)=b^{2(\varrho+1)^{2}} and \nu= unipCovolume(S).
The argument in briefBoth inequalities are numerical: after the reduction to j=\top(D=\mathsf{d},\min(\varrho,\top)=\varrho), the first follows from \tau^{44}\mathsf{d}_1\le\mathsf{d} and the bound b^{11n^{3}} for the leading minors of a near term; the second is the logarithm of the bound on the packet constant, packetConst \le(1+b^{-9})\mathsf{d}\cdot\deg\cdot c_G^{f}/\operatorname{vol}(\hat{O})^{n}\cdot(\nu\cdot\mathtt{frameScaleProd}/\#\mathtt{BesselCoord})^{2}, combined linearly with the size inequalities.
