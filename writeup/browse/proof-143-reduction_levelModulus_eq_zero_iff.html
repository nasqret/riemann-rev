lemma reduction_levelModulus_eq_zero_iff

What it saysFor a field E with a CM type, finite types r and n, an auxiliary pair O : AuxPair E r n, a natural number c and an integral finite adele x \in \hat{O}_E: x reduces to 0 modulo M^c (the chosen reduction of O.levelModulus c false) if and only if t^{-c}\cdot x lies in \hat{O}_E, where t = O.genIdele is the chosen generator of M.
The argument in briefPlace by place: reduction modulo M^c vanishes iff x has valuation at least c at each chosen place; the generator t has valuation 1 there and is 1 elsewhere.

    Using that O.levelModulus 1 is a modulus (its second field), the specification of the generator and of the reduction (generator_spec), and valuations: x reduces to 0 modulo M^c = \prod w_i^c (distinct primes, multiplicity of a power) iff v_{w_i}(x) \ge c at every chosen place w_i; since v_{w_i}(t) = 1 there (valued_generator) and t is 1 elsewhere, this holds iff v_w(t^{-c}x) \ge 0 at every finite place w, i.e. t^{-c}x \in \hat{O} (mem_Ô).
