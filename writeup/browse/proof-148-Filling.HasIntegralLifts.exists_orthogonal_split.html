lemma Filling.HasIntegralLifts.exists_orthogonal_split

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S filling data, c a class, \gamma\in G(E),\tau a finite type, m a rational matrix with \tau columns, s\in\mathbb{N},y a rational matrix with s columns, and f\in\operatorname{GL}_{\tau\oplus s}(\mathbb{A}_f). Suppose (o) m^{*}\cdotlinkMetric(S)\cdot y=0. If HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,(m|y)\cdot f), then there are a\in\operatorname{GL}_{\tau}(\mathbb{A}_f),v\in GL_s(\mathbb{A}_f) and an adelic \tau\times s matrix z with HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m\cdot a), HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m\cdot z+y\cdot v) and linkDiscSq(S,m,a)\cdotlinkDiscSq(S,y,v)= linkDiscSq(S,(m|y),f). (Notation: T= ratMiddle(S) is the Gram matrix of the rational middle form X_0\oplus Ez with z of square h(z,z)=-N(S) (T= middleGram(N(S)): 1 on X_0 and -N(S) at z), J= ratGram(S) its split Gram matrix on BesselIndex(\mathfrak{s}); Z(\gamma)= frameProducts(S,\gamma); D_j(\gamma)= leadingMinor(Z(\gamma),j); \mathsf{g}(\gamma)= termDisc(S,\gamma); \operatorname{link}(\gamma)\subseteq(X_0\oplus Ez)^{2} is the image under linkMatrix(\gamma) of the J-orthogonal of the isotropic frame pair (f_a,\gamma f_a); zFormPair(T)=\operatorname{diag}(T_z,T_z) takes the z-pairing in both halves; linkMetric(S)=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\operatorname{diag}(T,T)+N(S)^{-1}\cdotzFormPair(T)^{*}zFormPair(T), the diagonal matrix with 1/2 at the X_0-coordinates and -N(S)/2+N(S)=N(S)/2 at the two z-coordinates; linkDiscSq(S,v,e)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(\det(v^{*}\cdot\mathtt{linkMetric}(S)\cdot v))|\cdot[\det e]^{2},[\cdot] the generalised index of a finite adele; minorNorm(S,j,\gamma)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(D_j(\gamma))|/N(S)^{2(j-\varrho)_+f}; termDiscNorm(S,\gamma)=\prod_v|\Phi_v(\mathsf{g}(\gamma))|; HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m): m= linkMatrix(\gamma)\cdot t\cdot w for a rational t with (isotropic frame pair of \gamma)*\cdot J\cdot t=0 and adelic w with classCoord(S,c)\cdot t\cdot w integral; f=f(E) the number of infinite places.)
The argument in briefComplete f to a primitive block matrix [[a,z],[0,v]] (exists_isPrimitiveMatrix_mul_fromBlocks); the columns of (m|y)\cdot f\cdot(\text{that block})^{-1}-pieces are lifts, so m\cdot a and m\cdot z+y\cdot v have integral lifts; the discriminants multiply because m\bot y for linkMetric (o) makes the Gram matrix block diagonal and the block matrix has unit generalised determinant.
