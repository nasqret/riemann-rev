lemma twoSidedSubst_firstColsCoord

What it saysLet \Phi, N and L be finite types, p a natural number, c a family of complex N\times N matrices and g a family of complex L\times L matrices indexed by \Phi, F and F' polynomials in the variables X(\tau, a, b) (a \in N, b \in \operatorname{Fin} p \oplus L) and s \in \mathbb{C}, with twoSidedSubst c (\tau \mapsto \operatorname{diag}(1_p, g_\tau)) F' = s\cdot F (hypothesis H). Then for every weight \kappa and every standard filling l of weight \kappa: twoSidedSubst c g (\sum_{l'} S_\kappa(c)_{l l'}\cdot \operatorname{firstColsCoord} \kappa F' l') = s\cdot \operatorname{firstColsCoord} \kappa F l, where S_\kappa(c) = schur \kappa c is the Schur matrix of c.
The argument in briefCommute the substitution past inFirstCols (it becomes a row substitution by c^{\mathsf{T}} on the first columns and the substitution c, g on the coefficients), and move the row substitution across the Fock pairing, where it becomes the Schur matrix.
