lemma star_inequality

What it saysThe inequality (\star ). For parameters P and admissible data D for P (Lean's Data P): m_D\cdot \operatorname{rem}(D) \le (\log b_D)^{2}\cdot \mathcal{B}(D), where m_D = D.m is the number of theta lifts, \operatorname{rem}(D) = D.remainder is the remainder of the weighted prime sum (unit 151: the sum over the tops \tau of D.primeSum(a_D, \tau ) - D.mainAsk), b_D = \exp(D.logB) is the third scale of D, and \mathcal{B}(D) = D.budget = f_D\cdot a_D\cdot (\log b_D)^{3} is the error budget (f_D the number of infinite places of the field E of D, a_D = D.a the least multiple of N_1 that is at least x_D\cdot b_D).
The argument in briefTheorems A and B give a large independent family of rows with small index and short untwisted lifts; Theorem C bounds its covolume from below by the main amount h_{\mathrm{main}}; comparing the two bounds and dividing by \dim rows, h_{\mathrm{main}} cancels and m\cdot \operatorname{rem} is at most a small multiple of the budget.
