lemma Filling.le_genIndex_det_of_fixesNormAtLeast

What it saysAgreeing characters force shared vectors up the flag. Let S' be filling data over a stably unramified CM number field E with its CM type (\mathfrak{s} implicit), k\in K= latStabilizer(S') whose reduction \overline{s} agrees (Agrees(S',\overline{s}); s), x a number, t an integral matrix (rows the basis of V, columns \tau', ht) and P= (framePair k){}^{H}\cdot t (i) its matrix of pairings with f and kf, whose rows for e_a and k e_a vanish (z: the forms vanish on X and kX), \rho:\tau'\simeq\{1,2\}, and \delta the determinant of the 2\times2 submatrix of P at the rows z,kz. If \delta is a unit of \mathbb{A}_f and FixesNormAtLeast(S',\overline{s},x), then x\le[\delta]= genIndex(\delta).
The argument in brief[\delta]=\#(\hat{O}/\delta\hat{O}) and x\le\prod_{p\in A}q_p=\#(\hat{O}/\ker) for the reduction \hat{O}\to\prod_{p\in A}\mathbb{F}_p; it suffices that \delta\equiv0 at every p\in A. If not at p, a descending induction over the frame shows that no nonzero vector of the flag can be fixed by \overline{s} modulo p, using integral Eichler elements and the agreement of the unipotent characters; this contradicts FixesNormAtLeast.
