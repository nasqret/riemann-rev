lemma IntegralPlace.IsStdLevel.floorZeroForm_le_main_term

What it saysAt a finite place: A a semilocal field with involution and integral place structure P (integers R, additive character \psi, modulus md), T a middle form with invertible hermitian whole Gram matrix J. F is a level of T with m+1 pairs, a standard level where stated (IsStdLevel). Lemma (bottom value). For a standard level, Z\in IntermediateGroup(F) and t+10^{10}n^{4}\le k: floorZeroForm(F,Z)\le\mathsf{M}(Z)\cdot\mathsf{M}(K(g))\cdot(\mathsf{M}(Z)+\operatorname{vol}(R\setminus R^{\times})^{t+1}\cdot2^{-(\varrho+1)}).
The argument in briefSplit \varkappa(Z) into the part over K(g) (exactly \mathsf{M}(Z)^{2}\mathsf{M}(K(g))) and over the complement; on the complement write the inner integral as an upper pairing, bound it through the pairing kernel and the shell bound with e=\min(\operatorname{vol}(R\setminus R^{\times}),1/2), using that points outside K(g) have floor values capped by e.
