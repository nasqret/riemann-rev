lemma Grading.exists_integral_eq_sum_over_cut

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, V a hermitian space (indices \iota, signs \varepsilon) with a grading Y (X plus a string of length s, norm N), A a level (k) and a a function on the real points. There is \kappa \in \mathbb{C} such that for every function P on the adelic unitary group G, every y automorphic on V, every level J, every finite set T of cosets of J and every b : G_f/J \to \mathbb{C} the following holds. If P is generic for Y (Grading.IsGeneric), y is automorphic, f = P\cdot y is integrable for the measure computing integrals over B(E^{+})\backslash G (B = Grading.besselGroup, B(E^{+}) its rational points Grading.ratBessel), T is stable under B_f \cap A (B_f = Grading.besselFin), coverMultiplicity(B_f, A, T, g_f J) \ne 0 whenever f(g) \ne 0, and \int_{[B]} f(r\cdot t) dr = [K_{0} : J]\cdot a(t_\infty)\cdot b(t_f J) for every t with t_f J \in T, then \int_{B(E^{+})\backslash G} f = \kappa\cdot\sum_{t\in T} b(t)/coverMultiplicity(B_f, A, T, t).
The argument in briefUnfold the quotient integral in stages over the finite Bessel group with a cutoff; the fibres are integrals over [B] given by hb, and the finite part is a sum over T weighted by 1/coverMultiplicity, because the average over B_f of \psi(u^{-1}t) = 1_{T}(u^{-1}t)/coverMultiplicity(t) is 1 where coverMultiplicity(t) \ne 0 (the u with u^{-1}t \in T form coverMultiplicity(t) cosets of the compact open B_f \cap A) and 0 where it vanishes, and f vanishes there (hs). (Lines 70–73 are the rest of the statement after its let f :=; the line census reads them as part of the value, so they stand in the introduction.)
