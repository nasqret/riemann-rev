lemma IsEquivariantLift.apply_stdColDatum_eq_zero

What it saysLet E be a field which is a CM number field with a CM type, with Weil data H (\jmath = H.j); n, a, b, m finite index types; s, t, r signs; \mathfrak{c} a split tame character of weight \mu (places S, local character \chi_{\mathfrak{c}}); \rho a character of the local product at S; X : Space(E, m, r), W_{1} : Space(E, n, s), W : Space(E, a, t) hermitian spaces; M, \mathcal{D} an equivariant lift for \mathfrak{c}, X and \rho (h : IsEquivariantLift \mathfrak{c} X \rho M \mathcal{D}); D a splitting of X over W_{1} along b and D_{1} a splitting of W_{1} over W along one line, both base-adapted (IsBaseAdapted D, IsBaseAdapted D₁); t' signs and V' : Space(E, b, t'). Let \Psi_{0} = stdColDatum(\chi_{\mathfrak{c}}, L') be the standard datum of the block L' of the frame adaptedFrame(readsAt(\mathfrak{c}), D). Assume (h_{0}): for all T, c, z, q, g, if either (i) there are an adelic b \times b matrix Y and k \in \mathrm{GL}_b(\hat{O}_{E}) with scaleIdele(E)\cdot x_{V'}\cdot Y = q, (finite part of Y)\cdot k unitary for the identity form, and J_{V'} = T, or (ii) T is invertible and, as an adelic matrix, not represented by t' (RepresentedBy is the \operatorname{Gram} equation at the infinite places: at some infinite place v there is no complex c with c^{*}\cdot \operatorname{diag}(t')\cdot c = T_v), then the coefficient centreCoeff(D, \jmath T, \mathcal{D}(\Psi_{0}), radicalElt(D, c, z)\cdotlevi(D, (q, g))) vanishes; and assume (h\Psi) \Psi_{0} \in M. Then for every \sigma : b \sqcup b \sqcup n \simeq m and every matrix z with entries in O (i): \mathcal{D}(stdColDatum(\chi_{\mathfrak{c}}, z)) = 0.
The argument in briefApply IntegralFrame.partner_mem_of_unimodular_vanishing to P = M \cap ker \mathcal{D}: it suffices that \mathcal{D}\Psi = 0 for every \Psi \in M killed by the admissible functionals. By weak approximation and the Fourier expansion along the centre, \mathcal{D}\Psi = 0 once all its coefficients vanish; these are admissible functionals of \Psi, so it remains that the coefficient of \mathcal{D}(\Psi_{0}) at a unimodular T vanishes, which Freitag's lemma reduces to h_{0}, via Landherr's theorem (an orthonormal frame over \hat{O}).
