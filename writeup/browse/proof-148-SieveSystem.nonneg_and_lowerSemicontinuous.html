lemma SieveSystem.nonneg_and_lowerSemicontinuous

What it saysLet C carry a topology that is first countable, let K= SieveSystem(u,\iota,n,J,\psi,En,\Lambda,\beta) be a sieve system on C (C,U,\tau,u,\iota,n,J,\psi,En,\Lambda,\beta as in SieveSystem, \tau finite), i\le n (hypothesis h), b:C\to(\tau\to(\mathbb{N}\to U)) a family of sequences depending on x\in C and c:C\to U such that b(x)_p has entry c(x) at i for all x and p (hypothesis e). Put F(x)=\sum_{p,q}J_{i+1}(b(x)_p,b(x)_q). Then F(x) is a nonnegative real number for every x (0\le F in the order of \mathbb{C}), and at every x at which all the maps x'\mapsto b(x')_p are continuous, x\mapsto\max(\operatorname{Re}F(x),0) (as an element of [0,\infty]) is lower semicontinuous.
The argument in briefDownward induction on i from n: at i=n positivity is the posSemidef field and lower semicontinuity comes from continuity; from i+1 to i,F is the integral of the corresponding function one level up (the step property), so nonnegativity integrates and Fatou's lemma gives lower semicontinuity.
