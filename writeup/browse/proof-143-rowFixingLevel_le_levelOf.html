lemma rowFixingLevel_le_levelOf

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type, r, n, N, m and a finite types, O : AuxPair E r n, z \colon r \hookrightarrow m and V a divided splitting (DividedSplitting N m a O z), and s an a\times N matrix over O_E/M^2. Then rowFixingLevel O.1 (V.stackOverGenScalar s) V.fixedEmb \le levelOf O.chosenPlace \perp: every element of that level lies in K_0 and reduces to 1 at every chosen place of O.
The argument in briefThe lower block of w\cdot A = w (w = [s; \overline{\varpi}\cdot 1]) is \overline{\varpi}\cdot \overline{A} = \overline{\varpi}\cdot 1, which by genResidue_smul_eq_iff means \overline{A} \equiv 1 at every chosen place.
