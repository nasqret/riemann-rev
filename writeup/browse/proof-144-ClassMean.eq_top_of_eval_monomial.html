Structured proof · Unit 144

lemma ClassMean.eq_top_of_eval_monomial

What it saysZariski density. Let W be a commutative local ring, O a commutative ring, \iota : W \to O a ring homomorphism, \sigma a finite type, n, e natural numbers, d \in W[x_{\sigma}] homogeneous of degree e (hd) with n + e \le Nat.card of the residue field of W (hW; Nat.card is 0 for an infinite field), and b_{0} \in W^{\sigma} with d(b_{0}) a unit (hb). If an O-submodule p of O^{\text{Monomials} \sigma n} contains the vector (\iota(b^{\gamma}))_{\gamma \in \text{Monomials} \sigma n} for every b \in W^{\sigma} with d(b) a unit (hp), then p = O^{\text{Monomials} \sigma n}.
The argument in briefPull p back along v \mapsto \iota \circ v to a W-submodule q of W^{Mon}; it suffices that q is everything. By Nakayama it suffices that every W-linear e from W^{Mon}/q to the residue field \kappa vanishes. The polynomial \sum_{\gamma} e(e_{\gamma})x^{\gamma} times the reduction \overline{d} of d vanishes on all of \kappa^{\sigma} (where \overline{d} \ne 0, the point lifts to b with d(b) a unit and \mathit{hp} applies) and has degree n + e \le |\kappa |, so it is 0; as \overline{d} \ne 0, all e(e_{\gamma}) vanish.
