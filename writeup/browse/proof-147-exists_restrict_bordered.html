lemma Data.exists_restrict_bordered

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data and g a real point. There is a linear map r from gridLevelForms(D, 0) (forms of principal level gridLevelGen on Z_{0,0} = Y_{0} \oplus \langle 1\rangle) to the forms on Y_{0} (the space gridSpace(0, false, 0, 0)) of level chosenLevel = K(g_{0}g_{1}) such that for every \varphi \in gridLevelForms(D, 0), every m-fold twist \vartheta and every form y on Y_{0} of principal level at the places of levelPlaces(D, \vartheta) (fixed by levelOf(placeAt, \perp)): petersson(Y_{0}, y, r(\varphi)) = diagonalPeriodForm(\vartheta, 0 \le m, g)(\operatorname{conj}(y), \varphi), i.e. \langle y, r\varphi \rangle = \int \overline{y}(x)\cdot \varphi((x \oplus 1)g) \mathit{dx}.
The argument in briefr is the adjoint (on the finite-dimensional space of level chosenLevel) of the period functional for the trivial twist; for general \vartheta the period of a level-\vartheta form equals that of its average over chosenLevel, because the twisted and untwisted periods differ by translations that fix \varphi.
