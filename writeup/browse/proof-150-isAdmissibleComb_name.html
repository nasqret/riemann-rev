lemma isAdmissibleComb_name

What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, n a finite index type, V a hermitian space over E of rank n (signs s), \mathfrak{m} a modulus of E (a nonzero ideal of \mathcal{O}_E never divisible by both a prime and its conjugate), \Psi \colon(n \to \mathcal{O}_E/\mathfrak{m}) \to \mathbb{C}, p a proposition, B \in \mathbb{R}, u, v \in \mathcal{S}(V), g \in \mathcal{O}_E and q \in \mathbb{N}. Assume g\cdot q \in \mathfrak{m}, every value \Psi(x) is integral away from q (integralAway q: |\iota(\Psi(x))| \le 1 for every ring map \iota \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_l, l prime to q), if p holds then \sum_x |\sigma(\Psi(x))| \le B for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}, and P^\mathfrak{m} u = v (Space.projOn at the places of \mathfrak{m}). Let d be a finite adele (in Lean an element of the subtype \{d | \mathfrak{m}.\operatorname{reduction}(d\cdot q) = 0\}, by default g). Then \Psi \otimes u = \operatorname{name}_V(\operatorname{liftFn}_\mathfrak{m} \Psi)(u) (Space.name of the lift of \Psi to finite adelic vectors) is an admissible combination (IsAdmissibleComb) of the vectors \operatorname{finHeisOp}_V(x)(v): \Psi \otimes u = \sum_x r(x)\cdot \operatorname{finHeisOp}(x)(v) for a finitely supported r whose support consists of x with \overline{d}\cdot x_i integral at every place not dividing q (translations n q \overline{d}), with N(g)^{|n|}\cdot r(x) integral away from q for every x, and with \sum_x |\sigma(r(x))| \le B for every \sigma if p holds.
The argument in briefExpand \Psi by finite Fourier inversion on (\mathcal{O}_E/\mathfrak{m})^n into characters b \mapsto \psi_\mathfrak{m}(\cdot b); the name of a character applied to u is a root of unity times a finite Heisenberg translate of P^\mathfrak{m}u = v. So \Psi \otimes u is a two-layer combination: outer coefficients \Psi(a) (integral away from q, size \le B) and inner coefficients |\mathcal{O}_E/\mathfrak{m}|^{-n} times roots of unity, which N(g)^n makes integral away from q.
