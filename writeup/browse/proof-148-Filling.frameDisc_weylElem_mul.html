lemma Filling.frameDisc_weylElem_mul

What it saysFor a shape \mathfrak{s}, a field A with involution, a Gram matrix T on V_{\varrho} over A, a sequence p:\mathbb{N}\to A^{\times},\sigma\in U(\mathtt{T.splitGram}) with \sigma\in\operatorname{GT}_{\varrho} (\sigma fixes every e_a and e_a^{*}; h\sigma) and N\in A: with t=Z(\sigma)_{\varrho\varrho}=h(z,\sigma z) and d=p_0p_1\cdots p_{\varrho-1}, frameDisc(N,Z(w_p\sigma))=d\cdot\overline{d}\cdot(t\cdot\overline{t}-N^{2}), where w_p= weylElem(\varrho,p).
