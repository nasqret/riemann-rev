lemma Filling.sum_le_of_positive_type

What it saysLemma (second moment with constant one). Let E be a number field with a CM structure and Weil data, \mathfrak{s} a shape, S admissible filling data over E of shape \mathfrak{s}, and V a complex inner product space. Let \Theta:\mathsf{G}\to\mathbb{R} with (h0) \Theta\ge0, and u:\mathsf{G}\to V with (hu) \langle u(a),u(b)\rangle=\Theta(a^{-1}b) for all a,b (\Theta has positive type). Let A be a finite set of chosen places of S above q_0(S) and D frame-vector data (for each chosen place w above q_0(S): a flag and a nonzero vector over \mathbb{F}_w). Suppose (hB) \sum_{s\in\Omega}\Theta(s)\le\operatorname{energy}(\mathtt{amplitudeSum}(S,G(E), \cdot, \cdot, \cdot),\Omega) for every subset \Omega of \mathsf{G}, and (hA) \Theta(s)\ne0 implies s\in vectorFixer(S,w,D_w) for every w\in A (s, or its inverse adjoint by the flag of D_w, fixes the frame vector of D_w modulo w). Then \sum_{s\in\mathsf{G}}\Theta(s)\le(1+9b(S)^{-4})\cdot\mathsf{d}(S)\cdot(b(S)^{6k(\mathfrak{s})})^{-\#A}. (Notation: \mathsf{G}= GRes(S)=\prod_{i\in\{0,1\}}\mathsf{G}_i,\mathsf{G}_i=\prod_{w\in\mathtt{chosenPlaces}(S,i)}\operatorname{GL}_{\mathtt{BesselIndex}(\mathfrak{s})}(\mathbb{F}_w); \iota_i(s)= Pi.mulSingle(i,s)\in\mathsf{G} for s\in\mathsf{G}_i; amplitudeSum(S,T,c,s,c')\in\ell^{2}(\mathtt{BesselCoord}\times\mathtt{BesselCoord}) is the sum of \operatorname{amplitude}(S,c,c',\gamma) over the rational \gamma\in T that are terms over (c,s,c'), with the full Bessel functions; \mathsf{S}(E)= besselS of the rational middle form; \nu(S)= unipCovolume(S); \operatorname{energy}(A,\Omega)=\sum_{c,c'}\sum_{s\in\Omega}\lVert A(c,s,c')\rVert^{2}/\nu(S)^{2}; \mathsf{d}(S)= mainEnergy(S)= columnDim(S)^{2}/(\mathtt{besselDegree}(S)\cdot\operatorname{mass}(E,r(\mathfrak{s}))); \mathsf{d}_1(S)= besselEnergy(S)= \#reducedImage(S,\mathsf{S})\cdotcolumnDim(S); columnDim(S)= \#Classes(S)\cdot\#BesselCoord(S).)
The argument in briefStrong downward induction on A. If the bound failed for A, the energy over fixingSet(A,D) (\le(1-b^{-4})(1+9b^{-4})\mathsf{d}y_A) would be less than (1-b^{-4})\sum\Theta, so the \Theta-mass off the fixing set would exceed b^{-4}\sum\Theta. Off the fixing set (and with \Theta\ne0, so the vectors of A are fixed) some leading minor of frameGram(s_{0,w}) vanishes at a chosen w\notin A; for each order l\in\{0,\ldots,\varrho+1\}, card_mul_sum_det_le bounds the \Theta-mass of \{\det=0\} by (2\sum\Theta+q_0^{l}\cdot\sqrt{q_0^{2\varrho+k+1}}\cdot Y)/q_0 (the leading minor of order l is the determinant of an l\times l matrix: F has l columns), where Y bounds the \Theta-mass of the fixers of a vector at w — by the induction hypothesis for A\cup\{w\},Y\le b^{-6k}\sum\Theta. Summing over the \varrho+2 orders gives \le3(\varrho+2)\sum\Theta/b^{10}<b^{-4}\sum\Theta, a contradiction.
