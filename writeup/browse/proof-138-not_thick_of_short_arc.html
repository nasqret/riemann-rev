lemma not_thick_of_short_arc

What it saysSpacing of \operatorname{thick} places: for admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, e : E \to \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell , g, g', g_1 \in G_\ell and a, i, n \in \mathbb{N} such that the places of complexEmbOf e g and complexEmbOf e g' are \operatorname{thick} (t, t'), (g'g^{-1})(e(\zeta )^{\ell ^a}) = (e(\zeta )^{\ell ^a})^{\ell ^n} (h), (g'g_1^{-1})(e(\zeta )^{\ell ^a}) = (e(\zeta )^{\ell ^a})^{\ell ^i} (h_1), \ell \le k\cdot t (hk; k = D.k, t = D.t), 0 < i (hi), i < n (hn) and n \le 27s (hL; s = D.s): the place of complexEmbOf e g_1 is not \operatorname{thick}.
The argument in briefSuppose the place of g_1 is \operatorname{thick}. The squared ratio relations of placeClass_ratio give \ell ^{2i} as a class of a ratio of \operatorname{thick} places; for \ell = 2 this contradicts the order 3^{K-1} > 27s of 4 modulo 3^K, K = \lfloor \log _3 s\rfloor + 5, and for \ell odd the three-term condition modulo 2^{s+1} forces \ell ^{2i} \equiv 1 \bmod 2^{s+1}, impossible for small i.
