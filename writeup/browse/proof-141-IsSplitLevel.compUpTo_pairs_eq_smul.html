What it saysLet E be a field with a CM type and Weil data, P a split level for the prime N (instance \Lambda), X_j finite index types, \pi_j representations of \mathrm{GL}_{X_j+1}(\prod_{w\in P} E_w), L_j lift laws from \pi_j to \pi_{j+1} with constants \gamma_j (R, for all j), d_i \in \mathbb{C}, \varphi \in \operatorname{ramified}(\pi_0) (h) and p \in \mathbb{N}. The composite of the p maps \psi \mapsto d_i\cdot\pi_{2i+2}(u_{0})(\sum_{y} L_{2i+1}[\mathbf{c}_y](L_{2i}[\mathbf{b}_y]\psi)) (i = 0, \ldots, p-1; y over ((\mathbb{Z}/N)^{P})^{X_{2i+1}+1}) sends \varphi to (\prod_{i<p} d_i\cdot o_i\cdot\prod_{l<2i+2} \gamma_l)\cdot L_{2p-1}[\mathbf{b}_e](\cdots L_0[\mathbf{b}_e](F^{p} \varphi)), where F = fourierOp(\pi_0) and o_i is the number of y \in ((\mathbb{Z}/N)^{P})^{X_{2i+1}+1} that are non-zero at every w \in P (some entry y_a(w) is a unit).
The argument in briefInduction on p. In the step, the next pair applied to the image of F^{q} \varphi: each sum over y collapses to o_i copies of the y = e term (the non-unit terms vanish by ramification, the others are moved to e by the equivariance), and u₀_charDatum_eq_fourierOp turns the e-term into F.
