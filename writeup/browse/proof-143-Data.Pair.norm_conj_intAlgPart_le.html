lemma Data.Pair.norm_conj_intAlgPart_le

What it saysLet P be parameters, D admissible data over P, p a pair (Data.Pair: an integer 1 \le z \le t and a character \nu of U(1)(\mathbb{A}) of weight -c with z \le c(v) \le 4b), n a finite type, H a proof that p is flat for n (p.IsFlat n: c(\tau) = z at every infinite place \tau and |n| = 2z \ge 4), and \sigma an automorphism of \mathbb{C}. Then |\sigma(\operatorname{intAlgPart} H.2 1)| \le \exp(x^{34}), where intAlgPart H.2 1 is the normalised value Mk\cdotden^2\cdot \lVert \Xi \rVert^2/\operatorname{Nm}(Q)^{|n|-1} at the trivial twist (\Xi the lift of the trivial character from the definite U(1) to the definite U(|n|), Mk = D.chosenGroupOrder, den = denomBound).
The argument in briefBound the squared norm of every conjugate of the trivial lift by the conjugate bound for jets, then the class sums by the number of classes, all within \exp(x^{34}) by the scales.
