    The restriction of \sigma to K is one of the two embeddings of the imaginary quadratic field K into \mathbb{C}: the inclusion or its complex conjugate (mk_eq_iff). In the first case \sigma itself fixes K, and \sigma' = \sigma. In the second case \sigma' = \sigma followed by complex conjugation fixes K, and |\sigma'(z)| = |\operatorname{conj} \sigma(z)| = |\sigma(z)|.
