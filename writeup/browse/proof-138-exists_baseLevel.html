lemma exists_baseLevel

What it saysFor admissible data D, a prime \ell with \iota \ell fixed, an auxiliary pair O (n nonempty), labels c apt for a twist \vartheta, with the set Q of \vartheta auxiliary for badPlaces D \ell: there are jumps J and a base level B of forms on the standard definite space of rank |n| of the weight of O whose stabiliser orders divide Mk = D.chosenGroupOrder (BaseLevel \ldots Mk: places S, a space Y of forms with an open-level character above S \cup \overline{S}, stable under the operators commuting with the translations there), such that (1) every y in the range of tupleProj O c has its smooth form in B.formsOfLevel (for the empty family of places); (2) for every form y whose smooth form is \ell-integral, Mk\cdot(tupleProj O c y) has \ell-integral smooth form; (3) for every finite type K, every family u : K \to places of E with no u_k in badPlaces D \ell, and every eigensystem x of B of level u, the representation R = normalisedRep O x.galRep is a labelled column IsLabelledColumn O (|n| - |r|) J (i \mapsto twistedLabel O c i\cdot (X - 1)^{|n|-|r|}) (Q \cup \overline{Q} \cup range u \cup its conjugate) R, and (R(g) - ϑ.invAvatar(g)\cdot 1)\cdot (R(g) - 1) = 0 for every g in the inertia above Q \cup \overline{Q}.
The argument in briefLet \rho be the action of the idempotent e_c through the representation of \prod \operatorname{GL}_r(k_w) on the level space; B is the base level whose space is the image of the vectors fixed by \rho. Membership and integrality follow from the structure of e_c (character values, roots of unity, |G| dividing Mk); for an eigensystem, the eigenform has product labels at the chosen places and parahoric types at the twist places, so its normalised representation is a labelled column with the quadratic relation.
