lemma exists_modKernel_integral_bound

What it saysLet L and N be finite types (the index sets of the two definite spaces I_L and I_N; the explicit section variables of line 134), P parameters, D admissible data over P (with field D.E) and d a natural number. Then there is c \ne 0 such that for all characters a, b with weights ea, eb and tame parts \psi, \psi', every modulus \mathfrak{m}, natural number q and function \Psi on matrices modulo \mathfrak{m} with q\cdot g^2 \in \mathfrak{m} (g = D.gProd) and every value of \Psi integral away from q (integralAway q: integral under every embedding into \mathbb{C}_\ell, \ell \nmid q), every polynomial Q that is homogeneous of degree d (Q(c'\cdot X) = c'^d\cdot Q \text{ for } c' \ne 0) and all finite points g of U(I_L) and h of U(I_N), the value v = c\cdot \theta^{I_L, I_N}_{a, b}[\Psi, Q]((1, g), h) (modKernelI for the definite spaces of D.E) satisfies: (i) for every prime p with a chosen embedding \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}_p, p coprime to q, if g and h are integral above p and Q has coefficients in the valuation ring, then |\iota_p(v)| \le 1; (ii) if |L| \le 40k, |N| \le 40k, d \le D.gProd and q = 1, then for every automorphism \sigma of \mathbb{C}, natural number M and real B such that |\sigma'(\Psi(x))| \le M for all automorphisms \sigma' and all x, and |\sigma(Q(z))| \le B whenever |\sigma(z_i)| \le D.gProd⁴ for all i: |\sigma(v)| \le \exp(x_1)\cdot M\cdot B (k = D.k, x_1 = D.x₁). The constant c depends on L, N, D and d only.
The argument in briefThe constant c is the one that turns the kernel into a jet of a scaled Taylor series; integrality comes from the standard data module, the conjugate bound from norm_jet_le_of_isAdmissibleComb with the size of the vacuum datum.
