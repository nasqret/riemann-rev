lemma Filling.exists_link_split_le_classGauge

What it saysLemma. Let E be stably unramified, S filling data, c,c' classes, s\in\mathsf{G} agreeing (hs), \gamma\in G(E) a term over (c,s,c') (h) of level j (hc), x real with FixesNormAtLeast(S,s,x) (hq), and \gamma'\in G(E). Write T^{\circ}= commonLink(\gamma,\gamma')\subseteq X_0 (the X_0-part of \operatorname{link}(\gamma)\cap\operatorname{link}(\gamma')\cap\ker zFormPair(T)). Then (i) 0< latticeDisc(S,c,T^{\circ}) and 1\le frameValueIndex(S,c,\mathtt{termPosition}(S,c,c',\gamma),1,T^{\circ}); and (ii) there are i\in\mathbb{N}, a finite type \rho, a rational matrix m with \rho columns, a rational matrix y with i columns, v\in GL_i(\mathbb{A}_f) and an adelic \rho\times i matrix z such that, with L=\operatorname{link}(\gamma)\cap\operatorname{link}(\gamma'): i=k+1-\dim L; k\le\dim T^{\circ}+i+(j-\varrho)_+; if i=0 then L=\operatorname{link}(\gamma); the columns of m\operatorname{span}L; the columns of y lie in \operatorname{link}(\gamma); y\cdot t\in L only for t=0; the columns of (m|y)\operatorname{span}\operatorname{link}(\gamma); HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m\cdot z+y\cdot v); and (pairInvariant(S,c,c',\gamma,\gamma')\cdot x'){}^{2}\cdotlinkDiscSq(S,y,v)\le classGauge(S,j,c,c',\gamma)^{2}, where x'=x if \varrho<j and i=0, else 1. Here pairInvariant(S,c,c',\gamma,\gamma')= latticeDisc(S,c,T^{\circ})\cdotframeValueIndex(S,c,\mathtt{termPosition}(c,c',\gamma),1,T^{\circ})\cdotsaturationIndex(S,\mathtt{termPosition}(c,c',\gamma)) and k=k(\mathfrak{s}). (Notation: T= ratMiddle(S) is the Gram matrix of the rational middle form X_0\oplus Ez with z of square h(z,z)=-N(S) (T= middleGram(N(S)): 1 on X_0 and -N(S) at z), J= ratGram(S) its split Gram matrix on BesselIndex(\mathfrak{s}); Z(\gamma)= frameProducts(S,\gamma); D_j(\gamma)= leadingMinor(Z(\gamma),j); \mathsf{g}(\gamma)= termDisc(S,\gamma); \operatorname{link}(\gamma)\subseteq(X_0\oplus Ez)^{2} is the image under linkMatrix(\gamma) of the J-orthogonal of the isotropic frame pair (f_a,\gamma f_a); zFormPair(T)=\operatorname{diag}(T_z,T_z) takes the z-pairing in both halves; linkMetric(S)=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\operatorname{diag}(T,T)+N(S)^{-1}\cdotzFormPair(T)^{*}zFormPair(T), the diagonal matrix with 1/2 at the X_0-coordinates and -N(S)/2+N(S)=N(S)/2 at the two z-coordinates; linkDiscSq(S,v,e)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(\det(v^{*}\cdot\mathtt{linkMetric}(S)\cdot v))|\cdot[\det e]^{2},[\cdot] the generalised index of a finite adele; minorNorm(S,j,\gamma)=|N_{E/\mathbb{Q}}(D_j(\gamma))|/N(S)^{2(j-\varrho)_+f}; termDiscNorm(S,\gamma)=\prod_v|\Phi_v(\mathsf{g}(\gamma))|; HasIntegralLifts(S,c,\gamma,m): m= linkMatrix(\gamma)\cdot t\cdot w for a rational t with (isotropic frame pair of \gamma)*\cdot J\cdot t=0 and adelic w with classCoord(S,c)\cdot t\cdot w integral; f=f(E) the number of infinite places.)
The argument in briefTake a basis m of L_0= link \gamma\cap link \gamma'\cap\ker zFormPair; p= its X_0-rows spans T^{\circ}= commonLink, and m^{*}\cdotLM\cdot m=p^{*}p. Extend m to a basis (m|u) of L= link \gamma\cap link \gamma' and then to a basis (m|u|y) of link \gamma by vectors y independent modulo L (exists_basis_extend_ker twice). Lift the whole basis (exists_lifts_linkDiscSq_le), split off y and then u orthogonally (exists_orthogonal_split); bound latticeDisc(c,T^{\circ})\cdotframeValueIndex by the discriminant of the m-lifts (sublatticeDisc_mul_index_sq_le), and minorNorm{}^{2} by linkDiscSq(u,\cdot)\cdot\max{}^{2} (minorNorm_sq_le_linkDiscSq_mul); the product of the three discriminants is the discriminant of the full lift, \le minorNorm{}^{2}/w^{2}, which assembles to the class-gauge bound.
